Who can use RM

The McGhee Lab Research Management application (“RM”, hosted at mcgheelab.com/rm/) is an internal tool for members of the McGhee Lab at the University of Arizona. Access is granted at the principal investigator’s discretion. By signing in you confirm that you are an authorized lab member, collaborator, or invited guest.

Access and revocation

The PI (Alex McGhee) may grant, modify, or revoke access at any time. New Google sign-ins start as guest and have no read access until promoted by the PI. You may also voluntarily delete your account by emailing mcgheealex@gmail.com.

Acceptable use

By using RM you agree NOT to:

  • Attempt to access another user’s private data without consent.
  • Probe, scan, or test the security of the system except as part of authorized lab security work.
  • Upload content that violates U.S. or Arizona law, university policy, or research ethics requirements (e.g. unredacted PHI without IRB approval).
  • Use RM for non-lab purposes (e.g. personal businesses, political activity).
  • Share your sign-in credentials with anyone else.

Content you create in RM that relates to lab work (publications, grant drafts, protocol records, inventory, etc.) is treated as McGhee Lab property under standard university IP policy. Personal notes you choose to keep in your private namespace remain yours. The lab retains the right to archive any content for academic record-keeping.
