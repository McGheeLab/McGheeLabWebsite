# PROTOCOL 

---

## 

## **Making the gel:**

1. Calculate needed agarose. Ex. 5 mLs of 2% wt agarose: 5 mL \* 0.02 \= 0.1 g agarose.  
2. Measure out agarose on analytical balance, add to 40 mL conical.  
3. Sterilize the outside of the beaker with 70% EtOH and bring it into the hood. Add 5 mL of 1X PBS to agarose, mix with serological pipette, add a stir bar, then place on the hot/stir plate.  
4. Heat on dot 3 (keep watch- don’t let boil\!), spin as low as possible while still mixing. Keep bubbles to a minimum by not letting the mixture boil or stirring too vigorously.   
5. Once liquid gel is clear, take it off the plate to cool. The gel should be completely solid (does not move when tilted) before sieving.

## **Sieve stack:**

6. While the gel is cooling, assemble your sieve stack. Start with the collection pan then sieves smallest to largest.

   Example sieve order: bottom \= 200 \-\> 300 \= top.

   Make sure the sieves are clean\!

    

## **Sieving (using example sizes):**

7. Once the gel has set, add it to the top sieve (300 um). Use the burger press to press the gel through.   
8. Once pressed, use the sterile water spray bottle to wash off the bottom of the beaker into the 300 sieve.  
9. Spray the gel down through the 300 into the next sieve (200 um), tapping/shaking the stack to encourage LLS and water through.  
10. Use the gel cutter to push and scrape the remaining gel through the top of the 300 sieve.  
11. Take the 300 sieve off the stack, but still hovering over it, and scrape what gel is clinging to the bottom of the 300 sieve off and into the 200 sieve using the gel cutter.  
12. Put the 300 sieve back on the stack and repeat steps 9-11. Spray, scrape the top, scrape the underside into the 200\.  
13. Once most of the gel is through the 300 sieve, sieve it again. Add all the LLS sitting on top of the 200 sieve back into the 300 sieve and repeat steps 7-12 to ensure the LLS is uniform.   
14. Once the gel has been passed through the 300 sieve again and only a small amount of gel remains in/on the 300 sieve, set it aside.  
15. Collect the LLS sitting on top of the 200 sieve into a labeled 15 mL conical, this is the 200-300 um LLS.

**Clean the used sieves:** 

16. Before the used sieves dry, clean them:  
    1. Over the collection pan, hold the sieve at an angle. Spray down the top of the sieve with sterile water so all particles flow down into the pan. Work your way down from the top,  tapping the sieve on the collection pan to shake off remaining water. Block the back of the sieve with your hand to reduce mess in the hood while spraying.   
    2. Flip the sieve over and spray down the underside in the same way.  
    3. Flip over again, spray down the top with 70% ethanol in the same way.  
    4. Set aside in the hood on a kimwipe, stack as you clean each sieve.  
    5. Dump excess water+EtOH from collection pan into sink.  
    6. Spray and wipe out the collection pan before putting it back into the hood.

## **Consecutive batches:**

17. Repeat the steps above (1-16) for each batch.   
18. Clean up\! Make sure to clean the used sieves as described above. If sieves are starting to build up residue or you are using them with a different type of gel next, deep clean them. Any LLS waste can be disposed of down the sink.

# CLEANING SIEVES

---

## **Materials:**

| 2-3 towels from under the sink Clean metal collection tray Kettle DI water sprayer | DI water Dirty sieves Clamp/handles |
| :---- | :---- |

## **Cleaning / “resetting” sieves:**

1. Take sieves out of the hood. After this, they are not sterile anymore and must be fully cleaned and autoclaved before next use.   
2. Set out two towels, put a clean collection tray on one, the other is to set the sieves on once they are clean.   
3. Boil the max amount of DI water in the kettle.  
4. Once the water is boiling, set a dirty sieve in the tray and pour the boiling water over it until the mesh is submerged (CAREFULLY\!\! BOILING WATER IS HOT\!\!).   
5. Use a clamp to grab the sieve. Swish it around in the hot water (CAREFULLY\!\!) to dislodge agarose and fully saturate the mesh. Side/side and up/down until the mesh is clean.   
6. Remove the sieve from the tray and hold it over the sink using a clamp. Use a DI water sprayer to spray down the mesh to remove any final particles.   
7. Set the sieve on a towel while you wash the others. Try not to touch the mesh to anything.   
8. Repeat steps 3-7 until all sieves are clean. Once they are cool enough to handle, add them to autoclave bags.   
9. Autoclave all sieves using the 20 min dry cycle (at least. If autoclaving with other items, always use the highest/longest setting necessary).
