TAB 3Boundaries block slip

Goal: show that the same load bends a coarse-grained metal but not a fine-grained one.

Predict

At the same applied stress, the metal with SMALLER grains will have…  ☐ more slipping grains   ☐ fewer slipping grains   ☐ the same.

Do

  1. Click Coarse grains, low load. Slowly raise the applied stress until the first slip lines appear. Record that stress. Keep raising until ~50% of grains slip.

TAB 5Hall–Petch: strength you can dial in

Goal: verify the real engineering law σy = σ₀ + k_y/√d with your own hands.

Do

  1. Click Annealed (coarse, 32 µm): σ₀ = 70 MPa, k_y = 0.74. Record σy.
  2. Click Fine-grained (8 µm). Record σy.
  3. Hand-check the 8 µm case: √d = √(0.000008 m) = 0.00283. Compute k_y ÷ √d = 0.74 ÷ 0.00283 = MPa, then add σ₀ = 70 MPa.
  4. Click Ultrafine (0.8 µm) and read the verdict box carefully.
