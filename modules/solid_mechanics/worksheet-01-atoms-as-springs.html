TAB 4Read an energy valley

Goal: connect the shape of the bond's energy valley to real material stiffness — no calculus needed, just the marble-in-a-bowl picture.

Predict

A deeper, narrower energy valley should make a material…  ☐ stiffer   ☐ softer   ☐ heavier

Do

  1. Click Argon (weak, van der Waals) and fill in the first table row from the stats.
  2. Click Metal-like (deep well) and fill in the second row.
