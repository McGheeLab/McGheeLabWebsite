Lab Foundations

Getting started in the lab

A one-stop shop for the techniques you'll actually use on your project. Each track is a short series of hands-on modules — built for newcomers, with no assumed background — that takes you from the core idea to doing it yourself at the bench. Pick the track your project needs and jump in.

How to use this series

three steps
1

Pick a track

Choose the topic that matches what your project needs to measure or make.

2

Work through it in order

Each module builds on the one before — start at the top and go straight down.

3

Apply it to your project

Take the technique to the bench, then bring your results and questions to your mentor.
