Big picture — put it together

1. Fourier's law is often written q = kAΔT/L, and engineers rewrite it as q = ΔT/R. In three sentences, explain what each of k, A, L and R does to the heat flow, using the words hand-off, doorway, journey and resistance.

2. Challenge (optional): a cast-iron skillet bottom is 5 mm thick, 0.05 m² in area, k ≈ 80 W/m·K, with 50 K between the burner side and the food side. q = kAΔT/L = W. Thermal resistance R = L/(kA) = K/W. Compare with the window in row B of Tab 4 — which is the bigger resistor, the skillet or one air film?
