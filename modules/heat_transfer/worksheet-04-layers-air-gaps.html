Big picture — put it together

1. A down jacket, a foam cooler, a double-glazed window and a fiberglass batt are all the same trick. In three sentences, say what the trick is. Use still air, convection and resistances in series.

2. Challenge (optional): insulation sold as "R-30" in US units has R_SI = 30 ÷ 5.678 ≈ m²K/W. With 20 K across it, one square metre leaks q = ΔT/R ≈ W. Build it in Tab 4 (fiberglass, k = 0.04): what thickness gets you there? R = L/k → L = R × k. L ≈ mm.
