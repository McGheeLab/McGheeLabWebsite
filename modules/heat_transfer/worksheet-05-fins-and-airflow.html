Big picture — put it together

1. Newton's law q = hA(T_s − T∞) gives an engineer three knobs. In three sentences, explain which knob blowing on soup turns, which knob a heatsink turns, and which knob is usually "fixed by the job".

2. Challenge (optional): your coffee (0.3 kg, c = 4186) sits in a mug with 0.03 m² of outside surface, at 60 °C in a 20 °C room, still air h = 10. Loss: q = hAΔT = W. (Think about which surfaces the breeze actually reaches.)
