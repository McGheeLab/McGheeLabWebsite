TAB 4Level Up: Gas → Chemistry

Goal: prove the process gas sets the surface chemistry — O₂ makes –OH, N₂ makes –NH₂, CO₂ makes –COO⁻ — and watch the contact angle fall as coverage grows.

Predict

Which gas will make the surface most water-loving (smallest contact angle)?  ☐ O₂ → hydroxyl   ☐ N₂ → amine   ☐ CO₂ → carboxyl

Do

  1. Click O₂ → hydroxyl. Drag Treatment time from 0 to 120 s and watch the groups accumulate while the contact-angle curve dives. Fill in the O₂ row (keep RF power at 50).
  2. Click N₂ → amine, then CO₂ → carboxyl, at the same time and power. Record the Grafted group, Coverage %, Contact angle, and Rate constant τ stats for each.
  3. On O₂, halve the RF power and see how much longer you must treat to reach the same coverage.
