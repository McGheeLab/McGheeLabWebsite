TAB 3Cassie–Baxter: Trapped Air

Goal: show that trapping air under the drop (small solid fraction f_s) gives BOTH a high angle and low stickiness — the lotus effect. Equation: cos θ* = f_s(cos θ + 1) − 1.

Predict

As the drop touches less solid (smaller f_s, more trapped air), the apparent angle θ* will head toward…  ☐ 90°   ☐ 120°   ☐ 180°

Do

  1. Click Lotus (f_s ≈ 0.1) at θ = 114°. Read Solid fraction, Air fraction, and Apparent θ*. Confirm f_s = 0.1 → θ* ≈ 160°.
  2. Click Dense pillars (f_s = 0.4), then Extreme (f_s = 0.03), recording each row. Watch the blue air pockets grow under the drop as f_s shrinks.
  3. Set θ = 100° (weak wax) at f_s = 0.1. Is θ* still superhydrophobic (≥ 150°)?
