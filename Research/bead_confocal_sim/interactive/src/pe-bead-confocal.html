Confocal image of a PE shell bead

An 8 µm bead carrying PE only on its surface, imaged on the Nikon AX R in one focal plane whose thickness the pinhole sets. Change the microscope, the bead and the background, and watch the image, the ring, and what each way of measuring it does.

Model: Research/bead_confocal_sim/optics.py, ported to this page and checked against it (interactive/tools/check_confocal.py: PSF, H(z) and integrated F within 0.2 %, rendered F and rim peak within 0.6 %); report Part B. Bead material from a geometric ray trace (report_calcs/ray.py), an estimate only. McGhee Lab · October 2026.
