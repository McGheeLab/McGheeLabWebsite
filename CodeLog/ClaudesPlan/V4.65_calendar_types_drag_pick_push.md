# V4.65 — Scientia Calendar: event types of your own, drag to create, a note from the recent ones or the Portfolio, Barrier → Custom… on the calendar

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "Calendar should give you the option to add kinds (rewrite as event type) to the calendar. There should be a drag option precise within 15 minute increments. Link to a note should be a click and popup of the recent notes, with an option to go in Portfolio and click on a note. Barrier should have a Custom button to allow you to click on the calendar of when it is going to be pushed back."

## What changed
- **"Kind" is now "Event type"** (Colour by, the create window, the card that opens in place).
  - **＋ Type** at the end of the types: a name and one of eight colours (`CAL.addType`, kept in `settings().calTypes`, so they follow you) adds a type and chooses it.
  - **Manage types…** (`CAL.manageTypes`) lists the built-in ones (Meeting · Experiment · Other · Focus) and yours, to rename, recolour or remove. A removed type's events show as Other.
  - `CAL.KINDS` is now a getter over `CAL.types()`.
- **Drag to create** (`CAL.dragNew`): press on an empty stretch and drag up or down. A dashed outline shows the time and the length as you go, in 15-minute steps ("10:00–11:30 · 1 h 30"). Letting go opens the create window with exactly that time; with Plan focus on, it places a focus block that long. A click without a drag still opens it for an hour.
- **Link a note…** (`CAL.notePick`, the create window and the card): a click shows your recent notes (by last change, with project and date), a search, and **Pick in the Portfolio…** (`CAL.pickInPortfolio`).
  - The Portfolio opens with a teal bar, "Click a note to link it to …", and Cancel.
  - A note opened there (`App.go` to a note, or a link to one) is linked instead (`CAL.pickDone`), and you are back in the calendar. A new event's window comes back as you left it (title, day, time, type), with the note.
- **Barrier → Custom…** (`CAL.pushStart`): the bar says "Click where … moves to", and a dashed outline follows the pointer with the new time and how far ("15:30–16:30 · +2 h 30").
  - A click there pushes it back by that much, and what depends on it as much (`CAL.pushBack`, whose toast still offers Custom… for each).
  - An earlier spot shows red and is refused. Esc or Cancel leaves.
- **Windows stay on the screen** (`CAL.keepIn`): the create window and the card move up when a list opens in them. The card that opens in place measures its height after its width.
