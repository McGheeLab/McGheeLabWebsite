# V4.82 — connect components to one step of a protocol, and change a link later

## Why
Gabe: "Have external component connect to specific protocol steps. Direct links from protocol steps can be modified, not
permanent to what you set them to."

Wires could end on a component or on one image of an image panel (`wire.fromCell` / `toCell` = the image's media id), never
on a step of a protocol. And a connection could be re-related, reversed or removed, but not pointed at something else.

## What changed
- **A wire end can be a step.** The same field the image cells use: `fromCell` / `toCell` = `'st:<step id>'`. Steps already
  had a stable id (`uid('st')` on every creation path), so nothing to add. `DocView.partRect` (override in the new
  `13zpb-steplinks.js`) gives the step's row at the box's width, with `only: ['l','r']`, so the wire leaves by the box's left
  or right edge at the step's height (`ROUTE.route` honours `A.only` / `B.only`; the no-route fallback too). A step that was
  reworded in a registered protocol (superseded) keeps its wire: it follows `supersededBy` to the live wording. A step that is
  gone, or a folded box, leaves the wire on the box. Works in a protocol note and in a run of it.
- **Making one.** Each step has a link circle on the box's left edge (rest on the step ~1 s, or "Always shown" in
  Settings) — drag it to a component. Dragging from any component and dropping on a step connects that step (the step is
  outlined while dragging); a drop on the rest of the box still connects the box. The step's ⋯ menu has "Connect this step to a
  component…" (a picker), in a protocol note and in a run.
- **Changing one.** The connection's menu (click its label) gains: Reconnect either end to another component…; for the end on
  a step, Move it to another step of that protocol… and Connect it to the whole protocol instead; for an end on a protocol box,
  Connect it to one step of it…. Reverse direction now swaps the parts too. A step's ⋯ menu lists the connections on that step,
  each opening this menu. A connection of an iteration ("what prompted it") in the step's hover list gets a × to take it off.
  (Relation, undirected and Remove were there.)
- Settings: new feature "Links to protocol steps" (`stepwires`, Canvas & notes, needs Connections) with a switch, the circle's
  display (hover / always) and a live preview. Off: no circle, no drop on a step, no new links; the links made stay drawn.
