# V4.22 — The References Map

## Goal
Gabe (17 Nov 2026): "If there are embedded links in the PDF from the citation in text to the actual reference at the end of the paper, have a section in the Highlights sidebar that is the References Map, which allows you to hover on a citation and see the associated paper without having to scroll down all the way. If you click on it, it will automatically bring you there. If there is a superscript number or a citation style based on the paper that you can associate to the journal, automatically find the citations if no links are evident for this to still work on older papers."

## Approach
- **The finding is the lab's own, unchanged** ([[reuse-lab-features-in-scientia]]). `scripts/scientia_build.py` writes `scientia/vendor/paper-cite.js` (`PCITE`, tracked, held by `--check`):
  - `rm/js/paper-citations.js`: its whole body (the reference-list parser, the citation scanners for every style, the link mapping, the style gate);
  - `rm/js/pdf-viewer.js`: `_resolveDest` and `extractTextItemsFromPdf`, cut by name.

  Only the wrapper is ours. `getDocument` hands back the app's own open document (`PDFW.load`) as a thin view whose `destroy()` does nothing. `PCITE` returns the pieces `analyzeDocument()` strings together, so the app keeps each citation's place.
- **`REFMAP` (`scientia/src/17h-refmap.js`, new) reads a paper once per file and mode:**
  - its words and links (`PCITE.extract`, scale 1, top-down);
  - its reference list (`parseRefsFromAllItems`: n, authors, year, title, journal, DOI, the entry's page and y).
  1. **Links first:** internal GoTo links mapped onto the entries (`_linkMatchesFromAnnotations`). They decide when they cover half the list, or always in "links only".
  2. **Without links:** citations found in the text (`_matchItemsOnPage`: numbered superscripts, [12], (12), Smith et al. 2014), tallied by style (`_pickFamily`), validated against the list (`_filterValidated` / `_matchedRef`). The flattened-superscript pass runs for superscript papers.
  3. **The journal says which style to expect** (`REFMAP.JOURNALS`): Nature, ACS, RSC and medical journals → superscripts; Science and PNAS → numbers in parentheses; Cell Press, JCB, eLife, Development, JCS, MBoC, EMBO → author–year; IEEE, APS, AIP, PLOS, BMC, Biomaterials, Advanced Materials → brackets. It is read from the Library entry, the PDF's XMP (`prism:publicationName`) / Subject, or a short line heading its first page. It decides only when the text is ambiguous ("none" / "mixed") or its style is at least 60 % as frequent as the top one, so the paper's own text wins.
  4. **Enriched:** the Library entry's own `references` fill missing DOIs and titles. "In your Library" when a DOI or title matches a Library paper.
- **Interaction**, in every reader (a maximised PDF and the reader beside a note via `MXHL.open`; the Library's reader via `ReaderView.drawSide`, its instance kept on `.reader._rv`):
  - **Hover:** geometry, not elements, so the page's text stays selectable and the pen keeps its highlighter. The citation lights and the pointer changes at once; the card comes after the hover delay (1 s, Settings), and moving away cancels ([[scientia-nothing-opens-uninvited]]).
  - **The card:** number, author, year, title, journal, DOI, In your Library, how often cited.
  - **Click** (not a drag, not a selection, never the pen): the PDF goes to that entry of the list, lit for 2.6 s, with "‹ Back to where you were" (or Esc). A one-page view turns its page.
- **The section in the Highlights panel**, foldable and kept per device: the references numbered as in the paper, each with ×n citations and ◆ when in the Library, and how they were found ("From the PDF's links" / "Found in the text · Nature style, numbered superscripts").
  - Hovering a row lights its citations at once (its card after the delay); clicking a row goes to the reference; ×n goes to its first citation.
  - The panels redraw their own children, so a MutationObserver keeps the section last, outside the `.rd-sh` headings the V4.10 side fold counts.
- **Settings → Reading & sources → References Map** (in Simple too):
  - the switch (off: no section, no marks, no card, a click does nothing);
  - "The card shows after resting on a citation for" 0.6 s / 1 s / 1.5 s;
  - "Citations come from" Its links, then the text / The PDF's links only;
  - a live preview.

## Testing
`ff_t` builds two PDFs by hand: one with internal Link annotations (an explicit and a named destination); one without links, with superscripts and "nature physics" in its running head. At 1400 and 400 px, and on the build without the lab layer:
- the map;
- the card after the delay, and not before or after moving away;
- click → the entry, lit, then Back and Esc;
- a drag-selection onto a citation does not jump;
- the map's rows (light, card, go, ×n), the fold, a panel redraw;
- the reader beside a note; the Library's reader; the side fold's count;
- Settings off and on, links only, the delay, the preview, `FEAT.audit()`, no errors.

Regression runs: `sf_t`, `lib_t1001`, `rep_t`, `t_995`.
