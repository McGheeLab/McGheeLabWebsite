# V4.71 — Scientia: captures on the Today tab, each with a Move to… footer

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "Captures show up in the Today tab for the user to come back during the day and potentially move them to projects. Additionally, each capture entry has a move to button footer right below them to bring up a move panel."

A capture (Ctrl+Shift+Space, `DAY.capture`) goes to the day note and was only reachable by opening the day note. Today showed a count ("3 entries today") and a timeline, nothing to act on. Filing an entry (`DAY.file`) never moved it: it made a structure on another component pointing back.

## What changed
- **Today lists the captures** (`14a-todaycaps.js`, `TCAP.section`, in the right-hand column under the capture box): the components of the day note, newest first (the time they were made, then their place in the note). Each is a card: its time, its kind chip when it has one, its words (a click opens it in the day note), and under it a footer holding **Move to…**. Nothing waiting: a line saying where captures wait.
- **Move to…** (`TCAP.move`) asks where it goes (for now an `optionPicker` of notes, recent first, and projects / themes; the next version, the move panel, replaces it) and moves the component with the existing `MOVE.ask` (13zb): it, its structures, marks and time belong to the chosen note, nothing stays in the day note.
  - A **note**: it lands beside what is there (`MOVE.spot`).
  - A **project or a theme**: a Note is made there first (`M.createDoc`, titled with the first words of the capture, its empty starter box removed) and the capture is moved into it.
  - One undo group: Ctrl+Z puts the capture back in the day note and takes the made note away.
- **Once moved** it leaves the list; this session's moves stay under it as a line each ("capture" → note, a link, "Ctrl+Z puts it back"); a line goes when the capture is no longer in that note.
- **Settings → Tabs & overview → Captures on Today** (FEAT `todaycaps`, in the Simple and Standard presets): the switch (off: `body.f-off-todaycaps`, the list is gone, captures still go to the day note) and one setting, **Captures shown**: today's (default), the last 3 days or the last 7 days (not moved yet, dated when not today's). The preview is the list as set, with made-up captures; Move to… in it says what it opens.

## Choices where Gabe's words left room
- Only the day note's components are captures; General notes (quick notes) are moved as notes through the move panel (next version), not listed here.
- A project or theme is not a canvas, so moving a capture there makes a note in it.
- The earlier days are off by default (today only), as asked; a setting reaches back.
