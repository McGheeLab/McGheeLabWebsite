# V3.935 — One-on-ones: a calendar tab on Lab Meetings where students subscribe to a recurring meeting with the PI or their mentor

> **Status: BUILT 2026-10-29.** Alex: *"In the meeting page, i want a tab for setting up one-on-one meetings with the
> PI or their mentor. To do this i just want a calendar, and students can subscribe to a recurring meeting. Do
> everything you think you should to get this working correctly."*

## Goal

A fourth kind of meeting the page did not know about: the weekly (or fortnightly) one-on-one between a student and
the PI, or between a student and the mentor named on their profile (`users/{uid}.mentor_uids`, the field Settings →
Permissions → Mentors and Team Focus already write). The PI or a mentor **offers recurring times**; a student **picks
one and is subscribed** — the same time every week, on a calendar everyone involved can read, with reminders, a skip
for a week away, and an `.ics` so it lands in their own Google / Outlook / Apple calendar.

## What a person sees

- **A new tab, One-on-ones**, between Schedule and Chores (`#oneonone` deep-links to it).
- **My one-on-ones** at the top: each recurring meeting the viewer is part of ("Every Tuesday 2:00–2:30 PM with
  Alex · BSRL 100 · next Nov 3"), with *Add to calendar*, *Can't make a date*, *Unsubscribe*. A student with no
  meeting yet is told which of their hosts has open times — or that none are offered yet.
- **A month calendar** (Monday-first, like My Week): a chip per occurrence. Solid chips are booked ("2:00 Gabe" to the
  host, "2:00 Alex" to the student); dashed chips are open times the viewer may take; a struck chip is a skipped week.
  Tap a chip → the slot sheet.
- **Weekly pattern** under the calendar: the recurring times grouped by weekday — time, cadence, host → subscriber or
  **Subscribe**, room, *Details*.
- **Hosts** (the PI, any admin, anyone who is somebody's mentor) get **Offer a time**: weekday, start, duration,
  weekly / every other week, room, from / until (until defaults to the semester end), *back-to-back slots* (one form
  → up to six consecutive slots), a note, and optionally *Reserve for* a student. On a slot they host: *Reserve
  for…* / *Change*, *Edit*, *Skip a date*, *End*, *Delete*. Admins have a **Mine / Everyone** switch.
- **My items** gains a My one-on-ones block; the tab badges when a host of yours has an open time and you have none.

## Data

`oneOnOneSlots/{slotId}` (route `meetings/one-on-ones.json`, lab scope, SHORT cache, hydrated by `api.subscribe`
like every other meetings route):

```
hostUid, hostName,
weekday 0–6, time 'HH:MM', duration (min), cadence 'weekly' | 'biweekly',
location, note,
startDate 'YYYY-MM-DD' (first occurrence; the fortnight anchor), endDate 'YYYY-MM-DD' | null,
active true | false (false = ended by the host; endDate is set to the day it ended),
subscriberUid | null, subscriberName, subscribedAt, subscribedBy 'self' | 'host' | null,
skips: { 'YYYY-MM-DD': { by, name, reason, at } },
updatedBy, lastReminded 'YYYY-MM-DD' (function only),
createdBy, createdAt, updatedAt
```

One doc = one recurring time. "Subscribe" is a write of `subscriberUid = me` on an open, active slot; that is what
makes the rule checkable with no second read.

## Rules (`firestore.rules`, `oneOnOneSlots`)

- read: lab members.
- create: a lab member whose `hostUid` is themself, or an admin.
- update: admin, or the host; or a lab member touching only
  `subscriberUid · subscriberName · subscribedAt · subscribedBy · skips · updatedBy · updatedAt`, and then only to
  **claim** an open active slot for themself, or — as the current subscriber — to **release** it or write a skip.
- delete: admin or the host.

Not enforced in rules: "is this person really a mentor or the PI". Any lab member may offer a time; the UI shows
*Offer a time* only to hosts, and a stray slot from a student is harmless (nobody is made to see it: a student sees
only slots from *their* hosts).

## Cloud Functions (`functions/meetings.js`)

- `onOneOnOneSlotWritten` — pure `slotTransition(before, after)` → events: `assigned` (host reserved a student),
  `subscribed` (student picked a time → host), `left` (student released → host), `removed` (host unbooked →
  student), `ended`, `deleted`, `skipped` / `unskipped` (→ the other party), `changed` (host moved the time → the
  student). All through `notifyUsers` with app key `meetings`.
- `meetingReminders` (existing 09:00 / 16:00 Phoenix pass) gains `planOneOnOneReminders`: at the 16:00 pass, every
  subscribed slot that occurs tomorrow → one notice to the student, and one notice per host listing all of their
  one-on-ones tomorrow. Deduped with `lastReminded = tomorrow` on the slot.
- Occurrence math (`slotOccursOn`) lives in both the function and the client, pinned by the function tests.

## Decisions

- **A slot is a recurring time, not a list of dates.** Weeks away are `skips` on the slot; the calendar and the
  `.ics` (RRULE + EXDATE) both derive from it. Nothing is materialised per week.
- **The student writes their own uid.** Same trick as `presenters` / `completions`: the rule can say "you may set
  this field to yourself on an open slot, or clear it when it is yours" without a second document read.
- **Reminders the afternoon before, grouped for the host.** The PI with six students gets one notice ("Tomorrow: Gabe
  2:00, Ally 2:30, …"), each student gets theirs.
- **No My Week card.** A one-on-one is a calendar event, not a task; the reminder lands in the My Week feed through
  `notifyUsers` like every other meetings notice, and *Add to calendar* puts it where people actually look.
- **Monday-first month grid**, the same orientation as My Week's month view.
