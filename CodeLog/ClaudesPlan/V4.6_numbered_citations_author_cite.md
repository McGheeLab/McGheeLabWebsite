# V4.6 — Numbered citations, "Style et al.", /author-cite

## Goal
Gabe (16 Nov 2026): /cite should cite the paper as a number in superscript, starting at [1]; Settings can switch to the first author ("don't do the parentheses"); /author-cite cites by the first author just for that one.

## What was there
Cite marks (`{type: 'cite', ref}` on an object character), a per-note `citeMode` (title | author | number, set from the note's ⋯ menu, copied from `settings().citeMode` when the note is made), numbering in order of first appearance (`ED.citeNumbers`) and a references list under a numbered note. The default was *title*, and author citations read "(Style et al., 2014)".

## Approach
- `settings().citeStyle` (new, default `number`) is what every note shows unless it chose its own (`d.citeOwn`); `ED.citeModeOf(d)` decides; the note's menu sets `citeOwn` and offers *As in Settings*. A new key rather than `citeMode`, so everyone moves to numbered without rewriting their settings, and notes that never chose follow Settings.
- `LIB.authorCite(r)`: first author (two: "A & B"; three or more: "A et al."), no brackets; the year when `settings().citeYear`.
- `/author-cite` (typed, or from the slash menu) opens the same picker; the mark carries `style: 'author'`, shows by author whatever the note shows, takes no number, and its paper is listed unnumbered under the references when it has no number.
- Settings → Library: *Citations show* bound to `citeStyle`, *Citations by author add the year* to `citeYear`; preview redrawn as set.
