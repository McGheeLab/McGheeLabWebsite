# V3.879 — Inventory: category pills replace the tab bar and the filter dropdowns

**Date:** 2026-10-23
**Request (Alex):** *"on the lab inventory page, i dont like the top row of filters, these are not commonly used. we
should have a set of pills for each category, and by default all but consumables, research are off."*

## What was there

Two rows of filtering above the table:
1. In the header, **All Categories ▾** (every raw `category` value) and **All Stock ▾** (full / low / out), beside
   the search box and the action buttons.
2. A **tab bar**, choosing ONE view at a time: All Items, Research, Custom Items, Consumables, Equipment, Computers &
   Software, Furniture & Office, Infrastructure, Preferred, Low / Out of Stock.

## What replaces them

One row of **pills**, one per category group, any number on at once:

| pill | items |
|---|---|
| Research | `category` starts `research_` |
| Consumables | `consumable` |
| Custom | `lab_made` (custom aliquots / materials / media) |
| Equipment | `equipment` |
| Computers & Software | `computer`, `software` |
| Furniture & Office | `lab_furniture`, `office` |
| Infrastructure | `infrastructure` |
| Other | anything else (`remodel`, `other`, blank, unknown), so every item is reachable |

**On by default: Research and Consumables only** (Alex's list; Custom is off with the rest). A pill shows its count;
a pill with no items is hidden unless it is on. After a divider, two narrowing pills keep what the old Low / Out and
Preferred tabs did: **Low or out** and **Preferred** filter whatever the category pills show. The dropdowns are gone.

- **Remembered per viewer** (`localStorage` `rm.inventory.pills.v1`, every read/write in try/catch; a blocked store just
  means defaults each visit). Only the person's own clicks are saved.
- **Nothing is ever unreachable by surprise.** When a search matches items in categories that are off, a line under
  the count says *"3 more matches in Equipment and Other"* with **Show them** (turns those pills on). Opening an item
  from a scan, a `?item=` link or a just-made custom item turns its pill on **for this visit only** and lifts the
  narrowing pills, then opens it.
- **Subcategory buttons** (Antibodies, Media, …) appear when exactly one category pill is on, as they did under a tab.
- Phone width: the pill row scrolls sideways instead of wrapping into several lines; the page itself never scrolls
  sideways.
