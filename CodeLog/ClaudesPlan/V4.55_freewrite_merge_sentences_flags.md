# V4.55 — Freewrite: merge two sentences into one component; a flag for two kinds or two titles

## Why
Gabe, 19 Nov: "With the option of freewrite makes each sentence its own component, have a little button in between sentences that indicates merging the two sentences in a single component. If two components are merged in one, each part retains their structure, but kind has to be one, so it raises a flag for the user to correct. Same thing for if each component had a component title."

## What changed
- **Where:** Settings → Freewrite → "Sentences no rule reads: Each its own component". Between two sentences that each made one plain component, a small ＋ sits under the gap after the full stop. Once they are merged it shows a link, and a click splits them again.
- **Kept as:** `fw.merges` `[{ a, b }]`, by the sentences' words like `fw.over`. `FREEW.convert` returns `seams` (where the buttons go) and merges:
  - the first sentence's part takes the next one's words, as a stretch of its own;
  - a chain of three or more is one component;
  - links to a merged sentence move to the component.
- **Each sentence keeps its structure:** it becomes a structure on that sentence's words inside the component (as in V4.53). On Apply each is an item and its mark.
- **One kind:** sentences of different kinds raise a flag (`p.flag.kinds`). Two titles do the same (`p.flag.titles`). The flag shows:
  - on the label ("2 kinds · 2 titles");
  - on the component's box in the preview;
  - in a line above the preview.

  **Apply waits:** it says what to correct and opens that component's popup.
- **The popup of a merged component:**
  - the flag, in words;
  - Kind, which sets the kind of all its sentences; the kinds in conflict are dashed;
  - its sentences, each with its structure ▾;
  - Keep one title;
  - Split.
- **Change ▾ on a merged component:** a kind is set for all its sentences, and the structures section says each sentence keeps its own.
- What each sentence had (`fw.over` by its own words) stays, so Split gives each back what it was.
- **Layout:** the name of a structure on a merged sentence starts a little in, clear of the button before it.
