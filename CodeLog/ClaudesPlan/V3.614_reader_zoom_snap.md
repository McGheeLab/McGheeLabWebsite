# V3.614 — Mobile reader: pinch zoom snaps to page fit / column fit

## Goal

Alex, after simulator testing of V3.613: "We will need a way to zoom into
the papers to read them. They should snap to half the page zoom to fit the
columns." Two-column papers on a phone are read one column at a time; the
useful zooms are exactly two — the whole page, and half the page across the
viewport (one column). Free pinch shouldn't leave students hunting for that
level by hand.

## Approach

All in [rm/js/viewer-touch.js](../../rm/js/viewer-touch.js) (mobile-only
module; desktop untouched):

- `_anchorZooms()` — `[fitZoom, fitZoom × 2]` (column fit = half page fills
  the viewport), clamped to ZOOM_MAX.
- `_snapZoom(z)` — a pinch release within ±12% (`SNAP_TOLERANCE`) of an
  anchor returns the anchor; otherwise the free zoom stands. Applied in
  `_endPinch` before the single real `viewer.setZoom()`.
- `_snapColumnEdge()` — after a snap to column fit, if the horizontal
  scroll is within 18% of a viewport width of the page's left or right
  column edge, nudge onto it so the column sits framed. Runs after the
  midpoint scroll re-anchor.
- Double-tap already toggles fit ↔ 2×fit — pinch and double-tap now land
  on identical states.

## Deploy

FTP-only; ships inside the V3.613 release batch (same TestFlight build).
sw 584 → 585.

## Verification

Simulator/device: pinch near column width on a two-column paper → releases
land exactly on half-page zoom with the column framed; pinch to clearly
different levels (e.g. 3×) → no snap; double-tap round-trips the same two
levels; highlights re-align after every snap (patched setZoom path).
