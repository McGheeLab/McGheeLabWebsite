# V4.42 — Look and motion: the notebook in B's edge-rail style

## Goal
Gabe (19 Nov 2026): "B is the best. Rethink the UI and animations of the entire lab notebook to match this style. Bring them all up in the artifact as a new pull request for lab notebook motion and look overhaul" — after: "Update both sidebars to have cool UI that is more modern and aesthetically pleasing by building a few options with animations in an artifact. The collapsed bar does not have to be a bar." and "The left library left sidebar should display the list of papers in book mode or list mode (as a button toggle)."

The design canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/CymaRzoNRZi1pLMnEtnrCk, "Scientia — motion and look overhaul") has a board for each surface and one for the motion. This version builds what those boards show for the shell, notes, menus, dialogs, toasts, the readers' two sidebars, Settings and the pills (by CSS). The rest waits for Gabe's word on the boards.

## Approach
- **One switch:** Settings → Canvas & notes → **Look and motion** (`FEAT 'look'`, on in every preset). Everything is under `body:not(.f-off-look)`. Off is the look from before, with nothing added.
- **`36-look.css`** (new, loaded last):
  - the B tokens, light and dark: warm surfaces, rules, ink, teal, radii, shadows;
  - the motion variables: glide, spring, rise, pop, hover;
  - the restyle of the shell, the tabs (a pill marker), cards (a kind dot, a light wash, a teal ring when selected), wire labels as capsules, menus, popovers, dialogs, toasts, the search palette, Settings and the pills;
  - the readers' side panel docked, with a width glide.
- **`20d-look.js`** (new, `LOOK`) adds what CSS can't draw, by wrapping, never editing:
  - **Right sidebar, every reader:** `SIDEFOLD.aria`, `wrap` and `setB` add the host's `.lk-fold` (the column glides shut) and a capsule rail (`.lk-rail`): the highlighter, then one dot per highlight colour with its count. A dot opens the panel at that colour's first highlight. A fitted page refits after the glide (`RVIEW.resized`).
  - **Left sidebar, the mini library:** `RLIB.fill`, `grip` and `paint`. A **Book / List** toggle shows spines on a shelf (staggered heights and entry) or the rows, kept on this device (`_ls 'lookLib'`). The grip becomes a capsule of the project's shelves, with dots and counts.
  - **Navigation:** `#view.lk-nav` for a moment on a route change, so only real navigation animates.
  - **Movement:** Full / Calm / Still (`body.lk-calm` / `.lk-still`). A computer set to reduce motion gets fades only.
- **Settings:** the description, Movement, the mini library's view, and the live preview (fold and open the panel; spines or a list).
