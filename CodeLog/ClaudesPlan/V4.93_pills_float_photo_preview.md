# V4.93 — Scientia: the ＋ Quick cast gets Float and Photo; Settings → Pills shows each pill's extended look, and a click turns it

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "Add float button in quick cast pill. Live preview of pills should show their extended look as well, and clicking on it sets it horizontal/vertical and vice versa." Then: "Include a capture image in quick cast that routes to the device's camera to take a picture, and logs it into the note you were in as a 1x1 image panel."

## What changed
- **The lab layer's pills** (`rm/scientia/lab-mode.js`, section 11 — pill changes are allowed on Gabe's request, Alex reviews; Gabe's repo gets the same code through the cut, `scientia/vendor/lab-pills.js`):
  - **Float**, third in the ＋ Quick cast (after Pull in and Flag, which the v3869 harness checks):
    - the selected components (a click, the lasso, the bulk ticks) become floats (`FLOATS.mark`);
    - with none selected, the floats waiting come up over the note (`FLOATS.open`) — what `/float` does;
    - its words say which, and how many are waiting.
    - Label's Float (by clicking components) stays behind Label.
  - **Photo**, fourth: the app's camera (`CAMERA.take`).
  - Two icons (Heroicons v2 cloud and camera) join `PILL_ICONS`.
  - **`LAB.pillPreview(name)`** gives what a pill holds — its items' icons and words, read only — for Settings' canvas.
- **The camera** (`14fh-camera.js`, `CAMERA`; `48-camera.css`): the ＋ Quick cast's Photo and `/photo` open a window.
  - It shows the camera live (the back one first; ⇄ with two cameras), then Take, then Retake or Use it.
  - Used, the picture goes into the note you were in as a **1×1 image panel** "Photo · hh:mm" in the middle of what you see (`FIG.create`). One Undo takes it out.
  - Where the browser cannot reach a camera, the device's own camera or a file chooser opens instead (`capture="environment"`).
  - **Settings → Pen & touch → Photo from the camera** (`camera`): *Start with* (back / front camera) and *Keep the photo at most* (1280 / 2048 / 4096 px), with a preview.
- **Settings → Pills, the canvas** (`20c-pillcanvas.js`, `34-pillcanvas.css`):
  - each pill shows its extended look beside it: its row or column of the icons it has on a note. The Quick cast's row sits either side of it; a row goes toward the side with more room; a column goes up from the bottom half.
  - **A click** on the pill or its strip (not a drag) turns a row into a column and back. It is kept in `labPills.orient` (this shape's), which docked pills read on every note and device. A drag still places it.
