## Wire routing (`12b-wires.js`)

**Before:** each wire was a cubic curve between two sides chosen by a rule of thumb (side by side → right/left; above → bottom/top;
something in the corridor → both top, or both right), with control points pushed out by up to 240 px; a curve that hit a box was
bowed ("arch") and, if it still crossed, drawn faintly over it. In a note with a few components in the way, wires swooped across the
canvas over other components.

**Now — the shortest way round:**
- Every box on the paper is an obstacle, inflated by `M` = 16 px. A wire runs from a stub `M` out from one end's side to a stub on the
  other's, through a visibility graph of the inflated boxes' corners (the corners inside another inflated box dropped), searched with A*
  lazily (a node's neighbours are tested for sight when it is expanded; a cheaper-already test first). All four sides of each end are
  tried at once (multi-source, multi-target). Costs: length, `BEND` = 14 px per corner, and up to `DEV` = 40 px for leaving or
  entering a side askew. Shortest-path routes only bend at corners, so a route is a taut string round what is in its way.
- Only the boxes in a window round the two ends (padding 140 px + 30 % of their distance) are considered; with no way through there,
  the whole note. A route is cached on its ends and that window's boxes, so redrawing costs nothing and a move reroutes only the wires
  near it. A drawing spends at most 24 ms routing; the rest keep their last route while their ends haven't moved (or the old curve for
  the first frame) and are routed 60 ms later.
- **Readable:** square-on stubs (the arrow meets the side straight), rounded corners (radius 22), ends sharing a side spread along it
  in the order their routes head off (so they don't cross at the box), wires rounding the same corner 9 px apart, labels placed along
  the route away from other labels and boxes.
- Kept: colours per relation, labels, the end dot, arrows, per-image ends (`partRect`), folded regions, the iteration deck's hidden
  wires (13zzq wraps `WIRES.layout`), the lifted drawing for whatever a route still crosses (a boxed-in end), the old curve as the last
  resort. `DocView.geom` routes the provisional wire too (wire drag, cross-pane drag, a timer's region tag).

## The lit review flicker

Literature Q2 could not be opened from here: `.scientia/record.json` in the notebook folder is sealed (`SiSLock1`, the private app's
lock, which only its own app opens with Gabe's password), the lab app running on this computer refuses a sealed notebook, and the web
app needs a sign-in. A lit review of the same make was built in a test browser instead (four PDFs, sixteen quotes wired to their pages,
notes, figure and equation clips) and watched frame by frame:

1. **45 opacity fades on every click, edit or redraw.** `drawWires` makes every wire anew; 13zza's wrapper then dims a paper's wires to
   quotes of other pages (`pw-dim`). `REGION.layout`, in between, reads layout, so the new wires were styled once at full opacity — and
   `transition: opacity .25s` (21-sync) faded each of them back. Fix: `pw-now` on the paper while the wires are drawn (no transition),
   taken off two frames later; a paper scrolled to another page (13zza's `rewire`, not a redraw) still fades.
2. **Headers jumping for two frames.** `SLIM.fit` (13zg) removed `two` and measured in the next frame; a box rebuilt by a redraw
   (every image panel and equation, on every edit) showed its two-row header on one row for two frames, 2–24 px shorter, and the size
   change re-triggered its ResizeObserver (the "ResizeObserver loop completed" notices). Fix: try, measure and keep in one go; a box
   not yet on the paper takes the answer remembered for its id and width (`SLIM.rows`), checked in the next frame.

Checked and not a cause: PDFs (kept since V3.980; no flat-card switching while panning or wobbling round 33 %, no pages drawn again),
the quote highlights (rebuilt identically, no animation), content-visibility (only over 100 components, from 60 %).
