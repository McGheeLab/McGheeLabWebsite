# V4.72 — Scientia: one move panel for every "send this somewhere"

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "Move panels (bridge to, flag for synthesis, etc.. show up as a search bar with results or a Portfolio view to click on a note in the Portfolio."

Each action that sends something to a note had its own picker: a plain list in a dialog (`optionPicker`), a different look and a different list each time (Move to another note, Bridge to…, a region's Bridge, Flag for synthesis, Project & theme…, Move note to theme). The calendar alone could choose a note by clicking it in the Portfolio (V4.65, "Pick in the Portfolio…").

## What changed
- **`MPANEL`** (`13zzzd-movepanel.js`, `42-movepanel.css`), `MPANEL.pick(o)` / `MPANEL.note(o)`:
  - **Search view** (default): a search bar and the notes under it, the ones touched last first, each row with its kind in its colour (`NOTEKIND`: Experiment · Note · Lit review · Synthesis), its title, its project › theme and when it was touched. Typing narrows by title, project, theme or kind; ↑ ↓ choose, Enter picks, Esc closes. Where an action can also go to a project or a theme they are listed under the notes (`MPANEL.projectRows`).
  - **Portfolio view**: the Search | Portfolio buttons at the panel's top. Portfolio closes the panel, opens the Portfolio and shows a bar ("… — click a note in the Portfolio", Search instead, Cancel); a click on a note picks it and returns to the page you left. A note that is not one of the choices (a Synthesis is wanted; a note's own) is refused with a toast and the bar stays.
  - What an action asks after the pick stays its own: a bridge's caption (`BRIDGE.ask`), the connections that would cross (`MOVE.ask`), a capture's new note.
- **`PFPICK`**: the calendar's Pick in the Portfolio generalised — the bar, the click on a note (the `App.go('#/doc/…')` wrapper and the click on a `#/doc/` link), Cancel, and now Esc. `23e-calendar.js` uses it (`CAL.pickInPortfolio` → `PFPICK.start`, same bar id `cal-pick`, still returns to the calendar and its create window itself); `CAL.pickBanner` and its two listeners are gone, the bar's CSS moved from `39-calendar.css` to `42-movepanel.css`. `cal65_t.mjs`: 23/23, both builds.
- **Adopted by** Move to another note (`MOVE.dialog`, the "Take other components along" list sits above the search), Bridge to… (`BRIDGE.pickNote`, so also a selection, pieces, words, Bulk, Freewrite), a region's Bridge (`REGION.copyDialog`), Flag for synthesis (`SYNTH.choose`: only Synthesis notes, the one that synthesizes this note first with its hint), Project & theme… (`GENERAL.move`: places only, no Portfolio view), Move note to theme (`DocView.moveDoc`), and Move to… on a Today capture (`TCAP.target`).
- **Settings → Canvas & notes → The move panel** (FEAT `movepanel`, Simple and Standard presets): the switch (off: the plain `optionPicker` list it replaced, same results) and one setting, **The panel opens in** Search (default) or The Portfolio (then it goes straight to the Portfolio, the bar offering Search instead). The preview is the panel as set, with example notes you can search and pick; its buttons try the other view.

## Choices where Gabe's words left room
- "Portfolio view" is the calendar's pattern, not an embedded Portfolio inside the dialog: the real Portfolio, with the bar over it.
- Other pickers I left alone: notes added to a Synthesis (`SYNTH.pick`, a repeating choice), "Add it to…" on the phone page (`13ze-mobile`, no Portfolio there), `/move` and `/bridge` typed in the command line (they resolve a note by name in their own flow), and the pickers of components, structures or datasets (not notes).
- Day notes are in the list like any note; archived and deleted notes are not.
