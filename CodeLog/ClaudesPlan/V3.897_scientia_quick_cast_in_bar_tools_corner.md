# V3.897 — Scientia: the Quick cast built into the bottom bar; ✦ replaces the top-right row

**Ask (Alex, 2026-10-24):** "the top right bar of split, timer, pen, calculator etc, this should be replaced by the
wheel that sits there. the wheel at the bottom bar should be integrated into the bar"

Follows [V3.896](V3.896_scientia_pills_quick_cast_docks.md). Web build only (`rm/scientia/lab-mode.js` §11).

## ✦ replaces Gabe's top-right row

`.ctools` (Split, Timer + its ▾ head start, Pen, Calculator, Equation) is hidden on the web
(`.canvas-host.lab-pilled > .ctools`); the ✦ pill takes that corner (top 14, right 14). Its fan / docked row holds what
the row did: the timer (start / pause / resume), **the head start** (`HEAD.start` / `HEAD.add`), calculator, equation,
**Split** (`SPLITBTN.menu`, Gabe's own side-by-side / quadrant menu), plus focus mode and *put the pills back*. Pen is
✎'s cog (V3.896: pen options are not in the tools wheel).

## The Quick cast inside the bottom bar

Gabe's dock scrolls sideways (`overflow-x:auto`), which would clip a fan, so the wheel is not moved into it: a slot
(`span.lab-qc-slot`, 46 px) is put into the dock near its middle (by width), and the ＋ pill — 38 px, dark, `.in-bar` —
is drawn exactly over it from the pills layer (now z 21, above the dock's 20). `DocView.renderDock` (which rebuilds the
bar on every update) is wrapped to put the slot back; a ResizeObserver and the dock's scroll keep the pill on it. The
fan opens upward from the bar; left open 10 s it docks as a centred row **above** the bar (its strip covers only the
row). Dragged out of the bar, the pill is free (no slot); *put the pills back* returns it.
