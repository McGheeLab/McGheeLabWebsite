# V4.33 — Freewrite: a blank page for typing and the voice, read into components, kinds and links

## Goal
Gabe (18 Nov 2026): "Freewrite is a new button in Quick Cast. It brings up a blank white page which covers the whole Scientia In Situ screen. You may decide to place it beside a note or to close it, saving all changes and appearing in the note it was placed in. Freewrite works with typing and recording your voice, which you can play and pause at any time, as well as correcting with typing. The goal is that the final freewrite product automatically converts into a partial or complete note canvas. Words have identities and transform into note components, kinds, links, etc…
For example:
• "[…] supports […] → causes a supports link between the […] as a component and the second […] as another.
• "[…] is supported by […] → causes a supports link in the other direction.
• "One of the barriers is […]" OR "[…] is a barrier" → a sentence containing barrier makes it into a component highlighted as a barrier.
• "[…] which results in […] → legend becomes the second […] with a placeholder result-labelled component allowing the user to add any quick cast option into it.
• "Evidence from […] explains […]" → from will look into […] nearby to see if any notes in the Portfolio contain any words in their title. Any flagged title will appear in the placeholder bridge for you to choose a component(s) to bridge from the note you choose manually afterwards.
• Think of more words that can cause these patterns. After the automatic wiring of the transcript or text to associated note components, links, kinds, etc… it becomes color-coded with clicking on color-coded components to change them manually before approving the final freewrite result. A preview is displayed by color code for the user to see how the note will be created, and clicking on a color-coded freewrite component will bring up its translated note component for you to see directly. The user may also add freewrite components via highlights, and once the highlight is done, will be asked to associate it to a new or existing component. That association will be asked if it is temporary (i.e., just for that freewrite) or permanent (i.e., for all future freewrites). Additional freewrite association rules can be added, modified, or deleted in Settings. The final result transforms the freewrite into that note canvas preview. The freewrite is kept, automatically collapsed."

V4.33 is the page, typing and voice, the rules, the colour-coded preview and Apply. The associations by highlight and the rule editor in Settings are V4.34.

## Approach
- **One module, `scientia/src/14j-freewrite.js` (`FREEW`), and `34-freewrite.css`.** Nothing else in `src/` is edited except the features register and BUILD; the app's own code is reused through wrappers.
- **Ways in:** the lab's Quick cast (lab-mode §11, one entry after Label, the same style: `icon: FREEW.ICON`, `run: FREEW.open(d, { pane })`; the build cuts §11 into `vendor/lab-pills.js`, so the build without the lab layer has it too), `/freewrite` (`CMD.add`), and ⋯ on a note (`DocView.docMenu` wrapped, before Tidy layout), so it can be reached when the pills are off.
- **The page:** fixed over the whole app (`z-index 1200`; menus, dialogs and toasts raised above it while it is open). A textarea over a copy of its words (`.fw-back`, same font and wrapping, colours only); the textarea grows with the copy, the page scrolls. Nearly white in a light theme (`color-mix(var(--surface) 30%, #fff)`), the dark surface in a dark theme. Top bar: the note's name, the voice, Convert, Apply to the note, Beside the note, Close. Esc closes.
- **Kept in the note:** a block `kind: 'freewrite'` (`WIDGETS.freewrite`, 448 px) made at the first word (or Speak), placed under the component it was opened from or at the middle of the view (`auto`, so the settle moves it clear). `body.text` holds the words (search and export see them). `fw = { state, shown, over, lover, made, applied, dict, voice }`. Its card shows Draft / Applied, the words (coloured once converted), Play, and Open; double-click opens. A page closed empty leaves nothing.
- **Beside:** `body.fw-beside` makes `#app` two columns (the header across both, the note in one, the reader dock hidden meanwhile); the page takes the other half under the header (Settings: right or left). The note stays live.
- **Voice — reusing REC (13zn):** `REC.start` records into the freewrite block itself (same audio file handling, the lab's parts on the web, the level meter and clock through `data-reclevel` / `data-recclock`). Pause / Go on / Stop; Play at any time — while paused, a Blob of what was recorded so far; after Stop, `REC.ensure`. Live words: `webkitSpeechRecognition` / `SpeechRecognition` (continuous, interim results under the page; finals at the caret or at the end, capitalised after a stop). Without it: the desktop's Whisper writes the transcript after the words (`REC.watch` → `REC.apply`), the website's Transcribe (`SIS_LAB.transcribe`) when there is no live text. `REC.apply` is wrapped: a freewrite whose words were written as you spoke keeps only the timings. One recording per freewrite (pause and go on as often as you like).
- **The rules (`FREEW.BUILTIN`, 28):** a rule has patterns (`[A]`, `[B]` parts, words matched as written), what each part becomes (`as`: component / task / slot / bridge, `kind`, `struct`) and the link. Read per sentence: two-part rules (each part read again: chains, and one-part rules inside), then one-part rules (patterns describe the whole sentence, anchored), then a word anywhere (`whole`: the whole sentence). Between two parts ", which supports" reads as "supports". The same words twice are one component (`key`: no case, no leading article, no closing stop). Gabe's five plus: contradicts / is contradicted by, resolves / is resolved by (a Resolution, and the item resolves the barrier on Apply), explains, because / due to / is caused by (caused, reversed), causes, leads to / therefore / so, tests, needs / requires, We hypothesize / I think (Hypothesis), The question is / I wonder (Question), Method: / We used (Method), Results show / We found (Result), Surprisingly (Anomaly), This suggests (Insight), We need to / To do (a task), The problem in the literature / a challenge (Challenge), We decided (Decision), We claim (Claim), a sentence with "unexpected" (Anomaly), a sentence ending in "?" (Question).
- **"Evidence from […]":** the part becomes a placeholder bridge; `FREEW.cands` finds Portfolio notes (not deleted, not Day/Review) whose titles share a word of 4+ letters with it (stop words left out), most shared first. On the card: those notes as buttons, and Another note… (`BRIDGE.pickNote`); a note → its components with checkboxes → each bridged in (a `ref` block, as `M.pullInto` makes), the placeholder's wires copied to each, the placeholder removed; one Undo.
- **"[…] which results in […]":** a placeholder Result — a component with `ckind 'result'`, the words as its title, `fwslot 'result'`; ＋ Add opens + Insert (`INSERT.menu`) and what is inserted is put under it and wired `illustrates` (`M.addBlock` wrapped, as 13zzo does).
- **Colours:** each coloured stretch has its colour (`--fc`: the kind's, the structure's, green for a bridge, dashed for a placeholder) and a tag in words above it (`::before` from `data-t`): "Result", "Barrier", "Task", "Result to add", "Bridge from a note"…; linking words a dotted line and "→ supports" / "← supports".
- **The preview:** `FREEW.layout` lays the parts out as Tidy does (`LAYOUT.pack` per connected group, `LAYOUT.square`), at real sizes; `FREEW.mini` draws it scaled to the side panel (boxes with tag and words, lines with arrowheads and the relation). The same layout places them on Apply.
- **Choosing:** a click on coloured words selects the part (its box lit and scrolled into view) and puts Change ▾ beside the words; Change (or a click on a box) → its kinds, structures, task, placeholders, Plain words, As the rules read it. Linking words → the relation, Turn it round, No link. Kept by the words (`fw.over` / `fw.lover`), so corrections elsewhere keep them.
- **Apply:** one undo group — blocks made with `M._newBlock` and wires with `DB.put` (no `changed()` in between, which would split the group), then one `changed()`; under the freewrite; the note's settle keeps boxes apart; the freewrite folds (Settings), `state: 'applied'`, `made` (key → block) so Apply again adds only what is new.
- **Settings → Canvas & notes → Freewrite** (`freewrite`, presets incl. Simple): *The words are coloured* (when you press Convert / as you write), *A sentence no rule reads* (stays words / becomes a component), *Words appear as you speak* (switch), *Beside the note, the page goes* (right / left), *After Apply, the freewrite folds* (switch). Preview: a sentence coloured and its mini canvas, following the settings. Off: no Quick cast entry, no /freewrite, no ⋯ entry; freewrites stay, without Open (`body.f-off-freewrite`).

## Decisions where the brief was silent
- The page in a dark theme is the components' dark surface (no white flash at night).
- Colours appear when Convert is pressed (Settings can make them follow as you write); once converted they follow every correction.
- Sentences no rule reads stay words (a partial canvas); Settings can make every sentence a component.
- Leaving the note while the page covers the app saves and closes it; beside, it stays.
- One recording per freewrite; a second recording needs a new freewrite.
- Freewrite components are made without a title (the words are the body), except the placeholder Result (titled with its words) and the placeholder bridge ("Bridge from a note").
- Links between kinded components are made as the rule says (the kinds' own ways are not enforced on Apply).
