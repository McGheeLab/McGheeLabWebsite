## 1. The lasso on Scientia's whiteboard (`scientia/board/pen.js`)
- ◌ in the board's ink bar (back from V3.941), or the Slim Pen's barrel button held (`buttons & 2`) whatever tool is on —
  the shortcut it is in notes (13zu).
- The engine draws the loop as a probe stroke (as ◧ does); its points, in board units, choose the strokes with ≥ 60 % of
  their points inside and the cards whose centre is inside. A shape's fill strokes go with it.
- Where the board is on the screen: the engine keeps its view to itself, but its handle gives the scale (`getView`) and
  the plane's origin (`getOrigin`); `u = A·(x − left)/scale + originX + kx` (v likewise), with `A`, `kx`, `ky` learnt from
  every stroke drawn here (its samples on the screen against its saved points). The selection's box follows pan and zoom.
- With a selection: drag inside to move, a corner to scale (cards move, keep their size); a bar — Copy, Duplicate, ⇋, ⇵,
  Pen ↔ Highlighter, ◧ Fill, Unfill, Delete, Done. A colour or width picked in the ink bar restyles it. A barrel tap on
  it copies; a barrel tap (or a ◌ tap) elsewhere pastes there; Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V, Delete, Esc. All of it is undoable
  (the journal of V3.941).

## 2. No limit to resizing
- The shared side grip (`DocView.pointer`, every component): no largest width (was 900, 1600 for widgets). A corner grip
  on a component whose height follows its width — an image panel, slides (a corner of their own now) — follows a drag
  down too. `body.resizing` while dragging, so an HTML page's frame never takes the drag.
- HTML pages (2400 × 2400), PDF cards (2400 × 3000), tables (2000 high), plots (1600 high): no largest size. The single
  dish (a 1-well plate) grows with its box. A PDF page's canvas stays ≤ 3600 px a side (`PDFW.res`), so a huge card
  still draws in every browser.

## 3. The whiteboard crops to its drawing
- `13zzd` measures what is drawn (strokes and cards, board units + a margin) on every save (`row.ext`; a board saved
  before is measured once). The image cell takes that shape (`FIG.aspect`) and its preview frames just that
  (`board.html?…&crop=x,y,w,h,page` → `zoomToRect` in `store.js`). A click still opens the whole board; Done re-crops.
- On by default; *Whole page* on the cell turns it off (`cell.board.crop = false`), *Crop to the drawing* back on.

## 4. No outlines in pen mode
- Any hand but pan (lab-mode marks the host `lab-pilled` / `lab-hand-move`): no hover border, ink frame or header, grip,
  ⋯, port, embed tools, "+ label" or crop button answers the pen hovering. A component being edited keeps its frame.
