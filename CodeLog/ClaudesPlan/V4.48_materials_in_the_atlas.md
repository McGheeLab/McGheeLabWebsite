# V4.48 — Materials is a section of the Atlas

## Goal
Gabe (19 Nov 2026): "Merge Materials into Atlas, and create data tab in Atlas, which organizes all trees recorded there for a master tree." This version does the first half: Materials goes into the Atlas, which gets sections. V4.49 adds the Data section.

## Approach (`14fb-atlashub.js`, new; `37-atlashub.css`, new)
- **`ATHUB`:** the Atlas page has sections, in a row of pills at its top, placed inside the section's own page (the Atlas's flex column, the Materials `.page`) so it lines up with it.
  - The pill under the section shown glides from one to the next. It starts where the last one was and moves after the page is drawn again; Still and reduced motion make it jump.
  - A section a feature turns off leaves the row; with one section left there is no row.
  - `ATHUB.add({ key, label, order, on, count, render })`.
- **Map:** 14f's room (`ATLAS.render()`). The row goes into its column, and the room keeps fitting the window.
- **Materials:** `VIEWS.materials.render()`, looked up when drawn. The lab layer replaces `VIEWS.materials` after the app's files, so the website keeps its inventory card. The page's own filters redraw it in place.
- **Routes:** `#/atlas` · `#/atlas/materials`. `App.parse` reads `#/materials` as `#/atlas/materials`, for old links, the material sheet's "All materials" and the lab harness.
- **The tab row:** the Materials tab leaves `TABS`; `TABOF.materials` and `TABOF.mat` light the Atlas tab.
- **Settings:** `materials` is no longer a tab feature (`TabF` → `D`, no `tab`). "Materials (in the Atlas)": its switch takes the section out of the Atlas, and its preview shows the row.
- **Also fixed:** V4.47's preview boxes in the note used `var(--f-s)`, which doesn't exist. The text is now the components' serif (`--f-b`) and the link labels the sans (`--f-d`).
