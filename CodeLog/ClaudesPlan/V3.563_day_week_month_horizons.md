# V3.563–V3.564 — Day / Week / Month horizon tabs (My Day + Team Focus)

## Goal

Extend the "My Day" planning concept to two longer horizons — **My Week**
and **My Month** — presented as tabs within the existing My Day page, and
mirror the same horizon idea on the **Team Focus** page (which is
week-native today) by adding a **Month** rollup tab. One page, three (My
Day) / two (Team Focus) time lenses; the composer and per-item actions
stay reachable so planning never leaves the surface.

## Problem

My Day only ever shows a single day's timeline. To see the week you have
to click through seven days; to see the month there is no view at all.
Team Focus is locked to one week — a mentor can't glance at how a
mentee's focus has trended across the month without stepping week-by-week.

## Approach

### My Day (V3.563) — `rm/js/my-day.js` + `rm/css/my-day.css`

- New `state.view` = `'day' | 'week' | 'month'`; a `renderTabs()` pill bar
  renders above every view. `?view=` URL param opens a specific tab.
- **Day tab** = the existing surface, unchanged (datebar, week strip,
  focus panel, pending, today's timeline, composer).
- **Week tab** (`renderWeekView`): month/week nav bar + the week's focus
  panel + the 7 days of the viewed week, each a card listing its items via
  a compact `renderMiniItem` (icon, title, time, 🎯 focus, ✓ done). Empty
  days show a "＋ plan something" jump. Day header / date cells switch to
  the Day tab for that date. Composer stays mounted.
- **Month tab** (`renderMonthView`): month nav bar + a Monday-aligned
  calendar grid (`monthGridDays`, always whole weeks). Each cell shows the
  day number, up to 3 title pills + "+N" on desktop, and a count badge on
  phone (pills hidden — 7-wide is too narrow for titles). Tapping a cell
  drops into the Day tab. Month is read-first: the composer is hidden.
- Data: `loadMonth()` = one `DD.listRange(gridStart, gridEnd)` (no new
  Firestore query shape, no index). `findItem` also searches
  `state.monthItems`; `refresh()` reloads the month when that tab is open.

### Team Focus (V3.564) — `rm/js/team-focus.js` + `rm/css/team-focus.css`

- New `state.view` = `'week' | 'month'`, `state.monthOffset`,
  `state.monthFocuses`; `renderTabs()` pill bar.
- **Week tab** = the existing mentor surface, unchanged.
- **Month tab** (`renderMonthView`): month nav + the Monday-weeks
  overlapping the month, each week a group listing that week's focus areas
  read-only (title, status chip, N/M task progress, reported outcome, 🎯
  research-focus link). "open ›" on a week jumps to the Week tab at that
  week for editing. Switching people or months reloads via
  `loadMonthFocuses()`.
- Data: `FOCUS_AREAS.listForRange(uid, minWeekId, maxWeekId)` — already
  exists (PMR uses it). No new data-layer function, no rules change.

## Data model changes

None. Both tabs read existing collections (`userData/{uid}/dayPlans`,
`userData/{uid}/focusAreas`) through existing helpers (`DD.listRange`,
`FOCUS_AREAS.listForRange`). No new Firestore paths, indexes, rules, or
Cloud Functions.

## Out of scope

- Monthly focus *rollup analytics* on the My Day month tab (success-rate
  trend, streak) — the calendar is item-level only for now.
- A "Day" tab on Team Focus (focus areas have no day granularity).
- Drag-to-reschedule from the week/month views (tap-to-day, then edit).

## Release ritual

- sw.js bump ✅. No rules/index/function deploy. **FTP-only** — one
  network-first reload picks it up.

## Rollback

Pure additive UI on existing data — `git checkout` the four source files +
sw.js and re-FTP. No data written in a new shape, nothing to clean up.

## Verification (after FTP + reload)

1. My Day: Day tab looks/behaves exactly as before.
2. My Day → Week: 7 day-cards for the current week; prev/next week nav;
   tap a day → Day tab on that date; ✓ marks an item done in place.
3. My Day → Month: calendar grid for the month; prev/next month; tap a day
   → Day tab; phone shows a count badge, desktop shows title pills.
4. Team Focus → Month: week groups for the month with status/outcome/task
   progress; "open ›" lands on the Week tab at that week; person + month
   switches reload correctly.
5. Non-admin member sees their own horizons; mentor sees a mentee's month.
