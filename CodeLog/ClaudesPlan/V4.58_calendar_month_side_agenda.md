# V4.58 — Scientia Calendar: Month with a day on the side, Load in hours, Agenda

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "The month view should allow you to click on the day, bringing the day as a side panel on the right side of the monthly planner. Add another sorting to be load (time-wise), since this view does not directly correlate card size to time."

The calendar's core is V4.57. Load was already one of the Colour by choices there; this version makes it what the month needs, since a month's chips don't show their length.

## What changed
- **Month (M)**, in whole weeks.
  - Each day is a tile with its all-day items, then its events (a colour swatch, the time, the title), and "+N more" when they don't fit.
  - ‹ › go a month at a time.
- **A click on a day opens it on the right** (`CAL.side`), on the tile or its number. The panel is that day's planner column, built by the same code as the week (`CAL.planner([day])`): the same scroll, 12 hours to the height (`sc._days`, so `CAL.fit` opens it on now or 8:00), a frozen header with the all-day row, cards, and click-to-create.
  - The grid narrows to make room rather than being covered, and the open day stays outlined.
  - Another day switches the panel. The same day again, its × or Esc closes it. "+N more" opens it, and "Open as a day" switches to Day view.
  - It opens only on a click, never on hover.
- **Load in the month:** with Colour by Load,
  - each day shows its planned hours ("5.5 h") and a thin bar, full at 8 h;
  - each chip shows its length on a tag in its band's shade;
  - the bands can be singled out like any other value.
- **A chip dragged onto another day** moves there at the same time (one of a repeat leaves its series, as in the week).
- **Agenda (A):** the next two weeks as a list by day, today first, all-day items first each day. Each row has its time, colour, title, kind and length, and its note. A click opens the note; resting on a row opens it in place.
