# V4.7 — Floats, and the golden reference tag

## Goal
Gabe (16 Nov 2026): a float is something that belongs to a note not made yet; /float brings a hemisphere dome of floats to drag into the current note; a placed float is assigned, no longer a float, and shows as a bridge from where it was written. On the prototype: not a kind but tracked like one; right-click, or the command on Ctrl-selected components; light purple / sky blue; a cloud; the dome flush with the canvas's bottom, blending into the note through a gradient of its palette, the glass clear and glistening; the reference tag golden.

## Approach
- `b.afloat = { at, t }` (marked), `b.afloat.to = { docId, blockId, at }` (placed). Not `b.float` (CLIP.lift). Like V4.5's references: a flag beside the kind.
- `14h-floats.js` wraps `DocView.box` (cloud + "afloat", "assigned float"; a placed float's bridge band "Float · from … · date" and *Back to the floats*), `DocView.boxMenu` (Make it a float / No longer a float / Make them floats (n) / Back to the floats), `INK.paintSel` (Make floats on the selection's bar), and adds the `/float` command (selected → mark; none → the dome).
- The dome is an overlay in the note's canvas element (`pane.paper.el`): a wide cap on its bottom edge (below), a skirt of the note's paper fading up, the glass clear (rim gradient, a fine edge, `BUBBLE.warp(D)` as a backdrop filter where supported, two highlights, a slow sheen). Beads on the cap's face (golden-angle azimuths, elevation by age, oldest lowest), projected with depth for size and order; a rAF loop turns it only while open, not under reduced motion, never while the pointer is inside or dragging; the bead under the pointer by geometry with hysteresis (as V4.0's Atlas). Rest (setting) or click → preview; drag → `BRIDGE.make(…, { quiet })` at `paper.toWorld(drop)`, `asFloat`; one Undo (`DB.beginGroup('float')`).
- API for the Monthly review (V4.8): `FLOATS.isFloat / waiting / placed / mark / unmark / place / back / open / close / old`.
- Settings → Features → Floats: switch + spin, by, old, rest, place; preview = a mini canvas and dome.
- Golden reference tag: `--gold-grad` on the card's bookmark, the gallery cards' corner, the kindless "Reference" chip and the Atlas's References tab (`data-t` on its tab buttons).

## The dome as a wide cap (Gabe's follow-up on the prototype; first proposed as V4.9)
Gabe (16 Nov 2026): "Have it not be a hemisphere, but an extended view of the tip of a hemisphere, spanning the whole horizontal space, and maybe 3/4 of the vertical space it currently takes."

A spherical cap: the circular segment whose chord is the canvas pane's bottom edge across its full width W and whose height is h = ¾ of a half dome's height (D/2, with D = max(220, round(min(0.74 W, 0.8 H, 820) / 8) · 8)), at least 84 px. The sphere's radius is Rs = (h² + (W/2)²) / 2h, its centre Rs below the apex; the outline is the arc from the bottom-left corner over the apex to the bottom-right corner. The same numbers are used by the prototype.

- The dome box spans the pane (left 0, right 0, height h) and is clipped with `clip-path: circle(Rs at 50% Rs)`; it slides up from the bottom edge as before; the skirt (the note's paper fading upward) runs the full width.
- Glass: radial gradients centred on the sphere's centre for the bright rim and an inner glint; an SVG edge along the arc (white inside, a faint ink line); two small highlights near the upper left of the arc, rotated to its tangent; the slow sheen. The rim's bend is a displacement map made for the cap (a thin band at the rim, scale ≤ band / 3 so nothing folds over — the first try mirrored text under the rim).
- Beads: golden-angle azimuths turned by the spin map across the width over which the cap is at least 40 px tall (cos of the azimuth); each sits between the base and the arc above it by its elevation (oldest lowest); depth scales size and stacking. `inDome` tests the segment.
- Settings preview: an elliptic segment through both bottom corners spanning the mini canvas.
