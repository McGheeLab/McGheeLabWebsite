# V4.14 — The Problem kind

## Goal
Gabe (17 Nov 2026): "Add a Problem kind: it highlights a barrier in literature (must be cited by a paper(s)) with opportunity for improvement or solving, but is not counted as a barrier towards your project."

## Approach
- **A new kind, `problem`** (`scientia/src/13zzyb-problem.js`, `PROBLEM`), pushed onto `CKINDS.BASE` after Result and Report (13zzi, 13zzy), for text components. Olive (#7D7A16), a hue no other kind uses (Barrier is a structure in `--bad` red; Troubleshooting is red too). Its ways, in the kinds' own vocabulary:
  - it fuels a question or a hypothesis; it leads to a method, a task or a decision;
  - a result, an Insight, a Method or a Verdict resolves it;
  - a paper supports it;
  - it refines another Problem.
- **It must be cited by a paper** (`PROBLEM.cites(b)`): a cite of a Library paper in its text (`marks[type=cite]`), a quote from a PDF (or from a page linked to the Library, `RDX.libOf`), or a wire to a paper (a PDF card, an RM paper, a component that cites one — `CKINDS.is(x, 'paper')`). Settings can count only a cite in its own text.
  - Until it is cited: "cite a paper" in its header (a `span` that gives way like V4.13's other header parts; a click opens `ED.citePicker` at the end of its text) and its left edge dashed (`.pb-uncited`). Once cited, the tag's title says who cites it.
  - Choosing Problem for an uncited component is allowed, and a toast says what it needs (`CKINDS.set` wrapped).
- **Never a barrier towards your project:**
  - The kind itself is not counted: `CKINDS.counted` counts Insights, Results and unexplained Anomalies only.
  - A barrier structure marked inside a Problem is the literature's barrier, quoted. `IX.status` is wrapped to read it as `literature`, not open. Every count built on it leaves it out — `IX.openBarriers` (the Portfolio, Today, the Monthly review), `IX.themeHealth`, the review lanes — and `IX.pullable` (Pull in) leaves it out too.
- **Settings → Features → Structures → Problems:** the switch (off: Problem is not offered in "what kind is this?" and the reminder goes; a Problem keeps its kind), "What counts as its citation" (a cite, a quote or a wire / a cite in its text), a live preview. The Atlas lists Problem with the kinds (it reads `CKINDS.all()`).
