# V4.32 — A paper or page read beside becomes a popup window

## Goal
Gabe (18 Nov 2026):

> Each one of these read beside now gains the option to become a popup that is a new window within SiS that can be moved freely (via dragging from four anchor points in the middle of each rectangle line), resized freely (via corners). These buttons only appear when the cursor is on the border of the popup. The popup does not have any buttons otherwise. If it just a rounded-rectangle blending in, but with a minor shadow or display to show it is superposed on top of the SiS. If you’re in the popup space, scrolling scrolls through it, panning pans through it, clicking and dragging through text selects it, interacting with an HTML interacts with it, etc… Holding your mouse still for two seconds within the popup space brings up a minimal interface with buttons for fitting the content to the popup window (horizontal only) along with +/- zoom buttons, maximize, which goes back to your previous read beside or maximize position before the popup was triggered.

## Approach
A new module, `scientia/src/13zzzc-rpopup.js` (`RPOP`), and `scientia/src/35-rpopup.css`. No edits to `13zzb-reader.js` or `13zz-maxhl.js`: everything is a wrap.

- **A popup is a pane without the reader.** `RPOP.make` builds the same pane object `RDOCK` uses (`p.i = -1`, `p.pop`), an `.embed-max.dk-max.rd-dock.rd-docked` element with a hidden bar and `.dk-maxc`, given its highlights by `MXHL.open`, its wheel to `RDOCK.wheel` and its scroll to `RDOCK.onScroll`. So `RDOCK.draw` draws it (the PDF through the V4.31 zoom, an HTML page as the pane's page), and `RDOCK.zoom`, `RVIEW.fit`, the pen highlighter, the selection menu, the References Map and the scissors work in it unchanged. `RDOCK.paneOf` (and so `RDOCK.has`) counts popups: the card says "In a popup window", quotes and the citation band go to it, and opening it beside while it is out brings the popup to the front instead.
- **In.** ⧉ on each pane's bar, before ⤢ (a wrap of `RDOCK.build`), and "⧉ Popup" on the maximised view's bar, before Close (wraps of `DOCS.max` / `EMBED.max`). `RPOP.fromPane` remembers where it was (`from: { k: 'pane', pane, side, n, full }`, its zoom, Fit, page and place, a page's scroll), gives the tab up (`RDOCK.closeTab`: the pane takes the next tab, or the reader closes) and opens the popup there; `RPOP.fromMax` does the same from the maximised view (`from: { k: 'max' }`, fitted) and closes it. The scissors stay on if they were.
- **The window** (`RPOP.mount`): `.rp-pop` (fixed, 12 px corners, a light shadow, `z-index` 1215 and up, under menus and popovers) holding `.rp-body` (the pane and the bar), four `.rp-rim` strips (the border's band, `--rp-reach` outside and 5 px inside), four `.rp-mv` anchors at the middle of the sides and four `.rp-rz` corners. Nothing shows at rest.
- **The border** (`edgeIn` / `edgeOut` / `edgeShow`): resting on the band or a handle shows the anchors and corners after the reveal time (Settings, 1 s by default, Gabe's rule for hover reveals); they can be grabbed before they show, the cursor saying move or resize. A tap of the border (touch, pen) shows them at once, and the bar too, for 4 s — a tablet has no hover.
- **Move and resize** (`RPOP.drag`): pointer capture on the handle, iframes deaf while dragging (`body.rp-drag`), `RPOP.geo` / `RPOP.fit` keep it on the screen and at least 260 × 180. The pointer's coordinates are the turned frame's when the app is rotated (`ROTATE` patches them), so the same code works turned. After a resize a fitted page fits again (`RVIEW.resized`).
- **The bar** (`RPOP.moved`, `RPOP.ui`): the pointer still inside the body for the still time (2 s) with no button pressed shows `.rp-ui` — Fit, −, the zoom, +, Maximize. Moving inside keeps it; leaving hides it (0.26 s). Over an HTML page the parent sees no pointer, so the page's copy carries one more small script (`RPOP.SCRIPT`, after V4.31's) that, once a popup asks (`rv-ask` with `moves`), reports its moves, presses and wheel (`rv-move`, `rv-down`, `rv-wheel`); a card's page is never asked.
- **Maximize** (`RPOP.max`): back where it came from at the same page and zoom — its pane (`RDOCK.open` with the pane and, when the reader was closed, the side; the split restored; full screen again if it was) or the maximised view (`DOCS.max` / `EMBED.max`, at the page).
- **Several popups**, one per file; a press on one (or in its page) brings it to the front (`RPOP.front`).
- **Kept on this device** (`_ls 'rpop'`): each file's place and size (`geo`), the last used (`last`, for a new one, moved along if another sits there), and the open popups with where they came from, their zoom and place (`RPOP.persist`, on changes, scrolling and `pagehide`). They stay from note to note, step aside on pages that aren't notes (`.rp-away`, `RPOP.sync` on `hashchange` and `DocView.render`), come back after a reload with the next note (`RPOP.restore`), and never open in the phone layout. A file that goes takes its popup; its PDF is never let go while it shows (a wrap of `PDFW.trim`).
- **Settings → Reading & sources → Popup windows** (`rpop`, Standard and Everything): "The bar shows after holding the pointer still for" (1 / 2 / 3 s), "The anchors and corners show after resting on the border for" (no wait / 0.5 / 1 s), "The border reaches" (narrow 8 / medium 14 / wide 22 px); a live preview — a popup over a note as if hovered (anchors, corners, the band at its reach, the bar), its caption saying the times. Off (its switch, or a preset without it — a wrap of `FEAT.apply`): no ⧉, and open popups go back where they came from.

## Decisions where the request was silent
- **Places and sizes per file**, a new popup taking the last used.
- **Popups behave like reading tabs across notes**: they stay from note to note and step aside on other pages; they do not belong to the note they were opened in.
- **The anchors' reveal waits 1 s** (Gabe's rule for hover reveals) but they can be dragged before they show; Settings can make it immediate.
- **No close button** (Gabe: no buttons otherwise): Maximize returns it, and its card's "Put it back here" puts it back in the card.
- **Fit in a popup is the width only**, as Gabe said; an HTML page wider than the popup is scaled to fit.
- The tab of a file in a popup leaves the tab row (the popup is where it is); Maximize puts it back.
- A tablet: a tap of the border shows the anchors, the corners and the bar for 4 s; the handles are larger under a coarse pointer.

## Testing
`rb_t32` at 1400 px with real pointer events, on the lab desktop build and the build without the lab layer:
- ⧉ on the pane: the popup at the same page, place and zoom; the reader gives it up; the card says "In a popup window";
- the look (12 px corners, a shadow, no buttons); no anchors with the pointer resting inside; on the border they show after the rest (not at 0.5 s, yes at 1.3 s);
- moves by the top and left anchors; resizes by two corners; the minimum; kept on the screen;
- the wheel scrolls inside; a drag over the text selects it and opens the highlight menu;
- the bar after 2 s still (not at 1.2 s), exactly Fit, −, the zoom, +, Maximize; Fit by a real click; −; the bar hides when the pointer leaves; a fitted popup made narrower stays fitted;
- Maximize back beside (pane, tab, side, Fit, page); from full screen, back to full screen;
- an HTML page: a click reaches the page; the bar shows from the page's own pointer and hides;
- two popups, the newest in front; a click on the one behind brings it forward;
- another note (they stay), the Portfolio (they step aside), a note again (they come back);
- "Put it back here" (`RDOCK.closeTab`) closes one; "⧉ Popup" on the maximised view → popup (a 1200 px page fitted) → Maximize back to the maximised view;
- turned 90°: a move drag at the turned screen point moves it by the turned delta; a touch tap on the border shows anchors and the bar; a reload brings it back where it was;
- the preview; a Simple preset and the switch off send it back beside; on again; the feature's page;
- FEAT.audit() empty, no sideways scroll, no errors.

`rb_t400` at 400 px: the two features' pages and previews fit; in the phone layout no popup opens (a saved one waits) and the reader stays shut.
