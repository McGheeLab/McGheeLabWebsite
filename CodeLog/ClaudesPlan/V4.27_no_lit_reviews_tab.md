# V4.27 — No Lit reviews tab: reviews live in the Portfolio

## Goal
Gabe (18 Nov 2026): "Remove the Lit Reviews tab."

## Approach
- **The tab is gone in every build.**
  - `15c-litreviews.js` no longer adds it.
  - The lab layer (`lab-mode.js` §13) still adds its own tab after the app's modules, and it is not edited here. So the tab is taken out where the row is drawn: `FEAT.tabOn('litreviews')` is false (wrapped in 15c), and `20a`'s `renderTabs` hides every tab whose `FEAT.tabOn` is false. The same check keeps it out of Settings' tab previews (`PV.tabs`).
- **What lights:** a review on screen, or the reviews' page, lights the **Portfolio** tab. `15c`'s `renderTabs` wrapper sets `TABOF.doc` and `TABOF.litreviews` to `portfolio` during the draw; it runs inside the lab layer's wrapper, so it wins.
- **Where reviews are now: the Portfolio.**
  - Each project's Lit reviews section is always drawn (in All with every theme, and in the Lit reviews switch). It lists the reviews linked to the project.
  - **+ Lit review** sits beside its heading. `LITREV.start([projectId])` calls the lab layer's own `SIS_LAB.reviews.create` where it is loaded (its first block, its signature), else `LITREV.create`. Either way the new review is linked to that project.
  - With no review yet, the section says "No lit review yet — + Lit review starts one, linked to this project".
  - A review linked to **no** project is listed with the notes outside any project (`PFLIST.parts` for the general row). It is still not a quick note (`GENERAL.docs` leaves reviews out).
  - The lab layer replaces the Portfolio's action buttons (`PV.actions`), so the button is in the section, which every build draws the same way.
- **The page** (`#/litreviews`: the app's, or the lab layer's) still opens from a link or search.
- **Settings → Features → Reading → Lit reviews:**
  - the description;
  - a live preview: the Portfolio's tab row without Lit reviews, a project's Lit reviews box with + Lit review and two reviews;
  - the switch, now working: off drops + Lit review and the empty sections, and the reviews you made stay listed.

  `tab: 'litreviews'` is gone from the feature.
- **The manual:** the Portfolio's paragraph says + Lit review, no tab, and where an unlinked review is.
- **The Lit reviews demonstration** (`demokit/litrev.mjs`) starts in the Portfolio: + Lit review in the project's Lit reviews, then its topic. It is linked to the project already. Recorded again.
- **The v3869 lab harness, section 17** (for Alex):
  - "a Lit reviews tab sits right after Portfolio" → "no Lit reviews tab in the row";
  - the page and the open review light the Portfolio tab;
  - "the tab lists it" → "the page lists it";
  - a new check: + Lit review in a project's Lit reviews makes the lab layer's review, linked to that project.
