# V3.771 — The Modules tab is a chosen list and a shelf of courses

**Alex:** *"on the teacher dashboard modules page I dont want all the modules
layed out like that. instead lets have cards that cluster the modules by
group. click on a card and it opens up with a list of the available modules.
No due date, No visibility, keep preview. make a button for 'make available'.
At the top of the page is a selected list of learning modules that will be
available to the students. how teachers assign the learning modules and assign
grades etc is up to them, we dont need to keep track of grades or anything
just yet. Worksheets should go along with learning modules not be separate
items."*

Plus, mid-build: *"when in the teacher dashboard, I do not want to see the hero
animation on this page, or any other page associated with classes. Also on the
footer, the links are a single column vertical, and its too long, lets make
this a 3 column link."*

## Goal

Reduce the teacher's job on this page to one question — **which lessons do my
students get?** — and make the answer visible in one glance.

## What was there (V3.769)

Three stacked cards: assigned modules (each row carrying order arrows, a due
date, a "visible" checkbox, preview and remove), then the whole published
library as a flat run of ~70 checkbox rows grouped by a bare `<strong>` folder
name, then an assignments editor (draft/publish/close, points, due dates)
feeding a gradebook on the Students tab.

## What it is now

**Top card — "Available to your students (N)".** The class page as the students
will see it, in order. Each row: position, title, course, ▲▼, **Preview**,
**Remove**. Nothing else.

**Below — "Module library".** A responsive grid of **one card per course**
(`manifest.json`'s folder label), each showing how many of its lessons are
already available. Clicking a card opens it **in place, spanning the grid**
(one open at a time), listing that course's lessons with **Preview** and
**Make available** — or a ✓ Available chip and Remove for ones already chosen.

**Worksheets travel with their lesson.** `worksheet-03-…` (also `hw-03-…`,
`hw3-…`) attaches to the lesson numbered `03-` in the same folder, matched on
the **number, not the slug**, because the two often disagree
(`worksheet-01-surface-energy` ↔ `01-surface-energy-and-adsorbed-water`). A
worksheet whose lesson isn't published stays an item of its own (bme466's
`hw-00-pretest` has no lesson 00). Making a lesson available brings its
worksheets; removing it takes them away.

**Gone: due dates, per-module visibility, the assignment editor, the
gradebook, and per-student module overrides.** Alex's line — *how teachers
assign and grade is up to them* — is the whole rationale: the site's job is to
put lessons in front of a class and show who is working through them.

## Approach

- **Presentation only, no data-model change.** Worksheet pairing is computed
  from file names at render time; a worksheet is still an ordinary
  `schedules.modules[]` entry, so the student class page and the V3.672
  engagement beacon are untouched.
- **One save path.** The tab holds `nodes` (`{item, sheets[]}`); order, add and
  remove all rebuild `modules[]` as `[lesson, ...its worksheets]` in node order
  and renumber. Because every entry is rebuilt from the normalized item,
  **`dueDate` and `published:false` drain out of the data on the next save**
  rather than lingering invisibly behind removed controls.
- **The open course survives the re-render** (`openLibFolder` lives outside the
  render function), so "make available" doesn't collapse what you were reading.
- **Dropped two of six reads per view**: `loadHub()` no longer fetches
  `assignments` or `submissions`.

## Key decisions

- **Per-student module overrides went too.** Not named in the request, but they
  are the same two controls (hide / due date) one level down, and they
  contradict *"how teachers assign … is up to them"*. `setModuleOverride` and
  its rules are untouched, so restoring the panel is re-adding its render call.
- **Assignments are dormant, not deleted.** `assignments` / `submissions`, their
  rules and `functions/class-notify.js` stay deployed; only the teacher's
  surfaces are gone. Nothing shipped to users yet (this whole program is still
  in the un-pushed batch), so no teacher has real coursework to strand — and a
  student who somehow does still sees it.
- **One course open at a time.** The cards are a grid and the open one spans it;
  two open cards read as one confusing block.
- **Hero hiding is by route family.** `#/teach`, `#/teachers` and `#/engage`
  join `#/classes` in one `isClassEnv` flag rather than four scattered tests.

## Verification

Self-driving headless harness (scratchpad `qa-modules.html` + `qa-serve.py`,
Chrome `--headless=new`): the real SPA in a same-origin iframe with the demo
teacher persona seeded in `sessionStorage`. **53 assertions, all passing** —
12 course cards all closed with **0 of 56 module rows visible**; opening one
shows 6 rows spanning 918px; worksheets folded in with 0 strays; preview still
lazy (0 iframes → exactly 1, pointing at the real file) and the card stays
open behind it; "Make available" moves a lesson into the selected list with its
worksheets, flips the row to ✓ Available, updates the card count and leaves the
card open; the saved `modules[]` has lesson and worksheet adjacent, contiguous
`order`, no `dueDate`, no `published:false`; ▲ reorders; Remove arms an inline
confirm and takes the worksheets with it; three dashboard tiles with no
assignment wording, no ungraded badge, no gradebook or per-student settings on
Students; the old `/assignments` deep link still lands on Modules; no hero on
teach/teachers/engage/classes but still on `#/mission`; footer links are three
columns and three rows; no console errors, no `prompt/alert/confirm`.

**Two real bugs the harness caught**, both fixed and re-verified:

1. `.teach-lib-body` is `display:flex`, which **beats the `hidden`
   attribute** — every course card rendered open. (Second time this trap has
   bitten in two releases; there is now an explicit `[hidden]` guard with a
   comment saying why it is load-bearing.)
2. **Every confirmation message was being thrown away**: mutations did
   `setStatus(msg)` then `await ctx.refresh()`, and the refresh rebuilds the
   shell — including `#teach-status`. Pre-existing since V3.769; the three
   sites now set the status *after* the refresh.

⚠ Harness lesson: `index.html` reloads on the service worker's
`controllerchange`, which **replaces the iframe document**. Held node
references then report `isConnected === true` while `document.contains(node)`
is false — a preview iframe that provably existed read as missing. Wait for
`navigator.serviceWorker.controller` before touching anything.
