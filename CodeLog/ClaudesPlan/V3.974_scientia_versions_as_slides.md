# V3.974 — Scientia: the versions of a component as slides on its left

**Date:** 2026-11-11 · **Branch:** `scientia/portfolio-slides` · **Gabe:** "The iteration cards should actually reside not as a deck, but a
set of 'slides' that you can scroll through vertical of the component on the left, like PowerPoint. Just click on the version you want
and brings it up in the component window."

## What changed (`scientia/src/13zzq-deck.js`, `24-kinds.css`)

- The component shows one version. Inside its left edge, a column of slides (`.iter-slides`, absolutely placed in the box's left
  padding, scrolling on its own) lists every version: "v1", "v2"… (or "attempt 1"… for Troubleshooting) over a thumbnail — an image
  panel's first picture, a page's, PDF's or website's name, or a text's first words. A click brings that version up; + at the foot
  makes the next. The other versions are not drawn; they keep the same spot, so a move moves them all.
- **The content keeps its room:** a line shown as slides is 104 px (`IDECK.SLW`, CSS `--slw`) wider. `IDECK.widen(b, ±1)` sets one
  width for every version (one at a time, the one-size rule of V3.970 copied a half-done width and widened twice) and marks the root
  `iterSlides`; Spread out narrows, Show as slides widens; a deck made before is widened once when first drawn.
- Kept: the version tag and its menu, "how it improved" under a version, one size for all versions, Spread out, ↑ ↓ / ← → with the
  component selected. Gone: the horizontal strip with ‹ › and dots, the swipe, the cards stacked beneath, `fit`/`watch`.
