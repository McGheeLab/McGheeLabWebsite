# V4.24 — Problem is now Challenge

## Goal
Gabe (18 Nov 2026): "Instead of Problem, name it Challenge."

V4.14's kind (a barrier in the literature, cited by its paper(s), never counted as a barrier towards your project) keeps everything it does; only its name changes, everywhere a person sees it and in the code.

## Approach
- **The module** `scientia/src/13zzyb-problem.js` → `13zzyb-challenge.js`; `PROBLEM` → `CHALLENGE`; the kind's id `problem` → `challenge` (label Challenge, "a Challenge"); the feature `problems` → `challenges` (Settings → Features → Structures → Challenges); the classes `.pb-cite` / `.pb-uncited` → `.cg-cite` / `.cg-uncited`, `body.f-off-problems` → `body.f-off-challenges`.
- **Nothing written under the old name is lost** (`CHALLENGE.migrate`, idempotent):
  - components of kind `problem` → `challenge`;
  - the switch (`settings().feat.on.problems`, or this device's own `feat` when the switches are per device) → `challenges`;
  - "What counts as its citation" (`tune['problems.cite']`) → `challenges.cite`;
  - a kind of your own (`settings().ckinds`) that links to `problem`, or one you saved over the built-in Problem, → `challenge`.
  It runs with the standard templates (boot, a changed profile, an import, as V4.15's title tidy does), and again 1.5 s after a component of kind `problem` is stored, which is what a device still on V4.14–V4.23 sends through sync. Until then `CHALLENGE.is` reads both ids.
- The colour stays olive (#7D7A16); Gabe has not asked for another.
- **The demonstration of Structures** (`demokit/structures.mjs`) names Challenge in its caption, and is recorded again.
