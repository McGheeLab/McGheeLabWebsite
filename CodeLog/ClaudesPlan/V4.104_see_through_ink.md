# V4.104 — Scientia: ink is see-through, and what it lies over can be clicked

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "The ink background should be invisible/see through. You may interact with components overlaid by ink components."

Look B (V4.42) gave every `.box` a card: its background, ring and shadow. Its rule, `body:not(.f-off-look) .box`, outweighs the ink's own `.box.w-ink{background:transparent}`, so since then ink has stood on a card. And the ink component's whole rectangle took the pointer, so a component under it could not be clicked or edited.

## What changed
**`21-sync.css`**
- **No card under ink, whatever the look:** no background, ring or shadow. The dashed outline shows only while a stroke is hovered or the ink is selected. The selector doubles the class (`.paper .box.w-ink.w-ink`) to outweigh the look's rules; the zoomed-out tint is cleared too.
- **Click through:** the ink component takes no pointer. Only these do:
  - its strokes;
  - its title, while it shows;
  - its tags and ports.

  A click, a drag or a double-click on its empty part reaches the component or the paper beneath. This is what ink written on an image already did (`.on-img`).
- **Easy to catch:** a pen line is thin, so each freehand stroke gets a see-through edge 10 screen pixels wide (`stroke: transparent`, `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`). It does not change how the stroke looks. Highlighter strokes and shapes are stroked already and keep their own width.
- **It stays on top:** the ink is lifted above the component it was written over (z-index 5, like ink on an image). Otherwise the component being edited (`:focus-within`, z 3) would rise over the ink and hide it.

**Settings → Ink & shapes** (`20a-features.js`)
- A new switch, *Click through ink*, on by default.
- Off: `body.ink-solid` (`13zf-ink.js` `INK.solid`, kept by `FEAT.apply`), and the whole rectangle takes the pointer again, as before. The ink stays see-through either way.
- The preview (`PV.inkThrough`) draws ink over a component. With the switch on, the component is outlined as the one the click reaches; off, the ink's rectangle is dashed.

## Unchanged
- **The pen:** it writes on the paper and on images as before. It still refuses to start on another component, and strokes still gather by where they are written (`INK.target`), not by what was clicked.
- **Lasso, eraser and fill:** they work by geometry, so they are unaffected.
