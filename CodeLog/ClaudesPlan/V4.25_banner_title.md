# V4.25 — Thinner theme banners, with the theme's name inside

## Goal
Gabe (18 Nov 2026): "Banners should be thinner and have the title within it (specifically to the left horizontally, and the middle vertically)."

## Approach
- **Thinner:** V4.17's 1600 × 320 px (5 : 1) becomes **1600 × 200 px (8 : 1)** — `BANNER.W/H`, `aspect-ratio: 8 / 1` (at least 52 px tall). At 1400 px a Portfolio banner goes from about 196 px to about 122 px tall. Images chosen at the old size are cover-fitted, as any other size is; the menu, the toast, the hover hint and Settings say the new size. On a phone (≤ 760 px) the banner is 6 : 1, so the name still has room, and the size hint (a hover) is left out.
- **The title inside, at the left and halfway down:** `BANNER.name(t, place)` puts the theme's name in the banner (`position: absolute; left: clamp(12px, 2.2%, 24px); top: 50%; translateY(-50%)`, one line with an ellipsis, at most 64% of the width). A wash of the page's own colour from the left (`.tb-ban::before`, 84% → 0 by 62%) keeps it legible on any image and in light and dark; the ink is `--ink`.
  - **In the Portfolio** it is `a.tb-name`, the link to the theme (its click does not open the banner's menu). The section gets `.tb-has`, which hides the name in the row under the banner; that row keeps the pill, the score, the open barriers and the weeks (V4.19's clicks on them are untouched).
  - **On the theme's page** it is `h1.tb-name`, the page's title: the banner replaces the page's `h1.title`, under the eyebrow (the project · theme), so there is one title.
  - With "Show the banner" set to one place, or banners off, the other place shows the name as before.
- **Settings → Theme banners:** the description and the live preview (the name inside each mock banner; the theme page's mock under its eyebrow) say 1600 × 200 and where the name goes.
