# V3.37 — News: Team (People Involved) + Feed Filters

## Goal
1. Let a news post author tag other lab members (mentor + contributors) on a post, mirroring the Team section on research stories.
2. Add a filter bar to the public News page so visitors can narrow the feed by post type, person involved, time range, and whether a post has comments.

## Approach

### Team on news posts
- Mirror the story editor's Team UI: readonly Author (from current profile), Mentor `<select>`, Contributor picker + chip list.
- Reuse existing DOM structure — same ids/classes as the story editor where it's safe. Use distinct ids on news-only controls to avoid collisions when both pages ever co-exist.
- Persist `team: { author, mentor, contributors }` on the post doc alongside the legacy `authorName`/`authorUid`/`authorPhoto` fields (don't remove those — they're consumed by admin panel and legacy cards).
- Load `team` on edit; restore mentor selection + rebuild contributor chips.
- Render `team` in the public card's expanded area by switching `buildStoryHTML(blocks)` → `buildExpandedHTML(blocks, post)` (already composes team + sections + refs).

### Filters on public News page
- New filter bar above `#news-feed` with:
  - **Type**: multi-select chips over `NEWS_CATEGORIES` (6 options). Click to toggle.
  - **Person**: dropdown — union of `authorName` + `team.mentor.name` + `team.contributors[].name` across all loaded posts.
  - **Time**: chips "All / 30 days / 3 months / 1 year" (single-select).
  - **Has comments**: checkbox.
- Live "Showing N of M" count + "Clear filters" link.
- Filters are pure client-side on the already-loaded posts (re-render from the cached list). AND across filters; OR within type chips.
- Comment counts are prefetched in parallel once after posts load (`Promise.all(getCommentsByStory)`) and stored on each post as `_commentCount`, so the "has comments" filter is instant. Counts also power the comment-count badges on cards (removes the per-card fetch in `wireNewsFeedInteractions`).

## Key decisions
- Don't remove legacy `authorName`/`authorUid`/`authorPhoto` — keep backwards compat with admin review panel and any pending/older posts. The `team.author` is an additive mirror.
- Filters are client-side so there's no new Firestore indexing required. If the news feed ever grows past ~hundreds of posts, revisit with server-side query params.
- Person filter shows names (not uids) for UX. If two users share a name, both are matched by name — acceptable for the lab's scale.
- No sort dropdown yet — newest-first stays default. Easy to add later.
