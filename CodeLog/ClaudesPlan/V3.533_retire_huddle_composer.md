# V3.533 — My Day R8: retire the huddle plan composer (flagged)

Part of the **My Day program** — see `V3.526_my_day_program.md`.

## Goal
First retirement: with My Day distributing to the huddle, the huddle's
own composer is duplicate input. Board/viewer, sign-ups, help requests
all stay.

## Approach
- `labConfig/featureFlags.myday_retire_huddle_composer` (**default
  false** — ships dark). Read via `DAY_DISTRIBUTE.getFeatureFlags()`
  (cached; absent doc ⇒ no flags).
- When flipped, the collapsed composer button becomes
  "➕ Plan your week in My Day → (it posts here too)" linking to
  `my-day.html?compose=1`. The composer CODE stays intact — editing an
  existing plan still opens it — so rollback = flip the flag, no deploy.

## Deploy
FTP only. Flip the flag in Firestore (labConfig/featureFlags) after a
week of R1–R7 adoption.

## Verify
Flag absent/false → old composer identical. Flag true → CTA routes to My
Day and a composed plan appears on the huddle board; editing/deleting
pre-existing native plans still works.
