# V4.19 — The Activity tab leaves; a project's or theme's activity opens from the Portfolio

## Goal
Gabe (17 Nov 2026): "Remove Activity tab → Make the Activity interactable in Portfolio for each Project or Theme." — "The project or theme activity health you see in the Portfolio can be clicked on."

## What was there
- **The Activity tab (19 `ActivityView`)** showed measured time for the whole notebook: ten weeks, every theme on one scale, and the weekly numbers. The lab's web build already dropped the tab (lab-mode §1); the desktop lab build kept it (23d `DESKLAB.KEEP`).
- **The Portfolio's "activity" (15d `PFKIT`)** is a different measure, the health:
  - a note scores only in the two weeks from its date (a component +1, a structure +3, a takeaway or a result +10);
  - the score is the last four weeks, and three weeks without any is a stall.

  It is drawn as heat strips (`PFKIT.heat`), "active 2d ago" / "stalled" (`PFLIST.when`) and "activity 34 · 4 wk" (`PFKIT.say`), none of which you could click.

## Approach
- **A new module, `scientia/src/19b-pfactivity.js` (`PFACT`).** It wraps the views rather than editing them.
  - **`PortfolioView.detail`:** the open project's eight-week strip, its score and its state become buttons.
  - **`PFLIST.section`:** a theme section's strip, its score and a "stalled" pill become buttons.
  - **`PortfolioView.render`:** a row's small strip in the list becomes a button. It picks the project first. Settings can turn this off.
  - **`ThemeView.render`:** the theme page's eight weeks and its two activity tiles become buttons.
  - **How they open:** by click, Enter or Space; never by hover. Their look is a teal ring on hover or focus, so it is plain they can be clicked.
- **The panel (`aside.pfa`):** fixed at the right under the header, 460 px wide (620 px for the whole notebook), and full width at 760 px or less. It closes with ✕ or Esc (Esc goes to a dialog or menu first if one is open), and focus returns to what opened it. Leaving the Portfolio, project or theme pages closes it, and a change while it is open redraws it. It holds:
  - **health:** the score, active or stalled, why in one sentence, what the score is made of in the last four weeks (components, structures, takeaways and results, with their weights), and how it is counted;
  - **activity:** eight weeks (`PFKIT.heat`);
  - **time recorded:** in a theme, or in a project's themes (`heatmapEl` gains `themeIds`), with the totals for four weeks and seven days. The notes outside any project have none of their own;
  - **its themes** (a project's, or every active theme for the notebook): each with its strip, score and state. A click opens that theme's own panel, and ‹ comes back;
  - **notes in their two weeks** (`PFKIT.noteAct`): each opens the note;
  - **what moved:** each moment that scored in the last four weeks, by day. It comes from the same `PFKIT.events` as the score, which now keeps each moment's block or item and note. Each line opens it in its note (`App.openBlock`).
- **The tab goes from `TABS` in every build.**
  - `#/activity` (Today's "Activity →") opens the Portfolio with the whole notebook in the panel: the old tab's parts, `ActivityView.byTheme` and `ActivityView.weekly`, split out of its render.
  - With the feature off, `#/activity` is the old page.
  - The lab's web build keeps its pointer to RM's tracker (lab-mode §2 replaces `VIEWS.activity` after the app). The desktop lab build restores the app's view (23d), so it gets the panel.
- **Settings → Tabs & overview → Portfolio activity** replaces the "Activity" tab switch:
  - the switch: off, nothing opens and the strips are plain, as before;
  - "The strips in the list open it too" (on);
  - a live preview whose strip opens a small panel.
