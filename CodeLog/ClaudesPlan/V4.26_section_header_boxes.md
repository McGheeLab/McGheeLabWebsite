# V4.26 — The Portfolio's section headers in colour-coded boxes

## Goal
Gabe (18 Nov 2026): "The experiments, syntheses, notes, and lit reviews should all have their section header (meaning symbol and title) be in a rounded rectangle box that is color-coded universally across projects for users to see fast."

## Approach
- **One colour per kind of section, the same in every project:**

  | Kind | Light | Dark |
  |---|---|---|
  | Experiments | `--sec-exp` #005A8F (blue) | #5BB6F0 |
  | Syntheses | `--sec-syn` #923670 (plum) | #E08FC0 |
  | Notes | `--sec-notes` #844B00 (ochre) | #F0A848 |
  | Lit reviews | `--sec-lit` #006A4A (green) | #3FCB9C |

  The colours are a darkened (light) / lightened (dark) Okabe–Ito set: blue, reddish purple, orange, bluish green. Their pairwise OKLab distance is 14 or more for normal vision. Under simulated colour blindness two pairs come closer (3–5), so the symbol and the word name each kind too, never the colour alone. Text in each colour on its own box passes 4.5 : 1, in light (box: a 12% tint of the colour into `--surface`) and in dark (15%). `forced-colors` falls back to the system's text and canvas. The tokens are defined on `:root` and redefined for dark under `prefers-color-scheme` (unless `data-theme="light"`) and under `data-theme="dark"`, like `10-base.css`.
- **The header is the box:** each group's `.pf-gl` (Experiments, Syntheses, Notes) holds its symbol, word and count. It becomes `.pf-box` with `data-sk` (its kind): `inline-flex`, `width: max-content`, radius 7 px, the tinted background, a 1 px inset edge of the colour at 32%, and the symbol and words in the colour.
  - **The Lit reviews heading** used to be a bigger `.pf-sn` title. It is now a `.pf-gl` keyed `lit` (`15-portfolio.js`), so all four headers are one thing.
  - `PFICON.dress` adds the box where it adds the symbol, so it is wherever PFLIST draws a group, whichever module drew it (Syntheses come from 15e).
- **The type switch:** each symbol on a button not chosen takes its kind's colour. The chosen button keeps its pill's own ink, which stays legible on the dark pill.
- **Settings → Features → Tabs → Section headers** (`pficons`, was "Section icons"):
  - the switch: off leaves the words alone, as before;
  - "Colour-coded boxes" On / Off — Off keeps the symbols;
  - a live preview of the switch and the four boxes, following both.
