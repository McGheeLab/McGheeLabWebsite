# V3.991 — Needs investigation

## Goal
Gabe: "A new type of reminder called Need to Investigate, which is saved as a reminder as a dot in the note that glows in an in/out phase in the portfolio. The Needs Investigation appears in the Today tab above recent notes, and joins where the resurfaced claims are."

## Approach
- **A fourth kind of the reminders the app has (`13s-remind.js` `REMKIND`), not a second system:** `investigate`, `undated: true`. The record is an ordinary `reminders` row (synced, in the notebook folder) with `at: null`, `docId`, `blockId`.
- **No date** keeps it out of everything that is about time: `REM.open()` (Coming up, 🔔's count, alerts, the day note, the mobile list), live views, and the hub's My Week (`whenCandidates` needs `remDay`). It waits until it is marked investigated (`done`).
- **A dot in the note:** on its component's header (wrapping `DocView.box`, as the Reminder structure's 🔔 does) or on the note's first box.
- **The Portfolio's dots (`PFKIT.dots`)** get one more, first and breathing; the halo animates opacity and scale only (V3.965: a box-shadow pulse repainted the page every frame) and pauses with the app's quiet mode.
- **Today:** the section sits right above Recent notes and the resurfaced claim's card moves into it ("joins where the resurfaced claims are").
- Not added to + Insert: its rows need an icon in the lab layer's map (the lab harness checks it), and the lab layer was not to be changed for this.
