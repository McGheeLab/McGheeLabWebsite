# V3.785–787 — Presenter sign-up page, teacher calendar, accept → email, visit reviews

Alex (2026-09-29):

> We also need a page that is public but not in the site navigation so people must have a
> link to view. This is for others to signup for highschool or community college engagement
> activities from the presenter side. The teachers can use this calendar on their end to sign
> up for days that would work for them. In the engagement section for teachers, they fill out
> a repeating pattern for availability, but a calendar with the ability to overview the days,
> and add days to the calendar would also be helpful. On the presenter signup end, they can
> see all of the availability for all the schools, and filter schools and times then sign up
> for a presentation. The teacher gets a response from a presenter and the teacher accepts and
> must send an email to the presenter to let them know when, where, and how many students to
> expect. After the presentation the presenters should fill out a review of the engagement.

## Decisions (Alex, asked before building)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who can use the presenter link? | **Anyone with the link** who signs in with Google. The link is the gate; each teacher vets by accepting or declining, and admin can block a presenter. |
| How does the teacher's email get sent? | **The teacher's own mail app** (a pre-filled `mailto:`). The project has no outbound mail server by design. |
| Who sees a presenter's review? | **The lab and that visit's teacher.** |
| High school vs community college? | **Add a school type** to the listing, and make it a filter. |

## Where things were before

- The presenter side already existed as `renderBrowse` in `engage-hub.js` at `#/engage`. The
  rules and the client both gated it on a lab member or the admin-granted `engage:browse` /
  `engage:request` caps, so outsiders could not get in without a manual grant.
- Teachers had only the weekly-pattern form and a list, with no calendar.
- Confirming a visit copied the address onto the request, and nothing else followed.
- Nothing asked for or stored a review of a visit. The `reviews` collection is the teachers'
  public class reviews, so the new one is `engageReviews`.

## What was built

### V3.785 — the presenter page (`#/presenters`, new `engage-presenters.js`)

- **Link-only.** The route is in `app.js` (added to `AUTH_GATED` and to `isClassEnv`, which
  hides the hero) and is linked from nowhere. Admin copies it from the Teachers page's
  Engagement tab (**Copy link**). `#/engage` sends every non-teacher to `#/presenters`
  (`location.replace`), so there is one presenter implementation.
- **Signed out:** a pitch, the four steps, and *Sign in with Google*.
  `mcghee_login_redirect = '#/presenters'` is set first, so the popup-blocked redirect
  fallback comes back here rather than to `#/research`.
- **About you** (`engagePresenters/{uid}`): name, affiliation, role, email for teachers,
  optional phone, and what they'd bring. It's required before the first request. Openings show
  behind it straight away, with "Add your details to request" on each.
- **Your visits:** review due → waiting on the teacher (Cancel) → coming up (the address the
  teacher shared, expected students, whether the teacher has emailed) → past (✓ reviewed,
  Edit your review).
- **Find a classroom:**
  - A static filter bar wired once, so only `#ep-results` re-renders and the search box keeps
    focus mid-word (the V3.770 lesson).
  - Filters: search, School select, date range (today → 8 weeks by default, capped at 120
    days), and chip rows for school type, activity, format, day and time of day.
  - The chip rows sit in a *Filters* `<details>`, open on desktop and folded on a phone (five
    rows of chips pushed every opening a screen down), showing "(N on)".
  - **List | Calendar** toggle: the list is grouped by date and capped at 80. The calendar is a
    month grid of per-day counts; tapping a day lists its openings.
- **Request** opens the inline form (activity, where, topic, note). The write denormalises
  `requesterTitle` / `requesterBio` from the profile, so the teacher sees who is asking. An
  opening the presenter already asked for reads *requested* / *booked* instead of Request.
- **School type:**
  - Step 1 of the teacher panel gains *Kind of school* (High school / Community college / Other
    → `engageProfiles.schoolType`).
  - It is part of `listingOf()` (fixed key order) and the step-1 summary, and shows as a chip in
    `listingHeadHTML`.
  - Not required: an approved listing without one must not be forced back into review.
- **`ML.EngageKit`** (exported by `engage-hub.js`) is what both sides render with:
  - labels (incl. `SCHOOL_TYPES`);
  - `isoLocal`, `fromISO`, `addDays`, `todayISO`, `fmtDay`;
  - **`expandRange(slots, from, to)`**: `expandSlots(slots, days)` is now a wrapper, and an
    occurrence in `slot.bookedDates` is no longer an opening;
  - `listingHeadHTML`, `ctxLine`;
  - **`monthGridHTML(opts)`**: a Sunday-first, pure-HTML grid, with clicks delegated on
    `[data-day]` / `[data-cal-nav]`;
  - `armConfirm`.
- **Local dates.** `todayISO()` used `toISOString()`, which is UTC. In Arizona it rolled over
  to tomorrow at 5pm, so "today", and every date a weekly slot expanded to, was a day ahead every
  evening. `isoLocal()` fixes it in `engage-hub.js`, and `teacher-teach.js`'s `todayISO()` got
  the same fix.
- **Admin (`rm/js/admin-teachers.js` `?tab=engage`):**
  - the presenter-link bar (Copy / Open);
  - a **Presenters** list (contact, request counts, what they'd bring) with **Block / Unblock**;
  - a **Visit reviews** list;
  - request rows show expected students and whether the teacher emailed;
  - admin confirm/cancel also keeps `bookedDates` in step.
- `rm/js/capability-registry.js`: the two engage caps are kept, so existing grants don't
  dangle, but relabelled "No longer needed"; `href` → `/#/presenters`.

### V3.786 — the teacher's calendar (step 6)

- A month calendar at the top of *When your class is free*, with prev/next up to 12 months
  ahead. Each day shows opening times (green), visits (blue), requests (amber) and skipped days
  (struck through).
- A day's own tone ranks visit > request > open > skipped, because on a phone the chips are
  hidden and only a coloured dot shows.
- **Tap days to multi-select.** The panel under the grid:
  - **From/To** and "Add an opening on these N days" create one dated slot per day, with the
    class details from the listing.
  - With **one** day selected it lists that day. A weekly occurrence offers **Skip this day**
    (a `blocked` dated slot); a skipped day offers **Un-skip**; a dated opening offers
    **Remove**; visits and requests link to step 7.
  - The day stays selected after Skip, so Un-skip is right there as the undo.
- `calMonth`, `calSel` and `calTimes` live outside the render, so a repaint or a host refresh
  keeps them. Enter in the calendar's time fields doesn't trigger the step's Next.

### V3.787 — accept → email, booked dates, reviews

- **Accept…** (was Confirm…) asks **How many students should they expect?**, pre-filled from the
  slot's class size, then the listing's.
  - Accepting writes `expectedStudents` and adds the date to the slot's **`bookedDates`**
    (`TC_DB.setSlotBooked`), so other presenters stop seeing a taken time. The rules also refuse
    a request on a booked date.
  - **Cancel this visit** on a coming-up row takes the date back out.
- **The email** (`visitEmail(r)`) is a `mailto:` to the presenter.
  - Subject: "Your visit to {school} on {date}".
  - Body: the date with year, time, where (the address + room; virtual and class-visits-UofA get
    their own wording), phone and arrival notes for in-person visits, expected students + class,
    the visit and topic, the teacher's note, and the review link.
  - A `mailto:` can't report whether it was sent. So opening it asks **"Sent it?"**, and only the
    teacher's **Yes, I sent it** writes `emailedAt`. The repaint after the click waits 400 ms so
    it never removes the link before the browser follows it.
- **"Must send"** is made visible:
  - the row is amber with *email not sent yet*;
  - step 7 is an action tagged **email owed**, and `autoStep` opens it;
  - the panel callout says so;
  - the hub's Engagement tab badge counts pending requests **plus** owed emails;
  - the Dashboard's *Needs your attention* list says "…still needs your email to the presenter".
- **Reviews** (`engageReviews/{requestId}`):
  - The presenter fills them in from *Your visits*: overall 1–5, student engagement 1–5, students
    reached, would you return, went well, next time, with "The teacher and the McGhee Lab will
    read this."
  - The teacher sees each one under that past visit in step 7 ("No review from the presenter
    yet" otherwise). Admin sees all.
  - They are read **only by query**, never by a get of a possibly-missing doc (V3.772).
- **`functions/engage-notify.js`:**
  - New **`onEngageReviewWrite`**: created → the teacher and admins; edits are silent.
  - Requester pushes now link to `/#/presenters`.
  - **Fixed along the way:** the trigger treated every `→ cancelled` as the presenter's own
    self-cancel and stayed silent. An accepted visit called off by the teacher would never have
    reached the presenter. Now `confirmed → cancelled` notifies the presenter and admins, while
    `pending → cancelled` stays silent.
- **Demo seam:**
  - `teacher-demo.js` gains a community-college listing (`Desert Valley Community College`,
    fictional) with an evening slot;
  - two presenter profiles, a reviewed past visit and an unreviewed one;
  - the matching `TC_DB` methods;
  - personas **Presenter — first visit**, **Presenter — visits booked, one review due** and
    **Teacher — accepted a visit, email not sent**.

## Rules (`firestore.rules`) — DEPLOYED 2026-09-29

- **`engageProfiles` read:** a signed-in user may read an `approved` listing (the clause comes
  first, before `isAdmin()`, so a presenter with no `users` doc still passes).
- **`engageSlots` read:** a signed-in user may read an `open` slot.
- **`engageRequests` create:**
  - no cap needed;
  - it may not carry `location` / `expectedStudents` / `emailedAt`;
  - the date must not be in the slot's `bookedDates`;
  - the teacher's listing must be `approved`;
  - the caller must not be blocked (`presenterBlocked()` checks `exists()` before `get()`).
- **`engageRequests` teacher update:** `expectedStudents` and `emailedAt` are added to the key
  set.
- **New `engagePresenters/{uid}`:** owner and admin read; the owner writes without touching
  `blocked*`; admin writes anything.
- **New `engageReviews/{reqId}`:**
  - read by the requester, the teacher, or admin;
  - created by the requester of a `confirmed` request, with the teacher matching the request;
  - `hasOnly` the field list, and ratings are int 1–5;
  - the requester may update with the same shape; admin deletes.

Before deploying, the local file was diffed against the live ruleset (fetched from the
firebaserules API; live since 2026-09-14). The only differences were these changes, so no other
session's rules went out with them.

## V3.791 addendum — opportunities replace dated slots (2026-09-29)

Alex, after the loop worked end to end: *"Teachers should make an opportunity, and that
opportunity has multiple dates associated with it, and once a presenter signs up for the
opportunity and the teacher accepts, that opportunity is taken off the list for signups. if a
presenter has signed up for an opportunity we need to flag it to show that this is pending
acceptance. This also changes how the list view works etc as opportunity centered instead of
date centered."*

**Decisions (asked first):** presenters tick every date that works and the teacher picks;
a pending sign-up flags the opportunity but others can still sign up; *several presenters
together* is a teacher preference on the opportunity (how many they'd welcome) with a matching
"happy to share the session" tick on the sign-up; the old test slots and request are deleted.

**The model.** `engageOpportunities/{id}` is the unit (see the CHANGELOG for fields).
`engageRequests` sign up for one, carrying the presenter's `dates[]`; the teacher sets
`date/startTime/endTime` on accept. A filled opportunity is `booked` and leaves the presenter
list; the other waiting sign-ups are declined with a note. `pendingCount` / `acceptedCount`
are written only by the Cloud Function, because a presenter can't read anyone else's request
but does need to see "pending acceptance".

**Why the teacher's client books (not the function):** the teacher is authorised for every
write involved (their own opportunity, their own requests) and needs the result in the same
refresh; the function's counts are the presenter-facing mirror and can lag a second.

**What stays:** the seven step cards, accept → the copyable email (now naming the opportunity
and co-presenters), reviews, the presenter page's calendar-first layout and filters.
