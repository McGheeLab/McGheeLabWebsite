# V3.899 — Scientia: share or collaborate on a lit review or a project

## Goal

Alex (2026-10-24): *"from the lit reviews tab, i want to be able to share, or collaborate with anyone in the lab. share
is a read only, collaborate means to be able to edit. this action should be accessable on the card at the top right.
Also do the same for projects"*

- **Share** = the person can read it (a read-only copy in their Scientia).
- **Collaborate** = the person can edit it; everyone's edits reach everyone.
- The control is on the card, top right: a lit review's card in the Lit reviews tab and a project's region in the
  Portfolio.

## The constraint that shapes it

Each person's Scientia is their own hub (`userData/{uid}/scientia/hub`, V3.857): their browser, their desktop and the
cloud converge on it through Gabe's sync protocol (ops with `prev` / `base`, three-way `merge3`, conflicts the person
decides). Nothing in his app can read a second hub. So sharing is a **relay between hubs, done by the hub function**:
the owner's hub holds the original; each member's hub holds a copy the relay keeps equal to it; a collaborator's
change goes into the owner's hub as one more device (so his merge rules apply), and the result goes back out to
everyone. His app, web and desktop, sees ordinary records arriving from a device called "Shared by <owner>".

## Data

- **The grant lives on the record**, like `share: 'lab'` (V3.880): `shareWith: { <uid>: 'read' | 'edit' }` on the
  review's `documents` row or the project's `projects` row. Only the owner's copy counts; it syncs to the desktop
  like any field.
- **Copies are marked**: `sharedFrom: { uid, name, role, share }` on copied `documents` / `projects` / `themes` rows
  (blocks, wires, items and media go with their note).
- **The index** `scientiaShares/{ownerUid}__{d|p}__{recId}` (written only by the function; no client rule):
  `{ ownerUid, ownerName, kind, recId, title, members {uid: role}, memberUids, names, docIds, blockIds, projectId,
  themeIds, mediaIds, passAt, lockUntil, dirty }`. It is the authority: a member's push is relayed only if the index
  names them, never because of anything in their own hub (their hub doc is theirs to write).
- **Shadows** `scientiaShares/{id}/shadow/{uid}__{store}__{id}` = `{ uid, store, id, recJson, ownerSeq }`: the value
  last given to that member (with its marker) and the owner's row seq at that time — the common ancestor for the next
  three-way merge (the V3.857 lesson: a relay without the ancestor reverts merges).

## What a share carries (its closure)

- review: the note, its components (`blockIds`), wires with both ends inside, structures (`items`) born in it, and
  the media its components use (any string `m_…` in a component that is a `media` row) with their files (the hub's
  `media` docs copied: the download-token URL works for anyone holding it).
- project: the project row, its themes, their aims, its terms, every note filed in it (not copies of someone else's)
  and all of the above for each note.
- Not carried: library rows outside the RM library (RM papers are mirrored into every hub with the same ids, so a
  review's citations resolve), registered equations, timer sessions (the owner's time).

## The relay (functions/scientia-share-core.js, I/O behind an `env` so the fixtures run it on MemoryHubs)

- **Incremental, on every push** (`relayPush(env, uid, commits)` after `api/sync/push` and `api/sync/resolve`; and
  after `api/media` for a file a share already wants):
  - shares the pusher OWNS → the committed records that belong (membership from the index: note ids, block ids,
    project id, theme ids, media ids) go out to every member: their row read, the desired copy pushed with
    `prev` = what was read and `base` = its seq (a change they made meanwhile merges, not lost), shadow updated.
  - shares the pusher is a COLLABORATOR on → each record goes into the owner's hub as device `share:<uid>` with
    `prev` = the shadow (marker stripped) and `base` = the shadow's `ownerSeq`; owner-only fields (`shareWith`,
    `share`, `projectId`, `themeId`, a review's `projectIds`) keep the owner's values; the owner's commits then go
    out to everyone (the collaborator's own copy only if the merge changed it).
  - shares the pusher may only READ → the record is put back to the shadow (a change only in `modified`-type
    timestamps is ignored, so opening a note never ping-pongs).
  - a root record whose `shareWith` differs from the index, or a new `shareWith` → a full pass.
- **Full pass** (`sharePass`): on a grant change, and every 10 min per share from `quietSyncs` (the safety net — a
  missed incremental, a `replace`, a new member's first copy). Lease on the index doc (`lockUntil`; busy → `dirty`,
  the running pass goes round again). Collaborators' differences from their shadows go in first; then the closure is
  recomputed and every member's copy made equal (ignoring `AUTO_MAX` timestamps); records that left the closure, and
  every copy of a member who lost access, are deleted from their hub.
- A member's own notes are never taken: a note they make inside a shared project goes to the owner only when their
  project row IS the shared copy (not when the project is also theirs from RM).

## Hub routes

- `GET api/rm/members` → lab members (`role` not guest, not hidden, category not collaborator/alumni), without the
  caller: `{ uid, name, category }`.
- `POST api/rm/sync?only=shares` → the passes due now (the panel calls it after a change).

## Web app (rm/scientia/lab-mode.js §15)

- A people button at the top right of each lit-review card and each project region header; a count when shared.
- Its panel (in the page, no browser popup): every lab member with **Share** / **Collaborate** (tap the chosen one
  again to take access away), a search box; for a copy: who shared it, what you can do, who else has it.
- Copies are labelled on their cards ("From Alex · read only" / "With Alex"); shared-in projects get their own
  Portfolio group, shared-in reviews their own section of the tab.
- A read-only copy opens in Gabe's read-only view (his time-travel mode with the current state: every widget already
  respects it); the dock says "Shared by … · read only". A collaborator's note head hides Project / Theme / Seen by
  (the owner's); the Quick cast is hidden on a read-only note.
- `scientiaNotes` (RM's index) skips copies — a shared note is listed once, under its owner.

## Known limits (said to Alex)

- Desktop Scientia shows copies and relays edits, but has no read-only view: an edit there to a read-only copy is put
  back on its next sync.
- A conflict between two collaborators editing the same field at once lands in the owner's conflict list (his
  usual "Online copy" choice).
