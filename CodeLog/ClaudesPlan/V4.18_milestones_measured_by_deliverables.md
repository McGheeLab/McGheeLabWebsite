# V4.18 — A theme's aims become milestones, measured by their deliverables

## Goal
Gabe (17 Nov 2026), from his list: "Change the aims in a theme to milestones, which are large overarching goals that are measured in completeness via deliverables: either a synthesis note or a specific set of results, takeaways, verdicts, etc…"

Until now a theme had **aims** (`aims` store): a statement and weighted "milestones", each ticked met with a piece of evidence (`15-portfolio.js` `aimEl` / `evidencePick` / `newAimDialog`, `M.createAim` / `reviseAim` / `meetMilestone`).

## Approach
- **The words:**
  - an aim is a **Milestone**, a large overarching goal of the theme;
  - its weighted milestones are its **Deliverables**;
  - its completeness is the share of them delivered — by weight when every one has one, equally otherwise.
- **Two kinds of deliverable:**
  - **A Synthesis note:** linked with Link… (the theme's Synthesis notes first, or ＋ a new one in the theme, named after the deliverable). It is delivered once its synthesis — the note's "what this adds up to" — is written. Settings can count the link alone.
  - **A set of components of chosen kinds:** results, takeaways, verdicts, insights, claims, decisions (component kinds and structures, `MILE.OF`), with how many it asks for. You pick them with Pick… (a checklist, this theme's first). It is delivered while that many are picked and still in their notes.
- **`metAt` is stamped when a deliverable is delivered and cleared when it no longer is**, on every draw of the theme (`IX.themeHealth` wrapped) and its card. Everything that read an aim's milestones keeps working unchanged: the weekly review's boost, the project page's bars, views of milestones, the export. New deliverables are written with equal weights that sum to 1.
- **Data:** the `aims` store and its record stay, for sync. A deliverable is a milestone row with `{ type: 'synthesis' | 'set', synth, kinds, need, refs }` beside its `{ label, weight, metAt, evidenceRef }`; `M.createAim` is wrapped to keep those fields. An old row (no type) is read as a set of one, its evidence its pick; one marked met without evidence stays met and says so. Nothing is migrated destructively.
- **`scientia/src/15g-milestones.js` (MILE, new)** replaces `aimEl` and `newAimDialog`:
  - the card shows the share, "n of m delivered", each deliverable with ✓, its link or picks, Link… / Pick…, and its weight;
  - the dialog takes the milestone and its deliverables (a Synthesis note, or a set: kinds and how many; an optional weight, where blanks share what is left);
  - Re-scope… makes a new version, as before, and what was delivered carries over by name.
- **The pages:**
  - the theme page says Milestones ("measured by their deliverables") and + Milestone;
  - the project page's theme cards keep their bars;
  - the Portfolio's pill says "n milestones". The lab layer's own pill (lab-mode §10 `themePill`) is reworded where it is drawn; lab-mode itself is not edited.
  - Wording in the weekly review (Deliverables met, the milestone boost, the lanes), views (Milestone deliverables), the project delete and the store's doc.
- **Settings → Time & review → Milestones** (`milestones`, in Simple too):
  - a switch: off, the milestones leave the theme and project pages and the Portfolio, and the data stays;
  - "A Synthesis note delivers its deliverable once": its synthesis is written / it is linked;
  - a live preview that follows it.
