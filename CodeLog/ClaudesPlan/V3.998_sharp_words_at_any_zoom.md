# V3.998 — Sharp words at any zoom

## Goal
Gabe (14 Nov 2026): "Zooming in and out of a note should not make the words in a component grainy."

## Cause
`.paper .world` had `will-change: transform` permanently (20-canvas.css). Chrome then rasterises the world once, as a picture, and the zoom (`transform: scale`) stretches that picture on the GPU: blurred zoomed in, gritty zoomed out.

## Approach
Keep the layer only while it moves: `Paper.apply` adds `.moving` (which carries `will-change`) and takes it off 160 ms after the last pan or zoom, so the browser draws the world again at its zoom when it rests. Panning and zooming stay as smooth as before. The translate is rounded to whole pixels. A fix, not a feature: nothing to switch in Settings.
