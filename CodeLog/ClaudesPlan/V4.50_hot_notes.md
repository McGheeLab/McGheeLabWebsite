# V4.50 — Hot notes

## Goal
Gabe (19 Nov 2026): "Then, on the right of Settings, add Hot Notes, which allows you to quicklly open notes that you set as favorites. It starts off by showing most visited (i.e., higher traffic), and the option to add a favorite via those suggested, or a + button. Simply deselecting the golden star when you hover on them removes them from the hot notes. The label of the note (experiment v. note v. lit review v. synthesis appears to its left with the color code. along with the banner of the project appearing lightly underneath it."

## Approach (`20e-hotnotes.js`, new; `38-hotnotes.css`, new)
- **The button:** a star, "Hot notes" and how many, at the right of the tab row after Settings (`HOT.mount`, after every `App.renderTabs`). It is not a tab: the tab pill stays on the page you are on.
- **The panel:** a click on the button opens it under the button. Esc, a click outside, the button again or opening a note closes it; nothing opens by itself.
- **Hot notes:** the notes you starred (`settings().hotNotes`, the notebook's settings, so they follow you to every device), the most visited first. Each row:
  - its kind at its left in its colour (`NOTEKIND`, V4.49: Experiment · Note · Lit review · Synthesis, the Portfolio's section colours);
  - its title, and its project › theme;
  - the banner lightly underneath (26 %, faded in from the left), taken from its theme (the image, else the one drawn from its name), else drawn from its project's name;
  - on hover its golden star shows, and a click on it takes the note out;
  - a click on the row opens the note.
- **Most visited:** the notes you open most on this device. Visits are counted as you go to them (`hashchange` and start; a split counts each of its notes), kept in `_ls 'hotVisits'`, 240 at most. Notes already starred are left out, and an empty star adds each. With no hot note the panel starts with them; with no visit yet, it shows the notes edited last ("Edited last").
- **+:** a search across every note's title, to star any of them.
- **Settings → Tabs & overview → Hot notes** (`hotnotes`, on in every preset):
  - the description;
  - "Most visited notes suggested" (3 / 5 / 8);
  - a live preview of the panel, with three made-up notes whose stars work there;
  - off: no button.
