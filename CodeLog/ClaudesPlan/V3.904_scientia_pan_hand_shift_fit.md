# V3.904 — Scientia: Shift-click with the move (pan) hand fits the component or region

## Goal

Alex (2026-10-24): "when im in pan mode a shift click will also zoom to the item or region". Since V3.895 the zoom hand's
Shift-tap fits the view to the component or region under the pointer (empty paper: the whole note), with its edge lit
while Shift is held. The move hand gets the same.

## The catch

The move hand is Gabe's ordinary canvas mode. There Shift-click already means things, and those keep their meaning:

- in the text you are typing in, Shift-click extends the selection;
- in text that holds the selection (a PDF's words, chosen for a quote), it extends that selection;
- on a plate's wells (`.wplate`, 13e), it selects a range;
- on an equation field, buttons, inputs and the pills, it does what it did before.

Everywhere else it fits: a component's frame, header, picture or PDF page, a component you are not typing in, a region,
or empty paper. A Shift-drag still pans or moves, because the press is not stopped.

## Approach (`rm/scientia/lab-mode.js` §11)

- `fitHand()` = the zoom hand or the move hand. `MOVE_SKIP` = the zoom hand's skip list plus `.wplate`, `math-field`,
  `.clip-layer`.
- `textBusy(t)` checks two things: whether the focused editable contains `t`, and whether the non-collapsed selection's
  `.textLayer` / `[contenteditable="true"]` contains it. Both are decided on **pointerdown**: by click time the browser
  has already focused the box it pressed.
- **pointerdown** with the move hand + Shift (no Alt, primary button, not a pen):
  - Ignored when `moveBlocked(target)`.
  - Otherwise `preventDefault()`, so no caret or selection starts.
  - It is not stopped, so the canvas still gets the press.
  - On pointerup within 4 px: `zfit(host, x, y)`, and the following click is swallowed (`ZF.swallow`).
- **The lit edge while Shift is held**: with the move hand too, but not while typing (a capital letter is a Shift) and
  not over a blocked target.
- The hand pill's Move tooltip says so. `LAB.version` V3.904.
