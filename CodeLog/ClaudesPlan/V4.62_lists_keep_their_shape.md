# V4.62 — Scientia: lists keep their shape when you add an item to an earlier part of them

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "Sometimes when in bullet point list, numbered list, etc..., going back into previous bullet points and adding a new one causes it to go into the previous one instead of maintaining the list architecture."

## What was wrong
Reproduced in headless Chrome with real clicks and keys. There were two faults.
1. **The new item's words ran into the next item** (`09-editor.js`, `ED.render`). An empty list item followed by a line break was drawn as a text node holding only "\n". Chrome replaces such a node with the first letter typed into it, so the line break went. "• one / • (new) / • two" was saved as "• one / • NEW• two". This happened whichever way the new item was made.
2. **A click left of an item put the caret before its marker** (`13u-write.js`, `WRITE.enter`). Enter there put the new marker inside the item ("• • two") under an empty line.

## What changed
- `ED.render`: an empty item with a line break after it is drawn as a zero-width space followed by "\n", as the last empty item already was (a zero-width space). The caret goes after it (`domPos` already skips zero-width spaces), typing keeps the line break, and `ED.read` drops the zero-width space.
- `WRITE.enter`: a caret in the indent or on the marker counts as at the start of the item's words. Enter at the start of an item's words adds a new empty item above it and puts the caret in it, so what you type is the new item. Bullets, numbers (renumbered), nested items and checkboxes all work this way.
- Unchanged: Enter at the end of an item adds one below; Enter in the middle splits it; Enter on an empty item leaves the list, or steps out one level; a click on a checkbox ticks it.
