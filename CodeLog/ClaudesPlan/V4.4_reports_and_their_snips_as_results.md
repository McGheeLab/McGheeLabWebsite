# V4.4 — Reports, and what is snipped out of them as Results

## Goal
Gabe (16 Nov 2026): "HTMLs and PDFs can be labelled as a report as a new component kind just for those. Any snipped figure out of it is instantly labelled as a result."

## Approach
- Component kinds (`CKINDS`, 13zzi) were for text components only: the label left of the title and its picker were drawn only on `kind === 'component'`. A kind may now say which components it is for (`on`). The new `13zzy-report.js` adds **Report** (`on: ['pdf', 'html']`, links: produces a Result, supports a Hypothesis or a claim) and gives **Result** `on: ['component', 'figure']` (and `report` among what produces it).
- `CKINDS.pick` offers only the kinds that fit the component (the list is filtered for the call); a wrap of `DocView.box` draws the kind label, colour and double-click picker on PDFs, web pages and image panels (`.kinded-w`, 24-kinds.css).
- Both ways of snipping a figure — `CLIP.take` (PDF) and `HSNIP` (web page) — end in `CLIP.fresh(b)`; wrapping it marks a new image panel whose `clip.src` is a Report as a Result (if it has no kind yet and Settings says so). Equations are not figures and stay as they are.
- `CKINDS.counted` also counts image panels that are Results as evidence (Portfolio, Synthesis — which pulls blocks in with `M.pullInto`, any kind).
- Settings → Features → Reports: switch + *A figure snipped from a report* (Becomes a Result / Stays plain) + live preview. Off: no Report offered, PDFs and pages show no kind, snips stay plain; labels already made stay.
