## Goal

Turn the 13 characterization experiments of `Research/PE_bead_confocal_experiments.md` into things Alex can run at the AX R:
one RM Protocols Builder import per experiment, each with purpose, model prediction, materials, settings, live calculators,
procedure, analysis, decision and record. Fold in revision 2 of the deep-research report, which finished during this turn,
and merge that revision into `Research/PE_bead_confocal_assay_model.md`.

## Approach

- `Research/bead_confocal_protocols/make_protocols.py` generates the 13 `pe-ax-NN-*.protocol-import.json` files
  (`protocol-import-v1`, per `.claude/skills/protocol-import/SKILL.md`). Calculators only where a number is a real parameter:
  irradiance and saturation (01), photon transfer (02), field and pixel size (03, 06, 13), pinhole/section/stack (04, 05), stack and
  series bleaching (05, 07), gain headroom (08), PE-antibody dilutions with a builder + pipetting table and the excluded-volume term
  (09), PE:IgG by absorbance, intensity → molecules, beads per well (10), post-wash loss, clamp labelled fraction, Stokes and
  plate-spin settling (11), the half-log dilution series with a 72-well builder and a 96-well plate map (12), throughput (13).
- `validate.cjs` checks them the way RM reads them: `rm/js/protocol-import.js` validate() in a vm sandbox; the symbol table built
  as `rm/js/protocol-formulas.js` does (inputs, then outputs in document order, rounded to `decimals` before later outputs read
  them — so every output is in a convenient unit and none rounds to 0); builders expanded as **Add all**; table columns and plate
  cells evaluated with RM's numeric-string coercion; every `[!var:]` token resolved; no `~`/`^` in prose (inline sub/superscript)
  and no colour emoji. mathjs 12 (the version RM loads) from a scratch install.
- Revision 2 changes carried into the protocols and the plan: power limits 2 µW (20x) / 6–7 µW (10x) for 5 % image
  sublinearity; transmitted-light channel with every PE frame (segmentation); bead material first and the integrated-signal
  stationary point as the routine focal plane (bead refraction); 4c via a chamber of known height (a refracting bead shifts its
  own top peak); 4d glass-to-solution edge for w(z) and T; Exp 5 modes 20x 1/2 AU + 10x 1 AU/largest with a core control and a
  symmetry test; 7g PE density, 7h 488 nm; 9c nonspecific binding at 0.5/1/2/5 × K_D2; 10 solution-reference and flow routes;
  11 plate-spin settling, logged temperature; 12 D₀ at 1/2/5 × K_D2, column-staggered washes, 1×/10× hook guard; 13 flow
  cytometry reference and concentration CV per well-minute.

## Key decisions

- One protocol per experiment (13), not one long protocol: each is run on a different day with different materials, and the
  lab-notebook protocol chip works per protocol.
- The report merge takes revision 2 whole (it is the critic-revised synthesis) and keeps the simulator results as Appendix S with
  an operating-point warning; Part D of revision 2 supersedes the first-pass recommendations (20x at 1 AU is the default; 10x
  at 3–4 AU is the throughput candidate decided in Exp 13).
- Checked revision 2's ray-trace claim against its own script: "lower again with excitation" holds at 20x but flips at 10x
  (thick section collects the ball-lens-concentrated upper cap) — added as an editor's note.
- The workflow's calculation scripts moved from the session scratchpad into `Research/bead_confocal_sim/report_calcs/` with
  relative imports, so the report's references resolve.
