# V3.864 — Scientia is a notebook of the Lab Notebook (no separate page)

Alex (2026-10-23, after V3.857): *"this needs to be embedded in the lab notebook, not a separate page it should be
unified so that lab notebook is gabes desktop app working in tandem"*

## Reading (assumption, stated)

V3.857 put Gabe's app (synced with his desktop through the cloud hub) on its own page, `rm/pages/scientia.html`.
Alex wants it **inside** the Lab Notebook, as one notebook: open the Lab Notebook and you are in Gabe's app, the same
notebook his desktop app holds, working in tandem. So:

- Scientia becomes a **notebook of the Lab Notebook's own tab row** (beside "My notebook" and any other canvas
  notebooks), its notes are the tabs, and a tab opens the note in the notebook's own sheet area.
- The sync controls (status, connect a computer, upload, download) move into the **notebook header** and a side panel.
- The separate page is retired (it redirects into the notebook).
- For a person who uses Scientia, the Lab Notebook **opens in Scientia** (their last choice; first time: yes when the
  hub has records and a computer is connected).

Not done (stated): the canvas notebook is not converted into Scientia records or vice versa. The two keep their own
data — the canvas feeds PMR / My Week / figure boards, Scientia syncs with the desktop — and they live in one notebook
UI. A translation bridge (Scientia notes ↔ canvas entries) remains the separate, lossy project from V3.832 §4.9.

## Approach

- **`rm/js/notebook-scientia.js`** (new, `window.NOTEBOOK_SCIENTIA`): live listeners on the hub doc, its
  `rows` (store == 'documents') and `keys`; `tabsHtml()` = the "Scientia" group (Today, the 20 most recently changed
  notes, All N, ＋ new note); `show(hash)` parks the canvas entry (saved + unmounted through the host's `park`) and shows
  Gabe's build in a same-origin iframe in the stage (his globals collide with ours, so it cannot share the document);
  `hide()`; `renderHeader()` = Scientia's header (sync dot, "N records · changed … · Office PC synced …", Desktop sync,
  ⋯ = connect / upload / download / open on its own); the sync panel (keys + the scientia-cloud.py steps, upload with an
  inline confirm, download). `shouldOpen()` decides the first view.
- **`rm/js/notebook-canvas.js`**: six small hooks — init in boot, a `#nbc-sis` host in the stage, the boot choice
  (`?notebook=scientia&note=<id>` or `shouldOpen()`, never when a link names a canvas entry / board / item / action),
  `parkCanvas()`, `hide()` at the top of `openEntry`, the header branch, the tab-row group + its clicks.
- **`rm/scientia/cloud-sync.js`**: a postMessage bridge with the notebook — `{sisCmd:'go', hash}` opens a note,
  `{sisCmd:'new'}` calls `App.newDoc()`, and every hash change posts `{sis:true, type:'route', hash}` so the notebook
  lights the tab and updates the URL. Same-origin + `e.source === parent` checks.
- **`rm/css/notebook-scientia.css`** (new): `.nbc-root.nbc-sis-on` swaps the stage between the canvas and Scientia;
  dot / panel styles; on a phone the panel covers the area (lifts the canvas sheet's 55% cap).
- **`rm/pages/scientia.html`** → redirect to `/rm/pages/notebook.html?notebook=scientia`.
