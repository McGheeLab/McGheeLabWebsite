# V4.100 — Scientia: the term card keeps its place and size while you point at dots and notes

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "Current term popup should not move too much/stagger when hovering over timeline points or notes displaying context around the term appearance."

The term card (V4.66, `16c-termlook.js`, `TLOOK.card`) is the Ledger with a vertical timeline down its left. Pointing at a dot or at a "nearest notes" row swaps the sentence at the top of the Ledger; the sentence area took the height of the sentence (1, 2 or 3 lines), so the whole card grew and shrank by 18 or 36 px at each swap — and since the card opens above the term when there is no room below (08-ui `hoverCard` places it once, from its height at that moment), growing moved its bottom edge over the term and the timeline. It also grew in by a scale-and-slide animation, so `hoverCard` measured it at 97% and the card settled about 13 px after it showed.

## What changed
- **One fixed sentence area** (`40-termlook.css`): `.tcd-q` is 92 px high whatever the sentence (3 lines), the sentence has its own scroll for more; "Used only here so far." sits in the same box. The card's height no longer depends on the sentence.
- **Placed once, measured true**: the card now only fades in (no scale or slide), so the one measurement `hoverCard` makes is the card's real size and it never settles afterwards.
- **A short cross-fade, nothing moves** (`16c-termlook.js`): the sentence fades in over 0.16 s when it changes (opacity only; not under reduced motion); pointing at the dot that is already shown redraws nothing.
- **Settings → Terms**: the description says the card is steady. No new switch: it is the card's look, not a choice.

## Measured (real pointer, `termcard_t.mjs`)
8 timeline dots and 3 nearest-notes rows, short and 300-letter sentences and a very long title, the card above the term and below it, looks A, B and C: the card's rectangle (and the Ledger inside it) changes by 0 px. Before: +18 / +36 px in height at 4 of 8 dots.
