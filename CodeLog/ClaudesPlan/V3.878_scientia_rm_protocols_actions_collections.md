## What this version does (web build; `scientia/src` untouched)

- **Run a lab protocol from RM** (`+ Insert → Protocols → Run a lab protocol from RM…`): the lab's protocols from RM
  (newest first; *Imported before* / *The lab's protocols*). Picking one turns RM's protocol into **Gabe's own protocol**
  — its steps (each RM section's name on its first step; notes, safety and callouts as *Note: …* steps; a calculator
  value `[!var:vol]` shown as ‹vol›) — so the run here is his run: check off, time, observe, register. In a note with a
  project it is registered (its own protocol note; a run takes its place here); in a note without one it stays in the
  note, runnable where it is. The same RM version picked again makes **a run of the same protocol**, not a copy; a
  version RM has changed since is imported beside the old, so past runs keep their steps. The protocol and every run of
  it say *From RM · <title> · in RM only: 1 formula, 1 table* with **Open in RM**.
- **Something I did** (`+ Insert → Lab → Something I did…`): your recorded actions from RM (items used with a scan or
  the dial, equipment booked — V3.844), listed and described by RM's own code in the notebook; the one you pick is
  placed as a card (what, when, the note; *The item in RM / Equipment in RM / My actions in RM*) and marked noted in RM
  (`notedIn: {entryId: 'scientia:<note>', cardId}`) so it leaves your to-place list.
- **Collection from the Lab Notebook** (`+ Insert → Reading`): one of your 📑 collections or claims from a canvas
  entry, read-only — title, claim, key points, quotes in their highlight colours, papers; a quote or paper opens it in
  RM's reader; *Open its entry*.

## Evaluated, not ported (the plan's last two Phase 2 rows)

- **Pen arcs and phone modes (RM V3.860 / V3.863) → keep Gabe's.** His ink (`13zf-ink`, `13zm-pen2`, `13zq-pressure`,
  `13zu-lasso2`) is a fuller pen system than the ✎ arcs: pressure curves per device, the pen-near cursor, two- and
  three-finger undo / redo, lasso → *Connect…* / *Merge* of ink components, strokes that gather into components that
  wire and join regions. His phone layout (`13ze-mobile`) is a different design — five screens (Today, Search,
  Capture, Timers, Reminders) and notes as one column — not modes on a canvas. Porting the arcs would add a second
  input model to his paper. If anything moves, it is the other way: his pressure feel and lasso-merge are candidates for
  RM's whiteboard engine (a separate decision for Alex).
- **Regions, wires, instances (RM V3.841) → mapped, not shared.** Both apps have named, coloured regions whose members
  move with them (`13v-region`; RM's explicit-member regions) and typed, coloured connections (`12b-wires`; RM's
  wires with the reader's relation vocabulary). They are the same ideas on two engines with their own data; a note and
  a canvas entry do not share a board, so there is nothing to synchronise. RM's *instances across boards* has no
  Scientia counterpart and is not needed: Gabe's *Embed in another note…* (a component appearing elsewhere with its
  origin kept) covers the same need inside his record.
- **Figure cards → mapped** (V3.876): his image panel already stores through the hub's media.
