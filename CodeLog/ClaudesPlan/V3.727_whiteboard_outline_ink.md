# V3.727 — Whiteboard ink: one filled outline per stroke (the real "low resolution" fix)

**Date:** 2026-09-03
**Trigger:** Alex: *"the resolution of what I can draw on the whiteboard is still
way below what my tablet is capable of, the drawings still come out fairly
pixelated. … we tried already, but maybe something got messed up."* Answers
to the follow-up: pixelated at **100 %** (no zoom), on the **iPad while
drawing AND on the laptop/projector**, Safari on the iPad itself.

## Diagnosis (what was measured, not guessed)

1. **Canvas resolution was never the problem.** A harness mounting the real
   `whiteboard.js` against a stub Firestore at `deviceScaleFactor` 2 and 1,
   in a 520-px panel and a 1300-px page, shows the bitmap is exactly
   `dpr ×` the CSS size in every mount, and a smooth synthetic stroke renders
   clean. The production copy (V3.683) has the same sizing code.
2. **Production is a half-deploy** (verified with `Last-Modified`): the
   root `whiteboard.js`, `styles.css`, `index.html` are the **Aug 28 21:42**
   V3.683 build, `whiteboard-board.css` is a **404**, while
   `class-builder.js`, `app.js`, `sw.js` (696) and the module HTML/panel are
   from **Sep 2**. On the live site the in-module panel therefore cannot
   mount (`mountBoardView` missing) and the class page runs V3.683 with no
   lens. Both devices converge on the server copy within two loads
   (stale-while-revalidate, no `Cache-Control` from GoDaddy), so what Alex
   sees *is* V3.683 output.
3. **The renderer, not the raster, is what looks pixelated.** Isolated
   canvas experiment, same 11-device-px sine, alpha histogram read through
   a scratch canvas:

   | method | edge pixels with partial alpha |
   |---|---|
   | one path, one `stroke()` | 12.2 % |
   | per-segment `stroke()` with round caps, once | 11.3 % |
   | per-segment, tail drawn 4× (`paintActiveSegment` on every sample) | **4.9 %** |

   Overlapping antialiased strokes compound their edge coverage
   (`1-(1-α)^k`), so the live stroke's edges go near-binary while the pen is
   down — that is the "pixelated while drawing".
4. **Per-segment pressure width steps** — `paintStrokeRange` sets
   `ctx.lineWidth` per Bézier segment from that segment's mean pressure.
   After RDP thins committed strokes to ~6–10 px between points, the width
   jumps at every join: a staircase outline that reads as low resolution on
   every viewer (laptop/projector included). The V3.683 spline smoothed the
   *centreline* and could not touch either mechanism.

## Approach

Render each stroke as **one filled, variable-width outline** — the standard
pressure-ink technique (perfect-freehand et al.):

- `flattenStroke(pts, W, base)` — walk the existing Catmull-Rom segments
  (same `crControls` + phantom-endpoint tangents as V3.683), subdivide each
  cubic into pieces ≤ ~2.5 device px, and carry a per-sample half-width from
  linearly interpolated pressure. Then smooth the half-widths with an
  arc-length box filter (±6 device px) so sample-to-sample Pencil pressure
  noise cannot ripple the edge, while genuine pressure changes survive.
- `buildOutlinePath(ctx, …)` — offset the centreline by ± half-width along
  the normal, add round **joins** (a fan of arc points on the outer side
  wherever the turn exceeds ~20°, so cusps and pen reversals in handwriting
  do not leave notches) and round **caps**, then a single `ctx.fill()`.
  Self-intersections at tight cusps are absorbed by the nonzero winding rule.
- **Live stroke on its own layer.** A third canvas `.wb-canvas-live`
  (`pointer-events:none`, above ink) is cleared and refilled with the *whole*
  active stroke on a rAF each time samples arrive. No overdraw, so the live
  edge keeps full antialiasing; on pen-up the layer is cleared and the stroke
  joins the committed repaint. Same lens transform as ink.
- `paintStroke` keeps its signature, so `repaint()`, the lens, and
  `exportBoardPNGs()` (homework export) all pick the new renderer up for
  free — exported pages cannot differ from the screen.

## Files

- `whiteboard.js` — shell gains `.wb-canvas-live`; `EL_SELECTORS.live`;

## Verification

- Harness (scratchpad `wb-harness/`): mount real engine at DPR 2, drive
  realistic Pencil input (0.6 CSS px spacing, ±0.1 pressure noise, a cusp),
  read pixels through a scratch canvas (never the live canvas —
  V3.684 lesson): live-stroke AA share back to the single-path baseline;
  committed strokes show no width steps at RDP joins; cusps have no notches.
- Lens on/off byte-parity is not expected to hold against V3.684 hashes
  (the renderer changed on purpose); lens-off must still equal unzoomed
  lens-on.
