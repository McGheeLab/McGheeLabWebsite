# V4.85 — Scientia: the Compendiums — a tab after the Atlas, a gallery of Materials, Data and Protocols; the Atlas keeps its Map

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "Remove all tabs from Atlas apart from its original function, and move them to a new tab called Compendiums, which acts as the gallery for all of these different things. Add registered protocols to Compendiums."

## What changed
- **`HUBPAGE(o)`** (`14fb-atlashub.js`): the Atlas's tab of sections with its pill row (V4.48), made into a factory. Two tabs are made with it:
  - **`ATHUB`**, the Atlas, which keeps only its original function, the Map;
  - **`COMP`**, the Compendiums.
- **The Compendiums tab**, after the Atlas (`#/compendiums`, `#/compendiums/<key>`):
  - **All**, the gallery (`CGAL`, `14fd-compendiums.js`): a card per compendium, with its icon, how many it holds, what it is for, and a few of its entries. A card is a link to its compendium.
  - **Materials** (13j's page, still dressed by the lab layer on the website) and **Data** (14fc), moved from the Atlas.
  - **Protocols** (`CPRO`), new: every registered protocol, last run first.
    - Each shows its steps (the first three, then "+ n more"), its runs, when it was last run and where it lives, with a search over names and steps.
    - Open goes to it in its note, centred (V4.74). Each run's chip goes to that run.
  - Topic syntheses join in V4.86.
- **The header** names the section: Compendiums on All, Protocols on its compendium; Materials and Data set theirs, as before.
- **Old links still work:** `#/materials`, `#/atlas/materials` and `#/atlas/data` lead to the compendiums (`App.parse` wrapped). A material's page lights the Compendiums tab (`TABOF.materials` / `mat`, read live).
- **Settings → Tabs & overview → Compendiums** (`compendiums`, on in every preset):
  - the switch; off, the compendiums are sections of the Atlas again, there is no Compendiums tab, and their links lead to the Atlas;
  - *Registered protocols*: that compendium on or off;
  - *Cards show a few entries*;
  - a live preview of the gallery as it is set.
  - The Materials and Data features now say "a compendium".
