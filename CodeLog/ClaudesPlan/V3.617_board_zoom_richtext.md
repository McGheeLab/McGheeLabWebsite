# V3.617 — Figure board: floating zoom cluster + rich-text lists in cards

## Goal

Two pieces of Alex's board feedback:
1. The zoom buttons should sit together as one group hovering over the
   canvas top-right, not scattered on the top bar.
2. Card text boxes (figures, experiments, notes) need lists — via a set of
   rich-text controls that pop up at the bottom of the box while it's
   actively being edited.

## Approach

### Zoom cluster
`− 100% ＋ ⤢` moved out of the toolbar into one `.pfb-zoomctl` pill
absolutely positioned top-right **on the canvas** (they act on the canvas,
so they live on it). Pointer/wheel events stop-propagate so the cluster
never pans/zooms the world underneath. z-index 40 — below the per-card
comments panel (50), which may open over it.

### Rich-text card bodies
- `.pfb-body` textarea → `contentEditable` div wrapped in `.pfb-bodywrap`
  with a `.pfb-richbar` underneath: **B**, *I*, `• List`, `1. List`
  (`document.execCommand`; mousedown-preventDefault keeps focus + selection
  in the editable). The bar appears only while editing via
  `.pfb-bodywrap:focus-within` — exactly "pops up at the bottom of the box
  as we are actively editing it".
- Storage: new `body_html` field (sanitized), with `body` kept as the
  plain-text mirror (search + legacy). Local `_sanitizeRich()` allowlist —
  DIV/P/BR/UL/OL/LI/B/STRONG/I/EM/U, zero attributes — runs on save AND on
  render (paste-safe both directions). Old cards without `body_html` render
  their plain `body` escaped with line breaks.
- The origin-discrimination guard (remote echo vs focused card) now also
  preserves the local `body_html`.
- Placeholder via `:empty::before`; empty saves normalize stray `<br>` to ''.

## Files modified

- `rm/js/paper-figure-board.js` — zoom cluster, richbar, sanitizer,
  `body_html` save/render/echo-guard
- `rm/css/paper-figure-board.css` — `.pfb-zoomctl`, `.pfb-richbar`,
  `.pfb-body` (div form, list styling, placeholder)
- `sw.js` — CACHE_VERSION 588 → 589

Data model: additive `body_html` on `papers/{id}/boardItems` — existing
rules cover it. No indexes/functions. FTP-only.

## Rollback

Revert + re-FTP. Cards saved with `body_html` still carry the plain `body`
mirror, so a rolled-back client renders their text (without formatting).

## Verification

1. Board: zoom cluster floats top-right over the canvas as one group; −/＋/⤢
   work; clicking/scrolling on the cluster doesn't pan or zoom the world.
2. Click into any card body → formatting bar appears at the bottom of the
   box; make a bulleted and a numbered list; bold/italic; click away → bar
   hides, content persists after reload and live-syncs to a second browser.
3. Old cards (pre-V3.617 plain text) render unchanged and upgrade cleanly
   on first edit.
4. Pasting styled HTML into a body keeps only structure/emphasis.
