# V3.722 — Who is on a grant, and the Team Focus → My Week loop recorded under it

## Goal

Alex: *"there is a section for 'work under this grant' and this currently lists
all the people in the lab. Do not put any students on this list that do not have
associations for this grant. I want to be able to edit the primary people
associated with this project, and add some people as secondary who should have a
tangential role in pushing this project. The semester focus for each person will
be assigned through the team focus page, and students will add their own sections
via the my week to add to the semester focus. All of this should be recorded
here."*

## The bug

`roster()` did this for a privileged viewer:

```js
var all = await FOCUS_AREAS.listMentees();
all.forEach(m => add(m.uid));
```

`listMentees()` returns **every non-guest user** when the caller is an admin. So
the section printed all nine lab members, seven of them saying "No focus aligned
to this grant yet" — burying the two people actually on it.

The intent behind it was real: someone can align a focus to a grant without ever
being added to a card, and that work should surface. The error was conflating
*scanning* with *listing*. Scanning the mentee roster is still how such a person
is found; it is no longer why they get a row. **A scanned person with no work
under this grant is never rendered.**

## Primary / secondary

`member_roles[uid] === 'secondary'` marks a tangential role; anyone else in
`member_uids` is primary. Only the exact string `'secondary'` demotes, so the
pre-existing free-text roles ('lead', 'contributor', …) keep meaning what they
meant — a migration that silently reclassified people would be worse than the
inconsistency.

`GRANT_REVIEW.openPeopleEditor` is one modal: the whole lab, three radios per
person (Not on it / Primary / Secondary), writing `member_uids` + `member_roles`
on the grant card.

Three groups render:

| Group | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Primary** | assigned, not demoted — *always listed*, work or not |
| **Secondary** | assigned and demoted — *always listed* |
| **Also contributing** | not assigned, but has work here |

Assigned people are listed even with nothing yet, because that is what assigning
them means; the empty state points at Team Focus rather than pretending they are
absent.

## Recording the loop

Each person expands to their **semester foci** (assigned on Team Focus) with the
**weekly foci** they added in My Week **nested under the semester focus each one
serves**. That nesting is the point: showing the weeks flat would lose which
commitment they were against, which is the only thing that makes a week
meaningful at grant scale. Each week line carries its outcome icon, week id,
title, the aim/output card it hit, task progress and the report summary.

Weeks aligned to the grant but under no semester focus collect in a "Not under a
semester focus" bucket — visible, not dropped.

## One section, one editor

The grant review's sidebar "Aligned people" block is gone. The roster now lives
in "Work under this grant" with roles and its own editor. Two lists with two
editors over the same `member_uids` is exactly how surfaces drift — the same
reasoning that collapsed `grant_meta` onto one renderer in V3.717.

## Verified

Against the real 9-member lab with a seeded scenario (4 assigned, 2 of them with
work, 1 unassigned contributor, 4 unassigned idle):

- **5 rows render, not 9** — the four unassociated never appear.
- Groups: Primary 3 · Secondary 1 · Also contributing 1.
- Nesting: "Finish Aim 1 this semester (Confirmed, 2 weeks)" over its two weeks,
  each `✅ 2026-W30 · Ran the stiffness sweep · 🎯 Aim 1 · 2/3 · 3 kPa done`, with
  the third week correctly in "Not under a semester focus".
- Idle assigned member: "No focus assigned under this grant yet — assign one on
  Team Focus."
- Editor: 9 rows, 3 options each, current members pre-checked.

Zero console errors. 40 + 17 node assertions green.
