# V3.165 — Training: Epi-Fluorescence Microscopy (Ti-e + Ti2-U)

> Status: **BUILT (2026-05-11).** See [rm/pages/training-epifluorescence.html](../../rm/pages/training-epifluorescence.html)
> and [rm/js/training-epifluorescence.js](../../rm/js/training-epifluorescence.js).
> CHANGELOG entry: V3.165.

## Goal

Make a new lab member operationally fluent on the lab's two Nikon
epi-fluorescence platforms: the older **Ti-e** and the newer **Ti2-U**.
The module covers the optical light path of an epi-fluorescence scope
and the specific quirks of each frame so a student can walk up, image,
and walk away without damaging equipment or wasting samples.

## Format

Full interactive notebook (MODULE_TEMPLATE.md pattern). 5 tabs.

## Tabs

### Tab 1 — The Epi-Fluorescence Light Path
**Bio context:** a DAPI/Phalloidin/GFP three-color spheroid on the
Ti2-U — what each piece of glass does on the way from source to camera.

- Background: source → excitation filter → dichroic → objective →
  sample → objective → dichroic → emission filter → tube lens → camera
  (or eyepiece)
- Interactive: animated SVG of the light path; click any component to
  expand its function + see the actual spec on the lab's scopes
- Concepts: Stokes shift, why dichroics matter, the "leakage" failure
  mode where a high-power excitation bleeds through a poorly-matched
  emission filter

### Tab 2 — Filter Cubes
- Background: how an ex / dichroic / em triple is matched to a
  fluorophore; bandwidth vs. transmission trade-offs
- Interactive: pick a fluorophore from a dropdown (DAPI, GFP, mCherry,
  Cy5, AF647) → page shows the matching cube + transmission curves;
  swap to a mismatched cube and see the predicted dim/dirty image
- Reference: per-frame cube turret inventory for Ti-e and Ti2-U

### Tab 3 — Sources: LED vs. Arc Lamps
- Background: LED engines (Lumencor, X-Cite XLED) vs. mercury / metal-
  halide arc lamps. Stability, spectral coverage, lifetime, warmup,
  bleaching
- Interactive: slider on excitation power → simulated bleaching curve
  on a sample fluorophore; the student finds the sweet spot for SNR
  without bleaching out the experiment
- Frame-specific: which lamps live on Ti-e vs. Ti2-U today, expected
  replacement cycle

### Tab 4 — Frame-Specific Operation
Two sub-panels side-by-side:

**Ti-e (legacy)** — manual condenser, Perfect Focus System (PFS),
typical filter wheel layout, known quirks (the focus drift on long
time-lapses without PFS), how to lock the stage.

**Ti2-U (current)** — motorized turret, NIS-Elements integration,
joystick stage, Multi-Point time-lapse module, where to find the
LUTs/look-up-table presets.

Each panel has a 6-step "first-time user" checklist.

### Tab 5 — Capstone: time-lapse plan worksheet
Given a hypothetical experiment ("8-hour live-cell GFP-actin time-lapse
on the Ti2-U"), the student fills in: objective + NA, exposure, binning,
interval, total light budget, PFS yes/no. Page critiques the plan and
flags photobleaching / phototoxicity risk.
