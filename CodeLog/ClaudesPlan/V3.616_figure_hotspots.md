# V3.616 — Click-to-snip figure hotspots in the reader

## Goal

Alex's feedback on V3.610: "the snip mode in the reader is not easy to
understand… We should just be able to identify figures in the pdf easily,
then if I click on the figure it will give me the option to snip." The
reader now detects figures on every rendered page and paints a click target
over each — hover shows a dashed outline + "🖼 Snip figure", one click opens
the capture dialog pre-scoped to that region. No mode, no toggle knowledge.

## Approach

### Detection (`rm/js/pdf-viewer.js` — `_detectFigureRegions`)
Figures = **connected areas of ink that no text item covers**. All text
lives in the text layer as items, so masking them from a downscaled ink
grid leaves only graphical content — this catches raster images AND vector
plots (an operator-list scan would only see raster XObjects).

Pipeline: canvas → ≤300-wide grid (the `_makePixelProbe` trick) → ink
threshold 0.045 (pale microscopy survives) → zero cells under text items
(padded 1 cell) → 8-neighbor connected components → merge boxes closer than
~12 natural px (multi-panel figures fragment around embedded labels) →
filters: components ≥25 cells, ≥60×45 natural px (drops icons/rules/logos),
<85% page coverage (scanned-page PDFs get no hotspot — it would be noise).

Runs in `_renderPage` after `_collectTextItems`; results stored on the node
at NATURAL scale; `getFigureRegions(pageNum)` rescales per zoom (the
`getLinkAnnotations` pattern). Any failure → `[]` → feature silently absent,
marquee/Alt-drag still work.

### Hotspots (`rm/js/figure-capture.js` — `refreshHotspots`)
Per page: a `.fc-hotspot-layer` (pointer-events none) inside the page wrap,
one `.fc-hotspot` per region (pointer-events auto, low-opacity 🖼 badge;
hover = dashed pink outline + "Snip figure" label). Click → `open()` with a
synthetic marquee anchor for exactly that rect. A pointerdown→click that
moved >6px is treated as a drag (marquee/Alt selection over the figure
still works — events bubble through to geom-selection).

Wired from library-paper's existing `onPageReady` (so rects track zoom and
tab switches), gated on `_canAnnotate` (the capture write would be
rules-denied anyway).

## Rollback

Revert + re-FTP; detection failure already degrades to no hotspots.

## Open questions

- Threshold/merge-gap tuning may need a pass on real lab PDFs (esp. very
  pale figures or dense supplementary layouts). The marquee fallback covers
  misses.
- The mobile reader (library-mobile-paper, V3.613) doesn't paint hotspots
  yet — follow-up if capture becomes a phone habit.

## Verification

1. Open a figure-heavy paper: each figure shows a faint 🖼 badge; hover
   outlines the region; click → capture dialog with the figure pre-snipped.
2. Vector-drawn plots (line graphs) get hotspots too, not just photos.
3. Text-only pages show nothing; a scanned PDF page shows nothing.
4. Alt-drag over a hotspot still marquee-selects (no dialog from the drag).
5. A guest without annotate capability sees no hotspots.
