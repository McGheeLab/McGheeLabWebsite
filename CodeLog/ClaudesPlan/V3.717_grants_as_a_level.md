# V3.717 — Grants become a level: Specialties → Concepts → Grants → Aims & outputs

## Goal

Alex: *"The research page currently lists out our specialties, concepts, and
outputs — let's change this to Specialties, Concepts, Grants, and then Aims.
Concepts use a select set of specialties, Grants comes from concepts, and
finally aims are a list of foci from grants."* Then, mid-plan: *"grants in the
grant section can have a status assigned to it as well of in preparation,
submitted, awarded, completed. All work a student performs under that grant
from semester focus, papers, and my week, should be associated and indexed to
this. When I click on a grant it should open into a review of everything about
that grant."* And: *"on the team focus page the 'aligns with research focus'
dropdown should select for grants only. in my week the user can select the
focus (derives from grant) for any new task they are creating and then
optionally a second dropdown selects for the aims and outputs tied to that
focus."*

## The problem with the old model

Since V3.558 a grant was a *facet of a concept*: `grant_meta` (agency /
mechanism / deadline / scope) plus `kind:'grant'` entries in the concept's
`links.draft_refs`. That model cannot answer the question Alex actually needs
answered — *what is happening under this grant?* A grant had no status of its
own, no page, and nothing rolled up to it.

## Decisions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What happens to Outputs? | **Not retired.** `aim` is a new level *alongside* `output`. The 4th band shows both, sub-grouped — "aims are a set of target results we want from the grant to be successful, and the outputs are the synthesis of these results. For book keeping we will keep these separated." |
| Grant status | **Manual, always.** The card is the one source of truth. A linked builder draft's status shows only as a muted secondary pill. |
| Roll-up scope | PI sees all, student sees self — per-member fan-out, rules quietly hide what a viewer can't read. No rules or index change. |
| Attribution | **Derived from the card graph**, never stored. Re-parenting an aim retroactively re-attributes every past week. |

## The model

Five levels in one collection, children pointing UP, every parent list derived:

```
specialty
  └ concept        links.specialty_ids[]
      └ grant      links.concept_ids[]   (+ specialty_ids)
          ├ aim    links.grant_ids[]
          └ output links.aim_ids[] , links.grant_ids[]
                   (legacy concept_ids / specialty_ids still honored)
```

Four hub BANDS, decoupled from the five levels: Specialties · Concepts ·
Grants · **Aims & outputs** (one band, two sub-groups).

`grantIdsForCard(card, allFoci)` is the resolver the whole feature rests on:
grant → itself; aim → its `grant_ids`; output → `grant_ids` ∪ the grants of its
`aim_ids`; concept → the grants built on it, flagged `indirect`; specialty →
nothing (a capability underpins everything and would attribute to every grant
at once).

## Migration

`⤴ Move grants out of concepts` appears on the hub only while a concept still
carries `grant_meta` or a `kind:'grant'` draft ref. Each becomes its own grant
card linked back to its concept, starting at `in_preparation`; the concept is
patched only *after* the card is created, so a failure loses nothing.
Idempotent — a concept whose grant card exists is skipped.

Existing `output` cards are untouched. They keep their roadmaps, claims and
Project Health ownership, and land in the Outputs half of the new band.

## Three bugs found only by driving it

1. **Nested anchors split every card.** Grant/aim/output cards are one big
   `<a>`, and `parentPillsFor` put `<a>` pills inside them. Invalid HTML: the
   parser closes the card early and re-opens empty clones, which painted as
   stray 26×28px bordered boxes beside every card. Pre-existing on output cards
   since V3.544; I had propagated it to two more levels. Pills inside an
   anchor-wrapped card are now spans.
2. **Analysis picker rows had no `links`.** `listFociForPicker` builds one row
   per open roadmap analysis but never copied the card's `links`, so the grant
   walk saw an empty graph and dropped every analysis into the "not under a
   grant" bucket while its own card sat in the correct one. Silent — nothing
   throws.
3. **`sectionOutputs` still rendered `grant_meta`**, which no longer lives on
   the cards that section serves. Extracted to `grantMetaBlock`, used by the
   grant review.

## Not changed

No `firebase deploy`. No new collection, no `collectionGroup` query, no
composite index (`listAllSemesterFoci` is single-field equality on `horizon`).
`researchFoci` rules already cover every level. No `focusAreas` schema change
and no backfill — attribution is derived. FTP-only.
