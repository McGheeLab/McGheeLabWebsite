# V3.895 — Scientia: Shift-tap with the zoom hand fits a component or region

**Ask (Alex, 2026-10-24):** "the magnification tool should be + - zoom like it is now with alt, I want shift click to
zoom to fit a region, or item. in this mode the outline of what we are zoom fitting to highlights its edge."

## Goal

The ✎ pill's zoom hand (the magnifying glass, web Scientia only) keeps tap = zoom in ×1.5 and Alt-tap = zoom out.
Shift stops being a second "zoom out" and becomes **fit**: Shift-tap fits the view to the component or region under the
pointer, and while Shift is held, the edge of what would be fitted lights up.

## Approach — `rm/scientia/lab-mode.js` §11 (`bindZoomHand`), the lab layer only

- **What is under the pointer** (`zfitTarget`): `document.elementsFromPoint`, topmost first. A region's name tab is the
  region, a component (`.box` directly in the paper's world) is the component. Otherwise, the smallest region whose
  rectangle holds the point. Regions are `pointer-events:none`, so a point in a region's margin is found by geometry.
  Nothing (empty paper) → Shift-tap fits the whole note, like ⊕ → Fit. The pointerdown filter (`ZOOM_SKIP`) is shared,
  so nothing lights up where a tap would not reach the zoom hand (pills, buttons, inputs, links).
- **Fit:** `Paper.fitTarget(bb, 48, P.o.maxZ)` + `animateView`. Up to the paper's max zoom, not the 100 % cap that
  `Paper.fit` uses, so a small component fills the screen.
- **The lit edge:** the class `.lab-zfit` on the target element itself, so it moves with the paper. The outline is
  `var(--lab-zw)` = 2 px ÷ zoom, kept at 2 px on screen by the existing `Paper.prototype.apply` wrapper. It is updated
  on host `pointermove` (Shift state read from the event) and on window `keydown`/`keyup` of Shift/Alt, so pressing
  or releasing Shift with the mouse still works. It clears on `pointerleave`, window `blur` and leaving the zoom hand.
- **Alt** also shows the zoom-out cursor (`.lab-zoom-out`).
- **The click after a zoom tap is swallowed** (400 ms, capture on the host), so a Shift-tap on a plate header doesn't
  also shift-select its wells, and a tap on a component only zooms.

## Not changed

Gabe's `src/` (Paper, regions, boxes) is untouched. The desktop has no zoom hand. Pen input still bypasses the
zoom hand. Touch has no Shift, and taps and drags behave as before.
