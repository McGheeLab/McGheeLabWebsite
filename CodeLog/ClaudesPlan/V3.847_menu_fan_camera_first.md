# V3.847 — One ☰ at the bottom fans out the quick links; Scan opens the camera at once

**Alex (2026-10-23):** *"the bottom of the page tool button should replace all of the quick page links. it should
not be a blue plus sign it should be a hamburger icon. all of the pages the user has selected as quick links
should fan out. the scan qr button should be a single click to open the camera to scan a qr code. no one wants to
ever add the code by text and the text isnt even on the printout of the label."*

Corrects V3.844 (a ＋ beside a row of tabs) and the V3.646 scanner default (typed field first, camera behind a
button).

## 1. The bar is one ☰ button

- `mobile-shell.js`: the bottom strip holds a single round **☰** (neutral, not blue; an inline SVG — icons.js has no
  hamburger). Tap → the fan: **Scan a label** first (fixed), then every quick link the person chose, in their order,
  then **More** (fixed). The row of page tabs and the ＋ are gone; nothing is highlighted because nothing is a tab.
- Quick links: up to **8** (`MAX_TABS`), default = the V3.835 six (My Week, Focus, Chat, Tasks, Library,
  Inventory). More → **Customize quick links** is the same sheet as before with the new words.
- Fan geometry (`ringLayout(n)`, pure): ≤ 4 items on one arc (160° → 20°); 5–10 items on two arcs — the first four
  inner (R ≈ 96), the rest outer (R ≈ 182, 150° → 30°), radii scaled to the viewport so nothing clips. `fanItems(keys)`
  (pure) builds the list; a chosen Scan page is not doubled.
- Navigation as before: a native page swaps webviews, any other opens in the misc webview, Scan always fresh.

## 2. Scan = the camera

- `scan.js` `open({ cameraFirst: true })`: when the camera is available the sheet starts it immediately and shows
  no typed field — only a small **Type the code instead** link that reveals the field (and the USB-wedge path) for
  a desktop without a camera or a denied permission. Without a camera the sheet behaves as before.
- `scan.html` and the app's inventory hero use `cameraFirst`; the desktop inventory toolbar's 📷 Scan keeps the
  wedge-first sheet (a USB gun types into the focused field).

## Decisions

- Scan and More are fixed in the fan rather than optional quick links: the one thing the app is for must never be
  un-ticked, and More is the only way to the rest of RM and to Customize.
- Two arcs rather than a grid sheet because Alex said "fan out"; ten is the ceiling (8 + 2) and still fits a 375 px
  phone with 60 px circles.
