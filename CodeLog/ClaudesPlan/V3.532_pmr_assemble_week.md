# V3.532 — My Day R7: PMR "⤓ Assemble my week" + week summary

Part of the **My Day program** — see `V3.526_my_day_program.md`.

## Goal
Program decision 6: the notebook (plus tracker/tasks/My Day) is canonical
for "what I did"; the PMR week becomes assembled + curated instead of
retyped, plus a 2-sentence "Summarize my week" narrative.

## Approach
- **`rm/js/pmr-week-assemble.js`** (new, `window.PMR_WEEK_ASSEMBLE`):
  gathers candidates for the week's date range from (1) notebook `_index`
  rows, (2) `ActivitySignals.listEvents` (collapsing `auto-dayplan-*`
  pairs), (3) completed subtasks (buckets walk, `done_at` in range),
  (4) `dayPlans` items — deduped against the WHOLE period's existing
  backrefs (`source_notebook_id` / `source_subtask_id` /
  `source_day_item_id` + text match) so re-runs are idempotent and items
  pulled into week N never re-suggest in week N+1. Checkbox review dialog
  grouped by subsection guess (task-pmr-sync keyword heuristic); Insert
  hands ready items back to the caller.
- **`rm/js/pmr.js`** — "⤓ Assemble" button in each week header
  (owner-only; sibling of the V3.193 auto-fill pattern) appending into
  the matching subsection + `scheduleSave()`; plus a per-week `summary`
  textarea above the subsections (schema-additive — old weeks simply
  have no summary).

## Deploy
FTP only.

## Verify
- Week with notebook entries + confirmed My Day items → Assemble lists
  them grouped, checked; inserting twice adds nothing the second time.
- Items already mirrored via task-pmr-sync (`source_subtask_id`) don't

- Reviewer (admin viewing a student) sees no Assemble button.
