# V3.215 — Pcard Business Purpose Helper

## Goal

A focused, single-purpose tool that drafts the UA Business Purpose statement
for the Engineering Business Center-North (EBC-N) pcard / reimbursement form.
The user picks the expense type, fills in 3–5 fields, and copies the resulting
1–3 sentence statement into the form's `UA Business Purpose - Benefit to the
University` field.

This page is **not** an EBC-N form reference. The form lives at the EBC-N
website; this page exists only to make the one hard field (Business Purpose)
trivial to write. Other field tips live behind a single collapsed `<details>`
at the bottom of the page.

## Revision note (V3.215 redesign)

The first cut of V3.215 dumped a full EBC-N field cheatsheet, the three
reasonable-expense tests, the AICPA / HEFC code table, a documentation
checklist, and a good-vs-weak examples gallery onto the page alongside the
builder. User feedback: "this page is terrible … just select a few pieces of
information, fill in a few text fields, and get a nicely formatted business
purpose that they can copy and paste." This plan reflects the redesign — the
reference content is gone, the builder is the entire page. See
[feedback memory](../../memory/feedback_focused_tools_not_references.md).

## Approach

Self-contained client-side tool, no Firestore reads/writes. Single-column
layout, max 760px, three numbered sections top-to-bottom:

1. **Pick expense type** — pill buttons for Equipment / Supplies / Conference
   travel / Sponsor review / Software / Membership / Business meal /
   Publication / Other. Active pill is filled with `--primary`. Selecting a
   pill swaps Step 2's fields and re-renders the output.
2. **Fill in the details** — 3–5 conditional inputs specific to the chosen
   expense type. Each template defines its own field list. Persistent state
   across pill switches: if the user filled `purpose` for equipment then
   clicks supplies, the equipment's `purpose` is kept in `state.values` so
   switching back doesn't lose it.
3. **Output** — green-tinted card containing the rendered statement, a
   character count (turns red over 500), and a green Copy button.

At the very top of the page is a single row of click-to-copy chips with the
user's persistent defaults (Dept: BME-(2328), Name, Email, PCard: 3230). One
chip = one click = one value on the clipboard.

A collapsed `<details>` at the bottom of the page lists the other PCard form
field tips (Capital Asset?, Tax Exempt?, Receipt $$, etc.) for the user who
needs a quick reminder while filling the form in another tab. The full
AICPA/HEFC table, the three-tests panel, and the verbatim UA examples are
removed — the user reads those at finance.arizona.edu when they actually need
them, and the page links out.

### Template grammar polish (V3.215 redesign)

- Sponsor-review template: "to attend a Office of Naval Research…" →
  "to attend the Office of Naval Research…" — `the` works for any sponsor
  regardless of initial vowel.
- Publication template: first letter of charge-kind is capitalized
  (`Open-access fee to Journal…`, not `open-access fee to Journal…`).
- Equipment template: vendor-type qualifier ("a reseller", "a refurbished")
  is only inserted when the type is non-OEM, non-authorized.
- Conference travel: a/an article picker for presentation format
  ("deliver an oral presentation" vs "deliver a poster presentation").

## Key decisions

- **Templates, not freeform.** Templates produce reasonable, UA-compliant
  prose without the user thinking about What/Why/Who/How/Where/When. They
  can always pick "Other" and write freeform.
- **No persistence.** PCard transactions are one-shot; drafts live in
  in-memory state and reset on reload.
- **No nav gate.** Page is information-only; useful even signed out.
- **Reference content stays off the page.** UA Business Purpose Guide and
  Reasonable Expense Guide are linked from the collapsed help footer, not
  reproduced.
- **Defaults live in the JS, not Firestore.** Single PI, single PCard — not
  worth a settings UI. If a second person ever uses this, surface a
  per-user override at that time.
