# V4.13 — ⋯ stays inside a component; the editor bar follows a growing component

## Goal
Gabe (17 Nov 2026), two of the batch's requests:
- "For some components (e.g., when a kind is assigned to it), the More button spans outside of the component area."
- "When writing in a component that keeps growing its boundary and the editor bar is located at the bottom, it should dynamically move with each new line added to the component automatically or manual resizing."

## What was wrong
- **⋯ outside the box.** A text component's header row (`.box .bh`, 20-canvas.css) is one flex row that never wraps. Its parts kept their size: ▾, the tag (the kind's label in V3.955 — HYPOTHESIS, MECHANISM — is wider than BLOCK), the name (`.blab`, min-width 90 px), the time stamp (`white-space:nowrap`, about 110 px), then the week ✓ and ⋯. Together they need about 345 px, more with a long kind, so in any component narrower than that (and in wider ones with a long kind tag and more chips) ⋯ was pushed past the right border. Measured: every plain or kinded component at 300 px and below overflowed by 48–219 px. Slim widget headers (13zg) wrap and were fine.
- **The editor bar left behind.** `EBAR.place` (13zzf) put the bar above the component, or below it when there is no room above, and ran again only on a pan or zoom (`Paper.apply`), a window resize or a rebuild. Typing new lines or dragging the component's edge changed the box's size without placing the bar again, so a bar below the component stayed on the old bottom edge, over the new lines.

## Approach
- **The header gives way** (`20-canvas.css`, non-slim headers only): the buttons (▾, ✓, ⋯, flags) keep their size (`flex:none`); the time stamp, the name, the kind's tag and any chip shrink with an ellipsis (`min-width:0`), in that order — the stamp first (`flex-shrink:4`), the name down to 28 px, the kind's tag last (`flex-shrink:.01`, so it keeps its whole word down to about 240 px). ⋯ stays at the right edge at every width.
- **The bar follows its component** (`13zzf-editbar.js`): `EBAR.watch(box)` while the bar is up — a `ResizeObserver` on the component (a new line, a wrapped line, a width drag) and a `MutationObserver` on its `style` (a move) call `EBAR.place` at once, in the same frame; `EBAR.unwatch` when the bar goes. The LAYERS wrapper (13zzn) still runs on every place.

## Files
`scientia/src/20-canvas.css`, `scientia/src/13zzf-editbar.js`.

## Testing
`fa_t1` at 1400 and 400 px (real mouse and keys): ⋯ inside and full size for 26 components (plain and kinded text at 392 · 300 · 240 · 200 px, a reference, PDF / page Reports, a Result panel); slim headers kept; a kinded component at 300 px shows its whole tag. The bar below a component near the top: five Shift+Enter lines, the bar 6 px under the bottom edge after each; a width drag of 150 px, the bar following during the drag, under the taller box, as wide as it; with room above it stays above; it goes on a click away; the text kept. Regression: `rep_t`, `refs_t45`, `t_995`, `rev_t`, `lbl_t`.
