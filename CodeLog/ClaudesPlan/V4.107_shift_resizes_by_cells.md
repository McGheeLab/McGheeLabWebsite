# V4.107 — Scientia: Shift held while resizing goes a grid cell at a time

## Why
Gabe, 23 Nov: "Expanding and holding Shift key should also expand grid block by grid block."

Since V3.992, Shift held while you drag a component moves it a grid cell at a time. Resizing ignored Shift: the edge followed the pointer, and the size was rounded to a cell only when you let go.

## What changed
- **`10-paper.js`:** `PTUNE.cells(v, min)` gives a size in whole cells of the chosen grid (Settings → Moving around the paper → *A component settles to*), never below `min` (the least size, rounded up to a whole cell).
- **With Shift held, every resize grip on a note goes a cell at a time while you drag:**
  - a component's right edge, and the corner grip of image panels and slides (`12-doc.js`);
  - an HTML page's grip, width and height (`13g-embed.js`);
  - a PDF's or a document's grip, width and height (`13zk-docs.js`);
  - a table's and a plot's height (`13zt-table.js`).
- **Without Shift:** as before, free while dragging. The HTML page's and the PDF's grips now settle to the chosen cell when you let go, as a component does; before, they always used 14 px whatever the setting.
- **Settings → Moving around the paper:**
  - the description and the grid setting say it;
  - the preview's box has a right edge to resize it, with Shift too;
  - the caption under the preview says so.
- **`MANUAL.md`** (Components → Resize) says it.
