# V3.844 — The ＋ fan (Scan a label first), actions attributed to the user, and "Something I did" in the notebook

**Alex (2026-10-22):** *"there should be a quick link to scan qr codes somewhere i think from the plus button when
clicked should fan out to make a selection from tools. one of those tools should be a quick link to qr code scanning
via camera. actions like using an item should be saved as an action attributed to the user that the user can add to
their lab notebook easily. for example a user uses antibodies, elisa kit, and fluorescent beads. each one of these
items is an action listed. on the notebook, there is an item that can be added that says they did an action. other
actions are things like they used equipment etc. users can choose to ignore the items use instead of placing it in
the notebook."*

Follows V3.840 (scan → the Woodchuck dial; user-chosen tabs).

## 1. The ＋ in the bottom bar fans out tools

- `mobile-shell.js` puts a raised **＋** in the middle of the bar (its own slot). Tap → a dim backdrop and the
  tools fan out on an arc above it (the canvas pills' gesture): **📷 Scan a label**, ⚡ My actions, ✎ Notebook,
  ✓ Task, 🧪 Equipment. A tool navigates the app way (native webview when the page has one, the misc webview
  otherwise). Scan always re-opens fresh (`scan.html?t=<now>`), so the camera comes up every time.
- With a ＋ slot the bar holds **5 chosen pages + ＋ + More** (7 slots, the V3.835 width). `MAX_TABS` 6 → 5;
  default = My Week · Chat · Tasks · Library · Inventory (Focus moves under More; it stays one tick away in
  Customize tabs). A saved six is trimmed to its first five.
- `scan` and `actions` join the CATALOG, so either can be a bottom tab in its own right.

## 2. `scan.html` — the scan tool

A light page (the scan stack only, no inventory table): the scanner opens on arrival (camera when the app grants
it, the typed field otherwise), a hit goes straight to `INV_USE.take` (the dial), then **Scan another**. Under it,
today's actions with ＋ Notebook / Ignore, and a link to all actions.

## 3. Actions attributed to the user — `rm/js/user-actions.js`

- `userData/{uid}/actions/{id}`: `{ kind: 'inventory_use' | 'equipment_booking', at (ISO), status: 'new' | 'ignored'
  | 'noted', notedIn[], … }` + the kind's fields (`itemId/itemName/qty/unit/delta/after/useMode/note/labelCode/usageId`;
  `equipmentId/equipmentName/date/endDate/startTime/endTime/durationMin/notes/bookingId`). Owner read/write under the
  blanket `userData` rule — no rules deploy. Ids are `inv_<usageId>` / `eq_<bookingId>` so a retry never doubles.
- Written at the moment of the deed: `INV_QTY.onLocalWrite` (every ledger `use` from any surface — the dial, the
  notebook's inventory card, stock-take; `write()` now puts `event/note/labelCode/context` on its result), the
  log/legacy routes in `INV_USE.run`, and `equipment.js` right after `EDB.createBooking`.
- `USER_ACTIONS_CORE` (node-tested): `fromInventory(out)`, `fromBooking(id, b)`, `describe(a, now)` ("Used 2.5 µL
  of Anti-tubulin", "Took 2 packs of Tips", "Logged a use of …", "Booked the confocal · Thu 23 Oct 10:00–12:00",
  which reads "Used the confocal …" once the slot has passed), `dayKey/dayLabel/groupByDay`, `pending`, `timeOf`.
- Page half: `record`, `list`, `get`, `ignore`, `restore`, `markNoted(id, { entryId, cardId })`, `onChange`.

## 4. `actions.html` — My actions

Grouped by day (Today, Yesterday, dates). Each row: the description, time, note; **＋ Notebook** (→
`notebook.html?action=<id>`) and **Ignore** (→ status ignored). "Show ignored" reveals them with Restore. Noted
rows show where they went.

## 5. The notebook card — "Something I did"

`notebook-canvas.js` registers kind `action` (group Lab, ⚡): with no action picked the card is an inline picker of
the pending actions (today first); picking one fills the card (text, time, note, a link to the item or the
equipment) and marks the action `noted` with the entry and card ids. `?action=<id>` on `notebook.html` drops the
card straight into the entry that opens (used by the ＋ Notebook buttons).

## Decisions

- Actions are MATERIALISED per user rather than queried from `inventoryUsage` / `equipmentBookings`: one place to
  list, ignore and link, no composite indexes, and a deed from any surface lands there.
- An equipment "use" is the booking: recorded when it is made, described as *Booked* until the slot ends and *Used*
  after. Nothing else in RM records the moment of use.
- The ＋ is in the bar, not a floating button: Library and Team Focus already own a floating ＋ for their own
  composer, and a second circle on top of them would be noise.
