# V4.36 — A popup's close button

## Goal
Gabe (18 Nov 2026): "Popups don't close even when you leave notes or move through the notebook. Add a close button to it when you hover on it."

## Approach
- **The button:** × on the popup's top edge, beside its top-right corner (`.rp-x`, 34 px from the corner, clear of the corner's resize handle and of the top anchor), in the popup's own layer (`RPOP.mount`).
- **When it shows:** hidden and not clickable at rest. It shows while the pointer is on the popup, anywhere in it, after the same rest as the anchors (Settings → Popup windows → "The anchors, corners and × show after resting on it for": no wait / 0.5 s / 1 s, default 1 s, Gabe's hover rule). It hides 0.3 s after the pointer leaves. On a tablet it shows with the anchors when the border is tapped (`.rp-edge`). It is hidden while dragging.
- **What it does:** `RPOP.remove(p)`. The popup closes; the paper or page is back in its card, and the popup is not reopened after a reload (`_ls 'rpop'`). The file's tab already left the reading tabs when it became a popup (V4.32), so nothing else changes.
- **Popups still stay from note to note** (V4.32: they step aside on pages that aren't notes). With × one click takes one away wherever it is.
- **Settings:** the description, the rest's label, and the live preview (× on the mock popup, the caption).
