# V3.890 — PE bead confocal assay: binding model, image simulation, measurement strategy

**Date:** 2026-10-24 · **Type:** research deliverable (no site code) · **Asked by:** Alex via `/deep-research`

## Goal

A model of PE surface coverage on 8 µm capture-antibody beads as a function of analyte concentration in the
one-step sandwich format (analyte and PE-detection antibody incubated together, so the pre-complex route
exists), including the two dissociation constants (detection antibody off the analyte; analyte off the capture
antibody) and the post-wash decay they produce. A model and simulation of what the Nikon AX-R sees when it
images that bead — a fluorescent **spherical shell**, not a fluorescent core — in a single focal plane, with the
pinhole, laser power, detector gain, PE surface density, photobleaching, pixels per bead, dwell time, focal-plane
offset, bead position in the scan and the objective (10x/0.45 vs 20x/0.75) all varied. A measurement strategy
derived from both.

## Approach

1. **Literature:** a deep-research workflow (`workflows/scripts/deep-research-bead-confocal-wf_465a4142-d61.js`,
   every agent on Opus 5.5 as Alex asked): 6 search angles → 15 sources fetched → 104 falsifiable claims →
   3-lens adversarial verification (source fidelity, physics, independent cross-check; 2 of 3 refutes kills) →
   two model drafts → integrated report → completeness critic → revision. 98 claims verified by ≥2 lenses,
   1 refuted.
2. **Model + simulation** (written in parallel, `Research/bead_confocal_sim/`):
   - `binding.py` — mass-action ODEs for A, D, AD (solution) and SA, SAD (surface), finite sites and volume
     (depletion), a Berg–Purcell transport factor per bead, perfect-wash dissociation phase, excess-D closed forms.
   - `optics.py` — explicit 3-D confocal PSF = Gaussian-beam excitation × pinhole acceptance (a non-central
     chi-square integral), tabulated per objective/pinhole; shell on a Fibonacci lattice rendered by z-slabs in
     Fourier space; point-scan sampling; saturable PE emission; Poisson + PMT excess noise + offset/ADC; uniform
     raster bleaching dose; bead autofluorescence and free PE-antibody backgrounds; metrics (integrated,
     ring-mean, rim peak, ring radius, SNR, LOD). Verified against the hat-box analytic integral to 4 digits.
   - `run_figures.py` — nine figures + `results.json`; `tables.py` — markdown tables.
3. **Report:** `Research/PE_bead_confocal_assay_model.md` merges the verified literature with the simulation
   results and ends with the measurement strategy and the first experiments to run.

4. **Experiments** (Alex, mid-turn: "design a set of experiments that you would want me to do on a real system"):
   `Research/PE_bead_confocal_experiments.md` orders 13 experiments instrument → optics → photophysics → backgrounds
   and calibration → assay kinetics → mode validation, each naming the model parameter it pins and the decision it
   makes, plus a one-day subset and the plate-map columns needed to refit the simulator.

## Key decisions

- **Two "section thicknesses" are reported, not one.** The on-axis z FWHM sets how sharp the rim ring is; the
  plane-integrated ("sea") response sets what the integrated metric sees. They differ by 2× at 1 AU and by 6×
  at 4 AU; a single Zeiss-formula number hides the distinction that decides the measurement strategy.
- **Integrated intensity over a bead mask is the recommended metric** (hat-box theorem: a uniform shell's
  integrated signal depends on z0 only through ∫H(z−z0)dz), with 10x + 4 AU as the focus-tolerant mode and
  20x + 1 AU as the high-contrast mode. Ring-mean and rim-peak metrics lose 20–30 % at a 2 µm focus error.
- **Bleaching is a global scale factor, not a rim bias**, because a full raster delivers the same fluence to every
  plane; the dose per frame scales with (pixels per bead)² at fixed dwell.
- The PSF widths are Gaussian-beam approximations (within ~30 % of the Zeiss formula); the report says to measure
  the real section with a z-series on a bead or thin layer before trusting absolute numbers.
