# V4.35 — Pills follow the turn; your pill layout in Settings; bridges over the pills

## Goal
Gabe (18 Nov 2026): "Switching from horizontal to vertical layout on a tablet via rotation of the device (now with the rotate button) should allow pills to move dynamically with that system and go back to their original position. Also, the settings tab should have an empty note canvas in which you can place your pill layout, which will be remembered across devices. Bridge half moons and dots when in full screen should appear over the pills."

## Approach
- **The pills follow the note's shape** (`rm/scientia/lab-mode.js` §11, the pills — a lab-layer edit Gabe asked for; pill edits are allowed, Alex reviews; the build cuts §11 unchanged into `scientia/vendor/lab-pills.js` for builds without the lab layer):
  - Places were already shares of the note (`labPills.pos[name] = {fx, fy}`), but they were painted only when something else repainted the pills. Now a `ResizeObserver` on each note's host repaints them, debounced 90 ms, whenever the note changes shape: a tablet turned, the app turned by ⊕'s Rotate (13zzz), the window or a pane resized. Each pill **glides** from where it was to its new place (Web Animations, 320 ms, measured in the note's own frame so it is right under Rotate too; none with reduced motion).
  - **Portrait keeps its own places.** A note taller than it is wide (`portrait(host)`: height > 1.05 × width) reads and writes `labPills.portrait {pos, orient}`. A pill not placed there yet keeps its landscape share, so it follows the turn. Turning back finds the landscape places untouched: "go back to their original position". Docked or not, and the Quick cast's list, stay shared.
  - `labPills.one` (Settings → Pills → Portrait and landscape: The same places) keeps one layout for both.
  - "Put the pills back" clears both shapes.
  - §11 exposes `LAB.pillDefs` (each pill's starting place) and `LAB.pillLayout.portrait(host)`.
- **Your pill layout in Settings** (`scientia/src/20c-pillcanvas.js`, `PILLCANVAS`; `34-pillcanvas.css`):
  - Settings → Pen & touch → Pills' preview is now an **empty note** in the shape of a note on this screen (the window's long and short sides, less the header), with the four pills where they sit. Drag one to place it.
  - It writes the same record the pills use (`settings().labPills`, synced), so every device has it. Against a side it docks as a column, against the top or bottom as a row, as on a note.
  - **Landscape / Portrait** shows each shape's places; **Reset** clears that shape's.
  - With the pills off, the old preview (the bottom bar) shows.
  - Every canvas on screen redraws after a change.
- **Bridges over the pills** (`34-pillcanvas.css`): a note's bridge circles (half-moons at its edges, beads in full screen) and threads, `.paper > .bw-edge` / `.bw-threads`, go to z-index 22, over the pills' 21. They share the note host's stacking context: `.paper` makes none of its own. An open wheel (`.lab-pills.fan-open`) goes to 23, so its items stay reachable. This applies in and out of full screen; out of it, the half-moons rarely meet a pill.
