## What was built

   - **`pe-bead-confocal.html`** (written in the main thread). Its engine `src/confocal-engine.js` is `optics.py` ported. `tools/check_confocal.py` checks it to 0.2% (PSF, H, F) and 0.6% (rendered F, rim peak). It adds two things:

     - the bead's excluded volume for free PE.

## Decisions

- **Set-up notes are `note`, not `resource`.** Scientia shows a resource as a material sheet (lot, batch, stored at), not as a canvas.
- **Experiments are `protocol` notes that follow Gabe's protocol template** (Purpose, Materials, Prepare, Run…). Their components are numbered as steps, as his are. The RM imports stay the calculator-rich version: the notes link to them, and nothing was imported into RM.
- **The report's 2 µW stays the default.** The exact saturation result (F stays linear to ≈5–6 µW at 20x) is reported alongside it, because PE's dark state is not in the model ([[lit review fB3]]).
- **The geometric ray trace's bright top cap is flagged as probably not real for a confocal.** It counts light reaching the objective, not light passing the pinhole (Vorselen 2020).
