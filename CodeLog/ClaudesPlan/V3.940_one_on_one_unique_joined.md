# V3.940 — One-on-ones: unique slots (one week at a time, every week on approval) and joined back-to-back slots

> **Status: BUILT 2026-10-29.** Alex: *"for the offering time, i want a toggle for make unique. unique slots mean that a
> person signs up for that slot for just that week. The student can request for that slot to become recurring, and
> does upon my approval. this can be changed after the fact as well. also if a student take two slots in a row they
> are combined to an hour."* Builds on [V3.938](V3.938_one_on_one_meetings.md).

## Model

A slot stays ONE recurring time (`oneOnOneSlots/{id}`). What a sign-up means depends on its new `mode`:

| mode | a student's sign-up | written as |
|---|---|---|
| `recurring` (default, every V3.938 slot) | every week | `subscriberUid = me` (unchanged) |
| `unique` | that week only | `bookings.<me>.<date> = {name, at, by}` |

- **Every week on approval.** A student with a booked week asks: `recurRequests.<me> = {name, at, from}`. The host
  approves → `subscriberUid = them`, `subscribedFrom = from`, request cleared, their own bookings from `from` folded
  in. Declining clears the request; their booked weeks stay.
- **Who holds a date** (`holderOn` in the client, `slotHolderOn` in the function): a one-week booking wins (the
  earliest if two ever collide); else the every-week holder from `subscribedFrom`, `skipped` if they skipped it; else
  open. A skipped week of a unique slot is open to a one-week booking.
- **Changed after the fact.** The host can flip a slot between every week and unique in Edit (it governs the NEXT
  sign-up; whoever has it every week keeps it), make any booked week every week ("Make it every week", no request
  needed), turn an every-week holder back into one week ("Make it one week only" — they keep the next date as a
  booking), book a week for a student ("Book for…"), and cancel a booked week.

## Joined slots

When one person holds back-to-back slots of the same host on a date (one ends as the next starts), the calendar,
the student's My one-on-ones, the reminder and the `.ics` show ONE block: `entriesOn(list, date)` merges per date;
`standingGroups(slots)` merges every-week slots with the same holder, weekday, cadence and dates (one `.ics` VEVENT
with the combined DTEND and the union of skipped / others' weeks as EXDATE); the weekly pattern brackets them
(`.oo-joined`, "10:00 AM – 11:00 AM — a one-hour meeting with Zeke (2 slots)"). Sign-up sheets offer "Also take
10:30 – 11:00 — a one-hour meeting" when the next slot is free; requests, approvals, skips, unsubscribe and "one
week only" act on the whole joined block.

## Rules

`oneOnOneSlots` member update (`oooMemberUpdate()`): keys ⊆ subscriber fields, `skips`, `bookings`,
`recurRequests`, `updatedBy/At`; `bookings` and `recurRequests` change only under the caller's own uid key, and a
booking needs an active slot; the every-week claim is refused on a `unique` slot (that is the host's approval).
Two students racing for one week are settled by the client's transaction (`bookWeek` re-reads and refuses a taken
week). The host / an admin write anything.

## Function

`slotTransition(before, after, today)` gains `booked` / `unbooked` (by self → host; by host → student),
`recurRequested` (→ host, kind `approval`), `recurApproved` (→ student; replaces the "set up a one-on-one" notice and
suppresses the folded weeks' "cancelled"), `recurDeclined`, `recurWithdrawn` (quiet), `madeOneWeek` (→ student, with
the kept date). `ended` / `deleted` / `changed` also reach students with an upcoming booked week. The 16:00 reminder
uses `slotHolderOn` (so one-week bookings are reminded) and joins back-to-back blocks per (host, person):
"Tuesday 2:00 PM–3:00 PM (1 hour)", host list "Gabriel 2:00 PM–3:00 PM · Zeke 3:00 PM".
