# V3.427 — Paragraphs break across pages (fill the column foot) + a customizable "Column fill" setting

> Alex: "there is still an issue with the text flow not allowing a paragraph to
> be broken between pages and this is causing blank lines between pages." Then:
> "The fix: let paragraphs fill down to ~1 line — make this a customizable
> setting."
>
> (Numbered V3.427 — parallel node-editor sessions took V3.425/V3.426.)

## Root cause

The per-column packer (V3.421) already splits a crossing paragraph via
`_splitColItem`, and body paragraphs carry `margin:0`, so measurement/splitting
were wired correctly. The blank came from two conservative guards:

1. **Orphan guard** `budget < lineH * 2` in `_splitColItem` — a paragraph that
   arrived at a column/page foot with **fewer than 2 lines** of room was NOT
   split; the *whole* paragraph jumped to the next page, stranding up to ~2
   blank lines.
2. **`FILL_FACTOR = 0.97`** — every page's column budget was 97% of the body
   height, leaving a ~3% (~1.3-line) blank strip at every page foot. That
   factor existed for CSS-multicol balancing drift; V3.421's explicit per-column
   fill + line-level splitting makes it unnecessary (splitting prevents clips,
   and measurement over-counts, so full items page-break early rather than
   clipping).

Together: up to ~3 blank lines at every page foot = "blank lines between pages."

## Fix

- **`FILL_FACTOR` 0.97 → 0.99** — columns fill nearly the full body height.
- **`_splitColItem` orphan guard** `lineH * 2` → `lineH * fillMinLines()`, and
  the part-A widow guard `lineH * 1.8` → `lineH * 0.9` — a paragraph now breaks
  once the foot has `fillMinLines()` lines of room, filling the column.
- **Empty-column oversized split** in `addColItem`: a paragraph taller than a
  whole column that lands in an empty column now splits (fill this column, carry
  the rest) instead of being dropped in whole and overflowing/clipping.

## Customizable setting — "Column fill" (Alex's follow-up)

The fill threshold is now a per-paper preset, `paperExports.columnFill`:

| Preset | Lines to break | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| `tight` (default) | 1 | fills the most; allows a one-line orphan at the foot |
| `normal` | 2 | breaks with 2 lines to spare |
| `loose` | 3 | keeps more of a paragraph together; more blank at feet |

`COLUMN_FILL_LINES = {tight:1, normal:2, loose:3}` + `fillMinLines()` in
paper-doc-view.js. **Preview-only** — it changes how the Document view breaks
paragraphs; the compiled PDF fills columns via LaTeX's own `\flushbottom` glue,
so nothing is emitted to the `.tex`.

Plumbed exactly like `floatSpacing`:
- **`rm/js/paper-doc-view.js`** — `loadConfig` default `columnFill:'tight'` +
  read; ⬒ Layout toolbar `#pdv-column-fill` (Tight/Balanced/Loose) with
  `_onColumnFillChange` (set-merge on paperExports + forced re-render).
- **`rm/js/paper-export.js`** — `_defaultConfig`/`_loadConfig`/`_saveConfigNow`
  + `_bindColumnFill()` on the Export panel's `#px-column-fill`.
- **`rm/pages/paper-export.html`** — "Column fill" row under "Float spacing".

Default `tight` means existing papers pick up the fill fix automatically; loosen
per paper if you prefer widow/orphan protection over a filled foot.

## Note on fidelity

`tight` can show a one-line orphan/widow the compiled PDF wouldn't (LaTeX shifts
lines via glue to avoid them). That's the accepted trade for a filled preview;
`loose` is the widow/orphan-safe end of the dial.
