## 1. The whiteboard's way of writing, in notes

- `scripts/scientia_build.py` generates `scientia/vendor/wb-ink.js` from `whiteboard.js`: the V3.727 stroke outline
  (`pressF`, `crControls`, `FLATTEN_STEP_PX`, `WIDTH_SMOOTH_PX`, `JOIN_ANGLE`, `flattenStroke`, `smoothHalfWidths`,
  `traceOutline`, `fillStrokeOutline`), cut out by name, unchanged, wrapped as `WBINK`. Tracked like the other vendor
  copies; `--check` fails when it differs from what `whiteboard.js` gives, and the build stops if a name is missing.
- `INK.path(s)` (13zf) draws a pen stroke with `WBINK.fillStrokeOutline` on a path recorder (a 2D-context stand-in that
  writes SVG path data): Catmull-Rom through every sample, half-widths smoothed over ±6 px, round joins and caps, one
  filled outline. No taper (the whiteboard has none).
- The notebook's pressure feel stays (Settings in the bar: sensitivity, thinnest / thickest, fade): a stroke with
  `wr` maps each sample to `wr0 + (wr1 − wr0)·p`, carried into the whiteboard's `pressF` range by a per-stroke scale
  (exact while one stroke spans ≤ 6× in width). Without pressure: the whiteboard's own `pressF`.
- Highlighter: the same Catmull-Rom centre line (smooth), still a translucent band. Shapes (`geo`) unchanged. Dashed
  strokes: `DASH.width` follows the new width (`INK.wmul`).

## 2. The notebook's pen on the whiteboard (Scientia's board only)

- `scientia/board/pen.js` (inlined into `board.html` after the lab's scripts) drives the engine through its handle
  (`NOTEBOOK_BOARD._state.inst`): the pen in hand is the notebook's (`window.parent.INK`) — colour, width (a notebook
  width in px = that many thousandths of the board's width), highlighter colour, eraser.
- The notebook's ink bar (the same `INK.bar`: palettes with ⊕ and the colour editor, widths, fill, pressure, ↶ ↷) sits
  at the top left of the whiteboard window (13zzd); every change reaches the board (`SIS_PEN.sync`).
- Pressure: each pointer sample's pressure is mapped through the notebook's curve before the engine reads it (live and
  saved strokes agree). Pressure off = even lines.
- Slim Pen eraser end (`button 5` / `buttons & 32`): the eraser for that stroke, then back.
- Two-finger tap = undo, three = redo, and ↶ ↷: a journal in `board/store.js` of what each touch of the board changed
  (strokes, cards, wires); an erased stroke comes back under a new id (the engine never re-erases an id it erased).
  The board's own "Undo my last stroke" goes through the same journal.
- Fill: with ◧, a tap is read where the engine puts it (a transparent probe stroke, removed at once); the smallest closed
  stroke around it is filled by fill strokes drawn just under it (`fillOf`), solid with the pen, a translucent layer
  with the highlighter. Erasing a stroke takes its fill with it.
- The board's ✎ fan keeps its modes; its width, eraser, add-a-pen and undo items act on the notebook pen.
- The port keeps its own `wb_*` browser settings (`sis-board:` prefix in store.js), so the website's boards on the same
  origin keep theirs.
- Not ported: shape snapping, the lasso, the ink library, convert to text (the board has its own cards and selection).

## 3. The palm

`PALM` (13zf) — one set of rules for notes and the board:
- a contact wider or taller than `PALM.SIZE` (where the screen reports it) is a palm;
- touches while the pen is near (hovering or writing in the last `PALM.QUIET` ms) are not gestures, and a pen coming
  near cancels a finger pan or pinch in progress;
- two or three fingers count (pinch, two- and three-finger taps) only when every pair is at least `PALM.GAP` apart;
  a second contact closer than that marks the touch as a palm until it lifts.
Applied in 13zf (pan / pinch), 13zm (taps), lab-mode §11 (the zoom hand's tap), board/pen.js (touches never reach the
engine when they are a palm).

## 4. Pills (lab-mode §11)

- ✎ docks too, like the others: left open `LP.dockMs` it becomes its column (Scientia sets 3 s for every pill in
  `99-boot.js`; the lab's default stays 10 s).
- Orientation: a pill dropped touching the left or right edge docks as a column; top or bottom, as a row. Defaults:
  ＋ ✦ rows, ⊕ ✎ columns.
- The Quick cast replaces the bottom bar: the dock is hidden in a note with pills (a read-only note keeps its "Back to
  now" bar); ＋ sits at the bottom centre and drags anywhere. Pull in… and Flag are the first two Quick cast entries.
- The layout (positions, docked, orientation, Quick cast list) is kept in the notebook's settings (`labPills`, synced),
  saved on every change, read on every paint — the same in every note, session and computer. The old per-device keys are
  read once.
