# V3.863 — The ✎ pill's fifth mode is Cards (move and edit cards), not Text

**Date:** 2026-10-23
**Alex:** *"instead of a text tool, it should be a card moving and editing tool"*

## Today (V3.860)
The ✎ pill's first arc is zoom · pan · pen · highlighter · **text**; text mode places a typed-note card where you tap. Cards are
moved by their header (any mode), resized by their corner grip, edited by clicking into their body; on a phone the whole card is
the handle (a drag moves it, a tap opens its sheet). Tools ⤧ Arrange lifts every item for moving / resizing / removing.

## Design
- **First arc:** zoom · pan · pen · highlighter · **Cards** (icon `cursor-arrow-rays`). Text mode goes: `placeTextAt`,
  `TEXT_SIZES`, `TEXT_TINTS`, the `text-size` / `text-tint` actions and their CSS. New typed notes still come from ＋ (and from the
  Cards arc, below).
- **Cards mode on the board** (`S.tool === 'card'`, root class `wb-hand-card`):
  - **The whole card is the handle.** Press anywhere on a card and drag (≥ 8 px) to move it (regions by their header as before,
    carrying their cards); figures and text items on the media layer are lifted above the ink and move the same way. Bodies are
    inert until the card is chosen, so a drag never selects text or presses a button by accident.
  - **A tap chooses a card for editing** (`S.cardSel`, class `wb-card-sel`, a solid teal outline): its body comes alive and the
    field under the tap gets the caret. A tap on another card moves the choice; a tap on the empty board, Escape, or leaving
    Cards mode lets it go. On a phone a tap opens the card's sheet, as today.
  - **The empty board pans** with a mouse or stylus drag (fingers already scroll), so moving cards around a large board never
    needs a mode switch. A double tap on the empty board still goes back to the last mode.
- **Second arc for Cards:**
  - a card is chosen → ✎ Edit (caret into it; a phone opens its sheet) · ● Colour (the card's palette; notebook) ·
    Collapse / Expand · ↑ Bring to front · ⋯ More (the card's own menu) · 🗑 Remove (two taps; through the host's own Remove
    so its clean-up runs, e.g. a collection's mirror) · × Done.
  - nothing chosen → ＋ Add a note card (at the centre of the view, chosen for editing) · ▭ Region (notebook) · ⊡ Fit everything.
- Double tap / last mode: unchanged; `toggleLastMode` treats `card` like pan / zoom (its partner is the pen).
