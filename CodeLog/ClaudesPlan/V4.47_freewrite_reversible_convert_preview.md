# V4.47 — Freewrite: Convert can be undone, shows the note as it will look, and Apply can be taken back

## Goal
Gabe (19 Nov 2026): "Also, for the freewrite, convert should be reversible. Convert can also be used to see a preview of how the note will look."

## Approach (`14j-freewrite.js`)
Two steps turn a freewrite into a note: **Convert** reads the words (colours, the small "note it will make" beside them), then **Apply** puts the components in the note. "Reversible" fits both, so both are now reversible.

- **Undo convert:** once converted, the Convert button says Undo convert (`FREEW.plain()`). The colours, the side preview and the preview in the note go. Every choice made by clicking (`fw.over`, `fw.lover`) is kept, so the next Convert brings it back. Ctrl+Enter only converts. Named "Undo convert", not "Plain words", because the menu for one word already has "Plain words — not a component". With "the words are coloured: as you write" there is nothing to undo, so it stays Convert.
- **The note as it will look, in the note itself:** `FREEW.ghost` / `ghostPaint`. Preview in the note, on "The note it will make", puts the page beside the note. The components Apply would make are drawn in the note's own canvas (`pane.paper.world`, so they pan and zoom with it):
  - outlined in their kind's colour and marked Preview;
  - exactly where Apply puts them (the same `FREEW.layout` and `FREEW.room`, snapped to 14 px);
  - with their links dashed;
  - components already in the note (made before, or associated) are themselves.

  The camera fits the freewrite and the preview once. The preview follows the words, survives a redraw of the note (after `App.refresh`), and goes on Hide in the note, Full page, Undo convert, Apply and Close. Beside the note it is on by default; nothing changes in the note until Apply.
- **Take back** (`FREEW.takeBack`), after Apply:
  - *Where:* on the freewrite's card. When the card is folded, as Apply leaves it, the button is in its header. Also in its ⋯ menu ("Take back what it made") and on its page.
  - *What leaves the note:* the components it made (`fwFrom` = the freewrite) and their links (`EDIT.deleteBlocks`). Components that existed before (an association) stay.
  - *The freewrite:* a draft again, unfolded, its colours kept.
  - *Asked first* (`pickModal`) when one of them changed since Apply.
  - The toast after Apply names it.
- **Settings → Freewrite:** the description.
