## Goal

Replace `Videos/Deposit In Lls Video.webm` (a 4.4 s, 1000×400 render from January 2024 with no source scene: a
cylinder dragged through grey spheres leaving red ones, which does not loop) with a video that

1. loops without a seam;
2. is a physical simulation: the bath yields, flows round the needle and re-jams behind it from contact mechanics
   alone, the ink is extruded through the needle, and granules visibly squash against the needle and each other;
3. shows what the lab's cells do in a granular scaffold: attach, spread, crawl, reach across voids and pull granules
   together;
4. is a close side view of a steel needle in a bed of rough, mostly transparent, self-luminous polygonal granules
   (bath FITC green, ink rhodamine orange-red, cells Cy5 magenta), with the bed's surface out of frame, the bath in
   front of the needle cut away, and the bath behind it in focus near the needle and increasingly blurred and dim
   with depth; shown at ~20 % opacity under the mission text as before.

## What the repos say (sources used)

- **Bath physics** — `Writing/papers/MEBP-Paper/research/lit_review/print_resolution_granular.md` (McGhee 2018,
  *Exp Mech* 58:137): displacement beside the needle ≈ one needle diameter, fluid-like beside the needle and
  solid-like ahead/behind/far away, re-solidification in ~0.3 s, line cross-section `A = Q/v`. Lab granules: 20–300 µm
  polyacrylamide/agarose crushed by homogeniser and sieved — angular chunks in the confocal montages
  (microfluidic-LLS methods; tf-ELISA methods: "the LLS overlay locally fluidizes around the descending needle and
  re-jams behind it"). Blunt needles G16–G32 (`papers/MEBP-Paper/author/hardware_specs.md`).
- **Cells** — the GELS CAREER text (`grants/GELS CAREER/Final package/…/paper.md`): cells live on granule surfaces
  and in the voids, never inside granules; network formation over 48 h; active bridges; four candidate ways a cell
  pulls two granules together. GELLS-DEM (`Simulations/models/GELLS-DEM/gels/kernels/cells.py`): attach → spread →
  migrate (persistent random walk on the surface) → bridge within a sensing range → contract with a capped
  motor-clutch force. Cell 20 µm, granules 40–60 µm.
- **Models borrowed** — the Teaching toy models `granular-bed.html` (needle as a kinematic body in a soft-disc bed)
  and `gels-assembly.html` (cells adhered to two granules contract with a capped force).
- **Vocabulary** — LLS = liquid-like solid; GELS = Granular Enabled Living Scaffold; the lab's confocal figures are
  FITC/rhodamine/Cy5 channels.

## Approach

Five scripts under `scripts/hero_video/` (not deployed; `deploy/site.json` ships `Videos/**` only):

1. **`simulate.py`** — Durian's bubble model in 3D (overdamped soft spheres, Hookean contacts, drag against the
   solvent at rest), numpy/scipy, periodic `cKDTree` neighbour lists. Units: mean granule radius, seconds. A tile of
   ≈4,700 polydisperse granules (radii 0.78–1.22) grown to φ = 0.72 — above jamming — in a 48 × 30 × 20 box periodic
   along x and relaxed to mechanical equilibrium with FIRE, then tiled five times. No-slip container: granules within
   1.5 of the floor or side walls are fixed; the lid is smooth. The needle is a rigid hollow blunt cannula (outer
   radius 3.5, bore 2.45, tip 11 above the floor) moving at 3 units/s: bath granules see a solid flat-tipped cylinder;
   ink granules (radii 0.68–1.02) are fed into the top of the bore on a periodic schedule (450 per period), pushed
   down by a piston force, confined by the inner wall, and leave through the bore's mouth, displacing bath granules.
   One cell per five ink granules. Cells: suspended particle → attach to the nearest granule (ink preferred) → spread
   over 1 s → crawl (persistent random walk) → after 1.4–2.8 s pick up to two granules across a void of 0.45–2.4 and
   grow protrusions over 0.8 s → contract (a spring whose rest gap closes over 2 s, capped), pulling the pair together
   through the same contact mechanics. 30 s of printing before the loop; records 480 + 72 frames either side.
2. **`loopify.py`** — (a) closes the seam: the strip, feed schedule and random streams are periodic, so frame T is
   frame 0 shifted by L, except that yielding is chaotic (the arrangement repeats, the identities do not); the last
   frame's granules and cells are matched to the nearest unclaimed partner of the same kind in the loop-start
   arrangement and glide onto its track over the last 72 frames, eased in and out; unmatched ones shrink away or grow
   in from spare slots; protrusions hand over. (b) works out the squash: every contact (granule–granule, and
   granule–needle) flattens the granule along the contact normal by its share of the overlap; the shape tensor is
   made volume-preserving and stored per granule per frame (`gdef`, float16).
3. **`render_blender.py`** — Blender 5.2 Eevee, headless. Orthographic camera along +y, tracking the needle
   (62 % across), 35 × 14 units (z 3–17: the bed's surface at 20 and floor at 0 are out of frame). Granule centres
   with y < −1.2 are cut away. Each frame is rendered once per depth band (centres 0, 2.5, 5.5, 9; the blurred bands at
   half resolution and 16 samples, the in-focus band at full resolution and 64); each granule is drawn into the two
   bands either side of its depth with linearly sliding weights, so nothing pops as granules flow round the needle,
   and dims with depth. Granules are 16 random convex hulls (bmesh), instanced through geometry nodes with a per-point
   4×4 transform (a `FLOAT4X4` attribute → Set Instance Transform) = squash tensor × random rotation × radius; the
   material is emission mixed with dithered transparency, brighter at grazing edges, mottled by noise. The needle is a
   brushed-steel cannula (metallic Principled BSDF, roughness 0.14–0.28 streaked by a stretched noise), lit by a
   studio HDRI that camera rays do not see (Light Path → Is Camera Ray) and two softboxes; raytraced reflections pick
   up the glowing granules. In the back bands the needle is a holdout.
4. **`optics.py`** — 8 processes: scales the back bands up, blurs each by its defocus (σ 0, 3, 7, 12 px at 1920
   wide), keeps the blur off the needle's silhouette (a fixed rectangle, since the camera tracks the needle; from
   `layout.json`), sums the bands in linear light (fluorescence adds), adds a faint glow and a light Poisson/read grain
   drawn afresh each frame.
5. **`encode.py`** — ffmpeg: VP9 `.webm` (the `<source>` the page prefers), H.264 `.mp4` fallback, and
   `hero-poster.jpg` (first frame) so the `<video>` poster no longer fetches a random picsum.photos image; scaled to
   1600 wide, since the page shows it at 20 % opacity.

## Decisions

- **Camera follows the needle**: distance behind the needle stands in for time (fresh ink at the tip, cells spread and
  contracting at the left edge), and the loop closes by construction.
- **No scripted bath behaviour.** Earlier drafts held each granule on a slipping "yield tether", jiggled granules near
  the needle and pushed bath granules out of a box behind the tip so the ink had room. All three are gone: the yield
  stress, the flow round the needle and the re-jamming come from the jammed pack's contacts, the bath is held by a
  no-slip container (a frictionless floor let the needle slowly shove the whole bath forward, which also broke the
  loop), and the ink makes its own room by being extruded through the bore.
- **Squash from contacts, volume-preserving.** Soft-sphere overlaps are the model's stand-in for flattened contacts;
  turning them into an incompressible shape change is what a hydrogel granule does and is what makes granules visibly
  flatten against the needle.
- **Rough polyhedra are drawn around the spheres the DEM moves**: 16 random convex hulls inscribed in the unit sphere,
  so contacts stay right and the chunks look crushed and sieved.
- **Depth of field by bands, not Eevee's DOF.** Eevee's post-process DOF misreads dithered transparency, and jittered
  DOF needed hundreds of samples for a smooth 20 px blur; four bands with sliding weights and a Gaussian per band are
  smooth, cheap (the back bands render at half size), and tunable without re-rendering.
- **Lights only on the needle.** The gel is emission + transparency (no lighting, no sorting of thousands of
  translucent instances); the needle reflects an environment the camera does not see.
- **The section/confocal drafts were dropped at Alex's request** (the cut-open needle and the confocal axial smear);
  the fluorescent dyes and polyhedral granules stayed.
- **Cell processes run on a compressed clock** (seconds for what takes days) — the hero is a schematic, not a
  measurement; the README says so.
- **The old video file stays in `Videos/`** (it is already live and in `deploy/manifest.json`); only the references
  move. It can be deleted with `deploy.py ftp --delete` later.
- **The hero's CSS is unchanged** (20 % opacity, the same scrim). Mobile and reduced-motion still skip the video.
