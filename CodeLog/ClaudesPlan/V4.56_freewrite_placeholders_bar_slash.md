# V4.56 — Freewrite: placeholders, from a bar of its own or a slash

## Why
Gabe, 19 Nov: "Additionally, you can add placeholder kinds in the freewrite via a text editor bar just for it, and shortcuts such as /kind or /structure, and the accompanying format of it (e.g., /result creates a placeholder empty result bubble, and within that bubble, you can type /html, which indicates that in this freewrite location, the user wants the result to be an HTML file. Any /kind or /structure creates a new component into the freewrite. Dragging it to another component (same behavior as described earlier) links it to it."

## What changed
- **A placeholder is written in the words:** `⟦Result · HTML: stiffness map⟧`. Its pieces are a kind, a structure or a format (`FREEW.FMT`: HTML, Image, Table, Plot, PDF, Slides, Website, Equation), then its words after the colon. The freewrite stays plain text, so Undo, saving and the voice keep working as before.
- **`FREEW.convert`:**
  - reads the words around a placeholder as if it were not there;
  - makes each placeholder a component of its own (key `ph:<n>`): a kind's, a structure's, or for a Result, a Result to add (`p.format`);
  - an empty placeholder is drawn dashed ("… to write").
  The bubble's name and brackets are tinted inside it. Its label is clickable (V4.54) and can be dragged onto another component to link them.
- **The slash:** `/` and letters, after a space or inside a placeholder, open a menu at the caret (↑ ↓, Enter or Tab, Esc).
  - **Outside a placeholder:** kinds (`/result` is an empty Result bubble, the caret inside it), structures (`/barrier`), and a Result with its format (`/html` → `⟦Result · HTML: ⟧`).
  - **Inside one:** its format first (`/html` in a Result), then its kind. A structure is offered only on a placeholder that is not a Result, which has words of its own to mark.
  - "and/or" and letters that nothing starts with open nothing. Esc closes the menu and leaves the words.
- **The bar** above the page offers the same with the pointer: Placeholder ▾ · Structure ▾ · Format ▾.
  - Structure ▾ in a Result, or Format ▾ in another placeholder, puts a new placeholder after it.
  - Settings → Freewrite → "A bar of placeholders above the page" (on by default) switches it; the preview shows it.
- **A kind or structure chosen for a placeholder** (its label's popup, or Change ▾) is written back into its words (`FREEW.retoken`), so the words stay what it is.
- **Apply:**
  - a Result to add keeps its format (`b.fwfmt`): in the note it says "Add the result here: an HTML file…", its ＋ HTML opens that insert directly, and Other… opens the rest;
  - an empty structure placeholder makes a component named after the structure.
