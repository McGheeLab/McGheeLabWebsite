# V4.64 — Scientia Calendar: focus hours and focus sessions (full screen, activity counted double)

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "Each day, you can allocate focus hours, Activating focus mode on the lab notebook, etc... The focus mode puts the notebook automatically in full screen and trying to close that maximized view asks you a confirmation with a warning sign, saying that you will disturb your focus time, invalidating that focus session. You then have a 5 minute timer to get back into focus mode with no penalty. As an incentive, any activity during focus mode is doubled. Make sure that it is explicitly mentioned in settings and before starting it." And later: "You didn't build the focus feature I asked you to include in the calendar."

## What changed
- **Focus hours.** Focus is an event type (`kind: 'focus'`, gold).
  - **Plan focus** in the bar, or P: a click on the calendar places an hour of focus there.
  - A focus block is warm gold, with a slow sheen and a 5-second breathing glow. Under Still or reduced motion it does not move.
  - The day's head says how many hours of focus it has.
- **Starting.** Focus in the bar (F), a click on a gold block, or Start focus in its card, opens a sheet that states the rules before it starts (`CAL.focusSheet`):
  - the notebook goes full screen;
  - your activity counts double (2×) while in focus;
  - leaving asks first;
  - after 5 minutes away the session ends and its activity no longer counts double; your work is always kept.

  With no block now it offers Focus now for 25 · 50 · 90 minutes (Settings: which first) and makes the block.
- **A session** (`ev.fs`: `{day, state live|away|done|invalid, segs [[from, to]], leftAt}`) goes full screen and locks Esc where the browser allows (Keyboard Lock).
  - A capsule at the top shows Focus, the time left, 2×, and Leave… (`CAL.fxPaint`, ticking each second, `CAL.fxTick`).
  - The block shows "In focus · 2×", and afterwards "Done · 50 min counted 2×" or "Ended away · not 2×".
- **Leaving asks first.** Esc in full screen, or Leave…, opens "Leave focus?" with a warning sign: "This disturbs your focus time…" (`CAL.confirmLeave`), with Stay in focus or Leave.
  - Leaving, or leaving full screen any other way (`fullscreenchange`), starts the 5 minutes (`CAL.away`). The capsule counts them down with Back to focus, which returns with no penalty (`CAL.back`), and End it.
  - After 5 minutes the session ends as given up (`invalid`).
  - A session left running when the page reloads counts as away.
- **The 2×.** `PFKIT.events`, the Portfolio's activity, is wrapped: a moment that scored inside the minutes in focus of a session not given up scores twice (`CAL.focusWins`, `CAL.inFocus`).
  - What is made while away is not doubled.
  - A session given up loses its doubling, its earlier minutes too.
  - A session that runs to its end keeps it ("Focus done").
- **Settings → Calendar:** Focus sessions (on/off) and Focus now's length. The feature's page states the rules, and so does the preview, which shows a focus block. The activity panel's "How it is counted" says it too.
