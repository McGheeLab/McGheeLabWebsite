# V4.31 — Read beside: Fit, zoom and 100%; full screen from where it is, and back; in a note and back

## Goal
Gabe (18 Nov 2026):

> When you maximize a PDF or HTML from the beside view and close it, it goes back in the beside view rather than fully closing it from the tab set.

> Read beside for HTMLs, PDFs, etc… gains a Fit feature along with manual zooms and resetting to 100%. It also gains the option to go full screen from where it is at, while being able to go back and forth between beside, and in the note based on the note selected for where it is related/mentioned.

## Approach
A new module, `scientia/src/13zzzb-rview.js` (`RVIEW`), wraps the reader beside a note (`13zzb` `RDOCK`) from outside; `scientia/src/34-rview.css` styles it. The one hook in `13zzb-reader.js`: `RDOCK.rel(p)`, the share of the width a pane's PDF is drawn at, read through a getter in `RDOCK.draw` when the pages are laid out (`PDFW.full` reads `pane.zoom` in its animation frame). By default it returns `p.zoom`, so the reader is unchanged without `RVIEW`.

- **The zoom becomes the page's own size.** Before, `p.zoom` was a share of the pane's width (1 = the page as wide as the pane), so a zoom changed size whenever the pane did — full screen made a 115% page 1212 px wide in a 1085 px column. Now 1 = 100% = a PDF page as wide as it is in points, the way the lab's paper reader (`rm/js/pdf-viewer.js`, scale 1) counts it. `RVIEW.rel(p)` turns it back into the share `PDFW.full` draws at: `zoom × page width ÷ (stage width − 16)`. The page width is read once from page 1 (`RVIEW.loadPw`, cached per file in `RVIEW.pw`); a PDF at a zoom waits for it before drawing (a fitted one does not need it).
- **Fit.** `p.fit` (kept per tab in `RDOCK.S_.fit`, beside `S.zoom`): drawn at a share of 1, so the page fills the stage. The stage keeps room for its scrollbar (`scrollbar-gutter: stable`), so the fitted page is exactly as wide as what shows. A fitted pane fits again when its width changes — the window, ⇄, ◫, full screen (`resize`, which `RDOCK.layout` also sends; `RVIEW.resized` redraws once the width has settled) — and a zoom by hand ends it. The bar shows the fitted zoom (the pane width ÷ the page width).
- **The bar.** `RVIEW.dress` (a wrap of `RDOCK.build`): − % + Fit — the % (`p.zl`) goes back to 100% when clicked; a step from a fitted zoom goes to the next size up or down (`RVIEW.step`, on `RDOCK.ZOOMS`, now .5 to 3).
- **HTML pages and saved websites** get the same group. The frame is scaled (`transform: scale(z)`, its size `100 / z %`, its box clipped to the pane, `RVIEW.hz`); Fit is 100%, or smaller when the page is wider than the pane: each page's copy carries a small script (`RVIEW.SCRIPT`, after 13zze's and 13zzm's, through `QUOTE.more`) that says it has loaded (`rv-hi`); a page in a pane is then asked for its scroll and width (`rv-ask` → `rv-size`), and a page can be scrolled (`rv-to`). A card's page stays quiet (asked nothing): an earlier version that reported from every page set the canvas cards' `SLIM` observer off ("ResizeObserver loop") in `sync9_rdr`.
- **A tab opened for the first time** opens as Settings says: Fit (default), the last zoom used (`_ls rview:last`), or 100% (`RVIEW.start`). Zooms kept before V4.31 were shares of the width; they are let go once (`S.zv = 2`, a wrap of `RDOCK.load`).
- **Full screen from where it is.** Today's ⤢ kept the page and the place (`RDOCK.where`) but not the size; with the zoom as the page's own size it keeps that too, and Fit fits the full-screen column. Coming back is the same.
- **Maximise, then Close → back beside.** In full screen, the pane's "Close ✕" called `RDOCK.closeTab` and the tab left the set. A capture listener on it now brings the pane back beside (`RDOCK.full`) and its tooltip says so; Esc and ◧ did already. Settings can make Close close the tab, as before.
- **In a note and back.** The arrow into a box on each pane's bar (`RVIEW.noteMenu`, `ctxMenu`): "Show it in a note" — its own card first, then the other notes the same paper is embedded in (`RDX.embedsOf`), and, apart, the notes that mention it (`RDX.citedIn`: a /cite, a bridge) where the reader stays (`App.jump`). Picking a card (`RVIEW.toNote`): where it came from is remembered (`RVIEW.remember`, `_ls rview:came`: pane, side, split, full screen, zoom, Fit, page and place, a page's scroll); the tab leaves the reader (`RDOCK.closeTab`); the note goes to the card, lit (`RDOCK.follow`); the card opens at the page you were on (`b.cur`, and `RVIEW.cardAt` for a viewer kept from before). The card shows "◧ Back beside" (`RVIEW.chip`, wraps of `WIDGETS.pdf.body` / `WIDGETS.html.body`); one click (`RVIEW.back`) opens it in the pane and side it came from (the split restored if it had one), at its zoom and place, in full screen if it was.
- **Settings → Reading & sources → Fit, zoom and full screen beside a note** (`rview`, in Simple too, registered from the module and moved after the other reading features, `RVIEW.late`): "A paper or page opens at" (Fit / The last zoom you used / 100%), "Close, in full screen" (Brings it back beside / Closes the tab); a live preview (a pane's bar with − % + Fit and a page that fits or keeps its size — try it). Off: no Fit, no zoom on pages, no In a note, no chip; a PDF's zoom is a share of the width as before (`RVIEW.rel`). Close in full screen follows its setting either way.

## Decisions where the request was silent
- **100%** is the page's own width in points (the lab's paper reader's scale 1), not the screen's physical size.
- **"In the note"** shows the file in its card on the canvas, not maximised: the file leaves the reader so it is drawn once. A note that only mentions the paper has no card for it, so picking one goes there and keeps the paper beside — the same as Cited in.
- **The chip on the card** stays until it is used, the file is opened beside another way, or × says "keep it here"; at most 20 are remembered.
- The tune "Close, in full screen" applies with the switch off too: Close coming back beside is the fix Gabe asked for, not a tool.
- HTML pages have no Ctrl + scroll zoom (the wheel goes to the page); − + do it.

## Testing
`rb_t31` at 1400 px with real pointer events, on the lab desktop build and on the build without the lab layer (a 6-page text PDF of 612 × 792 pt pages, a long HTML page, a 1200 px wide one):
- the bar; opened fitted; the fitted zoom = the pane width ÷ 612 and the page as wide as the stage;
- + (the next size up, the page `zoom × 612` px), the % (100%, 612 px), −; Fit again; the window narrower (still fitted); a zoom by hand keeps its size when the window widens;
- ⤢ at page 3, 40% down: the same page, place and size; ◧ back the same; fitted, full screen fits the column;
- Close ✕ in full screen: back beside, the tab still in the set, the same page; Esc; the setting "Closes the tab";
- an HTML page: − % + Fit, 115% scaled from the pane's left edge; full screen and back keep the paragraph at the top; the 1200 px page fitted to the pane ÷ 1200 with no sideways scroll inside;
- In a note: the menu (its card first; "Mentioned in" with the citing note); picked: out of the reader, the card at page 4, "◧ Back beside" under the pointer; clicked: back at the zoom and page; a mention: the note opens, the paper stays;
- the preview, the Features board order, off / on, the feature's page; FEAT.audit() empty, no sideways scroll, no errors.
