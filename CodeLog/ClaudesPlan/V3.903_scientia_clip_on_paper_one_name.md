# V3.903 — Scientia clips: the box drawn at the pointer, clips lie on the paper, One name for multi-letter terms

## Goal

Three fixes Alex asked for after using V3.901's clip tool (2026-10-24):

1. "when i try to snip an equation or a figure manually, the region is not drawing at the mouse, but the region is
   selecting the right part of the pdf."
2. "i just snipped an equation and it was MESF = C N the problem is the MESF is supposed to be a single variable name …
   there is no way for you to automatically predict this, so the user needs an override feature to combine part of an
   equation into one variable."
3. "When i snip out an equation or figure, i want that figure to appear on top of the paper, the user will then drag it
   where they want it."

## Causes and approach

### 1. The drawn box (13zza `CLIP.drag` / `CLIP.ask`)

The canvas zoom is a CSS `scale()` on the world (`10-paper.js` `apply()`). The box was measured in screen pixels and drawn
with those numbers as the layer's own pixels, which the zoom scales again. At 60% the box appeared at 0.6× the distance
from the page corner, at 0.6× the size. The region was right because it was computed as a fraction of the layer's screen
rectangle. The harness drew at zoom 1, where the two agree.

Fix: `k = L.offsetWidth / q.width` (layer px per screen px). The marquee, the kept dashed box and the Picture / Equation
bar are drawn in `box × k`. The region is unchanged.

### 2. One name (13h `MATH.editor`, Gabe's)

His parser (13n `TEX.parse`) already reads `\mathrm{…}` / `\text{…}` / `\operatorname{…}` as one term. He even has a
shortcut (Alt+T → `\text{}`) that nobody would find. Nothing can tell `MESF` from `M·E·S·F` on its own.

Fix: a **One name** button in the editor's toolbar (every equation editor: capture, edit, new).
- Select the letters, then press it: the selection becomes `\mathrm{MESF}`, and the terms legend shows MESF, C and N.
- It works on a selection in the LaTeX box too.
- It refuses anything but a name (letters, digits, Greek letters), with a line saying so. The field selects the whole
  equation when it opens, and wrapping that would make `MESF=CN` one name. The harness caught this.
- After a read, the capture note says to use it.

### 3. Clips lie on the paper (13zza `CLIP.spot` / `CLIP.lift`, 12-doc `settle`)

`spot(src, n, r, w)` picks the spot in this order:
1. Where the region is on the card, when the card shows that page and that point is in view (+14, lifted off it).
2. Over the part of the card in view.
3. The middle of the note in view, when the paper is read beside the note or maximised with the card scrolled away.

Gabe's canvas keeps boxes apart (`settle()` pushes an overlapping box below the one above it; only ink is exempt), so a
clip put on the card would be shoved under it at once. A clip therefore carries `float = {x, y}`, the spot it was put.
`settle()` leaves alone a box still at its `float` spot (one line beside the ink exemption, in Gabe's file). Once dragged
anywhere else it is an ordinary box. While floating it draws above the card with a lifted shadow (`.box.clip-float`,
z-index 5). A window `pointerup` drops the class as soon as it is moved.

Clips are never `auto` (`autoLayout` only marks boxes with no position), and `changed()` redraws on the next tick, so
`lift()` right after `FIG.create` / `EQ.create` is safe.

be pushed below the card by `settle()`.
