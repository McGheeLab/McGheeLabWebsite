# V3.921 — PLOS: the first-page sidebar, masthead and running head/foot

**Asked (Alex, 2026-10-26):** "why is the PLOS style have all pages with the text shifted over, typically I see that
only the first two pages of PLOS have the shift, and that area is taken up by some information and stuff"

## What a published PLOS page is (measured)

25 PLOS ONE / Biology / Computational Biology PDFs (2015–2024, Alex's Paperpile) and the Paper Lab ground truth:

- The body is set at x = 200 bp on **every** page (164 bp left margin, 36 bp right); page-wide figures and tables
  reach into the margin. The look already had this.
- Pages 1–2 fill the margin with the article-information sidebar: "OPEN ACCESS", Citation, Editor, Received,
  Accepted, Published, Copyright, Data Availability Statement, Funding, Competing interests. Helvetica Condensed
  8 / 11 bp, 6 bp between items, x 36–184. On page 1 it is bottom-aligned (last baseline 710.4) and starts no higher
  than 355.3; what does not fit continues at the top of page 2 (first baseline 84.7).
- Masthead "PLOS ONE" at the top left of page 1 (20.6 bp, baseline 52.3); "RESEARCH ARTICLE" above the title
  (8 bp, baseline 103); the title's first baseline at 127–131 (ours was at 93).
- Pages 2+: the journal name (11 bp, baseline 48.4) at the left, the short title right-aligned (8 bp, 47.9).
- Every page: "PLOS ONE | doi  date" at the foot left and "n / N" at the right (8 bp, baseline 757.7).

So the margin is PLOS's on every page; what we were missing is everything PLOS prints in it and around the page.

## Approach

One description in the look (`look.sidebar`, `look.furniture`, `title.before`), two renderers, as for every look:

- **Sidebar content** (`PaperLook.sidebarSplit`, `PaperLook.sidebarItems`): Citation and Copyright are written
  from the paper (authors as "Surname Initials", six then "et al.", the year, the title, the journal); Data
  Availability, Funding and Competing interests are taken OUT of the body — a section with that name (or special),
  or a back-matter paragraph that opens with that bold run-in label ("**Funding.** …"). A statement the paper does
  not have yet says to add it before submission. Editor / dates are the journal's and are left out of a draft.
- **PDF**: the sidebar is one `\vbox` (first baseline 11 bp below its top, no penalties, no interword shrink, fil
  ragged right — so its lines are the browser's greedy lines), `\vsplit` to the page-1 span; both parts and the
  furniture are drawn by `shipout/background` at absolute positions, so the body flow is untouched.
- **Page view**: the same prose rendered in the same face; split at the last line whose baseline fits the same span;
  page 1 shows the top part bottom-aligned, page 2 the rest (clipped copies). Furniture is absolutely positioned on
  each sheet with the same baselines. The grey "n / ≈N" indicator is hidden when the look prints its own folio.
- Journal name: the paper's target journal when it is a PLOS title, else "PLOS ONE". Short title: the title, or the
  part before its colon, or the first ~55 characters.
- New face: TeX Gyre Heros Condensed (GUST licence, from the Tectonic bundle), vendored in `rm/vendor/fonts/`.

## Found on the way (the PLOS ground truth and the case study, page view vs PDF)

- The PDF indented the first abstract line under a heading label; the view did not → flush in both.
- `\lineskiplimit=-\maxdimen` across the whole front let JACS's grey abstract box ride up over the affiliations (negative interline glue before a tall box) → title, author and affiliation lines only.
- The view's references for PLOS were the generic numeric format; plos2015.bst's Vancouver lines are shorter → `_formatPlos`.
- The packer had no shrink: a paragraph 0.46 px over moved whole to the next page. Now TeX's choice: shrink when the glue covers it and the badness is lower than stretching the shorter page.
- Table cells were typeset one by one with widths read in between (a typeset cell reflows the table).

## Results

- PLOS case study: page 1 baselines within 0.4 bp of the PDF (furniture exact); sidebar 21 lines on page 1 in both; with long statements it continues on page 2 in both; 26 / 26 pages.
- PLOS ground truth: page starts 4/13 → 9/13 (sync.py), line ends 0.871 unchanged, 13 / 13 pages.
- Regression (8 papers): page counts unchanged; NSF 16/16 page starts; bioRxiv 4 → 5, Nature Comms 1 → 2; JACS 3/11 and line ends unchanged.
- ACS / IEEE case studies: 13/13, 15/15 as in V3.920.

Residual: the view pads every table cell by \tabcolsep and ignores `@{}`, then fits an overfull tabular into the text width — its p{} columns come out ~5 px narrower than TeX's (which lets the table stick out).
