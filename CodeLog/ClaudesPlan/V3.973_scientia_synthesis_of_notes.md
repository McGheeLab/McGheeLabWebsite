# V3.973 — Scientia: a Synthesis of the notes you choose, outside an experiment

**Date:** 2026-11-10 · **Branch:** `scientia/web-capture` (after V3.971, V3.972) · **Gabe:** "I want to be able to create a synthesis
note outside of an experiment. In doing so, I can add a list of notes that get synthesized as a synthesis note for an experiment
would. For example, if there is a takeaway from a couple of lines inside a giant text component, then only that takeaway gets
synthesized."

## Approach

The Synthesis (V3.967) gathered from the experiment's chain. Now its notes are the chain's other notes **and** `d.synthOf`, a list
kept on the note, so a Synthesis can stand alone or take extra notes. Gathering is unchanged: it references **structures** (the
claims, evidence, results, takeaways, barriers and resolutions marked in the notes) with `M.pullInto({ itemId })`, so a takeaway
marked in two lines of a long component arrives as that takeaway alone.

- **Making one:** `/synthesis`, or "A synthesis of this note and others…" in any note's ⋯ menu. The note on screen comes first;
  the picker adds notes until Cancel. The new note is a Synthesis (the `reflection` profile) in the first note's project, with the
  Synthesis template, gathered and opened.
- **± Notes** lists the notes; + Add notes… adds and gathers; a click takes one out (what it gave stays).
- **Found while testing:** in the lab build the lab's pills hide a note's dock (`lab-mode.js`: `.canvas-host.lab-pilled > .dock`),
  so V3.967's ⟲ Gather and ☰ The rest were out of reach there. The Synthesis note's ⋯ menu now carries ± Notes, ⟲ Gather and
  ☰ The rest in both builds.
