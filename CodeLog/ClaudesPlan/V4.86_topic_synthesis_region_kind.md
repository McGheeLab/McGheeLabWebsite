# V4.86 — Scientia: region kinds, for now one, the Topic synthesis, with its compendium

## Why
Gabe, 19 Nov: "You may now label a region as a 'region kind', for now only one, being topic synthesis. Topic syntheses get logged into their own gallery in Atlas." On 20 Nov the Atlas's galleries moved to the Compendiums (V4.85), and he asked again: "the Topic synthesis region kind and its Atlas gallery".

## What changed
- **A region's kind** (`14fe-topics.js`, `TOPIC`): a region's ⋯ menu has **Mark as a topic synthesis** (and, once marked, *No longer a topic synthesis*).
  - It sets `r.kind = 'topic'` and `r.kindAt` on the note's regions, so it syncs like the rest. The region's name is the topic.
  - The menu's rows come from a hook, `REGION.kindRows`, so another kind is one more entry in `TOPIC.KINDS` and a row.
- **In the note:** the region's tab carries a "Topic synthesis" chip, and the region a faint ruled band of its colour (`REGION.render` wrapped).
- **Compendiums → Topic syntheses**, after Protocols:
  - every topic synthesis in the notebook, newest first, as a card with the topic, its note's path, how many components, a few of their words, and a small map of how they sit;
  - a search over the topics and what they hold;
  - **Open** goes to the region in its note, centred at 50%. `App.openComponent` now takes a region id, and the pending focus lands on a region.
  - It also has a card in the gallery.
- **Settings → Canvas & notes → Topic syntheses** (`topics`): the switch and *Cards show a map of the region*, with a live preview. Off: the menu row, the chip and the compendium go; regions keep their kind.
