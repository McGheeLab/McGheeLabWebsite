# V4.44 — Look and motion: the rest of the boards

## Goal
Gabe (19 Nov 2026), on the design canvas: "Don't change the Portfolio, it looks good as it is. The rest is approved"

Build what the canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/CymaRzoNRZi1pLMnEtnrCk) still showed unbuilt, except the Portfolio. That board (the project list docking into a capsule) is marked "Not built — Gabe: keep the Portfolio as it is".

## Approach
`20d-look.js` wraps existing code (no module is edited); `36-look.css` adds the rest. All of it sits under `body:not(.f-off-look)`, and its times come from the CSS, so the look off, Still and reduced motion stop it.

| Board | How |
|---|---|
| Highlights grouped by page on a thread | page markers added beside the highlights (not around them) in every reader's panel (13zz `.mx-side`, 17b `.rd-side`). The panel's filters and search, the count badge and the capsule's per-colour counts keep working; a redraw of the panel redraws the markers, without looping. |
| Beside ↔ full-screen glide | ⤢, ◧ and Esc: the pane uncovers or clips to its new size. The PDF is laid out at its final size from the first frame, so its width does not animate. |
| Popups | rise out of their pane on ⧉; shrink into their reading set's tab on × (V4.41's `RPOP.back`) and on Maximize. With no tab on screen, they shrink onto the middle of the pane or the card. |
| The Quick cast fan | the pills' wheels by CSS only: their buttons spring out one after another (at most 320 ms in all) and close quickly. `lab-mode.js` / `vendor/lab-pills.js` untouched. |
| Freewrite's Convert | its components come in one after another. |
| The data tree | the files drop in when a folder opens. |
| Settings | the sidebar is a rounded panel. |

**Three fixes to V4.42**, found by the Freewrite and popup suites. With the look off the same base passed them all.
- **Menus:** the 0.26 s pop with an overshoot moved the rows under a quick click, so the wrong row was picked (`fw_t33`). Now 98 % → 100 % in 0.16 s, with no overshoot.
- **The Freewrite page** slid up from below and for half a second didn't cover the app. Now it is in place from the first frame, and its bar, text and preview rise into it.
- **The header at phone width:** 28 px icons pushed the reader button off a 400 px screen (`fw_t34`). Back to 24 px below 760 px.
- **Popup corners** are 12 px, like cards.
