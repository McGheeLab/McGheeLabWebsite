> and [rm/js/training-paper-results.js](../../rm/js/training-paper-results.js).
> Module flipped to `available: true`.

## Goal

Teach a student to write Results sections that are
figure-anchored, neutral in tone, and don't sneak interpretation into
the wrong section. The Results is where Reporter voice lives.

## Format

Lecture-style. Two interactive widgets:

1. **Figure-prose alignment checker** — paste a draft Results
   paragraph; tool flags sentences that don't refer to a figure panel
   (most should). Surfaces the "I'm interpreting without showing"
   failure mode.
2. **Reporter-vs-Advocate toggle** — same paragraph in two voices.
   The Reporter version reports the observation; the Advocate version
   interprets it. The student learns to keep the latter for Discussion.

## Sections (8)

1. The Results' job — report what was found, with figure panels as
   anchors.
2. One subsection per figure; one paragraph per panel (roughly).
3. The Results paragraph skeleton: setup → observation → conclusion
   from this figure alone.
4. Voice: Reporter (per V3.182). No "interestingly," "importantly,"
   "remarkably."
5. Statistics inline (n, test, p-value, error bars defined).
6. The "interpretation creep" trap.
7. Negative results — how to write them without apologizing.
8. Capstone — paste a draft, run the alignment checker.
