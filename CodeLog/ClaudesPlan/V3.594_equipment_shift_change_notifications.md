# V3.594 — Equipment shift-change notifications + in-app feed

Second release of the smart equipment schedule-change notification
program (R1 was V3.588 criticality; = FuturePlan 08 Phase 3).

> **Numbering note:** planned as V3.589, shipped as V3.594 — parallel
> sessions claimed 589–593 while this was being built. A concurrent
> session also stamped "V3.588" on an unrelated presence-gate change in
> `functions/helpers/notify.js`; that is their in-flight work, left
> untouched here.

## Goal

Make an equipment handoff communicate itself. Two students sharing a
microscope back-to-back should each know what the other is doing, without
either of them having to ask — and without a stream of notifications
nobody reads.

Three things ship:

1. **Adjacency ("shift change") notifications.** When a booking lands
   right after yours, you're told to be off on time. When the booking
   after yours moves or cancels, you're told you gained slack. When the
   booking *before* yours shifts, you're told whether you can set up
   early or should expect a tight handoff.
2. **Co-operator notification.** Being named on someone's booking now
   tells you.
3. **🔔 Notifications section in My Day** — the catch-up surface for
   anything you missed.

## Problem

- **Cancellations notified nobody.** `functions/equipment.js` handled
  approval requests, displacement, and confirmation — and ignored
  `→ cancelled` entirely. The single most useful fact on a shared
  instrument ("the slot you were waiting for just opened") never left
  the database.
- **No concept of adjacency existed anywhere.** Nothing in the codebase
  compared two bookings' times to each other.
- **Push is write-only.** Miss the buzz and the information is gone.
  "The booking after yours was cancelled" leaves no trace on the
  calendar afterwards — it is not re-derivable, so there was nothing to
  come back to. (This got sharper mid-build: a concurrent session added
  presence-based push suppression, so a push can now be withheld
  entirely when you're looking at another surface.)

## Approach

### The engine is pure, and it is quiet

`functions/helpers/equipment-adjacency.js` has no Firestore and no FCM
in it — it is a function of `(before, after, the day's bookings,
config)`. That is what makes the decision matrix testable, and it is
tested: `functions/test/equipment-adjacency.test.js` runs 46 fixtures
with plain node, no emulator.

**Most of those fixtures assert that nothing is sent.** Over-notifying
would make students mute the category, which is strictly worse than not
building this. So criticality (V3.588's Soft/Firm/Hard) gates everything:

- **Soft** exerts no pressure and receives no nudges. A day of Soft
  bookings produces **zero** pushes.
- **Firm** — both sides of a handoff hear about it.
- **Hard** (and `maintenance`) radiates the most signal, and is the only
  level that earns the speculative "someone booked right *before* you"
  warning.

Also silent, always: your own back-to-back bookings, the actor's own
edit, maintenance blocks being offered "slack", gaps beyond the
threshold, different devices, different dates.

### Decision matrix

A = upstream (ends first), B = downstream. Adjacent when
`0 ≤ B.start − A.end ≤ adjacencyGapMin`.

| Event | Recipient | Fires when |
|---|---|---|
| B created after A | A's owner | B is Firm/Hard |
| B created before A | A's owner | B is **Hard only** |
| B cancelled/displaced | A's owner | B was Firm/Hard (relieve pressure we applied) |
| A cancelled/displaced | B's owner | B is Firm/Hard (you can set up early) |
| B moved earlier / newly adjacent | A's owner | B is Firm/Hard |
| B moved away | A's owner | B was Firm/Hard (gained slack) |
| A now ends later / newly adjacent | B's owner | B is Firm/Hard |
| A moved away | B's owner | B is Firm/Hard (setup room) |

Two subtleties worth keeping:

- **The move cases are independent ifs, not if/else.** A booking sliding
  from between one pair to between another means the old neighbor lost
  pressure *and* the new one gained it. An early draft used `else if`
  and silently dropped the old neighbor — caught by the cross-date
  fixture.
- **Moving later but still inside the window stays silent.** Gaining
  someone 20 minutes on a handoff that is still tight is not worth a
  buzz, and they were already told once.

### Anti-spam: three layers

1. **Change classification** — `classifyChange()` returns `none` for a
   notes-only edit or an approval flip, so the engine is never reached.
2. **Deterministic FCM tags** keyed to the **recipient's** booking +
   direction (`equip-adj-{targetId}-{up|down}`), so churn on the other
   side replaces one notice instead of stacking.
3. **Suppression stamp** `notifMeta.adj.{up|down}` written to the
   recipient's booking (Admin SDK). Inside `adjacencySuppressMin`
   (default 3 min) a repeat is dropped — five edits in two minutes buzz
   once. Accepted trade-off: the final edit of a burst may go
   unannounced, with slightly stale content standing.

`notifMeta` is a **server-only field** — absent from every client
`hasOnly()` whitelist in `firestore.rules`. Since those whitelists only
constrain keys the client actually writes, client edits keep working
untouched.

### The in-app feed

`recordFeedItems()` / `notifyUsers()` in `functions/helpers/notify.js`:
`notifyUsers` = send the push **and** mirror it to
`userData/{uid}/notifications`. Every equipment sender now uses it.

- **Independent of push prefs.** A student who muted equipment push can
  still read what happened. That is the entire point of a fallback.
- **Tag collapse mirrors FCM** — an existing *unread* item with the same
  tag is deleted before the new one is written, so a burst leaves one
  current notice. Read items are left as a record.
- **Never breaks the push path**: feed writes are individually
  try/caught.

**No rules change.** The existing `userData/{userId}/{document=**}`
wildcard already grants the owner read+write and denies cross-user
access. A narrower block could not take permission away (in Firestore
any matching allow wins), so none was added — the boundary that matters
(nobody writes into *another* user's feed) is already enforced. A user
can forge junk in their own feed; harmless. Documented inline in
`firestore.rules`.

### My Day surface

Renders directly under the horizon tabs on **all three views** — "be off
the scope by 12" is not day-specific, and burying it under the timeline
would defeat the purpose.

- Empty feed → renders **nothing**.
- All read → one collapsed header line.
- Anything unread → force-opened, amber, with a count badge.
- Click an item = mark read + follow its url; ✕ dismisses; "Mark all
  read" appears past one unread.
- Collapsing implies "I've seen these" (otherwise unread would spring it
  back open on the next render).

## Data model changes

| Path | What | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| `equipmentBookings.notifMeta.adj.{up,down}` | suppression stamps | server-only; no client whitelist entry |
| `userData/{uid}/notifications/{id}` | feed items `{title, body, url, tag, appKey, kind, readAt, createdAt}` | existing userData wildcard (owner read/write) |
| `equipmentSettings/config.adjacencyGapMin` (30) | handoff threshold | existing `allow write: isAdmin()`, no field whitelist |
| `equipmentSettings/config.adjacencySuppressMin` (3) | repeat cooldown | same |

**No new indexes.** Neighbor lookup uses the existing
`(equipmentId, date)` composite; the feed read is a single-collection
`orderBy createdAt desc`.

## Out of scope

- Reminders (morning-of / T-30) and the weekly manager digest → next
  release.
- Notify-me watch windows → the release after.
- Multi-day bookings are **excluded from adjacency entirely**
  (`endDate > date`), so cross-midnight handoffs are never detected.
  Flagged in the module header; overnight runs are rare here today.
- Criticality still doesn't affect displacement, approval routing, or
  conflict resolution — notification-only.
- Server-side quiet hours remain unimplemented (pre-existing gap).

3. FTP (My Day feed + admin panel).

No rules deploy required (the rules edit is a comment; it can ride the
next functional rules deploy). No index deploy.

## Rollback

Redeploy the previous revision of the two functions — `notifMeta` and
any written feed items are inert to the old code, and the old client
simply never renders the feed. FTP revert + sw bump for the client half.
Nothing to clean up; feed items age out of the 7-day window on their own.

## Verification

1. **Green tests** before deploying.
2. **The core case, two accounts:** A books 9–12 Soft. B books 12–2 as
   "Needed on time" → **A gets one push** telling them to be off by 12,
   and it appears in A's My Day 🔔 section. Book B as Soft instead →
   **A gets nothing** (this assertion matters more than the positive one).
3. **Burst suppression:** edit B's times three times inside two minutes
   → A sees at most 1–2 notifications total, and **one** feed item, not
   three.
4. **Cancellation:** cancel B → A is told the time opened up. Previously
   this notified nobody at all.
5. **Upstream:** cancel A while B is Firm → B is told the slot before
   them freed up.
6. **Feed mechanics:** unread badge counts correctly; clicking an item
   navigates and clears its dot; ✕ removes it; collapsing the section
   marks all read; a fully-read feed shows one line; an empty feed shows
   nothing.
7. **Mute check:** Settings → Notifications → Equipment **off** → no
   push arrives, but the feed item still appears in My Day.
8. **Admin config:** set the gap threshold to 5, re-run step 2 with a
   20-minute gap → silence. Set it back to 30 → notifies.
