# V4.98 — Scientia: a component's title takes highlighted words, or a click to type

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "Highlighting a set of words and clicking title should make that the title of the component. Clicking on title with no highlight lets you type the title. The title is editable at any time."

A component's title is its label (`b.label`), the input in the box header. Until now a single click on it only started a drag (13zz2: the box moves by its title) and a double click opened it for typing; a read-only view and a component held by someone else keep it read-only.

## What changed (`12f-title.js`, `TITLE`)
- **Highlight + click the title**: the highlighted words become the title: whitespace folded to one line, citation marks and end quotes dropped, cut at a word at 40 / 80 / 140 letters (ending in "…"). One Undo group; a toast "Title: …" with an Undo button (Ctrl+Z does the same). The highlight is put away.
- **Click with nothing highlighted**: the caret goes into the title where the click was (measured on the zoomed box). Enter ends it (on into the text; on a folded component, which has no text to go on into, it just ends and saves), Esc puts the old title back, a click outside ends it. A typed title is one Undo.
- **Any time**: no hover or selection needed; a folded component too. Not in a read-only view (time travel) or on a component someone else holds (their input is `readOnly`).
- **A drag from the title** still moves the component (4 px decides, as 13zz2 did).
- **Event order**: the highlight vanishes when the title takes focus, so the words are read on pointerdown (capture) before focus moves; mouse and pen decide on release, a touch tap on its click.
- **Where the words come from**: the component's own text (a PDF's text layer too) or its HTML page (that page's script already reports its highlight, `13zw` `sis:'sel'`; the parent keeps the last one per frame). Settings tune: only that component, or (when it has none) anywhere in the note.
- **Settings → Canvas & notes → Component titles** (`comptitle`): tunes "Highlighted words are taken from" and "Longest title", live preview. Off: as before (double-click to type; a click only drags).
- The shortcuts list (V4.79) has the row "Highlight words, then click a component's title".
