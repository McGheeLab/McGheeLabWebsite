# V3.848 — The Huddle's week view is an accordion

**Date:** 2026-10-23
**Alex:** *"also in the huddle, the week view should only have one day open at a time, all other days
are just slivers of color with no text, when viewing i will open day by day and the day expands to
show the text for that day like an accordian menu i think."*

## Goal

In This Week → Week, one day is open: its column is wide and its plan blocks show owner, text and
time. The other six days are narrow slivers whose blocks are solid bars in the owner's colour with
nothing to read. Tapping a sliver (its header, an empty cell, or a bar) opens that day and closes the
one that was open, and the columns animate between the two states. Today is open by default.

## Approach

- **State** — `_openDay` (YYYY-MM-DD) in `huddle.js`. `resolveOpenDay(days)` keeps it when it is on
  screen, else today when today is in the week, else the Monday. Every plans snapshot re-renders the
  grid, so the choice has to live outside the DOM.
- **Grid** — `buildTimeGrid(..., openDate)`: with `openDate` the grid gets `hud-accordion` and
  `data-days`, and every header / cell / block carries `data-day` plus `hud-col-open` or
  `hud-col-closed`. Headers are `role="button"`, `tabindex="0"`, `aria-expanded`, and show the day
  as two spans (`hud-grid-dayshort`, `hud-grid-daynum`). Blocks carry `--hud-bc` (the owner colour)
  so the closed style can paint them solid. The Day view calls `buildTimeGrid` without `openDate`
  and is unchanged.
- **Tracks** — `accordionColumns(days, openDate)` writes the inline track list in terms of CSS
  variables: `var(--hud-tcol) minmax(0, var(--hud-closed|--hud-open)) × 7`. `huddle.css` sets
  `--hud-tcol: 60px; --hud-open: 16fr; --hud-closed: 1fr` and
  `transition: grid-template-columns .28s`. Both states are `minmax(0, Nfr)`, so the transition
  interpolates (Chrome 107+, Safari 16+, Firefox 66+; older browsers snap). At 1140 px of day
  columns a sliver is about 52 px and the open day about 830 px; on a 2560 px screen (the Huddle is
  page-wide since V3.846) the slivers grow with it but stay text-free.
- **Switching** — `setOpenDay(date)` edits the live grid: new track list, classes and
  `aria-expanded` swapped on every `[data-day]` element. No re-render, so the scroll position and
  the composer stay. `wireAccordion()` binds header click/Enter/Space and a **capture-phase** click
  on the grid that opens the day for any click inside a closed column and stops the event, so a
  sliver block never opens the plan popup and a closed cell never starts drag-to-create (the
  pointerdown handler also returns early for `hud-col-closed`). Dropping a dragged plan onto a
  sliver still works (the cells keep `data-drop-date`).
- **Closed look** — `.hud-accordion .hud-grid-block.hud-col-closed` hides its children and paints
  `var(--hud-bc)` solid at .85 opacity, no left border, 1 px margins; the lane maths (percent widths)
  still applies, so two overlapping plans are two bars. All-day blocks become a 6 px bar. Closed
  headers shrink their type; on a phone they show the day number only and the time column is 36 px
  (the old `36px repeat(7, 1fr) !important` phone rule now applies only to
  `.hud-time-grid:not(.hud-accordion)`).

## Verification

The harness boots the real `huddle.js` on the in-page stub Firestore (the V3.837 pattern, plus
`collection.add`) with four plans in the current week (two overlapping today, a timed and an all-day
plan on another day): one open day (today), 7 headers with `data-day`, open column ≥ 6× a sliver
(681 vs 43 px at 1280), open blocks show text, sliver blocks show none and are solid `rgb(...)` while
the open block is tinted `rgba(...)`, the all-day sliver is 6 px tall with `font-size: 0`; header tap,
sliver-block tap and closed-cell tap each open that day in place (same grid element, new track list,
`aria-expanded` follows); pointerdown on a closed cell starts no drag while the open day still drags
and the composer opens with the same day open; next week opens its Monday and This Week returns to
today; the Day view is not an accordion; Enter on a header opens it; no popups; no `<svg` in
innerText. 26/26, no console errors. Screenshot taken with Thursday open.

## Decisions

- Exactly one day is always open (tapping the open header does nothing) — "only have one day open
  at a time", and an all-slivers state would show no text at all.
- The accordion is the Week view's behaviour, not a setting. If anyone wants the old seven equal
  columns back, a Settings toggle can add `openDate = null`.
- Tracks are fr ratios rather than pixel slivers so the width change is a real transition; a pixel
  track and an fr track do not interpolate.
