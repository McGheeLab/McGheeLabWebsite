# V4.10 — The reader's side panel folds

## Goal
Gabe (16 Nov 2026): "The side tab showing notes in PDFs, HTMLs, or websites should be collapseable."

The panel of highlights and notes (its quotes, your notes under each, the search) shows in four places:
- beside a maximised PDF, HTML page or saved website (13zz `.mx-side`, 330 px, always open until now);
- beside a pane of the reader in full screen (13zzb, the same panel);
- as the overlay that Highlights opens beside a note (13zzb);
- in the Library's reader (17b `.rd-side`, 320 px, always open until now).

## Approach
- **A new module, `scientia/src/17g-sidefold.js` (`SIDEFOLD`), with `30-sidefold.css`.** Each panel's own code stays unchanged. The panel redraws its own children (`box.replaceChildren`, `drawSide`), so the fold cannot live inside it. Instead the panel is wrapped in a column (`.sf-col`) with a title strip ("Highlights and notes", ›). It is hooked from `MXHL.open` (both the maximised views and every reader pane) and from `ReaderView.render`.
- **Folding:**
  - › folds the column to a 30 px strip; its width glides over .24 s while the panel keeps its own width, so the panel is covered or uncovered, never squeezed.
  - The strip shows the panel's count written down it ("3 highlights · 1 note"). The count is read from the panel's heading by a `MutationObserver`, so it follows every redraw.
  - A click anywhere on the strip opens the panel again. It is caught at capture, so the ‹ under the pointer doesn't fold it a second time.
- **One state, per device:** `body.sf-fold`, kept in `_ls 'sideFold'`, shared by every reader. A file opened later finds the panel as you left it, following Gabe's "nothing opens uninvited". Settings can make it always open or always folded instead.
- **Beside a note**, the panel is already the overlay that Highlights opens. It gets the same strip, and › there closes the overlay (removes `rd-side-on`); the fold doesn't apply to it. In full screen (⤢) the panel is back in the flow and folds like the others.
- **Narrow windows (≤ 760 px)** have no panel, as before.
- **Fixed on the way:** a pane of the reader in full screen sat under the app's header (since V4.2 the reader's wrap is a stacking context at z-index 20, under the header's 30). Its bar (◧, Close) and now the strip were hidden. `.rd-wrap:has(full pane)` is lifted to 1200.
- **Settings → Reading & sources → Fold the side panel:**
  - the switch (off: no strip, and the panel always open, as before);
  - "When a file opens, the panel is" As you left it / Open / Folded;
  - a live preview you can fold and open, which folds the real panels too.

  It is in the Simple preset too.
