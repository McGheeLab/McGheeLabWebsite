# V3.969 — Scientia: the term lens and the note terms are nests too

**Date:** 2026-11-10 · **Branch:** `scientia/export-term-nests` (with V3.968) · **Gabe:** "The Atlas reshaping should apply to
terms and temporary terms."

## What it means

The Atlas became nests in 3D in V3.966. Two other maps of terms still drew dense networks:

- **The term lens** (Terms → a term, `#/lens/<id>`): a board of full boxes in lanes and day columns, panned by dragging.
- **A term in one note** (V3.963, "temporary" because it lives in one note): a frozen snapshot drawn with the Atlas's old
  2D force layout, which 14d had kept alive only for it.

Both now open as nests. The term is the outer sphere, because everything inside uses it. Its themes are inside; for a
note term fetched from the whole notebook the projects are. Each theme holds its notes, each note the components that use
the term as dots. The other terms, people and papers those components carry orbit the note they belong to.

## How

- **`14d-nest.js` becomes a map you can make anywhere.** `NEST.make(o)` returns a map with its own camera, nest, pick and
  hover; `NEST.mount(I, host, inset)` adds the canvas, crumbs and tools and wires the pointer. Every function takes the map
  (`NEST.draw(I)`, `NEST.flyTo(I, n, ms)` …). Options: `center` (the node the map is about: the outer sphere is it),
  `rootLabel`, `group(doc)` (the level between the sphere and the notes; `NEST.GROUP.project` / `.theme`), `groupWord`,
  `sel` (the Atlas page passes `ATLAS.st`), `lit()`, `links` (draw every connection between dots in view, with the
  edge's own `col` / `dash` and an arrow when `dir`), and hooks `pick`, `panel`, `open`, `moved`, `say`.
  `NEST.graph(nodes, edges)` and `NEST.path(G, a, b)` are shared. `NEST.start` opens a map inside the only group when
  there is one. The Atlas page is `NEST.page`, kept between visits.
- **Two fixes found while testing small maps.** A satellite takes a click only when it orbits the open nest, one just
  inside it, or one around it (in a small map a note's satellite sat over its project's middle and stole the click). The
  second click of a double-click does nothing of its own, and the double-click acts on what the first click hit (the
  camera may already be flying, so the second click could land on a component and open it).
- **`14b-atlas.js`** loses the 2D engine (`start`, `tick`, `fit`, `draw`, `hit`, `wire`, its `render`, `size`, `rebuild`,
  `search`, `focusOn`, `shortest`): nothing drew with it any more. `render`, `rebuild`, `search` and `focusOn` are 14d's.
- **`14c-noteterm.js`:** each map (the box's and the whole window's) is a NEST map. The block's view is `{ nest, focus }`:
  both maps share the pick, and flying into a note in one is where the other opens. The side panel lists the notes of the
  nest you are in, or the components of the note. Fetch no longer settles a layout. `NTERM.as`, `wire`, `crumb`, `size`,
  `draw` are gone.
- **`16-lens.js`:** `LensView.nests(G, st, t)` builds the graph from `IX.termGraph` (components, the notes, the other
  terms and papers in them, the lens's edges with their inks: explicit magenta, pulled-in teal, via dotted, suggested
  dashed) and mounts a map over the canvas host, left of the side panel. `LensView.card` is the picked node's card.
  `LensView.hl` lights an insight's components on the map. **Nests · Chain** in the lens bar; Chain is the board as
  before (Export chain still writes it).
- **`22-atlas.css`:** `.nest-host`, `.nest-cv` on its own, `.lens-nest`, `.lens-card`, `.lens-foot`, the small map's crumbs
  and tools; the note term's old crumb and canvas rules go.
