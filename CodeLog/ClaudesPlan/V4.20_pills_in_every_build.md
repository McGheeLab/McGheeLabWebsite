# V4.20 — The pills in every build, Gabe's own repo included

## Goal
Gabe (17 Nov 2026), asked whether Rotate may go into the Zoom pill (the lab layer): "Edit it, and make sure pills go in the repo as well."

His repo (RTgabrieldeclercq/Scientia-In-Situ) is the McGhee `scientia/` folder cut by its `sync/lab_sync.py`, without the lab layer. The pills (✎ the hand, ＋ the Quick cast, ✦ tools, ⊕ zoom) are section 11 of `rm/scientia/lab-mode.js`, which is not in that cut, so his app has none.

## Approach — the lab's own code, cut out unchanged (the `wb-ink.js` pattern)
- **The generator.** `scripts/scientia_build.py` `lab_pills()` writes `scientia/vendor/lab-pills.js` (generated, tracked, held by `--check`).
  - It holds lab-mode.js's section 11, from `/* ---- 11. The canvas pills in a note` up to `/* ---- 12.`, verbatim.
  - It also holds the one lab-mode helper the section calls, `warn`, cut out by name. The section uses nothing else from the file: its only other names from lab-mode are `LAB` and two flags of the lab's simple notebook, and the rest are the app's globals. `btn`, `pointer` and `words` occur only inside words such as `.lab-pill-btn` and in comments.
  - Everything sits inside `LABPILLS.start(LAB)`, so nothing runs at load. Vendor scripts are inlined before the app's.
  - The build stops when the section is not found, when a helper is gone, or when the section starts calling another lab-mode helper (`PILLS_HELPERS`).
- **The start.** `scientia/src/99z-pills.js` `PILLS` (after `99-boot.js`, where lab-mode.js would run) starts it once, and only where the lab layer is absent (`window.SIS_TARGET !== 'web'`). The lab's builds run lab-mode.js itself, so they keep one set.
  - It passes a LAB of its own: the simple notebook off. It never sets `window.SIS_LAB`, which the app reads as "the lab layer is here"; the LAB is exposed as `window.SIS_PILLS`.
  - It creates `#lab-mode-css` (the style element the section appends its CSS to; in the lab, section 8 makes it) and gives the app's 3 s roll-up (`PILL_DOCK_MS`).
- **What his app gets** is exactly the lab's pills on its own tools:
  - INK (pen, highlighter, lasso, eraser, pen settings), INSERTS (the Quick cast replaces the bottom bar: Pull in, Flag, Label, then the rows he chose; More… = the whole + Insert list, with a bolt on each row), TIMER / HEAD / CALC / EQ / SPLITBTN, Paper zoom;
  - a pill dragged anywhere, docked as a row or a column, the layout in `settings().labPills`;
  - the lab's RM-only Quick cast defaults (`rmpaper`, `rmprotocol`, `rmaction`, `rmcollection`) are not in his + Insert, so they are left out quietly (`qcRows` keeps only INSERTS rows).
- **Settings → Pen & touch → Pills** (`pills`, in Simple too):
  - the switch, in every build: off hides the pills and gives back the bottom bar and the top-right row (`31-pills.css`, `body.f-off-pills`, over §11's `.lab-pilled` rules), and puts the zoom hand down;
  - a live preview: the four pills in their places, or the bar and the row when off.
