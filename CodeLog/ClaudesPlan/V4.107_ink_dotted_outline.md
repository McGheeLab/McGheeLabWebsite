# V4.107 — Scientia: the ink's dotted outline (pointed at, selected), none while the pen writes

## Why
Gabe, 23 Nov: "The ink board is white, when I said it should be transparent (it currently is when the pen is writing) at all times. When hovering over the ink board you should be able to see a dotted outline of the entire board. The same when clicking on it. During the pen writing, you should not see any outline."

**White at rest:** V4.104 (PR #104, not merged when Gabe wrote) already took the card away in every state. Before it, look B's `.box` rule gave ink a card, and only `body.ink-writing` made it see-through while a stroke went down: that is what Gabe saw. This version checks it again with pixels: the ink's empty part, and its rim, look the same with the ink and without it, in the light and dark themes.

**The outline:** since V4.104 only the strokes take the pointer, so `:hover` saw only the strokes. Nothing showed where the whole ink was.

## What changed
- **`13zf-ink.js`:** the paper says which ink the pointer is over. On each pointer move (once a frame), every ink component whose rectangle holds the pointer gets `.ink-hov`; the others lose it. A pointer held down (a drag) outlines none.
  - `body.ink-pen` is on while the pointer in use writes: the pen in pen mode, or the mouse when it draws too (the lab's pen hand, 🖱 in the ink bar).
  - It follows `INK.paintBars` (every change of hand, tool or bar) and each pointer's type. With the desktop's ink bar out, the mouse still sees the outline and the pen does not.
- **`21-sync.css`:**
  - a dotted outline (1.5 px, an outline not the border, so nothing moves) on `.ink-hov`, `:hover` and `.sel`; the selected one in a darker ink;
  - none under `body.ink-pen` or `body.ink-writing`, nor with the lab's pills on any hand but pan (V3.942's rule for frames);
  - the ink's title tab, shown while it is selected, keeps its own paper background (look B's tinted header let the card's header beneath show through it).
- **Settings → Ink & shapes:**
  - the description says it;
  - in the preview, pointing at the ink outlines it, dotted (`PV.inkHover`);
  - the caption says so;
  - with *Click through ink* off, its rectangle is dotted too.
- **`MANUAL.md`** (The pen and the ink bar) says ink is see-through, and when the dotted outline shows.
