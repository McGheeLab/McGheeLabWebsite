# V3.988 — the Portfolio as two panes; the results kinds

**Date:** 2026-11-13 · **Branch:** `scientia/portfolio-list-results` · **App + lab layer** (Alex reviews `lab-mode.js` §10 and the v3869 harness)

**Gabe:** "When you have a result labelled, then the possible links to it are insight, correlation, or more. Propose some
component kinds that mean most for Results in science. Also, the portfolio look should be reorganized to not look like
blocks, but be a relevant list of projects, that once opened, bring up the themes and notes (keep the dropdown system of
experiments, the dots, the separation of types of notes: Lit Reviews, Experiments, general notes). Propose a few designs
… before implementing it as a pull request." Three designs were proposed as live mock-ups (A Ledger, B Two panes,
C Threads) with the six kinds; Gabe chose **B**, all six kinds, correlation as a link, no facts line, and the same change
in the Lab Notebook.

## Decisions

- **Two panes, one view.** `PortfolioView` stays a canvas-flagged view (no page scroller) and draws its own two scrolling
  panes. The list's order is relevance = the four-week activity the Portfolio already scores (PFKIT), so "relevant"
  needs no new measure; Recent and A–Z for the rest. The open project is remembered per device (`_ls pf:view`), not
  synced: two devices open at once would otherwise pull each other's selection.
- **The three kinds of note kept apart:** within each theme Experiments beside Notes (an experiment is a chain entry or a
  note of a staged type: Planning, Run, Analysis, Synthesis…); Lit reviews are the project's own section (literature notes
  and the reviews linked to it, `LITREV.of`), as V3.975 put them in their own column; General notes are a row of the list.
- **What the canvas showed that a list cannot:** the dashed links between notes become ↔ n on a note and a hover that
  lights the linked notes and the projects in the list that hold them.
- **The lab fills the app's view instead of drawing its own.** The lab layer replaced the whole Portfolio with its region
  canvas; now the app's view has six hooks (groups, meta, actions, foot, notePills, themePill) and lab-mode §10 shrinks to
  an adapter: its groups (assigned, Quick notes, yours, shared, earlier), kicks and RM lines, its buttons. One view, two
  fillings, nothing drawn twice. The region canvas's drag / fold / pin / tidy go with it.
- **Results kinds:** Result shares the id `result` with the structure, so marked text and the kind are one class for
  links. Correlation is a relation (`dir: false`), never a kind and never "caused". The rest of the app reads them where a
  takeaway, evidence or a barrier is counted: the dots, the Synthesis's gather, an aim's evidence; an Anomaly counts as an
  open barrier until something `explains` it.
