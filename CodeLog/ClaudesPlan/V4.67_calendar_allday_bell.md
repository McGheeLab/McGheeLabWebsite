# V4.67 — Scientia Calendar: today's all-day events, once in the bell

## Why
Gabe, 19 Nov: "All day events might be tasks or events that are relevant to Reminders (which appear on the Calendar as well). All-day events also appear as a one-time notification in the bell button." Asked again on 20 Nov ("Work on … all-day items appearing once in the bell").

## What changed
- **The bell** (the reminders button; `REM.badge` and `REM.panel` are wrapped in `23e-calendar.js`) now also counts today's all-day calendar events (`CAL.bellItems`) until you open it.
  - Reminders and dated tasks were in the bell already, and still are.
- **Opened,** its panel has a section under the header (`CAL.bellSection`): "Today · all day" lists the new ones, in bold, with their event type and their note. Once you have opened the bell they move under "Seen" (`CAL.bellMark`, kept per day on this device as `calBellSeen`) and no longer count: a one-time notification.
- One added later in the day counts again, once.
- A click opens the calendar on that day.
- **Settings → Calendar:** "Today's all-day events, once in the bell" (on by default), and the preview says so.
