## The one recorder

One timeline model, used by all three hosts, and every capability in every place:

- **Voice** — chunked to Storage as RM does (crash-safe, any device), with pause / resume (Scientia's).
- **Ink in time** — strokes stamped `t0/t1` while recording, in RM boards (as now) AND in Scientia notes (the lab
  layer stamps Gabe's strokes); replay grows the ink; **tap a stroke → hear what was said while it was drawn**.
- **Marks** — ◆ *Important*, plus *Question*, *Decision*, *To do* (Scientia's structure kinds), as a moment or a
  section (press to start, press again to end); on the scrubber, in the transcript margin, in the replay; a *To do*
  mark can become a My Week item.
- **Transcript** — lines with times, click to play, search; the line being spoken highlights during playback and the
  ink drawn during that line lights up; edits are ordinary text.
- **After the recording** — a summary, the important sections found automatically, action items, open questions
  (Claude on the transcript text, `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` already in functions — the equationOcr precedent), each
  linked to its moment.

Shared code: a DOM-free `rm/js/recorder-core.js` (timeline, chunk plan, marks, pause map, transcript alignment, ink ↔
time lookup), used by `whiteboard.js` / `whiteboard-replay.js` and by the Scientia lab layer; node fixtures. Gabe's
desktop recorder stays his (Whisper on his computer stays the desktop's transcriber); the web Scientia's recording
component is upgraded from outside like every other Phase 2 card, and its block keeps Gabe's `rec` shape (`marks`,
`pauses`, `segs`) so the desktop shows it.

## Decision (Alex, 2026-10-23): **Google Speech-to-Text (Chirp)** on the lab's Google Cloud project — **on a button, with its cost shown first**

Alex: "the user should click a button for speech to text and they should be told how much it is likely going to cost." Nothing is transcribed in the cloud on its own: a recording shows **Transcribe — about $0.48 (30 min)** (its audio minutes × the per-minute rate, one constant `STT_USD_PER_MIN` in `recorder-core.js`, Google's standard rate $0.016 / min), and runs only when pressed. The desktop's Whisper stays free and automatic for Scientia on the computer.

The options that were weighed:

Speech-to-text in the cloud is a paid service (Claude does not take audio):
- **Google Speech-to-Text (Chirp)** on the lab's own Google Cloud project — no new account; about $1 for an hour.
- **OpenAI Whisper API** — Whisper quality, the same model as the desktop; a new key.
- **Free only** — the browser's live captions while recording, and the desktop's Whisper for Scientia; no cloud cost.
