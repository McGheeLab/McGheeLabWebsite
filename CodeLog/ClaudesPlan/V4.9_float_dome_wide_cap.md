# V4.9 — The float dome as a wide cap

## Goal
Gabe (16 Nov 2026), on the Floats dome (V4.7): "Have it not be a hemisphere, but an extended view of the tip of a hemisphere, spanning the whole horizontal space, and maybe 3/4 of the vertical space it currently takes."

## Shape
A spherical cap: the circular segment whose chord is the canvas pane's bottom edge across its full width W and whose height is h = ¾ of V4.7's dome height (D/2, with D = max(220, round(min(0.74 W, 0.8 H, 820) / 8) · 8)), at least 84 px. The sphere's radius is Rs = (h² + (W/2)²) / 2h, its centre Rs below the apex; the outline is the arc from the bottom-left corner over the apex to the bottom-right corner. The same numbers are used by the prototype.

## Approach (14h-floats.js, 28-floats.css)
- The dome box spans the pane (left 0, right 0, height h) and is clipped with `clip-path: circle(Rs at 50% Rs)`; it slides up from the bottom edge as before; the skirt (the note's paper fading upward) runs the full width.
- Glass: radial gradients centred on the sphere's centre for the bright rim and an inner glint; an SVG edge along the arc (white inside, a faint ink line); two small highlights near the upper left of the arc, rotated to its tangent; the slow sheen. The rim's bend is a displacement map made for the cap (a thin band at the rim, scale ≤ band / 3 so nothing folds over — the first try mirrored text under the rim).
- Beads: golden-angle azimuths turned by the spin map across the width over which the cap is at least 40 px tall (cos of the azimuth); each sits between the base and the arc above it by its elevation (oldest lowest); depth scales size and stacking. `inDome` tests the segment.
- Settings preview: an elliptic segment through both bottom corners spanning the mini canvas.

## Testing
`t_4.9` (the V4.7 suite with the cap's geometry: full-width base, ¾ height, segment, beads inside), at 1400 px and 400 px dark; the Monthly review, References and Settings suites; the v3869 / v3880 harnesses at integration.
