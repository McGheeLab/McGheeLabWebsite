# V4.54 — Freewrite: the labels are buttons; a drag links two components

## Why
Gabe, 19 Nov: "Dragging a component to another allows you to create a component link between them. The label on each freewrite sentence is clickable, bringing a clean popup that gives you quick modification possibilities for both kind and structure of the component (similar to highlight in PDFs)."

## What changed
- **The labels are buttons (`FREEW.tags()`).** The labels over the words were drawn behind the text box, so they could not be clicked. They are now buttons in a layer above it (`.fw-tags`), in the same places; the drawn ones are hidden on the page (the freewrite's card in the note keeps them). They follow the words when the page changes size (a ResizeObserver).
- **A click on a component's label → a clean popup (`FREEW.quick`)**, like a PDF highlight's:
  - Kind chips (None and the kinds of component) and Structure chips (None and the structures, each with its letter: a letter picks one, as on a PDF);
  - its title (with Take off), its links (each opens the link's menu), and More ▾ (the whole menu: a task, a Result to add, a bridge, plain words);
  - the popup stays open while you change it. Esc closes it, not the freewrite. Any other key closes it and goes to the words.
- **A link's label → the link's menu.**
- **Drag a label, or a box in the preview, onto another component** (its words, its label or its box) → which relation → a link of your own:
  - kept in `fw.links` as `[{ a, b, rel }]`, by the parts' words like `fw.over`;
  - `FREEW.convert` adds it as a link with no words of its own (`own: true`), and Apply makes it a link in the note;
  - a dashed line follows the pointer, and the component under it lights up;
  - a move under 6 px is a click; a drop on nothing does nothing;
  - onto two components already linked: that link's menu;
  - the label says "· 1 link"; the link's menu offers Remove this link.
- **The preview:** a line that would run under another box goes round it on the right, its name in view.
- **The preview's heading** no longer breaks "The note it will make" over two lines.
