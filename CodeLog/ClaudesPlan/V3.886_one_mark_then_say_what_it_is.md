# V3.886 — One Mark: simple up front, then say what it is

**Ask (Alex, 2026-10-23):** "no we want simple up front, then customize later. for example four kinds of mark:
Important, Question, Decision, To do; should be one thing you drop in, then you can change what it is when its in"

Follows [V3.884](V3.884_one_recorder_program.md) (R1), which put four mark buttons and a section toggle up front in
both recorders. The rule now (same as [V3.885](V3.885_scientia_note_details_in_first_block.md)'s note details):
**one control to drop something in; what it is is changed on the thing once it is in.**

## The board recorder (`whiteboard.js`)

- ✦ Tools while recording: **Stop · Pause · Mark** (Alt+M). Nothing else up front; the four kind items and the
  section toggle are gone.
- A Mark from the fan drops a plain mark (kind `important`, the ◆) and opens the **mark editor** on it, beside the ✦
  pill exactly as the pen editor sits beside ✎ (`markEditorHTML`, `S.markEdit`; never a browser popup):
  - **Important · Question · Decision · To do**: what it is;
  - a few words (optional), kept as typed;
  - **Lasts until now**: it becomes a span from its moment to now (pressed again later, it extends); **Just the
    moment** takes it back;
  - **Remove** (two taps), **Done**.
- Alt+M drops a mark without opening anything; the toast says it can be changed under ✦ Tools, where **Change the
  last mark** appears once one exists.

## Scientia (`rm/scientia/lab-mode.js` §12)

- The row of four kinds and the section button are gone. Gabe's ◆ (Alt+M) is the one Mark.
- In the recording's list each bookmark's glyph is a button (a plain ◆ shows no word; any other kind shows its
  word): it opens Gabe's own menu (`ctxMenu`) with the four kinds, **Lasts until now** (while it records or plays past
  the mark) and **Just the moment**.

## Shared (`rm/js/recorder-core.js`)

`updateMark(marks, id, patch)` (kind, label; its moment and id never move), `spanTo(marks, id, t)` (`null` or not
after it = back to a moment), `removeMark(marks, id)`. The open-section functions stay for old recordings (Stop still
closes one).
