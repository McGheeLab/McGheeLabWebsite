# V4.115 — Scientia: the lab app on a computer keeps its own settings, and says when its notebook folder is the private app's

## Why
Gabe, 24 Nov: "The button Open my computer does not work. It opens a local window with no lab notebook sync, which is the one I need to download as a local app, since I don't want the full website as the app, just the notebook"

## What was wrong (on Gabe's computer)
- **The lab app was running and connected:** `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ScientiaInSitu`, its ping said `connected: true`.
- **Its notebook folder was sealed:** `/api/sync/hello` answered 423, because Scientia In Situ, the private app, had locked that folder (`.scientia/access.json`, `record.json` sealed, since 9 Nov).
- **So the site opened an empty window:** the lab app had nothing to open or sync, but the site's *Open on my computer* opened `localhost:8766` anyway. The page showed no notebook, and only its sync dot turned red.
- **How it happened:** both apps read **the same settings**, `%APPDATA%\ScientiaInSitu\config.json` (and `devices.json`, `sessions.json`, `shadow.json` beside it), so each opened the notebook folder the other had chosen. The private app opened the lab app's folder and sealed it.

## What changed
- **`scientia/service.py`** (the installed lab app's launcher) gives the lab app settings of its own, `%APPDATA%\ScientiaInSitu Lab`, passed to `serve.py` as `SIS_CONFIG`:
  - they are made once, from the shared folder: the connection to the lab (`cloud.json`, so it stays connected) and the notebook folder, with its sync state;
  - a folder the private app has sealed, or carries its lock (`access.json`), is never taken. The lab app gets a fresh `Documents\Scientia Lab Notebook` (or "… 2"), which the lab cloud fills;
  - the shared folder is left as it is, for the private app;
  - the result goes into the app's log.
- **`scientia/serve.py`:**
  - the lab site's ping says `sealed`;
  - `/api/notebook` adds `sealed` and `suggest` (that fresh folder) when the folder is sealed.
- **The app:**
  - `SYNC.hello` reads the 423 and `SYNC.boot` returns `sealed`;
  - `DEVICES.screen('sealed')` says the folder is locked by the private app, with **Use a new folder for this notebook** (one click: `/api/notebook` with the suggested folder, then it starts again), **Choose another folder…**, or "take its lock off there";
  - the install card stays off that screen.
- **The lab site's *Open on my computer* (`23c-thiscomputer.js`):** with a sealed app it tells the person that the tab will show how.
- **`install/uninstall.ps1`** reads the lab app's own settings first; a note in `install/install.ps1`; `MANUAL.md` §21.

## On Gabe's computer
- **Once this version is on the site,** the installed app updates itself within the hour (`service.py`). It then makes its own settings, gets a fresh `Documents\Scientia Lab Notebook` (the Desktop folder is sealed), and the lab cloud fills it.
- **Open on my computer** then opens the lab notebook, and only the notebook.
- **Until then,** the local page offers the same new folder with one click.
- **The private app** keeps its own settings and its Desktop folder, untouched.
