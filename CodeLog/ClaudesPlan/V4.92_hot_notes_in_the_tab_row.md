# V4.92 — Scientia: your hot notes in the tab row, beside the Hot notes button

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "Hot notes appear in the tab next to it."

## What changed (`20e-hotnotes.js`, `HOT.strip` / `HOT.fit`; `38-hotnotes.css`)
- **The starred notes** sit in the tab row right after the ☆ Hot notes button, each a small tab with its kind's colour (Experiment, Note, Lit review, Synthesis) and its title.
  - The full title and the project › theme are in its tooltip. A click opens the note; the note you are in has its tab lit.
- **As many as fit:** the rest are behind **+n**, which opens the Hot notes panel. The fit is measured again on a resize.
- **Settings → Hot notes:** *In the tab row, beside the button* — None, Up to 3, Up to 5 (the default) or Up to 8. The preview shows a tab row with them.
- The button, its panel and its count are unchanged.
