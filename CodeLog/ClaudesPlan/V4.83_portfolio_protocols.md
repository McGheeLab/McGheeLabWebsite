# V4.83 — a Protocols section in the Portfolio

## Why
Gabe: "Make sure that Protocols are added in Portfolio view as a new section under the projects or themes they are a part of."

A protocol note (type `protocol`, scope project) was an ordinary entry among a theme's (or Project-wide) Notes.

## What changed
- A **Protocols** group in every section of the open project — each theme, and Project-wide — beside Experiments and Notes
  (same classes: `.pf-g[data-g="proto"]`, one more header colour `--sec-proto`, the clipboard icon through `PFICON`). The
  Portfolio's look is untouched; the group spans both columns under the grid.
- **What is in it** (decided here): (1) the protocol notes filed in that section — a theme's own (`themeId`), and a project's
  with no theme under Project-wide; they leave the Notes group and the Notes count; (2) the protocols the notes of that
  theme / Project-wide RUN (a `run` block of a registered protocol), even when the protocol is filed in another theme, in
  Project-wide or in another project: listed once, marked `⟲ n` (its runs here) and "from <theme | project-wide | project ›
  theme>". A protocol filed here shows its run count too. Settings can turn (2) off.
- The switch above the notes gains **Protocols** (before Lit reviews) with its count: only them, theme by theme.
  **+ Protocol** beside a section's header starts a protocol note in that theme / project-wide (`App.newDoc({type:'protocol'…})`).
- Settings: "Protocols in the Portfolio" (`pfprotocols`, Tabs & overview): the switch (off: protocol notes are Notes again),
  "Protocols run here too", "Show the section where there is none", live preview. "Section headers" (pficons) says rust
  Protocols and its preview has the Protocols header.
- Old tests updated because a protocol note now counts under Protocols: `t_984` (project-wide protocol note is under
  Protocols; seg counts), `fb_t416` (the switch has Protocols too).
