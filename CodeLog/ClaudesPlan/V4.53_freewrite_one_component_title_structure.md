# V4.53 — Freewrite: words made one component leave the components they were in; a title or a structure from a word

## Why
Gabe, 19 Nov: "When you highlight multiple sentences and transform them into a component, their old identity is removed from the conversion, so that there are no repeats of the same content. When clicking on a word, or after highlighting some words within the same component, it allows you to make it a component title for the component it resides in or make it a structure."

The first of four versions for that message: V4.54 is the label's popup and links made by dragging, V4.55 merging sentences, V4.56 the editor bar and the slash commands.

## What changed
- **No repeats (`FREEW.convert`).** Words associated as one component (Select → Make these words a component…) that reach over components take their words:
  - a component wholly inside them is gone;
  - one partly inside keeps only its words outside;
  - a link whose words were inside is gone, with its cues.
  The new part is marked `assoc`. Before, the component, the two it reached over and their link were all made.
- **Inside it, it wins.** A sentence the rules read (the "run" mode) whose words lie in an associated part goes into it, not into a part of its own.
- **A word clicked:** under the chip (what it became · Change ▾), a row for the word: “word” · Title · Structure ▾.
  - **Title:** the word becomes the component's title (`fw.over[key].title`). Its label in the freewrite says `Kind · “title”`, its box in the preview shows it in bold, and Apply gives the component that label. Clicking Title again takes it off.
  - **Structure ▾:** the structure kinds (Barrier, Result, Method…). The words get a structure inside the component (`fw.over[key].marks`), drawn on them in the structure's colour with its name under them. On Apply it is an item and its mark, as when you mark words in a note.
- **Words selected inside one component:** Title · Structure ▾ · A component… (the last is the old question, a component of their own). Words over several components still get "Make these words a component…".
- **A kind chosen from Change ▾ keeps the title and the structures.**
- **Drawing:** a component's title and its structures are spans inside its stretch, so it keeps one outline. The preview box's text gives up a line and a half to the title, so nothing is cut.
- **Found while testing:** after Associate, the selected words stayed selected. The next click on a word was read while the selection still stood (Chrome collapses it after the click), so it did nothing. Now the selection collapses after Associate, and a click read while words are still selected is read again a moment later.
- **Settings → Freewrite:** the description says so.
