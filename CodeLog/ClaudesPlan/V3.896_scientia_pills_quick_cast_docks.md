## Docking

`LP.dockMs` (10 000 ms) after a dockable fan opens, if it is still open, it docks (`dock(name)`, `_ls lab:docked`,
per device, kept across notes): the items travel from their fan places to the strip's (`rowPos`, CSS transition with
a stagger — the roll), a soft strip (`.lab-dock-rail`) fades in behind them. A docked row stays when you tap
elsewhere; its items work as in the fan; other wheels still fan over it. A tap on the docked pill folds the row back
into the wheel (`.unrolling`). *Put the pills back* resets places and docks.

## Quick cast

`_ls lab:quickcast` = INSERTS ids (default: component, lab paper, material, RM protocol, run, plate, task, something I
did, collection — V3.882's picks), shown in Gabe's order, plus *…* (Gabe's whole + Insert list). That list
(`INSERT.menu`, wrapped) gets a **bolt** toggle on every row (lit = in your Quick cast; a MutationObserver re-adds it
as the search filters); a tap changes the Quick cast and inserts nothing.
