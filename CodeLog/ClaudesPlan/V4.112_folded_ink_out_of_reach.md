# V4.112 — Scientia: fix — ink folded away in a region is out of the pen's reach

## Why
Gabe, 23 Nov, on "sometimes, the pen won't write on the screen": "It had to do with me collapsing ink components that were inside regions"

## The cause
- **Where a new stroke goes** (`INK.target`): into the ink component it starts near, or the last one written. It chose from every live ink component of the note, including those hidden in a folded region (`.rg-hid`, `display:none`).
- **So a stroke written where folded ink lay** (or near the last ink, if that was folded away) joined the hidden ink and was hidden with it. The pen seemed not to write.
- **Other pen tools reached it too:**
  - the eraser and the pen's back (`INK.erase`, `PENERASE`);
  - the lasso, the fill tool, the pen's loop (13zz2) and its dumbbell (13zp).
- **The test showed worse:** without this fix, an eraser pass over that place erased the hidden ink's strokes and removed the component altogether.

## What changed
- **`INK.reachable(docId)`** (13zf): the note's ink components minus those a folded region hides (`REGION.hider`), and minus ink glued to an image hidden there. When no region is folded it is the plain list.
- **It is used by** `INK.target`, `INK.erase`, `INK.lassoSelect`, `PENERASE.run` (13zm), the dumbbell (13zp), the fill (13zv) and the pen's loop (13zz2).
- **What still sees all ink:** the region's own hiding (13v) and the glue (13zzc).
- **What it means for you:** a stroke written there now makes new ink you can see. The folded ink is untouched, and comes back as it was when unfolded.
- **Strokes that went missing before this fix** were added to the folded ink: unfolding the region shows them.
