# V3.1000 — The Atlas on three axes, its dots the term page's bubbles

## Goal
Gabe (14 Nov 2026): "I want the Atlas to keep its 3D nature with a set of three axes defined by how they categorize or sort what you see. For example, one of the axes could be timeline (oldest notes start at the origin and continues growing). The Atlas dots representing structures and more should now copy the bubble feature from the terms page." Design: the published prototype (artifact BJAAj4bPNdij2LZvUGu3KT, `atlas_axes.html`).

## Decisions (the prototype's four open choices, built with its picks; easy to change)
1. The axes replace the nests in the Atlas tab (one view). 14d stays for 14c's note-term maps.
2. A click ports in (visits the note), Shift+click pins. After Gabe’s notes on the prototype only a click ports in: no ring. From a note with an action chip on, a click does the action.
3. It starts on Story over time (time × kind × project), then the axes used last (`settings().atlasAxes`); Settings can fix a preset or three axes.
4. The dots are structures and components; notes, terms, papers and people are one click away (the bar's kind chips).

## Gabe’s notes on the prototype (14 Nov), built into this version
- Only a click ports in (the bubbles get `ring: { on: false }`).
- Resting on a bead opens it after `hoverMs` (1 s; a ring closes in round it); coming close opens bubbles only once the camera has been still as long (`camAt`), so turning and zooming never pop them open.
- The camera: with Time on an axis the room stops growing at 1.6× (`ZT`) and coming closer spreads time instead (`TW`, `setWindow`, `spread`; down to nine days, anchored on the dot nearest the pointer); Shift+wheel slides the window (`panTime`). The axes tighten with distance: `adapt()` picks the finest time unit whose labels fit (`NEED`), thins numbers, shortens names; long labels where the larger unit turns and on the first tick in view.
- One bubble per component when they open by themselves; a bridge’s copy is a dot (`bridgeOf`, the green band).
- Bridges and connections are threads (`THREADS`, `resolveThreads`): an arrow at the far end and a light running from where each starts, in the colour of what it carries (`ck`: the bridged words’ structure, else the component’s kind; a connection, its origin’s first structure); a bridge ends at its copy. Threads chips in the side panel.
- Resting on a thread opens its popup (`openTP`): both ends as `BUBBLE.el` with what is near them, the light between, why, every note it is bridged from and to (each ports in).

## Third round (Gabe, 14 Nov): Shift+drag, no shaking, nothing opens unasked, a big thread popup
- Shift+drag pans the camera target in the plane of the screen (`panBy`; two fingers on touch, Shift+arrows); a plain drag still turns.
- Shaking (three causes, all covered): the room’s size following the side panel; the browser’s over/out events flipping between overlapping beads or a closing bubble; late scroll events cancelling a rest. Hover is computed from the dots' positions (`pointAt`) with hysteresis (bead + 3 px to take, + 8 px to let go; an open bubble its opening size + 10 px; a lit thread twice its tolerance), so no opening/floating/closing bubble and no pixel of jitter can flip it — and with it the whole room's drawing — frame by frame; the room takes a size only once it has held 160 ms; under 900 px the room has a fixed height; labels keep their places first; the ticks' ruler has hysteresis; the bubble being read holds still; a scroll cancels a rest only if it moved the room.
- Coming close opens nothing by default (`cap` 0); coming back from a note leaves nothing picked.
- The thread popup covers 88 % of the room (a phone: all of it); each end is a large bubble sized to its column, with as many neighbours as keep the cards at about normal size.
- Tested by `atl3_t1000` (desktop, 400 px dark, 860 px): Shift+drag, a no-shake check over 14 dots, a size flipping every frame, nothing opening in 3 s still, the popup's and its bubbles' sizes.

## Approach
- `14f-atlas3.js` (`ATLAS3`), loaded after 14b/14d/14e, takes over `ATLAS.render` / `rebuild` / `search` / `focusOn` / `panelDraw` / `draw`. Data: `ATLAS.graph()` read with every kind (threads and degrees whole), lit-review notes left out (`TERMLIT`), each node a dot with its time, kind, project, note, note type, first term (most used first), person (`@name` or a person term), status (`IX.status`) and degree; arcs from `TARC.all()` drawn between the structures.
- Layout and drawing from the prototype: axis info (number / category), cell spreading, room with floor and far walls, `EDGE()` placement of the axes, thinned HTML tick labels, a rAF loop only while something moves (float, easing, flight, drag), styles written only when they change.
- Bubbles: `BUBBLE.spec` / `BUBBLE.el` (new options `flat: false`, `ring: {on, ms}`) built lazily per dot and cached (rebuilt when the record changes), scaled from the bead; `BUBBLE.port` and `BUBBLE.back.arm({hash: '#/atlas', text})`; the camera and state live in the module, so the return finds the Atlas as it was.
- A crowded room (> 250 dots): beads drawn as canvas sprites and picked by position; elements only for open / opening / labelled dots; beads stop floating.
- `20a-features.js`: the Atlas feature gains its settings and a live preview (`ATLAS3.preview()`); `26-atlas3.css` the room, beads, side panel, phone layout (panel under the room, the bar's rows scrolled sideways).
