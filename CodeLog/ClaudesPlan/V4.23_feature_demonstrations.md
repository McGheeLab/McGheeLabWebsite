Gabe (17 Nov 2026), after V4.12 gave each feature a page with room for a demonstration: "Read the GitHub to understand what each feature does and come back to me with your own proposed workflow. I will give you the numbers and a description of the ones I would like you to change." The proposal covered all 65 features of V4.12 (what each is for, its test note, its steps); his answer was "go ahead".

## What was recorded
- 64 features, on the build of V4.22 (pills in every note, the synthesis box under the note's head, no Activity tab). The two recordings of V4.12 were made again on it.

## Approach
- **One made-up lab.** Every test note belongs to a project "Traction on soft gels": a daily note, a fibronectin series, seeding notes, gel batches, runs and their analysis, a small library of invented papers. Nothing comes from a real notebook, and the recorder's Chrome starts on an empty profile.
- **A script per feature**, `demokit/<id>.mjs`: the approved sentence as `use`, the test note in `setup`, three to five captioned steps driven with the real pointer and keyboard. Scripts were written in parallel in separate worktrees and collected here.
- **Review by the frames, not by the script.** A take that runs through can still show nothing (a click landing on a hover card, a menu closed before the step ends). Every take was looked at independently at the end of each step, and marked-down takes were fixed and recorded again.
- **Where the app differs from the proposal, the take shows the app.** Captions were reworded to what is seen; the differences are listed for Gabe in the PR and the CHANGELOG ("Found, not changed").
- **Two bugs were fixed** because each was one line with no choice in it and the take could not be honest without it (a saved page's styles; the Library search after a reading state). The others were left for Gabe.

## The recorder's additions
See the CHANGELOG entry. The ones that needed a decision:
- **Full screen.** Headless Chrome has none. The recorder answers `requestFullscreen` / `fullscreenElement` itself, so the app's own code runs; in the recording the window is the screen anyway.
- **The app's server.** Handwriting (Windows' recognizer), recordings (the audio file, Whisper) and images in wells need `serve.py`. The recorder starts it on a spare port with `SIS_CONFIG` pointing at a temp folder, so the person's settings and notebook are never read; the page is still the app by itself, with only `api/`, `images/` and `recordings/` passed on.
- **Waits.** `R.cut` leaves a wait out of the video (a minute's focus session, Whisper reading a clip); the caption says so. A reminder comes due at its own time: it is made in `setup`, never moved.
- **Timing.** Frames carry Chrome's capture time and are thinned to the video's frame rate; before, a burst of 60 frames a second played slower than it happened.
