# V4.17 — The Portfolio's section icons and every theme's banner

## Goal
Gabe (17 Nov 2026), from his list:
- "The Experiment Section should have a flask symbol next to it. The Notes Section should have a paper with notes symbol next to it. The Lit Reviews should have an open book symbol next to it."
- "Each theme has a horizontal banner with set pixel resolution, so that you can ask Claude to create a custom banner for it."

## Approach
- **A new module, `scientia/src/15f-banners.js`, with `31-banners.css`.** The Portfolio's own code (`15-portfolio.js`) is unchanged; PFLIST and ThemeView are wrapped.
- **PFICON — the section icons.**
  - Monochrome inline SVG, the repo's vetted paths (`rm/js/icons.js`): `exp` a flask (Lucide flask-conical), `notes` a page of notes (Heroicons document-text), `lit` an open book (Heroicons book-open), `syn` a stack (Heroicons rectangle-stack, for the Syntheses group a later version adds).
  - Applied by key wherever PFLIST draws them:
    - before each group's label (`.pf-g[data-g] > .pf-gl`);
    - before the Lit reviews heading;
    - on each button of the type switch (`[data-k]`).
  - `PFICON.dress(root)` is idempotent and keyed, so a group another module adds with its own `data-g` gets its icon.
- **BANNER — every theme has one, at a set resolution: 1600 × 320 px (5 : 1).**
  - **Where:** at the top of its section in the Portfolio (PFLIST.section wrapped) and of its page (ThemeView.render wrapped), always shown at 5 : 1.
  - **Choosing an image:** click it (or Enter). The menu says the size ("ask Claude for one at this size") and offers Choose an image…: `MEDIA.add`, stored as `theme.banner`, shown with `object-fit: cover`. Another size is kept and cropped to fit, and a toast says so.
  - **Removing it:** Remove the image goes back to the drawn banner.
  - **The drawn banner:** a theme without an image gets a quiet banner drawn from its id and name — seeded soft hills in one hue, contour lines and a pale disc. Its fills mix the page's own colour tokens (`color-mix(var(--surface)…)`), so light and dark follow the theme.
  - **Hover:** resting on a banner for a second shows "1600 × 320 px · click to change"; nothing appears by itself.
  - **RM themes** (`t.rm`, aims mirrored from RM) show the drawn banner and offer no menu: their record belongs to RM.
- **Settings → Tabs & overview:**
  - **Section icons** (`pficons`): a switch and a preview — the switch and the groups with their icons.
  - **Theme banners** (`tbanner`): a switch; "Show the banner" Portfolio and page / In the Portfolio / On the theme's page; a preview of both places with the size in words.

  Both are in Simple too. Off: `body.f-off-pficons` / `body.f-off-tbanner` hide them, and the code guards stop drawing them; the images stay with their themes.
