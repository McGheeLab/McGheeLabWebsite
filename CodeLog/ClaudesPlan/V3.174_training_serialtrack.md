## Goal

Operational fluency on SerialTrack_Python — the lab's preferred particle
tracking velocimetry (PTV) tool for measuring full-field displacements
and strains from 2D / 3D confocal image sequences. Primarily used for
traction force microscopy (TFM) data; also serves as an alternate
tracking backend for Cell Tracker (V3.171).

## Format

Full interactive notebook (MODULE_TEMPLATE.md pattern). 5 tabs.

## Tabs (preliminary)

### Tab 1 — What SerialTrack is + when to reach for it
**Bio context:** measuring the displacement of fluorescent fiducial
beads embedded in a hydrogel as a cell-laden spheroid invades.

- Background: PTV vs. PIV; the serial / ADMM approach (iterative
  refinement alternating between local topology matching and global
  displacement regularization) — why it survives large deformations,
  high density, and 3D much better than naive nearest-neighbor
- Decision rule: SerialTrack (large deformations, complex motion,
  TFM-style fiducial fields) vs. Cell Tracker's Hungarian / Spatial
  Fingerprint (well-resolved cell-like objects)

### Tab 2 — Detection: LoG + polynomial sub-pixel vs. blob + radial symmetry
- Background: the two detection backends in the repo
  - **LoG + polynomial sub-pixel** (TracTrac-style)
  - **Blob centroid + radial symmetry** (TPT-style)
- When each is appropriate; interactive: same field with both
- Sub-pixel localization accuracy: why this matters for strain estimation

### Tab 3 — Tracking modes: incremental / cumulative / double-frame
- Three modes explained with worked examples
- Trajectory stitching for broken segments + gap filling
- POD-GPR motion prediction for faster convergence on long sequences

### Tab 4 — 2D vs. 3D workflows
- 2D planar sequences (typical TFM)
- 3D volumetric stacks — the data structure differences; performance
  trade-offs; when you actually need 3D vs. 2D-per-slice
- Numba JIT — the warmup cost vs. steady-state speedup

### Tab 5 — Export + interop
- Multi-format export: HDF5 / `.mat` / `.npz` / per-frame CSV
- Importing tracks into post-processing (matplotlib, Cell Tracker as
  external tracker, custom analyses)
- Optional PySide6 GUI overview (protocol-based design)
- **Capstone:** run SerialTrack on a provided 2D bead displacement
  series; export to `.mat`; verify against a reference displacement field
