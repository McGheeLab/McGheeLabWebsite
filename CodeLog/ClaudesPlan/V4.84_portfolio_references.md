# V4.84 — a References section in the Portfolio

## Why
Gabe: "References becomes a new section in Portfolio that adds all papers in citation mode within the References page
referenced per theme."

A note's own References list (12-doc synthBox, export) numbers the papers it cites; nothing gathered them across a theme.

## What changed
- A **References** group in each theme's section, and in Project-wide (which also counts the project's lit reviews: they belong
  to the project, not to a theme; the notes outside any project have none). It lists every paper cited (`type: 'cite'` marks)
  in those notes, as a note's list does: numbered as cited — the notes in the order they were written, each note's citations in
  the order they come (`ED.citeNumbers`), a paper keeping the number it got first; papers cited only by author
  (`ED.citeAuthorOnly`) follow, unnumbered. Each row: its number, the full reference (`LIB.apa`) or the short one, "cited in N
  notes" (the notes in its tooltip), a PDF tag. A click opens the paper: its PDF in the reader (`#/read/<id>`), a link in the
  browser, else its Library card. A section shows where something is cited (or always, with a Settings switch).
- The switch above the notes gains **References** (last), with its count (papers, summed over the shown sections): only them,
  theme by theme. Not added to "All" (a paper is cited in notes that are counted already).
- Same plumbing as the Protocols section (15h): `PFLIST.section` / `items` / `seg` / `counts` wrapped, a group in the Portfolio's
  own classes, one header colour (`--sec-refs`, 43-pfrefs.css), the books icon (rm/js/icons.js) through `PFICON`.
- Settings: "References in the Portfolio" (`pfrefs`): the switch, "A reference reads" (in full / short), "Shown before Show all"
  (5 · 8 · 15 · All; the rest behind a button, nothing opens by itself), "Show the section where nothing is cited yet", live
  preview. "Section headers" (pficons) says indigo References and its preview has it. There is no existing "refs" feature to
  extend: the `references` entry is the bookmark flag on components (14g), a different thing.
- Old test updated: `fb_t416` (the switch has References last, too).
