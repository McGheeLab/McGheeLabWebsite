# V3.997 — A click outside lets a selection go; lit reviews out of the Terms tab

## Goal
Gabe (14 Nov 2026): "When you have items selected, simply clicking outside of it, removes the selection." · "Terms mentioned in lit reviews do not appear in the terms tab." The rest of his message (terms as bubble portals with synergies, bridges folded in, porting into notes) is shown first as a proposal page (`term_bubbles`), and will be built once he has chosen.

## Approach
- **Selection:** on the click (pointer up without moving), not the press, so a drag that pans keeps it; only a click on the note's paper or on a component outside the selection counts — toolbars, menus, the selection's own bar and its components keep it; Ctrl / Shift / ⌘ add to it.
- **Lit reviews:** one place decides (`TERMLIT.occ`): mentions in literature notes and Lit reviews are left out of the Terms tab's counts and the term page's occurrences; a term used only there isn't listed. Per the V3.995 rule it is a setting of the Terms feature (left out · counted) with its preview.
