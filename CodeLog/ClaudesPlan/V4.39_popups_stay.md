# V4.39 — Popups stay on every page

## Goal
Gabe (19 Nov 2026): "Popups are also closing when I'm leaving a note, which is not how it's supposed to work."

## What happened
V4.32 hid open popups on every page that isn't a note (`RPOP.sync`: `.rp-away`, `display: none`): the Portfolio, the Library, Today, Settings. They came back with the next note. Leaving a note therefore looked like closing them.

## Approach
- **`RPOP.sync`:** a popup is hidden only on a phone, where there are none; on every other page it stays where it is, on top.
- **`RPOP.restore`** (after a reload) no longer needs a note. It runs once the app has started (`window.__ready`), on whatever page, and on every page change until it has run.
- **`RPOP.max` away from a note:** Maximize first goes to the paper's note, then puts the paper back beside (or in the maximised view) there. It retries every 200 ms while the note opens, at most 25 times.
- **Settings → Popup windows:** the description.
