# V3.967 — Iteration as a deck, a Troubleshooting kind, and the Synthesis at the end of an experiment

**Date:** 2026-11-10 · **Branch:** `scientia/deck-synthesis` (on `scientia/atlas-nests`, V3.966) · **Gabe's request:** "Iteration should
stack as a deck of cards with an easy right or left swipe through iterations. Make a troubleshooting kind that also contains this
feature. Reflection at the end of an experiment becomes synthesis, which is a staple note in the project that summarizes all important
takeaways, evidence, claims and results into one note. The process lives in Planning, Run, and Analysis. All claims, evidence, results,
takeaways, barriers, resolutions, and a panel of the rest of the components in a scrollable list for you to pick any more, get
transferred to the synthesis note automatically, and you build/rearrange the note."

## The deck (`13zzq-deck.js`, `IDECK`)

- A line of versions (13zzo's `ITER.line`) stacks at one spot: the card on top is the newest (or the one chosen this session,
  `IDECK.shown`), the others sit beneath it offset 7 px per step (`deck-under`: pointer-events off, content hidden, the top card's
  height). `DocView.box` is wrapped to add the strip and the classes; `DocView.update` to converge every version's x/y on the top
  card's (a move of any card moves the deck) and to size the cards beneath.
- The strip (`.iter-deck`): ‹ ›, the dots, "v2 of 3", + for the next version; a horizontal drag on it is the swipe (its pointer events
  stop at the strip, so neither the card nor the paper move); ← → with the card selected (`App.pane.sel`).
- `ITER.spot` is wrapped: a new version lands on the deck; `ITER.attach` makes it the card on top. `WIRES.layout` trims the "next
  version" wires inside a deck from the caller's list. `DocView.settle` (12: "boxes never overlap", which pushed the stacked cards apart) runs
  without the cards beneath a deck, which then follow the top card. In an ordered note (a Run) the cards beneath are hidden rather than
  stacked, since that note places its boxes in a column. The version tag's menu gains Spread out (`root.iterSpread`, the versions laid
  beside each other from the first) and Stack as a deck.
- **Troubleshooting** (`CKINDS.BASE`): colour `#B2361F`, `attempts: true` (the strip says "attempt 2 of 3"), `skeleton` "Problem /
  Tried / Outcome" filled in when the kind is set on an empty component and in each new attempt; links: resolves a barrier, leads to a
  resolution, a decision or a method, iterates.

## The Synthesis (`13zzr-synth.js`, `SYNTH`; `02-schema.js`)

- The `reflection` profile is labelled **Synthesis** (stage too); a profile row saved with the old name takes the new one. Its template
  is Summary / What to change next time.
- `SYNTH.gather(d)`: for the experiment's other notes (`CHAIN.members`), every structure of kind claim, evidence, result, takeaway,
  barrier, resolution not yet referenced here is pulled in (`M.pullInto`, `refItemId`) in a column per kind (`COL` 440, `ROW` 150) under
  a heading component (`synthHead`), the first column right of the note's header and template boxes (`SYNTH.start`), a kind new since
  right of the last column; each stage's `synthesis.text` comes along once as a component (`synthFrom`). It runs on its own
  when `CHAIN.advance` makes the Synthesis note, and from ⟲ Gather in the dock.
- `SYNTH.panel(pane, d)`: ☰ The rest — a panel in the note (`.synth-rest`, in `pane.host`) listing every other live component of the
  experiment's notes not referenced here, by stage, each with Pull in (`M.pullInto`, `refBlockId`, at `MOVE.spot`).
