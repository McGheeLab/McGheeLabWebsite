# V3.980 — Scientia: pages, PDFs and whiteboards kept through edits; crowded notes; the edge pan in full screen

Gabe, 11 Nov 2026: "Notes that are ovecrowded sometimes bug with flickering components when zoomed out, and changing a thing or
two in any note causes whiteboards, PDFs, or HTMLs to refresh/take time to reload. The edge pan still needs refinement and should
only work in full screen."

## What was wrong (reproduced)
- **Reloads:** `DocView.fill` removes and rebuilds every box on every change. An iframe (an HTML page, a website, a whiteboard
  preview) reloads whenever it is rebuilt or moved; the PDF viewer, re-used by `PDFW.body`, was moved into the new box, which
  reset its scroll and had its pages released and drawn again. A test on `main` showed new iframes and the PDF back at the top
  after an edit to another component.
- **Crowded notes:** with more than 100 components, boxes far off screen are skipped (`content-visibility: auto`) and report
  a 160 px placeholder height. `settle` ("boxes never overlap") measured those and pushed boxes apart, saving it: a column of
  short boxes 126 px apart became 182 px apart on opening. Zoomed out, boxes at the rim switching between skipped and drawn
  had their pages drawn again; the zoom lines for PDFs (33 %) and flat tiles (10 %) had no room either side.

## Approach
1. **Keep what would be drawn the same.** For the slow kinds only (`DocView.heavy`), build the box on trial (`DocView.shape`,
   `DocView.dry` so the PDF does not take its live viewer) and compare its HTML (without place and selection) and record
   identity with the box on the paper; equal → keep it, set its place and selection; else draw it anew. Comparing the built
   HTML, rather than listing what a box depends on, catches every change the fifteen box wrappers make. Kept boxes are put back
   in order with `moveBefore`, which keeps iframes loaded.
2. **Measure whole:** `settle` lifts the skipping for its measuring (`.cv-measure`). **Skip only zoomed in** (on ≥ 60 %, off < 50 %).
   Hysteresis on the PDF (30 %/37 %) and flat-tile (9 %/11 %) lines.
3. **Edge pan in full screen:** gate on `HANDS.full()`; the screen's edges; a 140 ms rest first (a timer); ease in over 260 ms;
   stop off the edge; the window-mode logic removed.

## Decisions
- Only slow kinds are compared; everything else is drawn anew as before (cheap, and nothing to get wrong).
- Record identity is required: a record replaced (sync, undo) is drawn again even if it looks the same.
- F11 counts as full screen (the window equals the screen), as well as Focus mode.
