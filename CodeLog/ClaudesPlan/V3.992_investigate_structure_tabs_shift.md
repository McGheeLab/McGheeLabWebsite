# V3.992 — Needs investigation as a structure; tabs slide in; Shift+drag by grid cells; Full screen as ⤢

## Goal
Gabe: "Needs investigation needs to be a structure separate from reminder. I want the tab switching to have an updated clean UI animation like the Portfolio switching between projects. Also, when dragging components in the canvas, holding Shift moves components by grid cells rather than fine movement. Change the Full screen button in the pill to be two arrows in opposite corners such as the maximize symbol in HTMLs and PDFs and remove the focus mode description on that button."

## Approach
- **Needs investigation:** a new structure kind (`KIND.investigate`, key X — N and L are the strip's bridge keys, W the term's), lifecycle *open* (closed = investigated). Everything structures have comes with it: the strip, `/investigate`, Settings → Structures, the item menu, search, export. `13zzw-investigate.js` adds what V3.991 promised on top: the dot in the component's header, the Portfolio's breathing dot and Today's section (now reading open structures), and the right-click on a text component. V3.991's reminder kind leaves `13s`; its records (V3.991 lived an afternoon) are turned into structures once, each a component with its words in its note, so nothing is lost.
- **Tabs:** the Portfolio's project switch uses a view transition, which runs the page update a frame later. `App.refresh` is wrapped by other modules (13zzv, the lab layer) and the route is read right after it (the navigation bar, the reader), so a tab switch stays synchronous: the new view gets the Portfolio's `pfin` motion from the side of the tab chosen, the underline already glides (`.tink`), the title fades in.
- **Shift + drag:** the three box drags (one component, a region, a lasso selection) round their offset to 14 px while `ev.shiftKey`; the drop still snaps.
- **Full screen:** the ✦ pill's item (lab layer, as asked; Alex reviews) and the app's dock button: the ⤢ sign (Lucide *maximize-2* in the pill), "Full screen".
