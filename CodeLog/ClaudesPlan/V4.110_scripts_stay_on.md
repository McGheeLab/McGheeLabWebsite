# V4.110 — Scientia: super- and subscripts stay on until ^ (or _) again

## Why
Gabe, 23 Nov: "Superscripts or subscripts need to remain in that notation until the user hits that key shortcut again."

Before, both places that make super- and subscripts dropped back after one character:
- **In a component** (typing replacements, 13u): `^2` gave ² and `CO_2` gave CO₂, but the next digit was plain again (`10^-12` gave 10⁻¹2).
- **In the equation editor** (MathLive, 13h): `smartSuperscript` left a power after its first digit (`x^23` gave x²3).

## What changed
- **In a component (`13u-write.js`):**
  - the replacement that makes ² or ₂ starts a run (`WRITE.mode`): what is typed next is raised (lowered) until `^` (`_`) is typed again;
  - that key ends the run and does not stay in the text;
  - characters with a Unicode super- or subscript form change: digits, + − = ( ), and most letters (all lowercase but q raised; a e h i j k l m n o p r s t u v x lowered; many capitals raised). Others go in as typed, and the run goes on;
  - the run ends when the caret goes elsewhere: Enter, Esc, Tab, the arrows, Home/End, a Ctrl key, a click or tap elsewhere (`selectionchange`), or leaving the text;
  - a typo taken back inside the run keeps it;
  - Backspace right after `^2` puts back `^2` (as before) and ends the run;
  - typing `^` inside a subscript run ends it and may begin a power (`x_1^2` → x₁²);
  - while a run is on, a small hint by the caret says "x² · type ^ to end" (`.wscript`).
- **The trigger is unchanged:** `^` then a digit (or −digit), or a letter, `)` or `]` then `_` then a digit. So `snake_case` or a lone `^` never starts a run.
- **In the equation editor (`13h-math.js`):**
  - `smartSuperscript` is off, so a power stays open;
  - `^` pressed inside a superscript steps out of it (`moveAfterParent`), and `_` inside a subscript the same;
  - `MATH.branch(mf)` reads which part the caret is in (MathLive's model);
  - with a selection, `^` and `_` still wrap it;
  - → and Space still step out.
- **Settings → Writing in components → *Super- and subscripts stay on*** (on). Off: one character, as before, in both places. The preview shows `10⁻¹²` and `x₁₂`, and says which.
- **`MANUAL.md`** says it (the table of replacements, the equation editor).
