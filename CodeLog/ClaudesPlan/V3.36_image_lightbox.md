# V3.36 — Image Lightbox (Zoom/Pan) for Stories & News

## Goal
Users can click any content image on the Research Stories and News pages to open a full-screen lightbox with zoom and pan. This makes figures, diagrams, and photos legible on small screens and for close-up inspection.

## Approach
- A single lightbox DOM overlay is lazily created on first open, appended to `<body>`, and reused for the lifetime of the page.
- Event delegation on `#stories-feed` and `#news-feed` catches clicks on content images. Small identity photos (`.story-feed-avatar`, `.story-team-photo`) and clicks inside buttons/links are ignored.
- Zoom: mouse wheel zooms toward cursor, `+`/`1:1`/`-` buttons, `+`/`-`/`0` keys, double-click toggles fit vs. 2.5×, 2-finger pinch on touch.
- Pan: pointer events (mouse or touch drag when zoomed in).
- Close: X button, backdrop click, or Escape.

## Key decisions
- Vanilla JS, no external library — matches the project's "no frameworks" rule.
- One shared overlay rather than per-image instances, so it survives page re-renders and doesn't duplicate state.
- Delegation only on the research and news feeds (not team/projects/mission), so the behavior is scoped exactly to what was requested. Easy to expand later if desired.
- Skip the small avatar/team identity photos — those aren't content images and zooming them adds noise.
