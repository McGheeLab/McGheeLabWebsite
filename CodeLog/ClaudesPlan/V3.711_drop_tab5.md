# V3.711 — Lesson 4 loses "Now Go and Do It"

`modules/cell_culture/04-aliquoting-and-stewardship.html`. FTP-only; no Firebase deploy.

## The call

Alex: *"get rid of the 'now go and do it' tab"*.

Tab 5 held two things that were doing different jobs, and only one of them was
lesson 4's:

- **§5.1 and the `recap` widget** — a dial through five numbers from *each of the
  four lessons* in the track. A whole-track recap, sitting inside one lesson.
  Deleted.
- **"What to do next"** — take the bench sign-off, work the case files, walk it
  once with someone, then teach it, plus the closing note about every number
  coming with its domain stated. That is the end of the *track*, and deleting it
  would leave the last lesson with no pointer to the sign-off at all. **Kept**, as
  a closing `background-section` on §4.2.

So lesson 4 is now four tabs and ends where the capstone ends: the plan, the
label, the takeaways, then what to do next.

## Placement of the kept block

It went in as a third `background-section` inside §4.2's `lesson-block` rather
than as a new `lesson-block`, because the section rail is built from
`h2[data-nav]` anchors and a standalone block with only an `<h3>` would be an
orphan — present on the page, absent from the rail. §4.2 already carries the
label rules the same way.
