## Three ways, chosen per person in RM Settings → Speech to text

| Way | What runs | Cost (checked 2026-10-23) | Setup |
|---|---|---|---|
| **On this device** (free) | Whisper base.en in the browser, in a module worker (transformers.js 3.8.1 from jsdelivr; the q8 model, ~77 MB, from Hugging Face, downloaded once and kept by the browser) | free | none; "Download it to this device now" |
| **OpenAI Whisper** | `whisper-1`, `verbose_json` segments; the file as it is up to 24 MB, past that 10-minute 16 kHz WAV pieces | $0.006 / min | 5 steps: platform.openai.com, billing, a key, paste, Check |
| **Google Speech-to-Text** | V1 `speech:recognize` with an API key, 55-second LINEAR16 pieces, four at a time; word times → a sentence a line | $0.024 / min, first 60 min a month free | 6 steps: own project, billing, enable the API, a key, restrict it to the API and `https://mcgheelab.com/*`, paste, Check |
| Off | no Transcribe button | | |

**The key never touches the lab.** It lives in the browser (`localStorage` `rm:stt:<uid>`, under the person's sign-in,
so a shared lab computer keeps people apart) and is sent only to its provider, straight from the browser. No
function, no Firestore field, no Storage object. Each device is set up on its own (said on the page). **Check the key**
costs nothing: OpenAI lists models; Google hears a quarter second of silence.

Rates live in one table, `RECORDER_CORE.STT_RATES` (`sttCost(sec, provider)`); the button reads it.

## Where Transcribe appears

- **Scientia (web)**: a recording saved in the lab's cloud shows **Transcribe — about 18¢** (OpenAI, 30 min) or
  **Transcribe on this device — free**, or **Set up speech to text ↗** (opens Settings at `?tab=speech`). Progress
  and Stop in the card. The lines go through Gabe's own `REC.apply` (segs + one sentence a line in the transcript),
  exactly as his desktop Whisper writes them, plus `rec.stt = { provider, model, at }`. His desktop keeps its own
  free Whisper.
- **Board recordings (the replay)**: the owner sees the same button under the replay bar; the lines are kept on the
  recording doc as `transcript: { segs, provider, model, lang, at }` (audio seconds); a transcript panel lists them
  with times; a tap plays from there; the line being spoken lights up (`lineAt`). No rules change: the owner (class
  admin) already writes that doc.
