## What the five remaining failures taught (all in `_fr*` of page-layout.js)

1. **A caption BESIDE its figure** (bennett p3: legend down the left margin, plots to its right) overlaps it in y,
   not x. Seeds now fall back to blobs that overlap the caption's height across a clear horizontal bridge.
2. **A speck is only decoration when it is alone.** The dots of a scatter plot and the samples on a level-set curve
   are 1pt marks a line pitch apart (arxiv-2602 p19); the pre-filter dropped them before clustering and the rest
   died as "decoration". Tiny primitives are kept, chained to anything within 1.6·bodyH, and only an isolated
   speck is discarded. On a page with no prose at all, panels may sit up to 8 % of the page apart.
3. **A caption at the head of a page that paints nothing is the legend of the plate before it** ("caption on next
   page"); Alex labels that block as the figure's region (agarwal p10, p12), so it is one.
4. **A bordered text box is not a figure**: an orphan holding two or more text lines spanning 60 % of its width
   is a callout ("Cite this paper", bai-doo p1) unless real artwork fills 15 % of it (a logo does not rescue it);
   page-1 orphans in the bottom fifth are footers.
5. Set 2 (`label-figures.html`, 20 new PDFs) is still unlabelled — Alex labelled set 1 in the full harness instead,
   which is fine: it feeds both the figure scorer and the region classifier.
