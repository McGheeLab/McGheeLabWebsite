# V3.860 — Pen, pan and zoom are the three modes of the pen; a double tap goes back to the last one

**Date:** 2026-10-23
**Alex:** *"the pan button and zoom should be in the pen as a mode, the mouse is either a pan hand, zoom hand, or pen. double
tap changes modes to last mode used"*

## Today
On host boards (the lab notebook and a paper's figure board, the radial-pill boards), `S.tool` is `pen` / `eraser` / `pan` /
`zoom` / `fitregion` / `region` / `group`. ✋ Pan and 🔍 Zoom live in the **View** pill (⊕) next to zoom ± / home / fit; the
**Pens** pill (✎) holds the pen set, eraser, widths, undo and clear. Zoom mode zooms ×1.5 on every pointer-down. Nothing
remembers the previous mode, and the cursor never changes. A pill's double tap resets the pill to its default place.

## Design
- **The hand has three modes, all in the ✎ pill.** Its fan starts with ✎ Pen · ✋ Pan · 🔍 Zoom (the current one selected),
  then the pens, eraser, widths, ＋ pen / highlighter, undo, clear. The pill's own icon is the mode (✎ with the ink colour
  dot, ✋, or 🔍) and it lights up in pan / zoom / eraser. The View pill keeps zoom ±, home, fit everything and fit a region.
  The lecture whiteboard's linear toolbar is untouched.
- **The cursor is the mode**: `.wb-hand-pan` → grab (grabbing while dragging), `.wb-hand-zoom` → zoom-in (zoom-out with
  Alt held), pen → crosshair; classes on the board root, set by `trackMode()`.
- **The zoom hand**: a tap zooms in ×1.5 at the point, Alt / Shift / right button zooms out; a drag up or down zooms smoothly
  about where it started (up = in). A tap's zoom waits the double-tap window (300 ms) so a double tap can cancel it, and once it has zoomed the next tap starts afresh — tap · tap · tap at a steady pace zooms three times.
- **Last mode**: `trackMode()` (called on every pill render, i.e. every tool change) remembers the previous hand mode in
  `S.lastTool` whenever `S.tool` moves between `pen` · `eraser` · `pan` · `zoom`; region tools don't count. `toggleLastMode()`
  switches to it (default: pen ↔ pan); going back to the pen restores the last real pen if an eraser from the set was in hand.
  A short caption under the pill says where you are ("✋ Pan — double-tap to go back").
- **Double tap**:
  - on the **✎ pill** — always. A single tap opens the fan after 230 ms unless a second tap arrives (so a double tap never
    flashes the fan); the pill's reset-to-default-place moves to the other pills only.
  - on the **canvas**, two taps within 300 ms and 24 px (each under 300 ms and 8 px of travel):
    - a **finger** — any mode (fingers never ink);
    - a **mouse** — pan, zoom and pen; in pen mode the two dots those taps drew are removed (`removeStrokes`);
    - a **stylus** — pan and zoom only: in pen mode two quick taps are a colon, an ellipsis or an umlaut, never a switch;
    - never in eraser mode with a mouse or stylus (the taps would have erased).

## Revision — two arcs (same day)

**Alex:** *"the first arc should be zoom, pan, pen, highlighter, text — the second arc should be the options for the thing
selected in the first arc."* The inner ring holds exactly five places, so the five modes fill it and every option starts on the
second ring. Choosing a mode keeps the fan open and redraws its options. Highlighter becomes a mode of its own (a pen in hand is
`pen` or `hl` by kind); the eraser stays a pen option. **Text** is new: a tap places a typed-note card (the existing `note` kind,
so it is movable, connectable and searchable like any card) with the caret in it; S / M / L set the card width (a note's type
scales with its width) and three tints its colour; a tap away from an empty one removes it, so a stray tap leaves nothing.
Icons come from the site's monochrome set (the parallel icon sweep's rule), with plain-letter fallbacks.
