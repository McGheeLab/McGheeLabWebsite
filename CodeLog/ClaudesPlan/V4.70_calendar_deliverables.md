# V4.70 — Scientia Calendar: deliverables, on an event or at the end of the day

## Why
Gabe, 19 Nov: "Deliverables can be attached to an event or end of day, asking you to attach that deliverable, logging it in the note that it is assigned to, otherwise it becomes a float. Deliverables are inherently a structure such as result, takeaway, a kind, such as a verdict, or a mini synthesis into a region." Asked again on 20 Nov.

## What changed
- **A deliverable** is a record in the events store with `kind: 'deliv'`:
  - `start` is when it is due;
  - `deliv` is `{what, docId, eventId, state, blockId, regionId}`, where `what` is one of `result`, `takeaway`, `verdict` or `synth`, and `state` is `open`, `done` or `dropped`.
  - It is never drawn as a card, an all-day item or a bell item, and never gathers a region.
- **On an event:** the event's card (in place) has a **Deliver** row.
  - The chips are Result · Takeaway · Verdict · Mini synthesis, with an optional name, then Add.
  - It is due when the event ends, and follows the event if the event moves (`CAL.dvWhen`).
  - Its note is the event's note (one occurrence's note for a repeat).
- **At the end of a day:** the create window has a tab, Event · Deliverable (· Agent run).
  - It is due at 18:00 (End of day), or at a time you set.
  - It takes the same four chips and a note to link, or none.
  - On the planner it is a chip at its time.
- **Once due:**
  - the event's card shows an Attach button;
  - the bell counts it and lists it under "Deliverables due";
  - Today lists it under "From your calendar";
  - its note asks at the top of the canvas, with Attach… and Not needed.
- **Attach** (`CAL.attach`, `dvApply`) opens a window with the latest components of its note, and a box to write it in.
  - **Result or Takeaway:** the component gets that structure over its text (an item and a `struct` mark).
  - **Verdict:** the component gets the Verdict kind.
  - **Mini synthesis:** the component joins the event's region (from V4.69). An end-of-day one gets a region of what was written that day in the note, named after the deliverable.
  - **Written in the window:** it is placed under the event's region or under the note's last component.
  - **No note:** what is written goes into today's day note as a float, waiting for the note it belongs to.
- **Settings → Calendar:** "Deliverables: what an event or a day should produce" (on by default), in the preview.
