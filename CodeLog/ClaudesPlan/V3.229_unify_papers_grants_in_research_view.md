# V3.229 — Unify papers + grants between the research view and the builders

## Goal

The Research page (`/rm/pages/projects.html`, `PAGE_CATEGORY='research'`) and
the dedicated builder pages (`paper-builder.html`, `grant-builder.html`)
currently maintain independent copies of papers and grants:

| Source | Papers | Grants |
|---|---|---|
| `projects/papers.json` (paper-builder) | 3 rows | — |
| `grantDrafts/` Firestore collection (grant-builder) | — | drafts |
| `items.json` (research view) | 2 duplicates + 8 library entries | 6 rows (2 drafting, 4 active/awarded) |

Creating a paper in the paper-builder leaves the research view unaware of
it, and vice-versa. The user wants a single source of truth so a paper or
grant created in either place shows up in both.

## Approach

**Papers — full unification.** `projects/papers.json` is already the
paper-builder's source. Stop showing items.json papers on the research
view (they're either duplicates of paper-builder rows or library entries
that already have their own surface). Render paper cards from
`projects/papers.json`. "+ Add Item" type=paper redirects to the
paper-builder's create modal.

**Grants — additive unification.** Render the **union** of `grantDrafts/`
(drafts) and items.json type=grant (active/awarded) so the four real
awarded-grant entries (`mcghee-startup`, `mcghee-triff`,
`cancer-engineering-prostate`, `cancer-engineering-tf-elisa`) keep their
existing accounting/repo metadata that the draft schema doesn't model.
"+ Add Item" type=grant redirects to the grant-builder so new entries
land in `grantDrafts/` going forward. The two drafting items.json grants
(`prostate-gels-r01`, `mri-lymphatic-r01`) will be migrated to
`grantDrafts/` as follow-up work (deferred from this version because the
migration touches production Firestore and warrants its own review).

## Files modified

- [rm/js/paper-builder.js](../../rm/js/paper-builder.js) — auto-open the
  create modal when the URL carries `?new=1`.
- [rm/js/grant-builder.js](../../rm/js/grant-builder.js) — same `?new=1`
  hook so the research view's "+ Add Item" can deep-link straight into
  the create modal.
- [rm/js/projects.js](../../rm/js/projects.js):
  - In `loadAll()`, additionally load grantDrafts via `firebridge.db()`
    and convert papers from `_canonicalProjects` + grantDrafts into the
    item shape so the renderer treats them uniformly.
  - Filter items.json papers out of the research view entirely (they're
    duplicates of paper-builder rows or library entries).
  - In the "+ Add Item" handler, when the chosen type is `paper` redirect
    to `/rm/pages/paper-builder.html?new=1`; for `grant` redirect to
    `/rm/pages/grant-builder.html?new=1`.

## Key decisions

- **No items.json data migration in this version.** The four
  active/awarded grants are real funded accounts whose
  `award_number`/`funding_account_ids`/`start_date`/`end_date` fields
  aren't modeled in `grantDrafts/`'s draft-centric schema. Migrating
  them would either lose data or require extending the grant-builder
  schema — both bigger than the scope here. The two drafting items.json
  grants could be moved cleanly, but the migration touches production
  Firestore and is better done in its own version after we have a
  reversible dry-run plan.
- **No grant-builder refactor.** It keeps writing directly to the
  `grantDrafts/` collection via `firebridge.db()`. The research view
  reads the same collection directly when assembling its item list — no
  new `api-routes.js` entry needed.
- **Editing affordances on the research view stay where they are.**
  Card-level "Members" / "Tasks" / "Links" sections continue to operate
  on items.json (for items.json-sourced rows) or via
  `saveProjectDocSurgical` (for `projects/papers.json` rows, already
  wired up in V3.126). For grants sourced from `grantDrafts/`, the deep
  editor lives in the grant-builder — clicking the card title opens it.

## Out of scope (follow-up)

- Migrating `prostate-gels-r01` and `mri-lymphatic-r01` from items.json
  to `grantDrafts/`.
- A "promote draft → awarded grant" flow that moves a `grantDrafts/`
  entry into items.json once awarded (so awarded grants live alongside
  drafts in a single, lifecycle-aware collection).
- Read-only display of paper-builder/grant-builder metadata on the
  research card (target journal, deadline, agency, etc.).
