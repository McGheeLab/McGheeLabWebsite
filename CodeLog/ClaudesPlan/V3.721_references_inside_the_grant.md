# V3.721 — The bibliography moves inside the grant

## Goal

Alex: *"The references for each grant should be within the grant card. for
example the CAREER grant if i click on it i see all the aims, outputs people
etc, and I want a new section in this with the references cited."*

## Undoing V3.718's indirection

V3.718 gave references their own card and their own `reference` level. I had
offered exactly this section-not-a-card option at the time and Alex picked the
level; seeing it built, he corrected. He was right, and the reason is worth
recording: **the page anyone actually opens is the grant's.** Sending them to a
second card to read that grant's bibliography is a click that buys nothing —
the bibliography has no life independent of the grant it belongs to.

So `ref_source` moved onto the grant card, and the `reference` level is retired.
`research-references.js` survives unchanged in substance: same renderer, new
host.

## Shape of the section

Inline, **last**, and **collapsed** — a `<details>` whose summary carries the
live count.

- *Last*, because a bibliography is material to look something up in, not a
  status anyone scans the page for. Aims, outputs and people come first.
- *Collapsed*, because 103 entries expanded would bury everything the page
  exists to show. The count is visible without opening it, which is the part
  people actually want at a glance.

Opening it reveals the full grouped-by-year list in place, with no navigation.

## What did NOT change

The 103 references stay exactly where they were — in the lab library (`items`,
`type:'paper'`, `meta.library.cited_in_drafts`), stored once. The card still
holds a pointer, never a copy, so citing something new in the builder still
appears here with no re-sync.

## A symptom that fixed itself

Alex had reported "References Cited" showing under Concepts twice. The V3.718
diagnosis was right — an unknown level was being coerced to `'concept'` by a
build that predated the level — and the V3.718 fix was right, but it could only
take effect once that code was the code being served, and there was no data-side
workaround. **Deleting the card removes the symptom outright, on every build,
deployed or not.**

The `levelMeta` hardening from V3.718 stays regardless. The lesson it encodes is
worth more than the card that prompted it: when data and code ship separately,
the data arrives first at least once, and an enum that coerces unknown values to
a real member turns that into a silent misfiling instead of a visible gap.

## Migration

`scripts/seed_gels_career_references.js` rewritten to migrate rather than
create: stamps `ref_source` on `gels-career`, then deletes
`gels-career-references` **only after** the grant carries the pointer, so a
failure cannot leave the bibliography unreachable from anywhere. The card held
no unique data — only the same pointer — so nothing was lost.

## Verified

Against live data (14 cards, five levels, no `reference` anywhere): the grant
review's section order ends `… Work under this grant · 📚 References cited 103`,
collapsed by default, 103 rows across 24 year-groups inline once opened, the two
DOI-less entries flagged, and no link away to any card. The hub shows four bands
with Concepts holding only its two real concepts and the word "references"
appearing nowhere. 40 + 17 node assertions green.
