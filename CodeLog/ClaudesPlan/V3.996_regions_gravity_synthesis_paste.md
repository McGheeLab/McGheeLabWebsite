# V3.996 — Regions aside, gravity, synthesis flags, paste at the centre

## Goal
Gabe (14 Nov 2026), six points: regions push others to the side when expanded; collapsing pulls back what the expansion displaced, by the same amount; a Synthesis brings only the flagged item (no extra "Results" / "Flagged for synthesis" component); Flag for synthesis on Ctrl+Enter as an excerpt; right-click flags only the whole component; /synthesize asks which Synthesis for the selected components; paste and inserts at the centre of the view, on top.

## Approach
- **Build on REFLOW (13vb, V3.948), not beside it.** It already moved the neighbours on a fold and pushed them back on the unfold, but a push by an unfold was not remembered, so a later fold pulled by the height that went. Now every push is recorded in its list (`b.shift`, `push: true`, the pusher's height), from an unfold and from settle (a box growing as you type), and the fold gives each back by exactly as much. A shrink without a fold (12 settle) gives back too; the chain follows. A first attempt that did this separately in 13v moved things twice and was taken out.
- **Regions aside, whole (`REFLOW.aside`):** the other regions an unfolded region would cover move to the side as units (all their components, a folded one by its title), recorded like the rest. Settle waits while a region's reflow is pending, then REFLOW settles — otherwise the boxes went down before REFLOW could move them sideways.
- **Synthesis:** columns are kept on the note (`d.synthCols`), so nothing but the thing itself is made; old header components are used as columns. The Ctrl+Enter strip got an extension point (`ED.stripExtra`) rather than a wrapper (its own key handler would have stayed attached). /synthesize is its own command and always asks; Ctrl+Enter → F asks only when there is a choice.
- **Paste and inserts:** the centre of the view, and `b.float` (the existing "put on top on purpose" mark settle respects) on what they make, caught at `M.addBlock` during the paste or the insert (and, for a file picked later, by the spot the insert asked).
- **Gabe's V3.995 rule:** Flag for synthesis is registered as a feature; the new settings (where things land, gravity, regions aside) are real and their previews follow them.
