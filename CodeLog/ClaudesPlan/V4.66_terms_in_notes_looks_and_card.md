# V4.66 — Scientia: terms in notes — three readable looks (A by default) and one card, the Ledger with a timeline down its left

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "What I meant is the term look inside of notes themselves. The outline is distracting, hard to read, and highlighting text makes it go invisible. Give me three options on how to repurpose and relook it in the note canvas. I have an idea in mind already: hovering on it for 1 second brings a quick card of neighboring notes uses of it along with a metric dashboard style."

After the options on the design canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/KGVrFzrVNnKp3KQwdRoyFV): "Include all term options in settings, with Option A as default. The dashboard should be universal across all, and it needs some work. Bring three new designs to light." Then: "I like the ledger as the default view, with an accompanied timeline vertical, almost like a left side panel to the popup, where the viewer can interact with it just like the Timeline hover option."

Terms in a note were bold hollow letters (`color: transparent` with a text stroke). They were hard to read at body size, and when selected the transparent fill let the stroke sink into the selection colour, so the term vanished.

## What changed
- **Three looks** (`16c-termlook.js` `TLOOK`; `40-termlook.css`; Settings → Terms → "Terms in your notes", tune `look`). The choice is a class on the body (`tm-a` · `tm-b` · `tm-c`). All three are filled text, so a term stays readable and visible when selected; the outline is gone from notes and term bubbles.
  - **A · Quiet underline** (the default): ordinary text with a dotted teal line under it, solid under the pointer.
  - **B · Soft tag:** a faint teal tint, a little heavier.
  - **C · Ghost until you look:** plain words; a faint dotted line while the pointer is over the term's component.
  - Terms off: plain words.
- **One card for every look** (`TLOOK.card`, through `ED.termCard`). It opens after about a second of resting on a term (`hoverCard(…, 950)`; `hoverCard` gained a `wait`, and now cancels if the pointer leaves before then).
  - **The Ledger:** the term and its project, first used; uses · notes · this month (▲/▼ against the 4 weeks before) on hairlines; its 12 weeks as one soft line; open barriers.
  - **The pointed note:** its title, its relation to this note (same experiment, run before or after, same theme, same project, lit review), date and count, and its sentence with the term in bold. A click opens it there.
  - **The nearest notes** in time, then "All N notes →" and Open the term (its lens).
  - **The timeline down its left side:** every note that uses it, placed by date and sized by how often, this note ringed. Pointing at a dot shows its note and sentence in the Ledger; a click opens it there.
  - On a narrow screen the timeline folds away.
- The data comes from the index (`IX.termOcc`, lit reviews as Settings say through `TERMLIT`), counted as the Terms tab counts (`TSTAT.week0`).
