# V3.179 — Training: Tasks &amp; PMR in RM

> Status: PLANNED — not yet built. Infrastructure landed in V3.177.

## Goal

The two surfaces that drive accountability and 1:1s in the lab. By the
end, the student understands the bucket + subtask + tier-3-child
hierarchy, can pin a proposed subtask from Inbox to a real bucket, and
can complete a PMR weekly entry that's actually useful for Alex.

## Format

Lecture-style with three interactive widgets:

1. **The bucket model diagram** — interactive 3-tier tree: project
   bucket → sub-bucket (category / sub_category) → subtask (with
   nested children up to 3 levels). Click a tier to read its role.
2. **Task triage simulator** — a mocked Inbox with 5 proposed subtasks
   (some from email-review, some from calendar). The user pins each to
   the right bucket; each correct pin earns a point. Wrong pin → red
   feedback + the right answer.
3. **PMR weekly entry composer** — a stripped-down weekly-entry form
   (date range + what got done + barriers + next-week intent). On
   submit (preview only — no Firestore write) it shows what Alex would
   see during the 1:1.

## Sections (10)

1. Why tasks + PMR exist as a pair.
2. The bucket + subtask model.
3. Creating buckets and sub-buckets.
4. Subtasks — due dates, hours estimates, evidence, the 3-level child
   tree.
5. The "proj-inbox" Proposed bucket and pinning workflow.
6. Cross-user assignment (admin → student; student → student;
   the assigned-inbox tab badge).
7. PMR overview — what it is, who reads it, how often to update.
8. PMR weekly entries.
9. Barriers, paper writing, figure reviews — the three first-class
   blocks (V3.159 / V3.161).
10. Capstone — file your first weekly entry + close at least one
    subtask.
