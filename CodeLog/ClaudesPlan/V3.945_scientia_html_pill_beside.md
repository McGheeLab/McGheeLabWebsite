# V3.945 — Scientia: the ✎ pill over a maximised HTML page; fingers scroll it while the pen writes; HTML pages read beside the note

## Goal

Gabe:

> "The pill should be inside the maximized HTML rather than from the outside, since now, you can't scroll through an
> HTML, or select text unless you go out of maximizing. Also allow the user to read the HTML beside the note."

## Why it happened

- A maximised page (`EMBED.max`, 13g) is a full-window overlay (`.embed-max`, z-index 1200) on `document.body`.
- The ✎ pill (lab-mode §11) lives in the note's canvas host (`.lab-pills`, z-index 21), so it was under the overlay.
- In pen mode, the pen (and the mouse) writes on the page (V3.943), so it can't select text. The page's
  `touch-action: none` also stopped fingers from scrolling it. The only way to switch to Move was to leave the
  maximised view.

## Approach (no lab-layer change)

1. **The pill over the page** (`HTMLX.lift`, 13zze):
   - When an HTML page fills the window (maximised, or the beside-reader at full screen), the canvas host of that
     note's pane gets `.mx-pills`.
   - CSS (21-sync) lifts that host's `.lab-pills` above the overlay (z-index 1250, under popovers and dialogs at
     1300) and hides every pill but ✎. Gabe's pen settings bar (`.ink-bar`, opened from ✎'s cog) is lifted too.
   - If the ✎ pill would sit outside the page area (`.mx-main`), on the highlights panel, the layer is moved by
     `--mx-dx` / `--mx-dy` so the pill is inside, 12 px from the edge.
   - This is the real pill, lab-mode's own, with its fan, double tap and dock. Nothing is copied: no ancestor of
     `.lab-pills` starts a stacking context, so its z-index can reach above the overlay.
   - It is redone when the maximised view or the reader opens, goes full screen, comes back or closes; when an
     overlay leaves the body (a MutationObserver); on resize; and after the pill is dropped. Throttled to one frame.
2. **Fingers scroll the page in pen mode** (`HTMLX.INKSCRIPT`):
   - The page keeps `touch-action: none` while inking, because the pen must not pan it. A single finger now scrolls
     whatever is scrollable under it (the nearest scrolling element, else the page).
   - A palm is ignored the way the app's palm rejection does it (`SIS_PALM`, 13zf): a contact larger than `SIZE`, a
     touch within `QUIET` ms of the pen, or a second finger. `inkOn()` sends `[SIZE, QUIET]` to the page.
3. **An HTML page beside the note** (13zzb `RDOCK`, extended):
   - `RDOCK.KINDS` = pdf, html. For a page, the bar is the title, Highlights, ⇄, ⤢ and Close. 13zze adds ✎ Note and
     Export ink labels by wrapping `RDOCK.open`.
   - `RDOCK.draw` puts the page in once (`EMBED.frame`, with `EMBED._maxId` naming the block, so 13zw fills it with
     the quote, state and ink scripts). It is put in again only when the file changes, so going full screen and back
     doesn't reload it.
   - The card says "Reading beside the note" (`.embed.rd-away`). `RDOCK.card(b)` redraws an HTML card, which
     `DOCS.repaint` doesn't cover.
   - Page stepping, zoom, Ctrl+scroll and ← → stay PDF-only.
   - Entries: the book in the card's tools, after ⛶; and "◧ Beside" in the maximised page's bar, which closes the
     maximised view and opens the reader.
   - It belongs to the note as a PDF does: saved in `rdock:<doc>`, back when the note is, closed when it isn't.

## Tests (headless Chrome, the desktop build)

- **Maximised in pen mode:**
  - `.mx-pills` was set, and the ✎ pill was on top (`elementFromPoint`). It was inside the page area, moved from
    x 1324 to 996 to clear the highlights panel, and the other pills were hidden.
- **In the page** (scroll read through an isolated world in the page's frame):
  - a finger drag scrolled it 0 → 300 px;
  - a palm-sized touch (120 px) didn't scroll it.
- **The pill tapped for real:** its fan opened, and Move turned the page's pen off.
- **◧ Beside, clicked for real:**
  - the maximised view closed and the reader opened with the page;
  - the card said "Reading beside";
  - the bar had ✎ Note, Export ink labels, Highlights, ⇄, ⤢, Close.
- **A pen stroke in the reader** was kept on the page (`htmlInk`, 1 stroke).
- **Full screen:** the pill was lifted and on top. Back beside the note, it wasn't.
- **The note drawn again** kept the reader.
- **Close:** the page went back in its card, with the book after ⛶.
- **Unchanged:**
  - V3.944's PDF beside-reader test still passes;
  - harnesses: lab-mode 274/278 (the four known), notes 13/13, notebook 45/45.
