# V4.21 — Rotate: the whole app, a quarter turn clockwise at a time

## Goal
Gabe (17 Nov 2026): "Add a Rotate button to the Zoom pill, flipping the entire app by 90 degrees clockwise." He allowed the lab-layer edit (the ⊕ Zoom pill is `rm/scientia/lab-mode.js` §11; Alex reviews), and asked that the pills reach his own repo too (V4.20).

## Approach
- **The pill (lab layer, §11):** ⊕'s fifth item, after in · 100 % · out · fit, is Rotate, with Lucide's rotate-cw icon (`PILL_ICONS['rotate-cw']`).
  - Each tap calls the app's `ROTATE.turn()`; the wheel stays open for the next tap. It is shown only while the app has ROTATE and it is on.
  - V4.20's cut (`vendor/lab-pills.js`) is regenerated, so his repo has the same item.
  - The v3869 harness now expects five ⊕ items, with a check that Rotate turns the app and that it comes back upright.
- **The turn (`scientia/src/13zzz-rotate.js` `ROTATE`, `32-rotate.css`):** `<body>` becomes the turned frame: fixed, its own size (width and height swapped at 90° and 270°), `transform: translate(...) rotate(...)` about its top-left corner. The app is laid out in that frame, so it is simply upright in it. Everything fixed inside (menus, dialogs, toasts) turns with it.
- **Coordinates:** the app's own math (canvas pan and zoom, ink, drags, menus, hit tests) works in the frame, because while turned the browser's coordinates are given in it. The patches are installed once and pass straight through while upright. They cover:
  - pointer positions and movement: `MouseEvent` / `PointerEvent` / `Touch` `clientX`, `clientY`, `pageX`, `pageY`, `x`, `y`, `movementX`, `movementY`. Only events the browser made are mapped; a script's own event is already in the frame.
  - geometry: `Element` / `Range` `getBoundingClientRect` and `getClientRects`;
  - hit tests and carets: `document.elementFromPoint`, `elementsFromPoint`, `caretRangeFromPoint`, `caretPositionFromPoint`;
  - window size: `innerWidth`, `innerHeight`, and the root's `clientWidth` / `clientHeight`;
  - the edge pan's push from the computer's mouse counter (`HANDS.feed`).
- **Viewport units:** CSS sizes in vw / vh (dialog and popover caps) would still measure the screen. At a quarter turn, `#rot-units` holds the same rules with vw and vh swapped, built from the page's own sheets each time. It uses `:where(.rot-q)`, so it adds no specificity, and leaves out the turn's own `html.rot*` rules.
- **Kept on this device** (`_ls 'rotate'`). The way out is never lost: Esc three times, `/rotate reset`, `?rotate=0`, Settings → Upright, or the switch off.
- **Settings → Pen & touch → Rotate the app** (`rotate`, in Simple too):
  - the switch (off: upright, with the turn kept for later; no Rotate in ⊕; `/rotate` refuses);
  - "On this device, the app is" Upright / 90° / 180° / 270°;
  - a live preview of the screen with the app turned in it.

## What it can't turn
- The browser's own popups stay upright on the screen: a `<select>`'s list, a `title` tooltip, a drag's ghost image.
- CSS `@media` widths and `matchMedia` follow the real screen: a narrow phone turned keeps its narrow layout.
- Mouse wheels scroll along the app's own axes, as the turned lists do natively.
- Pages inside iframes are their own documents, which the browser maps for itself.
