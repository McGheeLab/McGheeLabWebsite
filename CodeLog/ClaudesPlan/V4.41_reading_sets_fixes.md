# V4.41 — Reading sets: popup × back in the reader, the open set folds, shelves place papers, the reader kept across a reload

## Goal
Gabe (19 Nov 2026): "popup closing should bring it back in the reader. Clicking on a reading set collapses it. A new shelf and new focus added to TF-ELISA still opens in Auto In-vitro. My reader, after hard reload, was not saved. … the cited in should go to the note when you click on the note it is cited in, without closing the reader."

## What happened
- **Auto In-Vitro.** A paper read from the Library sits on the reader's own literature note. That note is made in `READER.project()`, the first project unless one was chosen. V4.38 stopped such notes counting as appearances. But going to the note made `RSET.follow` pick that note's project's set (`keyOf`), and `RDX.goTab` filed the tab there. Shelves assigned to projects (V4.30) were never asked.
- **The reload.** `RDOCK.load` and `RSET.adopt` keep only tabs whose card and file are here (`RDOCK.live`), and an empty row sets `open: false`. If the state was read before the notebook's cards were in (a slow start, a sync on its way), the next save (`RSET.use` from the first `follow`) wrote that empty, closed state over the real one. A probe reproduced it: read and saved with the two cards hidden, the kept state had no tab and `open: false`.

## Approach
- **Where a paper belongs (17i):**
  - `RSET.shelved(b)`: the projects of the shelves of the foci its Library entry is in;
  - `appears` counts them;
  - `home` takes, in order: the note it is a card in (in a project), its shelf's project (of several, the one where it appears most), then where it appears most;
  - `RSET.docKey(d)`: the reader's own literature note follows its paper's home (else "No project"), never the default project. `ctxKey`, `keyOfBlock`, the tab-follow wrapper and `RDX.goTab` use it;
  - `target`: away from a note there is no "project you are in", so the paper's home comes first (before: the set shown).
- **Fold the open set:** `RSET.fold(v)` / `RSET.folded`. A click on the open band (its name, or the band outside its tabs; Enter on the name) folds it into its chip (`.rs-cur`, filled), and the paper read stays. The chip opens it again, and changing set unfolds.
- **Popup ×:** `RPOP.back(p)`. In a note, the paper goes back in the reader: a tab of its reading set (the `RDOCK.open` wrapper's `target`), read beside at the same page and zoom (Fit kept), clip mode kept. On a page that isn't a note, its card shows it again and the paper waits in its set (`RSET.carry(id, null)`). With reading tabs off, back in its card, as before.
- **Cited in / the title (V4.40's hook):** `RSET.carry(id, docId)` puts the paper in that note's set, shown there, before going, so the reader stays on it when the set changes.
- **The reader kept:**
  - `RSET.keep(id)`: a tab whose card is not here yet is kept; one deleted since (its card there, not live) goes;
  - `adopt` keeps them, and `open` stays open while they are on their way;
  - `sync` writes the row plus the tabs not here yet;
  - 13zzb's `load` / `save` do the same for the flat row;
  - nothing is saved before `window.__ready`;
  - `RSET.ready()` reads the state again then and brings the reader back with the note;
  - `RSET.arrived()`: when cards that were missing arrive (a change), their tabs come back in the row, and the reader opens if it was open.
- **Settings:**
  - Reading sets by project: the words (shelves, folding) and the preview (its name folds the open set, its chip opens it);
  - Popup windows: the words.
