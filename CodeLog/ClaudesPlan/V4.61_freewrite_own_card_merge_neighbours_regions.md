# V4.61 — Scientia Freewrite: highlighted words are one whole card; ＋ between neighbouring components; paragraphs as regions

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "highlighting a set of sentences or a portion of it, and selecting a new component assumes that the highlighted portion becomes a whole component, merging the components together, and separates it from the rest of the freewrite, as its own card. Each component is its own card."

"The merging of sentences individually stands, but also applies to components one right after the other"

"You can draw regions over sentences with a button on the side of paragraphs to click to transform it into a region."

What happened before, in the default mode (a sentence no rule reads stays in the component before it):
- words highlighted across two sentences became a component, but the run of sentences kept them too, so they appeared twice;
- a portion inside a sentence lost the rest of that sentence;
- the sentences after the highlight were pulled into the new component.

V4.53's "no repeats" only held in "Each its own component".

## What changed
- **Your words are carved out after everything else is read** (`FREEW.convert`). The associations (`fw.assoc`) and your phrase rules no longer run before the sentences are read; they run at the end.
  - The words become one whole component (`carve`). Every component they reach gives them up and keeps the rest of its words, on both sides; its sentences after them stay where they were.
  - A component's words are joined as they are written (`textOf`): a stop where the words had one, " … " where words between them were taken out, else a space.
  - Links and cues inside the words go. A link you drew to the new component keeps working, since `off` is worked out after the carving.
  - A rule of yours does the same, except inside a component that a two- or one-part rule found; there its words stay part of it, as before.
  - The components are listed in the order they are written, so the preview's cards and the note follow your words.
  - A component that runs over several sentences shows its label once, where it starts.
- **A new paragraph starts a new component** in the default mode, so a paragraph's region holds its own components.
- **The merge ＋ between any two components one right after the other.** Nothing but spaces and stops may be between them, they must be in the same paragraph, and it works in every mode (V4.55 had it only between single sentences in "Each its own component").
  - Merged groups are joined by their first component in the words. Each part keeps its structure, and two kinds or two titles are flagged as in V4.55.
- **Regions.** A button at the side of each paragraph (`FREEW.paras`, `paraRegion`) makes it a region, kept by the words of its first sentence (`fw.regions`). Its button again makes it plain.
  - It is drawn over its sentences: a dashed box, with its name and its count at its corner. The name is the paragraph's first words; a click renames it, and × makes it plain.
  - Selected words offer **A region** beside "Make these words a component…" (`wordsRegion`).
  - A region's components are those that start in it (`conv.regions`). The preview keeps them together and draws the region round their boxes.
  - **Apply** groups them in the note as a region with that name (`REGION.create`, kept in `fw.madeRg`, made once). **Take back** takes it out again.
