# V3.523 — Chat: navigation + search pinned at the top

**Alex request**: the chat navigation and search should stay fixed at
the top while scrolling through messages.

Since the DOCUMENT is the chat page's real scroller (V3.522), the chrome
pins with `position: sticky` below RM's own sticky `.top-nav` (56px,
z-index 100):

- **Desktop**: `renderShell` wraps the header + view-tabs slots in a new
  `.chat-sticky-top` (sticky, `top: var(--chat-nav-h)`, z-index 30). The
  channel/DM **sidebar** also pins (`min-width: 701px` only — mobile
  never renders it): sticky + `align-self: flex-start` +
  `max-height: calc(100vh - nav)`, which finally activates its own
  `overflow-y: auto` as an independent scroller.
- **Mobile (≤700px)**: the Overview/Chat/Files/Search tab bar pins at
  `var(--chat-nav-h)` and the channel header below it at
  `var(--chat-nav-tab-h)`.
- **Offsets are JS-measured** (`updateStickyOffsets()`, called from
  `render()` and the resize handler), not hardcoded: the top-nav is
  absent in embed popouts and its height shifts across breakpoints.
  A third var `--chat-chrome-b` (bottom edge of all pinned chrome) keeps
  the R3 date separators and the R6 history banner stacking BELOW the
  chrome instead of sliding under it.
- **Enabling change**: `.chat-layout` and `.chat-main` had
  `overflow: hidden` — a layout no-op in RM (no bounded heights) but a
  sticky-killer (sticky anchors to the nearest clipping ancestor). Both
  are now `overflow: visible`.

Files: `rm/js/chat.js` (shell wrap, `updateStickyOffsets`), `rm/css/chat.css`, `sw.js`. FTP-only.

Verify: scroll up through history on desktop — channel name, tabs, and
search stay visible under the RM nav; sidebar stays put and scrolls
internally. Mobile: tab bar + channel header pin; date separators tuck
under the header, not on top of it. Embed popout (no RM nav): chrome
pins at the very top.
