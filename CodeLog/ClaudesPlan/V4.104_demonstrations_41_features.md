# V4.104 — Scientia: demonstrations for 41 more features

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "Propose each one for me to approve, which will pass as a new pull request", then, on the 42 proposals: "go ahead".

Each feature's page in Settings shows what it is for and a recording of it being used in a made-up test note (V4.12). Before this, 42 features had none (`FEAT.undemoed()`). This version records 41 of them, all as proposed and approved. One is left: RM highlights in the Library (`labhl`, #28), which needs the lab's hub, and the recorder has none.

## How
- One script per feature, `scientia/demokit/<id>.mjs`. Each holds the approved sentence as `use`, a made-up test note in `setup` (the project "Traction on soft gels"), and the steps, driven with the real pointer and keys.
- The scripts were written by five helpers, each in its own worktree from one brief. The final takes were recorded together on one build (`demokit/all.mjs`).
- Two other helpers, who had not written them, judged every take from the frame at the end of each step (GOOD / MINOR / BAD). The five BAD steps were fixed and recorded again:
  - **expseries:** headless Chrome's own list did not fire its change, so the name and "Run 3 of…" stayed empty. The list is now closed with Escape and picked with ↓.
  - **pills:** the Quick cast was closed again before the step's end; it now stays open.
  - **camera, datatree, toasts:** the captions now say what the step's last click does.
- Two more were recorded again:
  - **templates:** the pictures added to the panel need the app's own server (`server: true`); without it, the cells stayed blank.
  - **The six takes that show their own page** (opencomp, paper, toasts, pills, today, pficons) were recorded again after a rebuild, so the page shows its recording.

## Where the app does something other than the proposal (each caption says what it does)
- **templates:** New note → Experiment starts at Planning, since the chain begins there. The take makes the Run from the template with Next on the plan.
- **freewrite:** Convert only previews; a sentence can be made a Result once it has its labels; "Apply to the note" puts the cards in.
- **opencomp:** Ctrl+K opens the Atlas; the search step uses the quick search list (Ctrl+Shift+K).
- **comptitle:** Ctrl+Z does nothing while the caret is in the text, so the take clicks the paper first.
- **navsettle:** following a bridged phrase is Ctrl+click; a plain click edits the text.
- **code:** Run asks to choose the file, since the recorder's app cannot read the logged folder.
- **toasts:** dragging a component shows no message, and the move panel's message has no Undo. The Undo is shown from the component-title message.
- **pills:** ✦ dropped at the edge takes its column at once and docks once it has been opened and left for about 3 s.
- **rotate:** back upright with Esc.
- **challenges:** @ names people; the paper is cited from the header's "cite a paper" chip.
- **milestones:** three deliverables, a Synthesis, a set of two Results and a takeaway set: 2 of 3, then 3 of 3.
- **datatree:** a folder is logged ("Add a folder…"); its subfolders start folded.
- **calendar:** the deliverable is added from the card's bubble. A free spot shows only on a day that has events.
- **atlasdata:** the merged tree has "in n notes", not Open ↗ (Open is in the by-project view).
- **selpop:** the menu shows under the highlighted line, not beside it.
- **pfprotocols:** a step for the Protocols switch was added. + Protocol is in the All view.
- **refmap:** a new made-up PDF with numbered citations, `assets/paper-castellanos-2020.pdf`.

## Bugs the takes showed (reported, not fixed here)
1. **+ Protocol in a theme** opens New note with the theme chosen, but the note is filed project-wide. `M.createDoc` keeps `themeId` only for theme-scoped types, and a protocol is project-scoped.
2. **The camera's Photo dialog:** in a short window (650 px), Take is cut off at the bottom until the dialog is scrolled (the stage is `max-height: 62vh`).
3. **Links to protocol steps:** the step's link circle sits about 7 px outside the row and takes the pointer only while the row is hovered. A slow approach loses it and drags the whole protocol.
4. **References Map:**
   - the older made-up PDFs' footer line is read as a reference;
   - a journal written "J. Demonstr. Mechanobiol." is cut at its first period;
   - one label read "Lindqvist 2015 2 ×0".
5. **Settings → Pills:** the preview's ✦ column overlaps the ⊕ strip.
6. **In the lab build,** the hand pill partly covers the Next button at the bottom right of a note.
7. **The Portfolio's References rows** are clipped on the right ("cited in 1 n…").
8. **Keys & commands:** after a change from the ? list, the search box still reads the old search while the list shows other rows.
9. **The ⊕ pill's zoom percentage** sits over a component near the top left.
10. **With the app rotated,** the top bar is squeezed against the right edge.
