## 1. A paper page is never a shop (`scrape-items.js`)

`isPaperPage()` — scholarly meta (`citation_doi/title/pdf_url`, `dc.identifier`, `prism.doi`) or a DOI / arXiv /
PubMed / bioRxiv URL. On such a page the title is not offered as an item and the "page as printed" rule is skipped
(its "Purchase PDF $35.95" is not a price). The result carries `paper` and `titleItem`; the popup says "Nothing to buy
here — this tab is a paper", and on a real shop page where nothing was found **add by hand** starts from the title
(the old low-confidence item, now behind the button).

## 2. The PDF viewer tab is the paper (`background.js` `_pdfTabDetection`)

Chrome's viewer runs no content script in most builds (in the headless build it does, and reports `is_pdf`), so the
worker builds the detection itself: `pdf_url` = the tab's URL (`looksLikePdfUrl`: `.pdf`, `/pdf`, `/pdfft`, `/epdf`,
`/content/pdf/`, the viewer's extension id), and the **article page this tab or its opener showed** lends its DOI and
title when `sameWork(prev, url)`: the DOI or arXiv id appears in the PDF URL, the Elsevier PII in the article URL
matches the `pii=` in the PDF URL, the PDF route sits under the article's path (MDPI), or it was one of the article's
`pdf_candidates`. The article is kept as `_prev` on the pdf-tab detection so a second look still has it;
`paper-detected` builds the pdf-tab detection instead of overwriting the article with a bare "pdf" record. Failing a
handoff, the DOI is read **out of the PDF itself** (`sniffPdfDoi`: two ranged GETs, head + tail 256 KB, XMP
`prism:doi`/`pdfx:doi`/`dc:identifier` or the Info dictionary's `doi:`), then an Elsevier PII → `10.1016/S…(..)…-x`
guess that **CrossRef must confirm** (for this very paper the guess 404s: the real DOI is 10.1038/sj.neo.7900180 — the
article page supplied it). Capture then runs the V3.822 ladder with the tab's URL first: a pre-signed S3 URL fetches
directly; a refused one falls to Chrome's download, which is the viewer's own download button. The record's
`source_url` is the article page, else `https://doi.org/<doi>`, never the pre-signed key (`publicUrl`).

## 3. The page's View PDF route, for the human (`popup.js`)

`get-detection` now reports `pdf_candidates` for every paper page (`pdf-links.js`, "View PDF"/"Get PDF" text now
scores), and the popup shows a **PDF route** row with an **open ↗** link that opens it in a new tab *with this tab as
opener*, so a human check can be passed by hand and the PDF tab still inherits the DOI and title. A failed capture
says so: "The journal may want a human: open its View PDF link ↗, pass any check, then press Capture on the PDF tab".
A PDF tab whose link has expired (ScienceDirect: five minutes) is told to re-open View PDF.

## 4. A DOI anywhere is the link (`content.js`, `capture.js`, `popup.js`)

Detection without `citation_*` tags takes the first `a[href*="doi.org/10."]` inside `article/main` (then anywhere)
or a "DOI: 10.…" line in the text (`source: doi-link | doi-text`). At capture time a PDF without a DOI is searched
(`doiFromPdfBlob`, head + tail 1 MB) and the DOI then drives CrossRef and the paper id; the result reports "DOI found
inside the PDF". The popup's DOI row is a `doi.org` link, labelled when it was read from the PDF, confirmed from the
Elsevier id, or found on the page.
