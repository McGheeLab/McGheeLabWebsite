# V3.976 — Scientia: the Synthesis asks for its notes; Flag for synthesis; lit reviews by flags only

**Date:** 2026-11-11 · **Branch:** `scientia/portfolio-slides` (after V3.974, V3.975) · **Gabe:** "Synthesis notes, when created, have a
component in them that ask you to add notes you'd like to synthesize. Additionally, you can right click a component or a highlighted
text in those notes to flag for synthesis, which automatically ports them into that synthesis note, like a pulled in component would.
For Lit Reviews, only the information that is flagged for synthesis may appear in a synthesis note, not the usual takeaways, results,
etc..."

## What changed (`scientia/src/13zzr-synth.js`, `24-kinds.css`)

- **The component that asks:** `WIDGETS.synthof`. It lists the notes the Synthesis synthesizes (an experiment's, marked "the
  experiment", and those chosen), a lit review marked "flagged only", with + Add notes…, ⟲ Gather and ×. It is made once
  (`ensureAsk`, `d.synthAsked`): with `/synthesis`, with an experiment's Synthesis, or the first time any other Synthesis note is
  opened (made with /new, a changed type, from before). Deleting it is respected.
- **Flag for synthesis:** in a note that some Synthesis synthesizes (`SYNTH.of`), the right-click menu of a component offers
  **Flag for synthesis**; with words selected, or on a marked passage (a structure's highlight, a term, a style), also **Flag these
  words for synthesis**. One row per Synthesis when there are several. The result is a pulled-in component, or a live excerpt of
  the words (V3.972's `refSpan`), flagged `synthFlag`, under a "Flagged for synthesis" heading; the same twice says it is there.
- **Found while testing:** at the menu the selection had already collapsed (the press handles the component first), so the words
  and the mark under the pointer are recorded when a right-click starts.
- **Lit reviews:** `gather` skips structures and "what it adds up to" from literature notes; ☰ The rest leaves them out.
