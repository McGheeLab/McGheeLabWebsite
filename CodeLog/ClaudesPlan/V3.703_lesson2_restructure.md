## The visit cycle

Openings do not arrive uniformly. Somebody comes to the incubator, opens it **X** times
**Z** minutes apart while they work, then nobody touches it for **Y** hours. Both tab-5
sims now run **one whole cycle, equilibrium to equilibrium**, so the recovery is visible
rather than assumed — and inside a burst the chamber never gets back to 5%, which is the
part a uniform-rate model hides. Openings are 8 s each (§4.1's good practice).

## The shelf split

Top two shelves in drawers for long-term maintenance, bottom two open for ongoing
experiments — so the chamber holds **two populations**, and §5.2 reports both.

| | no drawers | 2 shelves | all 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| free air | 165 L | 117 L | 70 L |
| τ_x | 20 s | 14 s | 8 s |
| CO₂ a day (24 openings) | 89 L | 76 L | 56 L |
| cylinder | 1.8 mo | 2.1 mo | 2.9 mo |
| air back to 37 | 5.9 s | 2.8 s | 1.2 s |
| dip on an **open** shelf | 1.38% | **1.65%** | — |
| dip **in a drawer** | — | 1.53% | 1.87% |

**And the split is defensible for a reason worth stating.** The gas dips get slightly
worse for the open shelves and the thermal recovery gets better for everyone. The
cultures that lose a little are the ones you are actively working on — which are the
ones you were going to open the box for anyway — and the cultures that gain a filter are
the long-term stocks nobody should be touching. *The split is by need, and that is what
makes it fair.* Split it by who got round to building a drawer and it is the same
arithmetic with none of the justification.

## Model correction found while building

The V3.702 thermal story was wrong. It treated the chamber's mass as a battery being
drained by the incoming air, which produced a 9 °C swing for bare shelves — absurd. The
chamber's *air* has almost no heat capacity (140 J/K); what matters is the metal as
**warm surface**. Every drawer adds 0.22 m² of 37 °C aluminium to re-warm against while
shrinking the pocket of air to be re-warmed, so the recovery time
`τ_air = C_air/(h·A_total)` falls from **5.9 s to 2.8 s to 1.2 s**. Also: openings were
30 s each rather than the 8 s §4.1 recommends, inflating the CO₂ bill by ~40%.
