# V4.101 — Scientia: two PDF fixes — the page arrows of one-page view, and pages upside down after zooming out

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "One at a time page view on PDFs arrows for page scrolling does not work. right and left arrow keys work." Then: "When zoomed out PDF pages appear upside down, include that fix in the same V as the PDF fix I have just mentioned." Asked where: the PDF card on a note's canvas, in the website's Lab Notebook, "most of them when zooming out. zooming in puts the page right side up again".

## 1. The ‹ › arrows of "One at a time"
- **Cause:** the page's text layer (`.textLayer`, `z-index: 2`, laid over the page so its words can be selected and quoted) covered the arrows (`.dk-nav`, no z-index). A click on an arrow reached the text layer; ← → went through the keyboard, so they worked.
- **Fix:** the arrows sit above it (`z-index: 4`, `21-sync.css`). This holds on a note's canvas, beside the note and maximised.

## 2. Pages upside down after zooming out
- **Cause, reproduced:**
  - zooming out redraws a page at each step, at the new sharpness;
  - a page's first draws go into its own canvas (so its text layer stays laid on it);
  - when a second draw of the same page started while one was still running, `PDFW.page` resized that canvas before pdf.js could refuse the second ("Cannot use the same canvas during multiple render() operations");
  - the resize cleared the canvas and its transform, including pdf.js's vertical flip, so the rest of the running draw came out upside down.
  - On the website a page draws slower, so draws overlapped as you zoomed out. Zooming in made one clean redraw, upright again.
  - A probe drawing one page twice into one canvas gave an upside-down "Page 1" four times out of six.
- **Fix** (`13zk-docs.js`):
  - `PDFW.page` stops a draw still running in the canvas (pdf.js `RenderTask.cancel`) and waits for it before resizing the canvas;
  - `PDFW.draw` does one draw of a page at a time (`s._drawing`); a newer sharpness asked meanwhile is drawn once when it ends (`s._again`).
  - The same probe now gives an upright page every time (the older draw cancelled cleanly).
  - The lab layer's own PDF section (§14) stands down when the app draws on demand (`PDFW.onDemand`), as it does, so nothing changed there.
