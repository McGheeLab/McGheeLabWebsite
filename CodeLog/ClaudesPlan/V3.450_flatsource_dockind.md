## Changes (`rm/js/paper-export.js`)

- **`_flattenBundle(bundle)`** — one flat `.tex`: each `\input{sections/X}` line in
  main.tex is spliced with that section file's content (client-side flatten — the service
  never needs latexpand). Sent as `flatSource` in the compile POST, alongside
  `docKind: 'main'`.
- **`mainCompiles()`** — `state.compiles` filtered to `(docKind||'main')==='main'`
  (missing field = pre-feature main compile). Routed through it: the staleness banner
  (`_refreshStaleness` — compares against the newest MAIN compile's bundleHash), the
  first-load auto-mount (`_subscribeCompiles`), and the main compile-history list
  (`_renderCompileList`). This is the guard that stops future diff / cover-letter /
  rebuttal compiles from hijacking the banner or the auto-mounted PDF.
