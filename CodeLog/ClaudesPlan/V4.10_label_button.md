# V4.10 — The Quick cast's Label

## Goal
Gabe (16 Nov 2026): "Add a label button in the quick cast pill, which contains the float and reference into it. Clicking on that button expands laterally if the pill is fully extended. If the pill is condensed, it expands outwards. Clicking on it makes your cursor that label's symbol, and clicking on a component labels it and goes back to the regular cursor, unless you are holding Control key."

## Approach
- **The mode lives in the app** (`scientia/src/14i-labels.js`, `LABELS`), so the standalone app has it too (`/label`) and the lab layer only arms it. It runs the features' own code: `FLOATS.mark([id])` (V4.7) and `REFS.toggle(b, true)` (V4.5) — one Undo and their own toast each.
- **The cursor** is the label's symbol as an SVG data URL (`cursor: url(…) 16 16, crosshair`) over the canvas while `body.lbl-armed`; a chip names what is in hand and offers Done (a touch screen shows no cursor).
- **Clicks:** a capture-phase pointerdown, so the click that labels never selects or edits the component. On a component: label it, then put the label down unless Ctrl/⌘ is held (a setting). On the empty canvas: put it down. Esc and leaving the note put it down. The pill, menus, dialogs and the chip are left alone. A bridged copy labels its origin. Add-only: what already has the label gets a toast.
- **The pill (lab-mode.js §11, the Quick cast only):** Label is a `keep` item third (Pull in and Flag stay first and second for the harness). When open, its two options are inserted right after it as `sub` items. The sub items are left out of `rowPos`'s and `fanPos`'s counts, so the other items keep their places. Docked, the two go beside Label on its far side from the pill and whatever lies beyond it on that line moves along by two (lateral); if that would run off the canvas (a narrow window), they take the nearest free places round Label, side by side. In the fan they go outwards along Label's ray, either side of it, at the first distance (from 58 px beyond Label) where they sit on no other item and inside the canvas; the rest of the wheel dims while they are open. A repaint of a wheel or row already showing keeps it (no second pop): each item carries `data-k`, what moved glides from its old place, and the two grow out of Label. The app fires `sis-labels` on every change and the pill repaints, so Label stays lit while a label is in hand (the folded ＋ carries the label on its button); tapping it then puts the label down.
- **The phone** has no canvas and no pills (its list view): `/label` arms it there and a tap on a card (`.m-card`) labels it; on a touch screen the chip says "tap" and drops the Ctrl hint.
- **Settings → Labels:** switch (off: no Label, no /label), "Ctrl keeps the label in hand", "The cursor" Small/Large, a preview.
