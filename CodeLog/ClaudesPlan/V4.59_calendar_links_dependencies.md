# V4.59 — Scientia Calendar: links between events (dependencies), pushed back together

## Why
Gabe, 19 Nov: "Allow links between events, which creates dependencies, and if barriers are experienced, pushing events back, it pushes the other one back the same amount with custom displacement offered as well."

And on 20 Nov: "Links should appear on a layer on top of the planner and should appear like the link arrows in notes, where it gets lighter and less opacity when it flies over other cards."

## What changed
- **A link:** rest on an event and its card opens. **Link → then…** puts the calendar in link mode: the bar says "Click the event that comes after …", and the other cards pulse. The next click links them (`CAL.linkTo`).
  - Stored on the earlier event as `then: [ids]`. Links join one-off events: a repeating one has none.
  - A link that would make a loop is refused, and the toast says why. Esc, Cancel, or a click on an empty spot ends the mode.
- **Drawn on a layer above the cards** (`CAL.drawLinks`, an SVG per day grid, z 6 over the cards' 2, pointer-events none), in the look of the note's arrows (`20-canvas.css`):
  - a curve 1.7 wide with round ends at .92 opacity, a dot where it leaves, an arrowhead where it arrives;
  - teal when both events have the same note, ink otherwise;
  - where it passes over another card, that stretch is masked out and drawn again at .42 with a soft drop shadow (a mask and a clipPath from the crossed cards' rectangles, as the notes do);
  - it is drawn again once the hour's height is known and on every resize;
  - a sort singled out fades the links that touch none of its cards;
  - a partner off the view is named on the card instead: "then → …" / "after ← …".
- **Barrier.** The card has +30 min · +1 h · +2 h · +1 day (`CAL.pushBack`). The event moves, and everything that depends on it, through any number of links, moves as much (`CAL.deps`, `CAL.carry`, `CAL.shift`, across midnight by days).
  - The same happens when it ends later by a drag, its bottom edge, or +15 in its card.
  - Moved earlier, what depends on it stays where it is.
- **Custom…** in the toast that follows (a toast with buttons, `CAL.tst`): a row for each event that depends on it, set to the same amount, with None and Same. Apply sets each one's own amount.
- The card lists "← after …" and "then → …", each with × to unlink. A card with links has a link icon.
- One undo group per gesture: Ctrl+Z takes back the push and what it carried together.
