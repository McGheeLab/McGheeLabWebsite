# V4.3 — The lab's Library on the reshaped page

## Goal
Gabe approved changing `rm/scientia/lab-mode.js` (15 Nov 2026, "yes"; Alex reviews) so the reshaped Library (V4.1: the one search box with fields, facets, suggestions and "why it matched", the shelf map, smart foci, the paper's sheet, reading states) shows in the lab builds too. Until now lab-mode.js §3 swapped the whole page for its own (`VIEWS.library = LabLibrary`), so only the tree (`LGROUP.side()`) reached the lab.

## Approach
Keep the app's page; let the lab add its parts through hooks instead of a page of its own.
- **App (`scientia/src`)**, small and generic (they go to Gabe's standalone too):
  - `17-library.js` — `LibraryView.hook(x)` / `LibraryView.ext`: `meta`, `eyebrow`, `stand`, `addersOpen`, `panel()`, `sections(R)` (the list in groups), `card(r, f)` (its own classes, attributes and actions), `sheet(r, f)` (its own actions and sections), `facets(R)`; `adding` starts as `null` (the hook decides). The page asks the sources to warm when you search with "inside" or `hl:`/`quote:`/`note:`.
  - `17e-libsearch.js` — `LSEARCH.addSource({ pdfs, hl, embeds, warm })`, `LSEARCH.addIs(word, test, hint)`, `LSEARCH.warm(papers)`; a source's highlights carry `src`, `by`, `hex`, `open()` and read "RM highlight · p. 3 · Mo".
  - `17f-libmap.js` — the sheet takes the hook's actions and sections; a source's highlight opens through its own `open()`; opening a sheet warms the sources for that paper.
- **Lab layer (`rm/scientia/lab-mode.js` §3)**: one function, `labLibraryHook()`, in place of the line `VIEWS.library = LabLibrary`. It registers the RM panel, the open adders, the title line, the two lists, every LabLibrary action on cards and sheets, `is:lab` / `is:mine` and a Source facet, and a search source for RM highlights (`api/rm/annotations`, the call the paper card already makes; fetched three at a time when you search or open a paper, kept ten minutes), PDFs pulled from RM (`media.rmPaper`) and paper cards in notes. LabLibrary, its cards and its helpers stay untouched as the fallback when the app has no hook.
- **Settings**: a new feature, **RM highlights in the Library** (`labhl`, defined by the lab layer, group Reading & sources): its switch stops the fetching and hides them; Whose highlights (yours and the lab's · only yours); a live preview.

## Where LabLibrary's parts went
| LabLibrary | Now |
|---|---|
| Eyebrow "The lab", the stand, the title line "N lab papers · from RM · M of your own" | `ext.eyebrow` / `ext.stand` / `ext.meta` |
| The DOI and paste adders, always shown | `ext.addersOpen` (open by default; + Add / − Add folds them) |
| RM card: Open RM's Library · Refresh from RM · Import from RM · the status line | `ext.panel()` under the adders (same functions, same `.lab-lib-status`) |
| The filter "Filter by author, year, journal, title…" | the one search box (plain words cover the same fields and more) |
| "Lab papers" and "In your notebook" lists (`.lab-papers`, `.lab-mine`), their empty texts | `ext.sections(R)` in the list view; `is:lab` / `is:mine` and the Source facet in the search |
| RM paper card: pdf pill, Read here, Read in RM, Pull into my notebook, Send the PDF to RM, Open in RM, the foci chips | `ext.card` (side actions; class `lab-paper`, `data-rm`) and `ext.sheet` (the sheet's actions + an "In RM" line) |
| Own paper: in RM / sent to RM / Push to RM, Send the PDF to RM | `ext.card` (`extra`) and `ext.sheet` (an "RM" section) |
| The tree beside the lists | unchanged (`LGROUP.side()`), now putting its words in the search box |
| `#/read/<RM paper>` (RM's reader, not the in-app one) | unchanged (§4) |
