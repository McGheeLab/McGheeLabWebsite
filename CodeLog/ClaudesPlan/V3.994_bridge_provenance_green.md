# V3.994 — Bridges say where they come from; bridged components are green

## Goal
Gabe: "Bridges should show their provenance more clearly than small dark text. Also, bridge components to another note should appear as green as well in the original note for the user to spot them easily."

## Approach
- **One band, everywhere a card says where it comes from.** Three cards wrote their own 10 px grey mono line (a component bridged: `12-doc` `refCard`; a passage: `13zzt` `EXCERPT`; a widget: `13zx`), and a pulled-in structure a fourth variant. `DocView.origin(d, ob, what, extra)` makes the band for all of them: what it is (Bridged / Passage / Pulled from), the note's title as the thing to click, the component, ↗ beside, the project and day underneath. Tinted in the bridge colour (`--bridge`, Settings), or the structure's kind colour when pulled in. The pill and label row above a bridge's preview repeated what the band now says, so it went.
- **The original component, green.** A bridge already has a green left edge in the note it went to; the component it comes from gets a green right edge, the same direction rule as the circles at a note's edges (left: comes from; right: goes to). Its "bridged" tag (components that aren't text only) became a green chip on every bridged component, "⇄ bridged to <note>", first among its chips: the header had no room for a note's name next to the label and the time. Click it (or double-click, as before) for Go to / Open beside.
