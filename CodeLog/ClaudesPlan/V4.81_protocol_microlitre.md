# V4.81 — typing "uL" in a protocol gives "µL"

## Why
Gabe: "In Protocol add the uL to mu symbol after a number."

The notes' editor already turns `5 uL ` into `5 µL ` (13u-write.js, group `science`). A protocol's steps are their own
contenteditable text (13b-protocol.js), outside that editor, so nothing was replaced there.

## What changed
- `PROTO.typing(el)` (13b-protocol.js) hooks a step's text to the notes' own rule list: `WRITE.autoReplace` on every
  `input`, and Backspace right after a replacement puts back what was typed (`WRITE.undoReplace`). The rules are not
  copied: one list, each group keeping its own Settings switch (Settings -> Writing in components -> Typing
  replacements), so `science` off leaves `uL` alone.
- Hooked into the step editor in a protocol note (`PROTO.editor`) and into a run's temporary steps (`PROTO.tempEditor`).
  Because it is the whole rule list, the other groups (arrows, dashes, quotes, superscripts) also work in steps now,
  each under its own switch.
- Settings: the "Writing in components" description says so, and its preview has a protocol step ("5 uL" / "5 µL").
