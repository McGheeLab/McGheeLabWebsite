# V3.181 — Training: RM for Admins (PI / Senior-Member Surfaces)

> Status: PLANNED — not yet built. Infrastructure landed in V3.177.
> Admin-gated audience: primarily Alex + select senior lab members.

## Goal

The admin-only surfaces in RM that the rest of the lab never sees.
PIs and senior lab members operate the compliance, procurement,
equipment, chemical-safety, and analytics surfaces — they need a
training pass on these even more than the day-1 trainee does, because
these surfaces touch dollars, safety, and federal compliance.

## Format

Lecture-style with one interactive widget:

1. **Admin surface map** — a stripped-down nav map widget showing only
   the admin-gated pages. Each tile expands into a one-paragraph
   description of what that page does + when to use it.

## Sections (10)

1. Who is an admin in RM (firestore.rules `isAdmin()` + `role` field).
2. Compliance — IRB / IACUC / Student Training tabs; how to verify
   submitted certificates.
3. Chemical safety — SDS storage, hazard tracking.
4. Inventory — chemical + reagent + equipment inventories, receipts
   ingestion.
5. People — lab members + important contacts (collaborators,
   administrators, vendors).
6. Career &amp; tenure — milestones, evaluation cycles.
7. Procurement &amp; receipts — purchase flow, budget tracking, grant
   account assignments.
8. Equipment booking — schedule + training-cert gating.
9. Calendar + Email — admin-gated for V3.157 + earlier; per-user
   OAuth lands in a future version that will unlock these for everyone.
10. Analytics — the lab-stats dashboard; member-activity overview.

## Out of scope

- The actual configuration of grant accounts (lab-finance-specific,
  better handled in a separate document).
- Federal compliance regulations (point students at the institutional
  IRB / IACUC offices, not at this training).
