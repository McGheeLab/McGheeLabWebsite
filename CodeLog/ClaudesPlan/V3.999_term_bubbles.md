# V3.999 — A term is its bubbles; the Terms tab for a long list

## Goal
Gabe (14 Nov 2026), over the prototype's eight versions: a term's components as realistic glass bubble portals showing their neighbours, sized to fit them, images from panels, transparent glass with reflection and a bent rim; the term again inside a bubble makes no new one; bridges folded in; synergies listed beside, Ctrl+click to pick several and look through them together; the order of the bubbles the user's choice; a Terms homepage that navigates a long list with stats; barriers that found a resolution and observations that became takeaways, through other terms, highlighted without a new feature; porting in visits the note, a small bubble brings you back. Then: "Bubbles is the only way to view it. I want the bubbles to be slightly dynamic with a clean animation." (Pointing with a ring + a click: not answered — my pick, the ring a setting.)

## Approach
- `14e-bubble.js` (`BUBBLE`): the bubble as a reusable piece (spec, geometry, element, warp filter, float, portal, back bubble, bead) — the Atlas build reuses it for its dots.
- `16-lens.js`: `TARC` (arcs from items' `resolves` and wires between components holding the two kinds; `through` = shared other terms), `TSTAT` + `TermsView` (the list: find, sorts with groups, sticky heads, rail, filters, per-row stats, side panel), `TB` + `LensView` (data: occurrences minus lit reviews, same-note neighbours, bubbles without repeats, beads, arcs and their other ends; layout: flow in rows by note/time/size with room for arc labels; threads: bridges, cross-note connections, arcs with labels kept clear; side: synergies with picks, bridges, wiring insights, key, actions). Nests and Chain leave the term (the chain board stays for Export chain).
- The float is CSS (transform on a composited layer), paused while scrolling, off-screen, while a ring closes, with reduced motion or the setting off. No backdrop-filter: the warp bends the grid drawn inside the bubble, so moving glass costs nothing per frame.
- `20a-features.js`: Term bubbles (switch, six settings, a floating three-bubble preview); Terms gains the starting sort.
