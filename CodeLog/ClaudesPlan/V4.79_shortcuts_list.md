# V4.79 — every key and gesture in one list, in Settings and behind ?

## Why
Gabe: "Add shortcuts list in Settings near command bar commands."

The app's keys were spread over the code (the shell's handler, the editor, the calendar's `CAL.key`, the freewrite, the reader, the monthly review…) and the `?` dialog listed only some of them. Settings → Keys & commands listed the commands but no shortcuts.

## What changed
- **`SHORT` (new `20f-shortcuts.js`)**: `SHORT.items()` is the one list, written by reading the handlers (135 rows): everywhere · in a note · typing in a component · saying what words are (letters read from `KINDS`, extra strip keys from `ED.stripExtra`) · mouse, pen and touch · command bar and menus · widgets · reader and Library · calendar · freewrite · review, terms and atlas.
- **One view, two places**: `SHORT.view()` — a search box (every word must match the keys, the words or the group; `ctrl k` finds Ctrl+K), a chip per group, the groups with their rows — is drawn in Settings → Keys & commands (just above the commands) and in the `?` dialog (`helpModal()` is now `SHORT.open()`), also `/keys` (alias `/shortcuts`) and Ctrl+P → Keys and gestures.
- **Rebindable keys show their binding**: the structure key and the term key show what they are now, say "the default" or "default X", and a Change link opens the page of the feature that sets it (`#/settings/feature/structures` / `terms`).
- **Features → Shortcuts** (`FEAT 'shortcuts'`, group Canvas & notes, in every preset): the switch hides the Settings list and the `?` key (`/keys` says it is off); settings: *The ? key opens the list*, *List the mouse, pen and touch gestures*; live preview of six rows as set now. A shortcut of a feature that is switched off stays listed, dimmed, tagged "feature off".
- Settings search: "shortcuts", "hotkeys", "gestures" point to Keys & commands.
- `41-shortcuts.css`: rows as the look's cards (`--lk-card`, `--lk-ring`), light and dark, phone width.
