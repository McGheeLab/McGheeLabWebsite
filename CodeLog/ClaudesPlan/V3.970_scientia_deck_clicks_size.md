# V3.970 — Scientia: the deck answers clicks, and has one size

**Date:** 2026-11-10 · **Branch:** `scientia/export-term-nests` (PR #45, after V3.968 and V3.969) · **Gabe, before merging:**
"fix iteration's new functionality. It stacks v1 and v2 together, but you cannot go back and forth between versions or
create a third iteration as of now. Also, resizing one of the components resizes all other equally to keep that card deck
feel."

## What was wrong

The strip under a deck's top card (V3.967) listened for a swipe by capturing the pointer on `pointerdown`. With the pointer
captured, Chrome sends the `click` to the capturing element, the strip, not to the button pressed. So a mouse or pen click on
‹, ›, a dot or + did nothing. A touch tap still worked, because a tap's click is aimed at the element tapped, and the V3.967
test pressed the buttons from script, which skips pointer events. A real-input test (`t_970`) confirmed it: ‹, the dot and +
failed with the mouse, ‹ worked with a tap.

The version tag's menu (v1, v2, …) jumped to the chosen version without putting it on top, so a version beneath stayed
hidden.

## What changed (`scientia/src/13zzq-deck.js`)

- **The swipe captures the pointer only once the drag has gone 8 px sideways.** A click stays a click; a swipe that starts on
  a button does not also press it.
- **`ITER.go` wrapped:** a version picked in the version tag's menu, or "on v1 ↑" under a card, goes on top of its deck.
- **One size for the deck.** `DB.put` is wrapped: when a version of a stacked deck is saved, `IDECK.size` gives its `w` (and
  `h` when the kind has one: an HTML page, a PDF) to the other versions. That covers every grip (12's, 13g's, 13zk's) without
  touching them. It is skipped while the sync writes what came from elsewhere (`SYNC.applying`) and for a spread-out deck. On
  screen, `IDECK.watch` puts a ResizeObserver on each top card, so the cards beneath follow while the grip is dragged
  (`IDECK.fit`). `IDECK.stack` gives every version the top card's spot and size, written before the deck is stacked so the top
  card's size wins.
