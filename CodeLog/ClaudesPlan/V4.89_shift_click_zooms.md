# V4.89 — Scientia: Shift+click a component and the canvas zooms onto it, in every build

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "In RTgabrieldeclercq repo, have the Shift+click on a component to zoom on it. Look for any missing shortcuts or features that do not reside in the current repo or pull requests."

The lab's canvas pills (lab-mode §11, carried to Gabe's repo as `vendor/lab-pills.js` since V4.20) already fit a component on a Shift-click, but only with the pills' move hand and a mouse. They skip the pen, which Gabe uses on the Surface, and need the pills to be on. A probe of his repo's main (V4.66) showed the mouse case working and the rest not.

## What changed (`12e-shiftzoom.js`, `SHZOOM`)
- **Shift+click a component** and the canvas zooms onto it. This works in every build, with any hand, the mouse or the pen.
  - It is decided on release (within 6 px and half a second), so Shift+drag still moves a component a grid cell at a time.
  - Not while you are typing in that component: there Shift+click extends the text selection, as in any text.
  - Where the pills' own Shift-click moved the view first, it stays theirs; the app checks the view 60 ms after the release.
- **Settings → Canvas & notes → Shift+click zooms** (`shiftzoom`): *It zooms* — to fit the screen (the default), or to 100% or 50%, centred — with a live preview.
- **The shortcuts list** (V4.79) has the row "Shift + click a component — Zoom onto it".

## The audit: shortcuts and features missing from Gabe's repo
- **Shortcuts:** none. The lab layer's own keys are Enter and Escape in its forms; its gestures are in §11, which his repo carries.
- **Features of the app that live only in the lab layer** (not RM's), so his repo lacks them:
  - §13 **Lit reviews**, a tab of literature reviews that serve several projects;
  - §14 **PDF cards drawn on demand**.
  - Both could be carried the way the pills were: cut from lab-mode.js by `scripts/scientia_build.py` into `vendor/`, and started where the lab layer is absent. Proposed to Gabe, not built.
- **Everything else** in the lab's open PR (V4.67 onwards) reaches his repo with the next sync.
