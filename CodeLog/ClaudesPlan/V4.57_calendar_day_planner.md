# V4.57 — Scientia: the Calendar tab, as the day planner

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "Add a Calendar tab right before Tasks. The calendar should look modern and refined. […] This calendar has all core features of a good calendar with quality of life features and contains extra adaptation for this lab notebook. For example, a calendar event for an experiment can be linked to a note, opening it when clicking on it on the calendar."

Then, on the design canvas: "The calendar should be one piece where you can scroll through, the halves was just for you to reference the ratio for the calendar. […] I like the Day Planner look the best. […] The calendar banner should freeze so that when you scroll, you can see it. Clicking on a free spot brings up a create event window that is simple to fill out, including time to add. You can click on the sorting types to single out the cards just for that sort."

The design canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/VA59gxjoG5fpxCgUMcSNWD) tried three models; Gabe chose the Day planner (board 3). This version builds its core in the notebook. The rest of what the canvas shows follows in the next versions: the month and its day panel, links between events, agent runs, focus, the bell, regions, deliverables and topic syntheses.

## What changed
- **A tab, right before Tasks** (`VIEWS.calendar`, `#/calendar`). The page fills the window (`canvas: true`); the calendar is a notebook card.
- **Week and Day.** Each day is one column from 0:00 to 24:00, in one scroll.
  - The hour is sized so 12 hours (Settings: 10 · 12 · 16) fill the visible height under the banner (`CAL.fit`, `--hpx`, a ResizeObserver).
  - It opens with now a third of the way down, or at 8:00 on a week without today.
  - The banner (day names with their planned hours, the all-day row) is sticky above the hours.
- **Events are their own records:** a new store `events` (DB_VERSION 12; in `DB.SNAP`, so Ctrl+Z restores them).
  - `{title, day, start, end (minutes), allDay, kind meeting|experiment|other, tags, projectId, docId, repeat none|daily|weekly, skip, notes {day: docId}}`.
  - Timed reminders with an end show as cards too. Reminders without an end, all-day ones and dated tasks sit in the all-day row: a bell, or a check that ticks the task.
- **A click on a free spot opens the create window there** (`CAL.create`). It is set to that time, snapped to 15 minutes and an hour long, with a dashed draft in the column. Fields: title (focused), day and All day, start and end, 30 min · 1 h · 2 h, the sort type (whatever Colour by is set to: project, tag, or else the kind), and a note to link. Enter adds it; Esc or a click outside cancels, and that click opens nothing else.
  - The free time between cards (7:00–21:00, and a gap of up to 2 h before the first or after the last) is drawn dashed.
- **A card.**
  - **Drag it** to another time or day: it lands at the time under the pointer, with a dashed outline where. One of a repeat leaves the series as a copy, and the series skips that day.
  - **Drag its bottom edge** to change its length in 15-minute steps.
  - **Rest on it about a second** and it opens in place (`CAL.bub`): title, −15/+15 for the time and the length, kind, tags, project, note, repeat, Duplicate, Delete (this one / every one). A quick pass does nothing. E on a focused card opens it too.
  - **A click opens its note**, or makes one and links it: a meeting note, an experiment at its first stage (`CHAIN.create`), or a quick note. With no project, the notebook's new-note dialog asks for one.
  - Two that overlap sit side by side, each marked "Overlaps".
- **Colour by** Project · Kind · Tag · Note type · Load.
  - Categorical colours in a fixed order; Load is five bands of one blue, light to dark, with its own dark-mode steps.
  - The legend counts what is shown. A click on a value singles out its cards and the rest fade to .2; Ctrl or Shift adds another value; a second click clears. A "Showing: … · Clear" chip says it is on.
  - A tag on a card shows only that tag's events ("Only #tag ×").
- **Keys on the page:** ← → (week or day), T (today), D, W, N (new).
- **Settings → Tabs & overview → Calendar** (`calendar`): the switch (the tab leaves the row), the hours that fill the height, the first day of the week, the free time drawn dashed, and a live preview.
