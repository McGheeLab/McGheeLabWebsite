# V3.983 — Lab Notebook: a menu — the Simple notebook or Scientia

**Date:** 2026-11-11 · **Branch:** `scientia/lab-notebook-two-ways` · **Lab layer only** (Alex reviews) · **Gabe:** "Have the lab notebook in
RM be two options: an empty note canvases that get created each week with minimal features: pills, image panels, PDFs, HTMLs, but no
other tabs, terms, etc... The other is the full Scientia as it is now with local dekstop syncing via cloud, etc.. The minimalist stable
version are for people to pick up slowly or for minimal work. It should be a menu interface that allows you to enter either or. The
Scientia page, if you choose to enter it, takes as much space as possible with the RM tabs just above it."

## Decisions

- **One notebook, two doors.** The simple notebook is the same app (the web Scientia) with `?simple=1`, reshaped by the lab layer
  (`lab-mode.js` section 19), not a second notebook: its weekly canvases are ordinary Scientia notes in the person's hub, so someone
  who started simple finds every week in the full Scientia (and on their computer, once it syncs) — "pick up slowly". "Stable": the
  simple notebook keeps a fixed list (a whitelist of five inserts, three pills, a short menu), so new Scientia features don't appear in
  it by themselves.
- **A canvas a week:** `wk<YYYYMMDD>` (that week's Monday), `simpleWeek: 'YYYY-MM-DD'`, title "Week of …", empty, no project. Made
  only for the current week, and only after the app has started and caught up with the cloud; the fixed id makes two devices write
  the same record rather than two weeks. Monday weeks, as RM's My Week.
- **The menu replaces the automatic choice.** Before, the page opened the person's last view (Scientia or the canvas). Now a plain visit
  shows the menu (the last used marked); deep links still go straight in.
- **The canvas notebook of before is kept**, one link under the choices (only when the person has entries): lab members' entries
  stay reachable, nothing is removed. Whether to retire it is a later decision.
- **The whole page:** the notebook's title line and tab row are hidden while either app is shown; the full Scientia's sync controls
  move into a small button at its bottom left (the app has its own sync dot in its header); the simple notebook's week bar is its only
  chrome.
