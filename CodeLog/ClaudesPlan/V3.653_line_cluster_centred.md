# V3.653 — The line cluster rides the middle of its line, both axes

## The ask (Alex, 2026-08-20, on V3.652)

*"the grip and +- should be in the middle of the line. this should be in the
horizontal and vertical lines."*

V3.652 parked each cluster at one **end** of its line — a column line's controls
above the table, a row line's controls out in the left gutter — which reads as a
label hanging off the line rather than as part of it.

## The change

The cluster is now centred on its line on both axes (`left/top: 50%`,
`translate: -50% -50%`) and runs **along** the line: stacked on a vertical
column border, laid out in a row on a horizontal row border. The `|||` grip
keeps its place between the `＋` and the `−`.

## Two bugs that centring exposed

Centring the cluster put it over cells, and that turned two latent problems into
visible ones.

### 1. Two clusters at every crossing
Reveal was CSS `:hover`, and row strips cross column strips at every
intersection. With the cluster at one end that rarely mattered; centred, a
column line's midpoint often lands right on a row line, so **both** lit up and
both peeked a band.

Reveal now goes through **nearest-line arbitration** instead: one `mousemove` on
the grid measures the pointer against every border, activates the single closest
line within 7 px (`.is-active`), and clears the rest. A tie at a crossing goes to
the column — arbitrarily, but consistently. The band-peek preview moved onto the
same switch, so it cannot be left behind by a `mouseleave` that never fired.

Two details the arbitration has to handle:
- the pointer moving **onto** the cluster is not leaving the line (the buttons
  stick out past the 11 px strip), so a pointer inside the active cluster's rect
  holds the line active;
- arbitration is suspended while a column drag is in flight, or the cluster
  would vanish out from under the grip being dragged.

### 2. A 5 px dead band down every cell edge
The strips were `pointer-events: auto`, so a click within 5 px of any border hit
the **strip**, not the cell — you could not put the caret at the start of a cell.
This was true since V3.652 and would have been reported as "I can't click the
left of a cell".

The strips are now inert: they are geometry, not hit targets, and arbitration
does not need them to receive events.

And the cluster's own card is click-through too — only its three buttons take
clicks. Otherwise the card's padding and gaps would be a fresh dead patch in the
middle of every line. Verified by probing `elementFromPoint` across a cell edge:
3 px in hits the grip (correct — that is the resize handle, right on the border),
8 px in reaches the cell.
