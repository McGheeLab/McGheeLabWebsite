# V4.75 — arriving by Alt+Left / Alt+Right settles the components' shadows

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov 2026: "Navigating between notes with Alt + left or right arrows should settle the shadows of components as if
they are hovered on initially, fading out."

## What was already there
Alt+Left / Alt+Right already go back and forward through the notes you opened: `HIST` (11b-commands.js, every `#/doc/` and
`#/split/` route is pushed on `hashchange`, 50 deep) and the key handler in 11c-navbar.js (not while typing in a field),
the same `HIST.go` the bar's ← → buttons and `/back`, `/forward` use. Nothing needed building there.

## What changed
- 20d-look.js: `HIST.go` is wrapped to remember that a back/forward is in flight; `App.refresh` then calls
  `LOOK.settle()`, which marks the canvases it drew with `.nav-settle` and removes it after the fade. Anything else that opens a
  note is not marked. Not done when the look is off, the feature is off, Movement is Still, or the system asks for reduced motion.
- 36-look.css: `lk-settle`: every component (not the selected one, which keeps its ring) starts with the shadow and ring colour it
  gets on hover (`--lk-lift`, `--lk-ring-2`), holds it for the first ~28%, then settles to its resting shadow; `--st-dur` is the
  length (1 s by default). `prefers-reduced-motion` and `body.lk-still` turn it off.
- Settings → Back and forward (`navsettle`, needs the look): a switch, "Fade out" Quick 0.6 s / Gentle 1 s / Slow 1.6 s (Calm
  movement shortens it by 30%), and a live preview with four components and a Replay button.
- Test `navsettle_t.mjs`: real Alt+Left / Alt+Right key events; the shadow at the start equals the hover shadow, is between the two
  mid-fade, equals the resting one after a second; a note opened by a link is unmarked; Slow; switch off; Still; reduced motion;
  the Settings page and its preview.
