# V3.828 — Reader: figure snipping reads the page's own artwork

**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Builds on:** V3.643/V3.644 (caption-anchored detector + Label mode) · **Backend:** none ·
**Site files:** `rm/js/page-layout.js`, `rm/js/pdf-viewer.js`, `rm/js/paper-label-mode.js`, `sw.js` 808 → 809
(auto-uploads at turn end)

Alex: *"we need to make a more robust figure snipping tool for the paper reader. For example this paper doesnt auto
snip Figure 2, and Figure 3 is half snipped. ERK reinforces actin polymerization to power persistent edge protrusion
during motility. Lets do a much better job at searching for and snipping figures from papers"*.

## 1. Why the two figures failed (reproduced offline, `mendoza-2015-erk`)

The V3.643 detector infers a figure from where the prose *isn't*: find a caption, grow away from it until a prose
line blocks, clamp to the caption's column. Two layouts in this Science Signaling paper defeat that:

- **Figure 2 (page 4, two-thirds width, text in the right third).** The caption's first line is 15pt from the right
  column's prose — under the loose 2.6× line-join factor — so caption and prose became one "line" reading as
  full-width; the prose directly above that line stopped growth at 9pt, under the 20pt floor. No region.
- **Figure 3 (page 5, page-wide, caption set in one column).** The caption lives in the left column, so growth was
  clamped to that column and panels D–F were cut off. Figure 4 (page 6) was half-snipped the same way, and page 9 (a
  text-only list of supplementary legends) produced a column-tall false hotspot.

## 2. The change: a graphics layer, not a better guess

A PDF says where its artwork is. `page.getOperatorList()` is the list pdf.js already built to paint the page;
walking it with a CTM + clip stack gives the device-space box of every image and every vector path.

- **`PAGE_LAYOUT.collectGraphics(opList, OPS, transform, W, H)`** (pure; node and browser) → `[{x,y,w,h,k}]`,
  `k` = img | mask | path | shade. Tracks save/restore/transform/form XObjects, clips (`constructPath` → `clip`),
  white fills and `ca`/`CA` = 0 (paint nothing), image repeats, shadings (clip box).
- **`figureRegions(items, {…, graphics, pageNum})`** with a layer in hand:
  1. furniture out (header/footer rules + bands, column rules, journal brand strips, page-size backgrounds; one
     image ≥ 85 % of the page = a scan → the layer is null and the text path runs as before);
  2. primitives cluster into **panels** (union-find, gap 0.7·bodyH); each panel swallows the non-prose text touching
     it — tick labels, axis titles, panel letters — because a figure *contains* text;
  3. a caption **seeds** on the panels directly beside it in its direction (figure above, table below; flips when
     that side is empty), with no prose line in the bridge;
  4. **every caption grows at once, ring by ring**, over unclaimed panels — never across a prose line, another
     caption, or into a column that holds prose at that height; a gap wider than a panel gap is crossed only when
     it is full of non-prose text (the cells between a table's rules); a table also chains every rule that shares
     its header rule's x-extent;
  5. **orphan** panels big enough to be a figure become unlabelled hotspots (a plate whose caption is on the next
     page, a caption the regex did not read); page-1 orphans in the top third are mastheads;
  6. with a layer present, a *figure* caption with no artwork beside it is a legend, not a figure; tables still fall
     back to text growth but must read as rows (cells outnumber prose inside the box).
- **Text-side fixes the data forced** (all in `page-layout.js`): body size = the height carrying the most ink width;
  column left edges from item x-positions *before* line grouping (`_frColumnEdges`), so a 14pt gutter still
  splits; spanning lines do not vote in `_frDetectColumns`; table-row demotion judged per column and never on a
  body-width line or a one-glyph fragment; a caption stops absorbing at the first table row; `_frIsXref` — a figure
  number followed by a lowercase word is prose ("Fig. 2(b) summarizes", the old stem list's trailing `\b` never
  matched); margin-hugging vertical text runs ("Downloaded from https://…") are furniture; a lone image or rect
  with prose running over it is not artwork.
- **`rm/js/pdf-viewer.js`** imports `OPS`, collects the layer once per page (`_collectPageGraphics`, natural
  coords, kept on the node), feeds it to `_detectFigureRegions` with `pageNum`, exposes `getPageGraphics(p)`.
  **`rm/js/paper-label-mode.js`** passes the same boxes to `PAGE_REGIONS.detect`, so Label mode's first draft
  matches the hotspots. `figure-capture.js` is untouched: an unlabelled region shows "Click to snip this figure".

## 3. Measured (training harness, LOCAL ONLY: `ResearchManagement/data/figure-training/`)

`extract-feat.mjs` now builds the per-page features offline with the vendored pdf.js in node (identical text
geometry to the browser dump) plus the graphics layer; `score-figs.mjs` grades figure + table boxes against the
archived v2 hand labels and `labels-mendoza.json`.

| | recall | precision | mean IoU |
|---|---|---|---|
| V3.643 caption growth | 36/44 (82 %) | 80 % | 0.92 |
| V3.828 graphics layer | **47/48 (98 %)** | **96 %** | **0.95** |

Mendoza: all four figures found with the right caption and no false hotspots on the other seven pages (was 1 of 4
and three false). The residue is label artefacts: acimovic p7 Fig 2 and arxiv-1706 p8 Table 2 are real figures
counted as false positives, angelini p2 carries a duplicate truth box.

`viewer-smoke.html` drives the shipping `pdf-viewer.js` end to end on a training PDF and writes the regions into the
DOM for a headless check.
