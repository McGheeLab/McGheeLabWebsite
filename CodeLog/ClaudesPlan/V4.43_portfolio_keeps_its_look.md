# V4.43 — The Portfolio keeps its own look

## Goal
Gabe (19 Nov 2026), on V4.42 and its design canvas: "Don't change the Portfolio, it looks good as it is. The rest is approved"

## Approach
- **`36-look.css`:**
  - the Portfolio section (`.pf-list`, `.pf-row`, `.pf-ind`, `.pf-gl.pf-box`) goes;
  - the general rules that reached inside it (`.btn`, `.btn.sm`, `.btn.xs`, `.inp`, `.tb-ban`) skip `.pf-root *`;
  - the page rise skips `#view > .pf-root`.
- **What keeps the new look:** the header around the Portfolio (the tabs' pill, search, buttons) and what floats over it (menus, dialogs, toasts).
- **The canvas's "project list docking into a capsule" board** was the Portfolio's list, so it is not built.
- **Found while testing: the page rise never played.** V4.42 added `#view.lk-nav` on `hashchange`, but `App.refresh`, run by the app's own `hashchange` listener just after, sets `#view`'s `className`, which wiped it. Now 20d marks the navigation and adds the class after `App.refresh` has drawn the page. V4.42's check only saw the class for an instant.
- **Settings → Look and motion:** says the Portfolio keeps its own look.
