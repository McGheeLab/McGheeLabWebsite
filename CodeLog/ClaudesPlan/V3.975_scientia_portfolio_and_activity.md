# V3.975 — Scientia: the portfolio reorganised; health as activity

**Date:** 2026-11-11 · **Branch:** `scientia/portfolio-slides` (after V3.974) · **Gabe** (11 Nov): lit reviews only in the project's Lit
reviews panel; an experiment's notes under one card with a dropdown that stays open until collapsed; experiments by note date; a sort
button per project; takeaways, open barriers and evidence as coloured dots with a count, opening onto the words on hover; project
health as activity, counted only in a note's first two weeks, from components made and structures marked, takeaways and results
weighing most, lit reviews not at all.

## Where

The website and the installed app draw the Portfolio with the lab's layer (`rm/scientia/lab-mode.js`); the standalone draws it
with the app's own `15-portfolio.js`. To keep one version of the rules, they live in a new app module, `15d-pfkit.js`, and both
draw with it. The lab-layer edit is limited to using it (Alex reviews).

## Decisions

- **Note date** is `created`, which a backdated note already sets to its day.
- **An experiment** is a chain (`d.chain`) with two or more notes in the column; its date is its first note's. One note alone is a
  note card.
- **Lit reviews:** every `literature` note of the project, wherever it was filed, plus the lab's linked review canvases.
- **Unfolded and the order** are kept on this device (`_ls 'pf:kit'`), like the lab's region folds.
- **Activity:** events inside `[note date, note date + 14 days]`: a component with content made (+1; pulled-in components and the
  Synthesis's own headings do not count), a structure marked (+3), a takeaway or a result (+10). The score is the last 28 days;
  the heatmap the last six weeks; *stalled* after 21 days without any. A backdated note stamps its components with its own date,
  so components now record `made` (the real time); older components fall back to `created`.
- **The dots' words** open in a bubble from the dot, in its colour; the dot and the bubble are one hover area (the first version
  closed itself when the cursor left the dot for the bubble).
