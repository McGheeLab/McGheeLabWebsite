# V4.11 — The Report tag can be reached

## Goal
Gabe (16 Nov 2026): "how do you label an HTML as a report?" — then, after the answer (double-click the tag left of its name): "I don't see that, even though I have pushed all changes."

## What was wrong
V4.4 (13zzy) made a PDF or a saved web page a Report from the tag left of its name (`.bh > .num`, "pdf" / "html page"), by double-click, as on a component. But PDF and HTML cards have a **slim header** (13zg: their toolbar sits in the title row — `SLIM.BARS.pdf` since V3.9xx, `SLIM.BARS.html` since V3.961, image panels from the start), and `21-sync.css` hides the tag there (`.box.widget.slim > .bh > .num{display:none}`). So the tag holding the choice was never on screen: a real double-click could not reach it on any PDF, HTML page, saved website or image panel, in any build. V4.4's tests dispatched the double-click on the element itself, so they passed.

Checked at the same time: the lab's `main` has V4.0–V4.10 in order (the closed PRs were restacks whose content is all on `main`; only superseded `BUILD` lines and the round dome Gabe asked to replace differ), and the website runs V4.10 (deploy of 16 Nov 16:52, verified). Gabe's standalone `main` stops at V4.3: #24 (V4.4–V4.8, with Reports), #26 and #27 are open.

## Approach
- **The tag shows in the slim header** when it is a kind tag (`num.ck-tag`, added by 13zzy's `DocView.box` wrapper): on a PDF or HTML card always while Reports is on ("pdf", "html page", or "Report" in its colour); on an image panel once it has a kind ("Result"), so image panels without one keep their header as it was. `24-kinds.css`: `.box.widget.slim:is(.w-pdf,.w-html,.kinded-w) > .bh > .num.ck-tag{display:block…}` plus a dotted underline on hover.
- **The card's ⋯ menu offers the same choice:** "What kind is this page… / PDF… / image panel…" (its sub says what it is now, or what Report does), before Bridge, opening `CKINDS.pick` at the row. A `DocView.boxMenu` wrapper, the pattern References uses.
- **Wording:** Settings → Reports and the Atlas's empty Reports page say "double-click the tag left of its name, or ⋯ → What kind is this…".
- Reports off (as in the Simple preset): no tag on PDF/HTML cards and no menu row, as before; what was labelled keeps its kind.
