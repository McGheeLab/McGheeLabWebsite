# V4.73 — the Story kind's complexity can be edited again

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov 2026: "Complexity cannot be edited in story kind anymore, fix that."

The footer of a Story beat (13zzl-story.js `STORY.foot`) is a row of ten buttons, `button.sf-n`, 1 to 10. They were
there but invisible and unclickable, so the number could not be changed.

**What broke it:** V4.10 (commit 9d80cba, "the side panel of highlights and notes folds") added the rule
`.sf-n{display:none}` to `30-sidefold.css` for the count badge in the reader side panel's top bar (`span.sf-n`, made in
17g-sidefold.js). The Story's buttons (V3.955, `24-kinds.css`) use the same class name, so the rule hid all ten of them.
Found with `git log -S".sf-n{display:none}"`.

## What changed
- `30-sidefold.css`: the rule is now `.sf-top > .sf-n{display:none}`, so it only reaches the side panel's badge (always a
  direct child of `.sf-top`). Nothing else changes: the story's own classes stay, the folded-capsule rule in
  `36-look.css` is untouched.
- Test `story_cx_t.mjs`: real clicks on the numbers of two beats; the arc follows. It fails on the build before the fix.
