# V4.63 — Scientia: the Terms page as cards, in the new look

## Why
Gabe, 20 Nov: "Change terms to look like a new UI in the cards based on the newly added theme."

"The newly added theme" is Look and motion's edge-rail look (V4.42–V4.45). The Terms page was still a table of rows.

## What changed
Under Look and motion (`body:not(.f-off-look)`, `36-look.css`); with it off, the table is as it was.
- **Each term is a card** in a grid (`repeat(auto-fill, minmax(250px, 1fr))`) under its group's name, which stays pinned as you scroll. The card has the look's warm surface, hairline ring and soft shadow, with 12 px corners. It lifts under the pointer, and the one the keys are on has a teal ring.
- **Inside a card:**
  - its name, large, with when it was last used at its corner;
  - its project and notes (up to two lines);
  - its uses, with the word "use"/"uses" (`.tl-ul`, new, since the column names are not shown) and its bar;
  - its last 12 weeks on a tinted strip, with the change;
  - its structures and open barriers.
- **The table's column names go.** Sort stays in the bar above (Most used · Rising · Recent · A–Z).
- **The cards rise in once** when the page opens (`lk-rise`, staggered by `--i`; the `.tl-in` class lasts 0.9 s), not on every keystroke in the search. Movement (Full · Calm · Still) and reduced motion apply.
- **Keys** (`TermsView.nav`): ← → go to the next or previous card, ↑ ↓ to the card above or below in the same column, Enter opens the term.
- The At a glance tiles take the card surface too.
- Settings → Look and motion says so.
