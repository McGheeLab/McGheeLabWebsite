# V3.990 — Bridges at a note's edges, the bridge bar, History on shared notes

## Goal
Gabe asked for the notes a note is bridged with to show as circled arrows in the canvas's faded margin — on the left the notes what is pulled or bridged into it comes from, on the right the notes its components are bridged to — "really nice with minimal animation"; for a temporary bridge bar under the tabs to go back and forth, with the three bridges used most, reset every two weeks. From the prototypes (https://claude.ai/artifact/1YNSSitx9SyEtz8x2BeeiJ) he chose:

1. **Half-moons**, becoming **beads** in full screen.
2. The **Thread** bar.
3. The bar is closed unless the note is in one of the top three bridges; crossing a bridge any way opens it; leaving the notes for another tab closes it; **Alt+← / Alt+→** back to the notes you were bridging bring it back; × closes it until the next day; Settings can turn it off.
4. In the Lab Notebook, the "awaiting approval" chip goes; it is shown in a new **History** button, only on shared notes.

Not answered, taken as proposed: structures pulled in show on the left (a dashed ring); the counts are the same on every device (the settings record).

## Approach
- **One app module, `scientia/src/13zzv-bridgeway.js` (`BWAY`)**, on what the app has: `BRIDGE.out`, `IX.refsOfItem` / `IX.itemsOf`, `DocView.rectOf`, `Paper.prototype.apply` (wrapped: the circles follow the view), `DocView.fill` / `drawWires` (wrapped: built and measured), `App.refresh` (wrapped: the bar follows the page), `HIST.go` (wrapped: Alt+← / Alt+→), `HANDS.full()` (full screen, V3.980), `ctxMenu`, `saveSettings`.
- **Why beads in full screen:** there the screen's edge pans the note after a rest (13zzh, V3.980); a half-moon on the edge would sit where the push starts. Beads sit inside the margin, and the edge pan skips `.bw-mk`.
- **A crossing** is marked where it starts (`BWAY.arm(from, to, via)`): the circles, the underline's menu, "bridged from …", a structure's "appears in", the bar's pairs. `BWAY.route()` consumes it when `to` is on screen (or after 700 ms when the note was already open in a pane), counts the pair and moves the trail. A crossing from a note not on the trail starts a new trail.
- **Counts:** `settings.bridgeUse { since, n: { 'a|b': count }, at }`; `since` is the fortnight's first Monday counted from Mon 9 Nov 2026, so an old record reads as empty and every device agrees.
- **The bar** sits after `#tabs` in the header (a grid row 0fr → 1fr, 180 ms), so the canvas below gives it room. `changed()` redraws the page after every edit, so the bar repaints only when its signature changes.
- **History (lab layer §17):** the pending list is unchanged (`api/rm/shares/pending`); a note's share is its copy's `sharedFrom.share`, or for the owner `<uid>__d__<note>` / `<uid>__p__<project>` (the hub's `shareIdOf` with `safeIdPart`). In this note = the note itself or one of its cards (`recs`).
