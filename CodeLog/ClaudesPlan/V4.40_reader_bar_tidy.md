# V4.40 — The reader's bar, tidier; the side panel folds beside a note

## Goal
Gabe (19 Nov 2026): "Show it in a note button should be removed, and the cited in should go to the note when you click on the note it is cited in, without closing the reader. Don't show the cited in button anymore, as it already has its own small tab (include the number of cited in on that header instead). Clip already suggests. Remove the Close this tab button, as you can already close the reader or individual papers from the reading set. You should be able to input a page number in the page count and make the UI for that more compact along with the zoom UI. Have the title be on its own row, and the buttons just below it to be able to see more of the title. Clicking on the title brings you to its note. Remove the highlight button and instead, have the right sidebar collapsed in the read beside as well."

## Approach
- **The title gets its own row.** The bar (`RDOCK.build`, 13zzb) wraps (`.rd-bar2`). The title fills the first row and the buttons sit on the row under it, so more of the title shows. A click (or Enter) on the title goes to its note through `RDOCK.toNote(p)`: the component the paper is embedded in comes into view, lit, and the reader stays open. Reading sets (V4.41) carry the paper along when that note is in another project.
- **The page is a box you can type in.** `p.pgIn` + `p.pgN` ("/ 6") replace the "3 / 6" text. A click selects the number; Enter goes to the page (`RDOCK.goTo`: scrolled to, or turned to for a PDF shown page by page, clamped to the paper's pages); Esc leaves it. `RDOCK.onScroll` writes the number only while you are not typing in the box. The page and zoom groups are compact: 26 px pills with borderless buttons.
- **Buttons removed from the bar:**
  - the scissors (`CLIP.btn`): pointing at a figure already offers a clip;
  - the HTML page's scissors (13zzm), for the same reason;
  - Highlights (`p.hl`): kept, hidden (`.rd-hl`), as the place 13zze puts ✎ Note and Export ink labels;
  - Close (`.rd-x`): a tab's × closes. Without reading tabs (Settings) there is no tab, so a small ✕ stays;
  - In a note (`p.inB`, 13zzzb): the title goes to the note, and "◧ Back beside" on a card still works;
  - Cited in · N (17e `RDOCK.extras`).
- **Cited in.** The strip under the bar says "Cited in · N". A chip (or a row of its list) carries the paper into that note's reading set before going there (`RSET.carry`, V4.41).
- **The side panel folds beside a note too.** `SIDEFOLD.dock` no longer makes the panel Highlights' overlay. Beside a note it has a fold of its own, folded at first (`body.sf-fold-b`, `_ls 'sideFoldB'`; `SIDEFOLD.setB`, `foldedAt`), whatever Fold the side panel says, since there is no other way to open it. Open, it lies over the paper (340 px). Folded, in every reader, it is a small capsule at the right edge rather than a 30 px strip: the highlighter icon with a badge of its count (`.sf-ic`, `.sf-k`, the panel's heading added up), its title in the tooltip. A click opens it, and › folds it again. The capsule is the B design Gabe picked ("Edge rail and bookshelf"); the overhaul PR adds its shelf dots. Popups keep no panel. `rd-side-on` (the desktop lab's RM reader) still opens it.
- **Settings:**
  - Fold the side panel: words and preview (folded, the round button);
  - Fit, zoom and full screen: words, the preview's two-row bar, and "✕, in full screen" saying it applies with reading tabs off.
