# V3.420 — resize the AREA boundary, not the figure

> Alex: "i dont want to resize the figure directly, I want to resize the
> boundary of the section and the figure just resizes to match."

## What shipped (rm/js/paper-doc-view.js + rm/css/paper-doc-view.css)

- Selecting a figure in a layout session now draws a **`.pdv-area-frame`
  boundary** (sheet-level overlay, dashed blue, zoom-corrected via
  `_syncAreaFrame`) around the figure's ALLOCATION (its box: image +
  caption) — and the E/W/S/SE handles ride the FRAME's edges instead of
  sitting on the figure. You drag the boundary; the figure auto-fits the
  area you give it (the V3.405 PaperFigFit machinery unchanged — image
  maximized around the caption, width snaps, span crossover, height cap
  with release-past-natural).
- `_onResizeMove` re-syncs the frame every step (the boundary is what
  the user sees moving); `_cancelResize` snaps it back; commit's
  re-render re-attaches at final geometry via `_applyLayoutSelection`.
- Frame is pointer-events:none except its handles — desk clicks/drags
  through it still work. `_detachResizeHandles` removes it.

## Verification

- `node --check`; all four harnesses green (interaction is DOM-only).
- Browser (Alex): select a figure in ⬒ Layout → a dashed boundary frames
  the figure area with handles on the boundary (none on the image);
  dragging the boundary resizes the area and the image re-fits live
  inside it, caption untouched; Esc snaps the boundary back.
