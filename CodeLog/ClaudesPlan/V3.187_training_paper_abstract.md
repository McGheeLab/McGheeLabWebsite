## Goal

Teach a student to write an Abstract that survives the 8-second test
(a reader skims it in 8 seconds and decides whether to read the
paper). The hardest section because it's both the shortest and the
most overdetermined.

## Format

Lecture-style. Two interactive widgets:

1. **Word-count budgeting** — slider for the abstract's total word
   budget (typical journal limits 150 / 250 / 300). Shows recommended
   words-per-job (motivation 20% / method 20% / result 40% / impact
   20%) and warns when the user's draft (pasted in) skews wrong.
2. **Structured vs unstructured comparator** — same content in both
   shapes. Some journals require labelled subsections (Background /
   Methods / Results / Conclusion); some don't. Show both, explain
   when each is required.

## Sections (9)

1. The Abstract's job — survive an 8-second skim.
2. Write last, not first.
3. The four-part shape: motivation + method + result + impact.
4. Voice: Storyteller + Reporter blend (per V3.182).
5. Word budgeting — typical 150 / 250 / 300-word ceilings.
6. Structured (labelled) vs unstructured abstracts.
7. The opening sentence — different rules than the Introduction's.
8. The closing sentence — your one shot at impact.
9. Capstone — write your abstract from scratch in 30 minutes.
