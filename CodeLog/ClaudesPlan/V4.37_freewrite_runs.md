# V4.37 — Freewrite: sentences no rule reads go on with the component before, or are one text component

## Goal
Gabe (18 Nov 2026): "I want the default to be that a string of sentences not recognized are their own text component in between the links. For example, describing a verdict in a sentence and you keep describing in more sentences with no trigger of other components, it remains in that verdict. Manual editing can override that. Keep the other every sentence into a component as a setting."

## Approach
- **The new default** (`FREEW.convert`, `o.plain` = `run`): after the rules and your own associations (V4.34), the sentences are walked in order.
  - **A sentence no rule read and no association claimed:**
    - **Stays in the component before it** when the sentence before it made exactly one component and no link — a Verdict you keep describing.
    - **Otherwise** it starts a text component of its own (`p.run`), and the next unread sentences join that: "their own text component in between the links".
  - A joined sentence is folded into its component at the end: the component's words grow, each sentence keeping its full stop, and its stretch in the text is coloured as that component. So the preview, the layout and Apply need no change. Each joined stretch keeps its own words (`span.own`), so a click finds it.
- **Manual editing overrides it:**
  - A click on a joined sentence opens its menu with "✓ Stays in the Verdict before it".
  - **Its own component**, a kind, a structure or a task takes it out (`fw.over[its words]`). "Stays in … before it" puts it back.
  - **Plain words** leaves it in no component; the sentences after it still go on with the component before.
  - A run component split from its component offers "Stays in … before it" too.
- **A Verdict rule** (Gabe's example): "The verdict is (that) …", "Verdict: …", "We conclude that …", "In conclusion, …", "The answer is (that) …" make a Verdict. The rules had none.
- **Settings → Freewrite → "Sentences no rule reads":**
  - **Stay in the component before** (default);
  - **Each its own component** (the old "Becomes a component", kept as Gabe asked);
  - **Stay words** (the old default).

  A setting already saved keeps its value. The live preview shows a barrier with the sentence after it staying in it.
