# V4.8 — The monthly review

## Goal
Gabe (16 Nov 2026): the weekly review becomes a monthly review with a rich report of deliverables (takeaways and results per note), every unassigned daily-note component, and a Review tab on par with the others that streamlines the work. Design: the Monthly review artifact; Gabe took its picks: a month opens the day after it ends (its last weekend as a setting); takeaways and results lead the deliverables, with resolved barriers, answered questions, Reports and References under them; Unassigned takes daily and quick notes; the monthly replaces the weekly.

## Approach
- `18b-monthly.js` (`MONTHLY`) after `18-review.js`: `ReviewView.render` delegates to it (the weekly bet stays as `renderWeek`, for a weekly pulse later); `IX.pendingReviews` counts waiting months; the `reviews` store keeps a record per month (`YYYY-MM`: summary, plan, picks, completedAt, docId).
- The report reads what the notes already hold: items (takeaway, result, resolution with `resolves`, question), `CKINDS.counted` (Insight, Result components, V4.4's Result image panels), `TARC.through` for the term that joined a barrier to its resolution, `answers` wires, `IX.openBarriers`, Reports (`ckind: 'report'`) and References (`isRef`), the day index for time.
- Unassigned uses the day note's unfiled rule (13l-day) plus bridges and waiting floats; the suggestion is the Portfolio note sharing the most terms. Filing is a bridge (`BRIDGE.make`) recorded in `b.filed`, as the day note's File does. The inbox keeps its own history for u, because a bridge's `M.addBlock` → `changed()` starts a new journal group.
- Floats come from V4.7 when present (`FLOATS.waiting/placed/mark/old/open`, and `place`/`letGo`/`unmark` when it has them); every use is guarded.
- Completing writes the review note as the weekly did (lanes → its components, the plan → its synthesis).
- Settings → Monthly review: when a month opens, what the report lists, what Unassigned takes in, the keys, the comparison; the preview draws a report card and an inbox row.
