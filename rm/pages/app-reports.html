App crash reports

When the Ourlab phone app closes while someone is using it, or one of its pages stops, the app writes a report and sends it here the next time it opens (build 19 and later). Each one carries the phone model, iOS, the app build, the last things that happened in the app, and — for a real crash — Apple's own crash report. Copy puts the whole report on the clipboard, ready to paste to Claude.
