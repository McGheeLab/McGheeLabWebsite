Gates the style list below. Every style today is a journal style — grant formats (specific-aims pages, NIH margins…) are a planned follow-up.
Submission follows the journal's float placement conventions (locked figures fall back to floats). Preprint pins locked figures exactly where you placed them ([H]).
Journal page compiles the published two-column look the Document view shows. Submission manuscript is what most journals ask you to upload (single column, often double-spaced with line numbers).
Sets \textfloatsep/\floatsep for the whole paper (Normal emits nothing — class defaults). Per-figure nudges layer on top in the Document view.
How far a paragraph fills a column before breaking to the next page in the Document view. Tight leaves the least blank (allows a one-line orphan); Loose keeps more of a paragraph together. Preview only — the compiled PDF fills columns via LaTeX.
Numbers sections and subheadings. Auto matches the journal (e.g. Nature is unnumbered, IEEE is I / A); On forces numbering even on unnumbered styles; Off suppresses it. Applies to the preview and the compiled PDF (secnumdepth).
