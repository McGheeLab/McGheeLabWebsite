Other PCard form field tips
  • Receipt $$ Amount: Exact total from the itemized receipt — must match to the cent.
  • Capital Asset? Yes if unit cost ≥ $5,000 with > 1 year useful life. Otherwise No.
  • Tax Exempt? Yes if you used UA's tax-exempt status at point of sale; many online vendors charge sales tax by default — request a refund of the tax portion.
  • Asset Location = Ship Address? Yes for normal lab equipment.
  • File Upload: Itemized receipt as PDF; combine multiples into one file (≤10 files). Must show date, total, line items, payment method.
  • Funding Account: Sponsored research → typically HEFC code 20 (Research).
  • Additional Comments: Travel Authorization #, sole-source justification reference, split-funding rationale.
  • Used / reseller equipment: May require a separate Sole Source Justification attached.
